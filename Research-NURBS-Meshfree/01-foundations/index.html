A degree-p B-spline is a blend of two degree-(p − 1) B-splines: the left one weighted by how far ξ has moved into its support, the right one by how far ξ still is from the end of the next support. Darjah 0 bernilai 1 pada satu knot span dan 0 di tempat lain.

1. The research problem: exact geometry meets meshfree flexibilityMasalah penyelidikan: geometri tepat bertemu fleksibiliti meshfree

≈ 6 min

A finite element mesh only approximates the geometry that a designer drew in CAD. The CAD model itself is made of NURBS: smooth, exact, and described by a handful of control points. Two families of methods try to close the gap between design and analysis, and this research area lives where they meet.Mesh finite element hanya menghampiri geometri yang dilukis oleh pereka dalam CAD. Model CAD itu sendiri dibina daripada NURBS: licin, tepat, dan diterangkan oleh beberapa control point sahaja. Dua keluarga kaedah cuba merapatkan jurang antara rekabentuk dan analisis, dan bidang penyelidikan ini terletak di tempat kedua-duanya bertemu.

Isogeometric analysis (IGA)Isogeometric analysis (IGA)Use the NURBS basis that describes the CAD geometry as the basis of the analysis too (Hughes, Cottrell & Bazilevs, CMAME 194 (2005) 4135–4195). The geometry is exact at every refinement; the price is the tensor-product structure of the patches, which makes local refinement and complex topology awkward.Guna NURBS basis yang menerangkan geometri CAD sebagai basis analisis juga (Hughes, Cottrell & Bazilevs, CMAME 194 (2005) 4135–4195). Geometri kekal tepat pada setiap refinement; harganya ialah struktur tensor-product bagi patch, yang menyukarkan local refinement dan topologi yang kompleks.
Meshfree methodsMeshfree methodBuild the shape functions from scattered nodes and their supports, with no element connectivity: EFG (Belytschko, Lu & Gu 1994), RKPM (Liu, Jun & Zhang 1995), RPIM and others. Nodes can be added anywhere; the price is paid at the boundary (no Kronecker delta, so essential conditions need care), in the integration, and in the cost of each shape function.Bina shape function daripada node yang bertaburan dan support-nya, tanpa element connectivity: EFG (Belytschko, Lu & Gu 1994), RKPM (Liu, Jun & Zhang 1995), RPIM dan lain-lain. Node boleh ditambah di mana-mana; harganya dibayar di sempadan (tiada Kronecker delta, jadi essential condition perlu dikendalikan dengan teliti), dalam integration, dan dalam kos setiap shape function.

2. B-splines from a knot vectorB-spline daripada knot vector

≈ 10 min

A knot vector is a non-decreasing list of parameter values Ξ = {t1, t2, …, tn+p+1}. It defines n basis functions of degree p. An open knot vector repeats its first and last values p + 1 times, so the curve starts and ends at its first and last control points. The intervals [ti, ti+1) of non-zero length are the knot spans.Knot vector ialah senarai nilai parameter yang tidak menurun Ξ = {t1, t2, …, tn+p+1}. Ia mentakrifkan n basis function berdarjah p. Knot vector open mengulang nilai pertama dan terakhirnya sebanyak p + 1 kali, jadi curve bermula dan berakhir pada control point pertama dan terakhir. Selang [ti, ti+1) yang panjangnya bukan sifar ialah knot span.

GlossaryGlosari

What to do Terms stay in English, as in the papers; the explanations follow your language. Type to filter.Apa yang perlu dibuat Istilah kekal dalam English, seperti dalam kertas kerja; penerangan mengikut bahasa anda. Taip untuk menapis.

knot vectorNon-decreasing list of parameter values that defines a B-spline basis.Senarai nilai parameter yang tidak menurun yang mentakrifkan B-spline basis.
open knot vectorA knot vector whose end values are repeated p + 1 times; the curve then interpolates its end control points.Knot vector yang nilai hujungnya berulang p + 1 kali; curve kemudian interpolate control point di hujungnya.
knot spanAn interval [ti, ti+1) of non-zero length; p + 1 basis functions are non-zero on it.Selang [ti, ti+1) yang panjangnya bukan sifar; p + 1 basis function bukan sifar padanya.
B-spline basis functionPiecewise polynomial NI,p built by the Cox–de Boor recursion; non-negative, local, a partition of unity.Polinomial cebis demi cebis NI,p yang dibina dengan rekursi Cox–de Boor; tidak negatif, setempat, satu partition of unity.
Cox–de Boor recursionBuilds NI,p from two degree-(p − 1) neighbours, Eq. (2.2).Membina NI,p daripada dua jiran darjah (p − 1), Pers. (2.2).
partition of unityThe functions sum to one at every point.Function berjumlah satu pada setiap titik.
local supportEach function is non-zero only on a small interval: p + 1 knot spans for a B-spline, radius a for a kernel.Setiap function bukan sifar hanya pada selang yang kecil: p + 1 knot span bagi B-spline, jejari a bagi kernel.
Greville abscissaAverage of the p interior knots of a support, ξ̄I; with them the B-splines reproduce ξ.Purata p knot dalaman bagi satu support, ξ̄I; dengannya B-spline reproduce ξ.
NURBSNon-uniform rational B-splines: weighted, normalised B-splines; exact for conic sections.Non-uniform rational B-spline: B-spline yang diberi weight dan dinormalkan; tepat untuk conic section.
weightPositive factor λI of a NURBS basis function; changes the shape without moving the control point.Faktor positif λI bagi NURBS basis function; mengubah bentuk tanpa menggerakkan control point.
control pointCoefficient of a spline geometry; the curve lies in the convex hull of its control points.Pekali bagi geometri spline; curve terletak dalam convex hull control point-nya.
geometry mapThe function x(ξ, η) that takes the parameter square to the physical patch.Function x(ξ, η) yang membawa segi empat parameter kepada patch fizikal.
Jacobian (row convention)[J] with rows ∂(x, y)/∂ξ and ∂(x, y)/∂η, so that {∂f/∂x} = [J]−1{∂f/∂ξ}.[J] dengan baris ∂(x, y)/∂ξ dan ∂(x, y)/∂η, supaya {∂f/∂x} = [J]−1{∂f/∂ξ}.
isogeometric analysis (IGA)Analysis with the NURBS basis of the CAD geometry.Analisis dengan NURBS basis bagi geometri CAD.
meshfree methodA method whose shape functions are built from nodes and supports, without elements.Kaedah yang shape function-nya dibina daripada node dan support, tanpa element.
reproducing kernel (RK)Shape function ΨI = kernel × polynomial correction that reproduces polynomials up to degree p.Shape function ΨI = kernel × pembetulan polinomial yang reproduce polinomial sehingga darjah p.
reproducing conditionsΣ ΨI(x){H(xI)} = {H(x)}: polynomials up to degree p are returned exactly.Σ ΨI(x){H(xI)} = {H(x)}: polinomial sehingga darjah p dipulangkan dengan tepat.
moment matrix[M(x)] = Σ {H(xJ)}{H(xJ)}T WJ(x); must be invertible.[M(x)] = Σ {H(xJ)}{H(xJ)}T WJ(x); mesti invertible.
kernel (window) functionNon-negative bell-shaped function WI centred on node I with support radius a.Function berbentuk loceng yang tidak negatif WI berpusat pada node I dengan jejari support a.
support radius · dilationa = ca h: the reach of a kernel, as a multiple ca of a node spacing h.a = ca h: jangkauan kernel, sebagai gandaan ca bagi jarak node h.
Shepard functionThe p = 0 RK function WI/Σ WJ.RK function bagi p = 0, iaitu WI/Σ WJ.
shifted basis{H(x − xI)} instead of {H(xI)}: same functions, better-conditioned [M].{H(x − xI)} dan bukannya {H(xI)}: function yang sama, [M] dengan conditioning yang lebih baik.
blossom vector{QI} = {1, qI,1, …, qI,p}, built from the interior knots of a support, Eq. (5.1).{QI} = {1, qI,1, …, qI,p}, dibina daripada knot dalaman bagi satu support, Pers. (5.1).
Marsden's identityΣ NI,p(ξ){QI} = {H(ξ)}: B-splines reproduce polynomials with blossom vectors.Σ NI,p(ξ){QI} = {H(ξ)}: B-spline reproduce polinomial dengan blossom vector.
elementary symmetric polynomialel: sum of all products of l distinct numbers from a list.el: hasil tambah semua hasil darab l nombor berbeza daripada satu senarai.
essential boundary conditionA prescribed value of the unknown; needs special treatment when ΨI(xJ) ≠ δIJ.Nilai unknown yang ditetapkan; memerlukan rawatan khas apabila ΨI(xJ) ≠ δIJ.
patch testCheck that a method reproduces a linear (or higher) field exactly with its own quadrature and boundary treatment.Semakan bahawa sesuatu kaedah reproduce linear field (atau lebih tinggi) dengan tepat menggunakan quadrature dan rawatan sempadannya sendiri.
known-method limitThe settings at which a new method reduces to a known one (FEM, IGA, plain RK).Tetapan di mana kaedah baharu menjadi kaedah yang sudah dikenali (FEM, IGA, RK biasa).
hidden parameterA choice a paper does not state but its results depend on (support convention, norm quadrature, BC reading).Pilihan yang tidak dinyatakan oleh artikel tetapi keputusannya bergantung padanya (konvensyen support, quadrature norm, tafsiran BC).
