B-spline and RK Explorer

Dashed: B-splines NI. Solid: reproducing-kernel functions ΨI. With blossom vectors and B-spline supports the two coincide for any kernel; with node monomials they do not.Putus-putus: B-spline NI. Garis penuh: reproducing-kernel function ΨI. Dengan blossom vector dan support B-spline, kedua-duanya sama untuk sebarang kernel; dengan node monomial, tidak.

Controls (top): degree, number of knot spans, the middle interior knot, which functions to build, the kernel shape, the support factor ca (standard RK only) and which function to draw thick.

Kawalan (atas): darjah, bilangan knot span, knot dalaman tengah, function yang hendak dibina, bentuk kernel, faktor support ca (RK biasa sahaja) dan function yang dilukis tebal.

Plot: knots are marked under the axis (×k: repeated k times; ▲: the knot you can move); the dot is the Greville point of the thick function. Hover or touch for values.

Plot: knot ditanda di bawah paksi (×k: berulang k kali; ▲: knot yang boleh digerakkan); titik ialah Greville point bagi function tebal. Hover atau sentuh untuk nilai.

Readouts: the largest errors over 600 points; Table view: the thick function at ten points.

Bacaan: ralat terbesar atas 600 titik; Paparan jadual: function tebal pada sepuluh titik.
