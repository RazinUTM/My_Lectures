1 Why influence lines?1 Mengapa influence line?

Key pointsIsi penting
  • An influence line gives one response at a fixed point as a unit load moves.
  • It is not a shear-force or bending-moment diagram: there the loads are fixed and the section moves.
  • Superposition makes it reusable for any pattern of loads.
  • Influence line memberi satu response pada titik tetap semasa unit load bergerak.
  • Ia bukan shear-force atau bending-moment diagram: dalam diagram itu load tetap dan section yang berubah.
  • Superposition membolehkan ia digunakan semula untuk sebarang susunan load.

Most structures are designed for loads that stay where they are: the weight of a floor, the water in a tank. A bridge, a crane girder or a gantry rail carries loads that move, and the designer must answer a question that a shear-force diagram cannot: where must the vehicle stand to produce the largest reaction at a pier, the largest shear at a section, or the largest moment at midspan? The influence line answers it directly.

Kebanyakan struktur direka untuk load yang kekal di tempatnya: berat lantai, air dalam tangki. Jambatan, crane girder atau gantry rail pula membawa load yang bergerak, dan pereka perlu menjawab soalan yang tidak dapat dijawab oleh shear-force diagram: di mana kenderaan perlu berada untuk menghasilkan reaction terbesar pada pier, shear terbesar pada sesuatu section, atau moment terbesar di midspan? Influence line menjawabnya secara terus.

DefinitionTakrifThe influence line of a response quantity \(R\) (a reaction, a shear force, a bending moment, a deflection) at a fixed point of a structure is the graph of that quantity against the position \(x\) of a unit concentrated load moving across the structure. Its ordinate is written \(\eta_R(x)\) and has the units of \(R\) per unit load (dimensionless for a reaction).Influence line bagi suatu response \(R\) (reaction, shear force, bending moment, deflection) pada titik yang tetap ialah graf kuantiti itu melawan kedudukan \(x\) bagi unit concentrated load yang bergerak merentasi struktur. Ordinate-nya ditulis \(\eta_R(x)\) dan mempunyai unit \(R\) per unit load (tanpa dimensi bagi reaction).

What the ordinate meansMaksud ordinate\(V\) or \(M\) at that section, for the given loads\(V\) atau \(M\) pada section itu, untuk load yang diberi\(R\) at the fixed point, for a unit load standing at \(x\)\(R\) pada titik tetap, untuk unit load di \(x\)

For a linear elastic structure the response is proportional to the load and the effects of several loads add (superposition). That is what makes the influence line useful: once \(\eta_R(x)\) is known for a unit load, the effect of any pattern of concentrated or distributed loads follows by multiplication and addition (Section 5).

Bagi struktur linear elastic, response berkadar dengan load dan kesan beberapa load boleh dijumlahkan (superposition). Inilah yang menjadikan influence line berguna: sebaik sahaja \(\eta_R(x)\) diketahui untuk unit load, kesan sebarang susunan concentrated load atau distributed load diperoleh melalui pendaraban dan penambahan (Section 5).

2 The laboratory model2 Model makmal

Key pointsIsi penting
  • ABC and DEF: beams on two supports with a 190 mm cantilever; CD: suspended span on hinges C and D.
  • 4 unknown reactions, 2 equilibrium equations + 2 hinge conditions: statically determinate.
  • So every influence line is straight-segmented, kinked only at hinges.
  • Reactions positive upwards; \(R_C\), \(R_D\) positive pressing down on the tips.
  • ABC dan DEF: beam atas dua support dengan cantilever 190 mm; CD: suspended span pada hinge C dan D.
  • 4 reaction yang tidak diketahui, 2 persamaan equilibrium + 2 syarat hinge: statically determinate.
  • Maka setiap influence line terdiri daripada segmen lurus, berbengkok hanya di hinge.
  • Reaction positif ke atas; \(R_C\), \(R_D\) positif apabila menekan ke bawah pada hujung cantilever.

The model is a cantilever bridge of the type patented by Heinrich Gerber in 1866 and used, at a very different scale, in the Forth Bridge: two anchor spans with cantilever arms, and a suspended span that rests on the tips of the arms through hinges.

Model ini ialah cantilever bridge jenis yang dipatenkan oleh Heinrich Gerber pada 1866 dan digunakan, pada skala yang jauh berbeza, pada Forth Bridge: dua anchor span dengan cantilever arm, dan suspended span yang terletak di hujung arm melalui hinge.

Members. Beam ABC is simply supported at A and B with a cantilever BC of 190 mm. Beam DEF is its mirror image. The suspended span CD (250 mm) rests on the cantilever tips: the hinges transmit a vertical force but no bending moment.

Anggota. Beam ABC disokong mudah di A dan B dengan cantilever BC sepanjang 190 mm. Beam DEF ialah imej cerminnya. Suspended span CD (250 mm) terletak di hujung cantilever: hinge memindahkan daya menegak tetapi tidak memindahkan bending moment.

Determinacy. No horizontal load is applied, so there are four unknown vertical reactions \(R_A, R_B, R_E, R_F\). Statics gives two useful equations for the whole structure (\(\sum F_y = 0\), \(\sum M = 0\)) and each internal hinge one more (\(M = 0\) at C and at D): four equations for four unknowns. The structure is statically determinate, every influence line follows from equilibrium alone with no need for \(EI\), and every influence line is made of straight segments.

Determinacy. Tiada load mendatar dikenakan, jadi terdapat empat reaction menegak yang tidak diketahui, \(R_A, R_B, R_E, R_F\). Statik memberi dua persamaan berguna bagi keseluruhan struktur (\(\sum F_y = 0\), \(\sum M = 0\)) dan setiap hinge dalaman memberi satu lagi (\(M = 0\) di C dan di D): empat persamaan untuk empat anu. Struktur ini statically determinate; setiap influence line diperoleh daripada equilibrium sahaja tanpa memerlukan \(EI\), dan setiap influence line terdiri daripada segmen lurus.

Sign convention. Support reactions are positive upwards. The hinge forces \(R_C\) and \(R_D\) are the forces the suspended span presses on the cantilever tips (positive downwards on the tip); they are what the gauges at C and D register. Positions are measured from A, \(0 \le x \le 1130\) mm.

Sign convention. Support reaction positif ke atas. Hinge force \(R_C\) dan \(R_D\) ialah daya yang dikenakan oleh suspended span ke atas hujung cantilever (positif ke bawah pada hujung); inilah yang direkod oleh gauge di C dan D. Kedudukan diukur dari A, \(0 \le x \le 1130\) mm.

3 Deriving the influence lines3 Menerbitkan influence line

Key pointsIsi penting
  • Analyse the suspended span first; take moments about a support every time.
  • On ABC: \(\eta_A = (250-x)/250\), \(\eta_B = x/250\); at the tip C: −0.76 and 1.76.
  • On CD: \(\eta_C = (690-x)/250\), \(\eta_D = (x-440)/250\); then \(\eta_A = -0.76\,\eta_C\), \(\eta_B = 1.76\,\eta_C\).
  • Check: \(\eta_A+\eta_B+\eta_E+\eta_F = 1\) at every \(x\).
  • Analisis suspended span dahulu; ambil moment terhadap support setiap kali.
  • Pada ABC: \(\eta_A = (250-x)/250\), \(\eta_B = x/250\); di hujung C: −0.76 dan 1.76.
  • Pada CD: \(\eta_C = (690-x)/250\), \(\eta_D = (x-440)/250\); kemudian \(\eta_A = -0.76\,\eta_C\), \(\eta_B = 1.76\,\eta_C\).
  • Semakan: \(\eta_A+\eta_B+\eta_E+\eta_F = 1\) bagi setiap \(x\).

The derivation uses one idea three times: take moments about a support. The order matters: the suspended span is analysed first, because it is the only member whose loads reach the others.

Derivation ini menggunakan satu idea sebanyak tiga kali: ambil moment terhadap support. Urutan penting: suspended span dianalisis dahulu, kerana hanya anggota ini yang load-nya sampai kepada anggota lain.

Step 1 · Unit load on beam ABCLangkah 1 · Unit load pada beam ABC \((0 \le x \le 440)\)

Isolate ABC (panel a). It carries the unit load at \(x\) and the reactions \(R_A\), \(R_B\); the hinge at C is unloaded because the suspended span carries nothing. Moments about B:Asingkan ABC (panel a). Ia membawa unit load di \(x\) serta reaction \(R_A\), \(R_B\); hinge di C tidak dibebani kerana suspended span tidak membawa apa-apa. Moment terhadap B:

\[ R_A\cdot 250 - 1\cdot(250-x) = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; \eta_A(x) = \frac{250-x}{250},\qquad \eta_B(x) = 1-\eta_A(x) = \frac{x}{250}. \]

Nothing in this result stops at \(x = 250\): for a load on the cantilever the lever arm \((250 - x)\) is negative, so \(\eta_A\) is negative (support A must hold the beam down) and \(\eta_B\) exceeds 1. At the tip, \(\eta_A(440) = -190/250 = -0.76\) and \(\eta_B(440) = 440/250 = 1.76\).Tiada apa dalam keputusan ini yang berhenti di \(x = 250\): bagi load pada cantilever, lever arm \((250 - x)\) adalah negatif, maka \(\eta_A\) negatif (support A perlu menahan beam ke bawah) dan \(\eta_B\) melebihi 1. Di hujung, \(\eta_A(440) = -190/250 = -0.76\) dan \(\eta_B(440) = 440/250 = 1.76\).

Step 2 · Unit load on the suspended span CDLangkah 2 · Unit load pada suspended span CD \((440 \le x \le 690)\)

CD is a simply supported beam of span 250 mm resting on the hinges (panel b), so the hinge forces follow the lever rule:CD ialah simply supported beam dengan span 250 mm yang terletak pada hinge (panel b), jadi hinge force mengikut lever rule:

\[ \eta_C(x) = \frac{690-x}{250},\qquad \eta_D(x) = \frac{x-440}{250},\qquad \eta_C+\eta_D = 1. \]

Step 3 · Carry the hinge force into beam ABCLangkah 3 · Pindahkan hinge force ke beam ABC

With the load on CD, beam ABC carries only the downward force \(\eta_C\) at its tip (panel c). This is Step 1 with the load \(\eta_C\) standing at \(x = 440\):Apabila load berada pada CD, beam ABC hanya membawa daya ke bawah \(\eta_C\) di hujungnya (panel c). Ini sama seperti Langkah 1 dengan load \(\eta_C\) di \(x = 440\):

\[ \eta_A(x) = \eta_C(x)\,\frac{250-440}{250} = -0.76\,\eta_C(x),\qquad \eta_B(x) = \eta_C(x)\,\frac{440}{250} = 1.76\,\eta_C(x). \]

At C both expressions for \(\eta_A\) give \(-0.76\), so the line is continuous; at D, \(\eta_C = 0\) and therefore \(\eta_A = \eta_B = 0\).Di C kedua-dua ungkapan bagi \(\eta_A\) memberi \(-0.76\), jadi garisnya selanjar; di D, \(\eta_C = 0\) dan oleh itu \(\eta_A = \eta_B = 0\).

Step 4 · Unit load on beam DEFLangkah 4 · Unit load pada beam DEF \((690 \le x \le 1130)\)

DEF is the mirror image of ABC about the centre of the model (\(x = 565\) mm), with E playing the part of B and F that of A:DEF ialah imej cermin ABC terhadap tengah model (\(x = 565\) mm), dengan E mengambil peranan B dan F peranan A:

\[ \eta_F(x) = \frac{x-880}{250},\qquad \eta_E(x) = \frac{1130-x}{250}. \]

For a load on CD the hinge force \(\eta_D\) is carried by DEF exactly as \(\eta_C\) was by ABC: \(\eta_E = 1.76\,\eta_D\), \(\eta_F = -0.76\,\eta_D\). A load on ABC never reaches DEF, and vice versa, because the suspended span is then unloaded and transmits nothing.Bagi load pada CD, hinge force \(\eta_D\) dibawa oleh DEF sama seperti \(\eta_C\) dibawa oleh ABC: \(\eta_E = 1.76\,\eta_D\), \(\eta_F = -0.76\,\eta_D\). Load pada ABC tidak pernah sampai ke DEF, dan sebaliknya, kerana suspended span ketika itu tidak dibebani dan tidak memindahkan apa-apa.

Step 5 · Assemble and checkLangkah 5 · Gabung dan semak

Three checks catch most mistakes: (i) \(\eta_A+\eta_B+\eta_E+\eta_F = 1\) for every \(x\); (ii) the two formulae agree at the hinges; (iii) the slopes are \(-1/250\) per mm on ABC and \(+0.76/250\) on CD, and a kink can occur only at a hinge, never at B, which lies inside the rigid beam ABC.Tiga semakan menangkap kebanyakan kesilapan: (i) \(\eta_A+\eta_B+\eta_E+\eta_F = 1\) bagi setiap \(x\); (ii) dua formula bersetuju di hinge; (iii) slope ialah \(-1/250\) per mm pada ABC dan \(+0.76/250\) pada CD, dan bengkokan hanya boleh berlaku di hinge, tidak sekali-kali di B, yang terletak di dalam rigid beam ABC.

4 The Müller–Breslau principle4 Prinsip Müller–Breslau

Key pointsIsi penting
  • Release the restraint, impose a unit displacement: the deflected shape is the influence line.
  • Exact ordinates for determinate structures (rigid links); shape only for indeterminate ones.
  • Kinks only at hinges and at the released restraint.
  • Lepaskan restraint, kenakan unit displacement: deflected shape ialah influence line.
  • Ordinate tepat bagi struktur determinate (rigid link); bentuk sahaja bagi struktur indeterminate.
  • Bengkokan hanya di hinge dan di restraint yang dilepaskan.
Müller–Breslau principlePrinsip Müller–BreslauRemove the restraint corresponding to the response \(R\) and give the released structure a unit displacement in the direction of \(R\). The deflected shape of the structure is the influence line of \(R\).Buang restraint yang sepadan dengan response \(R\) dan berikan released structure satu unit displacement pada arah \(R\). Deflected shape struktur itu ialah influence line bagi \(R\).

For \(R_A\): remove the support at A and lift that end by one unit. Beam ABC is rigid between its support and the hinge, so it rotates about B: A rises by 1 and the tip C, 190 mm on the other side of B, drops by \(190/250 = 0.76\). The suspended span follows C and stays straight, dropping linearly to zero at D; beam DEF does not move. The result is exactly the influence line of Section 3, signs included.

Bagi \(R_A\): buang support di A dan angkat hujung itu sebanyak satu unit. Beam ABC adalah rigid antara support dan hinge, jadi ia berputar terhadap B: A naik sebanyak 1 dan hujung C, 190 mm di sebelah lain B, turun sebanyak \(190/250 = 0.76\). Suspended span mengikut C dan kekal lurus, turun secara linear ke sifar di D; beam DEF tidak bergerak. Hasilnya tepat sama dengan influence line dalam Section 3, termasuk tanda.

Try it:Cuba: release a restraint and impose the displacement.lepaskan satu restraint dan kenakan displacement.
1.00

Two remarks. The principle gives exact ordinates only for statically determinate structures, where the released structure is a mechanism of straight rigid links; for an indeterminate beam the released structure bends and the principle gives the shape only. And the kinks of the deflected shape lie at the hinges and at the released restraint, never inside a rigid member: a quick visual check of any influence line you compute.

Dua catatan. Prinsip ini memberi ordinate yang tepat hanya bagi struktur statically determinate, di mana released structure ialah mechanism yang terdiri daripada rigid link yang lurus; bagi beam indeterminate, released structure melentur dan prinsip ini hanya memberi bentuk. Selain itu, bengkokan pada deflected shape terletak di hinge dan di restraint yang dilepaskan, tidak sekali-kali di dalam anggota rigid: semakan visual yang pantas bagi mana-mana influence line yang anda kira.

5 Using an influence line5 Menggunakan influence line

Key pointsIsi penting
  • \(R = \sum P_i\,\eta_R(x_i)\); for a UDL, \(R = w\times\) area under the line.
  • Piecewise-linear lines: the extreme of a load train occurs with one load on a vertex.
  • Compound load of Test 2: most negative \(R_A = -4.94\) kgf with the 5 kg mass at C.
  • A UDL over the whole model gives \(R_A = -42.2\,w\): the deck lifts at A.
  • \(R = \sum P_i\,\eta_R(x_i)\); bagi UDL, \(R = w\times\) area di bawah garis.
  • Garis piecewise linear: nilai ekstrem bagi load train berlaku apabila satu load berada pada vertex.
  • Compound load Test 2: \(R_A\) paling negatif \(= -4.94\) kgf dengan jisim 5 kg di C.
  • UDL pada seluruh model memberi \(R_A = -42.2\,w\): deck terangkat di A.

By superposition, for a single load \(P\) at \(x_1\), a series of loads \(P_i\) at \(x_i\), and a uniformly distributed load \(w\) between \(a\) and \(b\):

Melalui superposition, bagi satu load \(P\) di \(x_1\), satu siri load \(P_i\) di \(x_i\), dan uniformly distributed load \(w\) antara \(a\) dan \(b\):

\[ R = P\,\eta_R(x_1),\qquad R = \sum_i P_i\,\eta_R(x_i),\qquad R = \int_a^b w\,\eta_R(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = w\times(\text{area under the IL from } a \text{ to } b). \]

With masses in kg the reactions come out in kgf, which is what the laboratory gauges read.

Dengan jisim dalam kg, reaction yang diperoleh dalam kgf, iaitu unit bacaan gauge makmal.

5.1 Critical position of a series of loads5.1 Critical position bagi satu siri load

Every influence line of the model is piecewise linear, so \(\sum_i P_i\,\eta_R(x_i)\) is a piecewise linear function of the position of the load group, and a piecewise linear function reaches its extremes at a vertex. The largest (or most negative) value therefore occurs when one of the loads stands on a vertex of the influence line; only a few positions need to be tried.

Setiap influence line model ini adalah piecewise linear, jadi \(\sum_i P_i\,\eta_R(x_i)\) ialah fungsi piecewise linear terhadap kedudukan kumpulan load, dan fungsi piecewise linear mencapai nilai ekstrem pada vertex. Nilai terbesar (atau paling negatif) oleh itu berlaku apabila salah satu load berada pada vertex influence line; hanya beberapa kedudukan perlu dicuba.

Worked example 1 · The compound load of Test 2Worked example 1 · Compound load bagi Test 2

The 5 kg mass stands at a halt and the 2.5 kg mass 100 mm to its right; the pair moves to the right, so the 2.5 kg mass is in front. With the 5 kg mass at \(x\):Jisim 5 kg berada pada satu halt dan jisim 2.5 kg 100 mm di sebelah kanannya; pasangan ini bergerak ke kanan, jadi jisim 2.5 kg berada di hadapan. Dengan jisim 5 kg di \(x\):

\[ R_A(x) = 5\,\eta_A(x) + 2.5\,\eta_A(x+100)\quad\text{(kgf)}. \]

5 kg mass at A: \(R_A = 5(1.0)+2.5(0.6) = 6.5\) kgf. 5 kg mass at C: \(\eta_A(540) = -0.76\times(690-540)/250 = -0.456\), so \(R_A = -3.80 - 1.14 = -4.94\) kgf. 2.5 kg mass at C (5 kg mass at 340 mm): \(R_A = 5(-0.36) + 2.5(-0.76) = -3.70\) kgf. The most severe pull-down at A occurs with the heavier load on the vertex C. The largest \(R_B\), 11.44 kgf, is more than 1.5 times the total mass of 7.5 kg.Jisim 5 kg di A: \(R_A = 5(1.0)+2.5(0.6) = 6.5\) kgf. Jisim 5 kg di C: \(\eta_A(540) = -0.76\times(690-540)/250 = -0.456\), maka \(R_A = -3.80 - 1.14 = -4.94\) kgf. Jisim 2.5 kg di C (jisim 5 kg di 340 mm): \(R_A = 5(-0.36) + 2.5(-0.76) = -3.70\) kgf. Tarikan ke bawah paling teruk di A berlaku apabila load yang lebih berat berada pada vertex C. \(R_B\) terbesar, 11.44 kgf, melebihi 1.5 kali jumlah jisim 7.5 kg.

5.2 Distributed load: area under the influence line5.2 Distributed load: area di bawah influence line

The areas under \(\eta_A\) are, by the triangle rule, \(+\tfrac12(250)(1.0) = +125\) mm over AB, \(\tfrac12(190)(-0.76) = -72.2\) mm over BC and \(\tfrac12(250)(-0.76) = -95.0\) mm over CD, and zero over DEF.

Area di bawah \(\eta_A\), menggunakan petua segi tiga, ialah \(+\tfrac12(250)(1.0) = +125\) mm pada AB, \(\tfrac12(190)(-0.76) = -72.2\) mm pada BC dan \(\tfrac12(250)(-0.76) = -95.0\) mm pada CD, dan sifar pada DEF.

A load \(w = 0.01\) kg/mm (2.5 kg spread over 250 mm): over AB only, \(R_A = 0.01\times125 = 1.25\) kgf (half the load, as for a simple span); over BC and CD, \(R_A = 0.01\times(-167.2) = -1.67\) kgf; over the whole model, \(R_A = 0.01\times(125-72.2-95.0) = -0.42\) kgf. What is measured. A 2.5 kg mass is placed at each of the seventeen halts in turn (Test 1), then the 5 kg + 2.5 kg pair (Test 2). At each halt the six dial gauges are read. One division corresponds to 0.05 kgf, so the experimental ordinate per unit load is

Apa yang diukur. Jisim 2.5 kg diletakkan pada setiap tujuh belas halt secara bergilir (Test 1), kemudian pasangan 5 kg + 2.5 kg (Test 2). Pada setiap halt, enam dial gauge dibaca. Satu division bersamaan 0.05 kgf, jadi ordinate eksperimen per unit load ialah

\[ \eta_{\text{test}} = \frac{(\text{reading in divisions})\times 0.05}{m},\qquad m = 2.5\ \text{kg (Test 1)}. \]

For Test 2, reading × 0.05 is the reaction in kgf, comparable with the compound-load table.

Bagi Test 2, bacaan × 0.05 ialah reaction dalam kgf, boleh dibandingkan dengan jadual compound load.

How to compare. Report the difference \(\eta_{\text{test}}-\eta_{\text{theory}}\) at every halt. A percentage difference is meaningful only where the theoretical ordinate is not small: at halt 2, where \(\eta_A = 0.2\), a gauge error of one division (0.02 per unit load) is already 10 %, and where the theoretical value is zero the percentage is undefined. Judge the agreement by the absolute difference and by the shape of the line.

Cara membandingkan. Laporkan perbezaan \(\eta_{\text{test}}-\eta_{\text{theory}}\) pada setiap halt. Perbezaan peratusan hanya bermakna apabila ordinate teori tidak kecil: di halt 2, di mana \(\eta_A = 0.2\), error gauge satu division (0.02 per unit load) sudah 10 %, dan apabila nilai teori sifar, peratusan tidak tertakrif. 8 Beyond the laboratory8 Di luar makmal

Key pointsIsi penting
  • Bridge design places standard vehicle and lane loads using influence lines or surfaces.
  • Continuous girders have curved lines; Müller–Breslau still shows which spans to load.
  • Gerber bridges: forces by statics alone, at the price of end supports designed for uplift.
  • Rekabentuk jambatan meletakkan standard vehicle load dan lane load menggunakan influence line atau influence surface.
  • Continuous girder mempunyai garis melengkung; Müller–Breslau masih menunjukkan span mana yang perlu dibebani.
  • Gerber bridge: daya diperoleh dengan statik sahaja, dengan harga end support yang perlu direka untuk uplift.

Bridge decks are designed for standard vehicle and lane loads placed, by means of influence lines or influence surfaces, where they produce the largest effects at each section. For continuous (indeterminate) girders the influence lines are curved and need an elastic analysis, but the Müller–Breslau sketch still tells the engineer which spans to load. The cantilever bridge, with its determinate hinges, made the long steel spans of the late nineteenth century possible because its forces could be found by statics alone; the price is end supports that must be designed for uplift, as Worked example 2 shows.

Deck jambatan direka untuk standard vehicle load dan lane load yang diletakkan, menggunakan influence line atau influence surface, di kedudukan yang menghasilkan kesan terbesar pada setiap section. Bagi continuous girder (indeterminate), influence line melengkung dan memerlukan analisis elastik, tetapi lakaran Müller–Breslau masih memberitahu jurutera span mana yang perlu dibebani. Cantilever bridge, dengan hinge yang determinate, membolehkan span keluli yang panjang pada akhir abad kesembilan belas kerana dayanya boleh dicari dengan statik sahaja; harganya ialah end support yang mesti direka untuk uplift, seperti yang ditunjukkan dalam Worked example 2.
