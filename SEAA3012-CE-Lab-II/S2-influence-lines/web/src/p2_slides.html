Loads that moveLoad yang bergerak

A floor load stays put. A lorry on a bridge, a crane on its rail, a train on a viaduct do not.

The designer needs to know where the load must stand to produce

  • the largest reaction at a pier (bearing design, uplift)
  • the largest shear at a section
  • the largest moment at midspan

Load lantai kekal di tempatnya. Lori di atas jambatan, kren di atas rel, kereta api di atas viaduct tidak.

Pereka perlu tahu di mana load perlu berada untuk menghasilkan

  • reaction terbesar pada pier (rekabentuk bearing, uplift)
  • shear terbesar pada section
  • moment terbesar di midspan
DefinitionTakrifThe influence line of a response \(R\) at a fixed point is the graph of \(R\) against the position \(x\) of a unit load moving over the structure: ordinate \(\eta_R(x)\).Influence line bagi response \(R\) pada titik tetap ialah graf \(R\) melawan kedudukan \(x\) bagi unit load yang bergerak atas struktur: ordinate \(\eta_R(x)\).

The structure is linear elastic, so effects add: once \(\eta_R(x)\) is known for a unit load, any load pattern follows by multiplication and addition.

Why it is statically determinateMengapa ia statically determinate

Unknowns:Anu: \(R_A, R_B, R_E, R_F\): four.empat.

Equations:Persamaan: \(\sum F_y = 0\) anddan \(\sum M = 0\) for the whole model, plusbagi seluruh model, ditambah \(M = 0\) at hinge C and at hinge D: four.di hinge C dan hinge D: empat.

ConsequencesAkibatnya
  • every reaction from equilibrium; \(EI\) never appears
  • every influence line is made of straight segments, kinked only at hinges
  • reactions positive up; \(R_C\), \(R_D\) positive pressing down on the tip
  • setiap reaction daripada equilibrium; \(EI\) tidak muncul
  • setiap influence line terdiri daripada segmen lurus, berbengkok hanya di hinge
  • reaction positif ke atas; \(R_C\), \(R_D\) positif apabila menekan ke bawah pada hujung

One idea, three times: take moments about a supportSatu idea, tiga kali: ambil moment terhadap support

Order matters:Urutan penting: analyse the suspended span first; it is the only member whose loads reach the others.analisis suspended span dahulu; hanya anggota ini yang load-nya sampai kepada anggota lain.

Step 1 · Load on beam ABCLangkah 1 · Load pada beam ABC

Moments about B (hinge C unloaded):Moment terhadap B (hinge C tidak dibebani):

\[ \eta_A(x) = \frac{250-x}{250},\qquad \eta_B(x) = \frac{x}{250}\qquad (0\le x\le 440) \]
  • Nothing stops at B: past B the lever arm \((250-x)\) is negative
  • \(\eta_A \lt 0\): support A must hold the beam down
  • \(\eta_B \gt 1\): B carries the load and the pull-down at A
  • Tiada apa berhenti di B: selepas B, lever arm \((250-x)\) negatif
  • \(\eta_A \lt 0\): support A perlu menahan beam ke bawah
  • \(\eta_B \gt 1\): B membawa load dan tarikan ke bawah di A
At the tip CDi hujung C\(\eta_A(440) = -0.76\)
\(\eta_B(440) = 1.76\)

Steps 2 and 3 · Load on the suspended spanLangkah 2 dan 3 · Load pada suspended span

Step 2.Langkah 2. CD is simply supported on the hinges:CD disokong mudah pada hinge:

\[ \eta_C(x) = \frac{690-x}{250},\qquad \eta_D(x) = \frac{x-440}{250} \]

Step 3.Langkah 3. ABC carries only \(\eta_C\) at its tip:ABC hanya membawa \(\eta_C\) di hujungnya:

\[ \eta_A(x) = -0.76\,\eta_C(x),\qquad \eta_B(x) = 1.76\,\eta_C(x) \]

Continuous at C (\(-0.76\) from both sides); zero at D.Selanjar di C (\(-0.76\) dari kedua-dua belah); sifar di D.

Hinge force and the right-hand sideHinge force dan sebelah kanan

\(R_C\) acts only for loads on CD; \(R_D\), \(R_F\) mirror \(R_C\), \(R_A\). B and E carry up to 1.76 times a load on the tips.\(R_C\) hanya bertindak bagi load pada CD; \(R_D\), \(R_F\) ialah imej cermin \(R_C\), \(R_A\). B dan E membawa sehingga 1.76 kali load di hujung.

Müller–Breslau: the engineer's shortcutMüller–Breslau: jalan pintas jurutera

Release the restraint, impose a unit displacement: the deflected shape is the influence line.Lepaskan restraint, kenakan unit displacement: deflected shape ialah influence line.

For \(R_A\): ABC rotates about B as a rigid bar, C drops 0.76, CD follows, DEF stays still. Same line, no algebra.Bagi \(R_A\): ABC berputar terhadap B sebagai bar rigid, C turun 0.76, CD mengikut, DEF tidak bergerak. Garis yang sama, tanpa algebra.

Using an influence lineMenggunakan influence line

\[ R = P\,\eta_R(x_1)\qquad R = \sum_i P_i\,\eta_R(x_i)\qquad R = w\times\text{area} \]
Critical positionAll our lines are piecewise linear, so a sum over a load train is piecewise linear in its position: the extreme occurs with one load on a vertex.Semua garis kita piecewise linear, jadi jumlah bagi load train adalah piecewise linear terhadap kedudukannya: nilai ekstrem berlaku dengan satu load pada vertex.
UnitsMasses in kg give reactions in kgf, which is what the gauges read (1 division = 0.05 kgf).Jisim dalam kg memberi reaction dalam kgf, iaitu unit bacaan gauge (1 division = 0.05 kgf).
