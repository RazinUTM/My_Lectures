(a) Four vertical reactions against two equilibrium equations plus two hinge conditions (\(M = 0\) at C and D): four equations, four unknowns. No elastic analysis is involved, so the released structure in the Müller–Breslau construction is a mechanism of rigid links and every influence line consists of straight segments, kinked only at hinges and at the released support.(a) Empat reaction menegak berbanding dua persamaan equilibrium ditambah dua syarat hinge (\(M = 0\) di C dan D): empat persamaan, empat anu. Tiada analisis elastik terlibat, jadi released structure dalam pembinaan Müller–Breslau ialah mechanism yang terdiri daripada rigid link dan setiap influence line terdiri daripada segmen lurus, berbengkok hanya di hinge dan di support yang dilepaskan.

(b) The beam becomes indeterminate to the second degree. Releasing A and lifting it bends the beam, so the influence line becomes a smooth curve: positive over AB, negative over BE with its minimum inside span BE rather than at C, and slightly positive over EF. The vertices and straight segments disappear, and the ordinates need an analysis that uses \(EI\).(b) Beam menjadi indeterminate darjah kedua. Melepaskan A dan mengangkatnya melenturkan beam, jadi influence line menjadi lengkung licin: positif pada AB, negatif pada BE dengan nilai minimum di dalam span BE dan bukan di C, dan sedikit positif pada EF. Vertex dan segmen lurus hilang, dan ordinate memerlukan analisis yang menggunakan \(EI\).

(c) A negative reaction means the bearing at A must hold the deck down: it needs holding-down bolts, tie-down bearings or a counterweight, otherwise the end of the deck lifts off its bearing when a heavy vehicle is on the cantilever or on the suspended span. The same line tells the designer that the bearing at B must carry up to 1.76 times a load standing at C.(c) Reaction negatif bermaksud bearing di A mesti menahan deck ke bawah: ia memerlukan holding-down bolt, tie-down bearing atau counterweight; jika tidak, hujung deck akan terangkat dari bearing apabila kenderaan berat berada pada cantilever atau suspended span. Garis yang sama memberitahu pereka bahawa bearing di B mesti membawa sehingga 1.76 kali load yang berada di C.

Common slipsKesilapan biasa

  • Stopping \(\eta_A\) at B: the line continues, negative, to C.
  • Putting a kink at B: kinks occur only at hinges.
  • Forgetting that a load on CD reaches both halves of the bridge.
  • Quoting a percentage difference where the ordinate is near zero.
  • Menghentikan \(\eta_A\) di B: garis itu berterusan, negatif, hingga C.
  • Meletakkan bengkokan di B: bengkokan hanya berlaku di hinge.
  • Terlupa bahawa load pada CD sampai ke kedua-dua bahagian jambatan.
  • Menyatakan perbezaan peratusan apabila ordinate hampir sifar.
