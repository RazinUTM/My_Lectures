Block 1 · inputs the software asks for

Units first, then the five numbers of each problem

Cantilever (text §3.4) · Static Structural

  • Unit system: Metric (m, kg, N, s, V, A).
  • Geometry: 4 m × 2 m surface body in XY; Analysis Type = 2D (Geometry cell properties) before importing or sketching.
  • 2D Behavior = Plane Stress; Thickness = 0.01 m.
  • Material: Isotropic Elasticity, E = 2 × 10⁸ Pa, ν = 0.33 — the textbook’s value, a thousand times softer than steel; keep it so the numbers match the book.
  • Fixed Support on the left edge; the load on the right edge — converted in Activity 1.

Plate (text §2.3) · Steady-State Thermal

  • Same unit system; 1 m × 1 m surface body, Analysis Type = 2D.
  • Material: Isotropic Thermal Conductivity k = 300 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹ (the §2.3 figure says 400 — erratum).
  • Internal Heat Generation = 600 W m⁻³ on the body.
  • Temperature = 0 °C on the top and left edges; nothing on the other two.
  • Thickness does not change the temperature field; it scales the heat-flow reactions.

Watch out

A wrong unit system is the most common failure in student projects: a geometry drawn in mm with loads in N and E in Pa is a model of a different structure.

Activity 1 · solution

Total force or pressure: both put −10 kN on the edge, distributed consistently

The rest

  • E = 2 × 10⁸ Pa; ν = 0.33.
  • Edge Sizing: 2 divisions on every edge of the cantilever → the 2 m × 1 m elements of the text; Face Meshing for a mapped (structured) mesh.
  • Element Size 0.0625 m on the plate → 16 × 16.
  • Element Order: Linear for the hand element; Quadratic is the default.

Block 4 · the first check is always the reaction

Before a single contour plot: do the supports carry what was applied?

Force Reaction probe

Boundary Condition: Fixed Support. Expect X = 0, Y = +10 000 N for any mesh and either element order.

Moment Reaction probe

Summation about the support: 40 000 N m. Check the sign convention of the summation point before reading it.

What a wrong value means

The load was entered in the wrong unit or direction, or a support is missing or doubled. Fix that before anything else.

Block 5 · the first-run checklist

In this order; stop at the first failure and fix it

1

Units & 2D

Metric (m, kg, N); Analysis Type 2D; plane stress or strain stated

2

Geometry

dimensions checked against the sketch; thickness entered

3

Material

E, ν (or k) with units; source written down

4

Supports

only what is really restrained; nothing free is fixed

5

Loads

tractions on edges, body force; no vertex forces

6

Coarse mesh

quadratic, a few elements; it must solve

7

Reaction

equals the applied load, right sign

8

Hand estimate

deflection or stress within a factor of 2 — then refine

Check

Deliverable at 2:50: a screenshot of the reaction probe and the coarse-mesh result, and the hand estimate written on the model plan. The refinement study is homework for the report.

Watch out

A model that fails step 7 or 8 is not refined; it is fixed. Refinement never repairs an idealisation.
