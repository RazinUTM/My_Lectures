| 24 Nov 2026 | Annotation toolbar for teaching (the lecturer's request and specification, 24 Nov 2026): a small floating toolbar at the bottom right. Off, only a pencil button shows and the page works as usual. On, a transparent canvas over the page takes mouse, touch and pen input, the page cannot be clicked or scrolled, and the toolbar shows four pens (red, blue, black, yellow), a line-thickness slider, an eraser and Clear all; the toolbar can be dragged anywhere. One self-contained file, `shared/annotate.js`, inlined into every part page and the course home by `sitelib.page()` as its own script after the engine; this course is the source of record, and SEAA 4333 and SEAA 3012 Lab S2 carry a copy. Decided beyond the specification: the ink is recorded in page coordinates and kept per view, per slide and for the full-screen slide viewer, so it stays on what it was written on after scrolling or changing tab, and Clear all clears the screen in view; the toolbar follows the slide viewer into full screen; the black pen is white on the dark theme; the default place is above the Dr RJ button (≤ 900 px) and the exercise timer; a touch while a pen is writing, or within 0.7 s of it, is ignored (palm); Esc turns it off and Ctrl/Cmd+Z takes back a stroke; the ink is never stored or sent, and only the pen and the toolbar's place are remembered (`rz-annotate` in localStorage, one key for all the lecturer's courses on the site). The pencil button is shown to everyone, students included, as specified; open for the lecturer: show it only to the lecturer, or hide it on phones. Not tried on a real iPad with an Apple Pencil: touch and pen were synthesised in headless Chromium | The lecturer writes on the notes, slides and Explorers while teaching, on an iPad or with a mouse; one file with no dependencies and no hook in the engine, so the pages work exactly as before when it is off and the tutor files do not change; ink tied to the screen and not to the page would lie over the wrong text after the next scroll |
| 24 Nov 2026 | Annotation toolbar 1.1, after the lecturer tried 1.0 on his own device (his requests, 24 Nov 2026).
