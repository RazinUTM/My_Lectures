Which term, which edge? Trace the boundary term around the plate

For the text’s plate (top and left at 0 °C, right and bottom insulated)

  • Which edges contribute to {b}? Write the value of qb on each.
  • On the two Dirichlet edges the flux is unknown — what happens to the boundary term there?
  • How many equations does the weighted residual give for one 4-node element, and for the 2 × 2 mesh before boundary conditions?

6 minutes. Reveal follows.

SEAA 4243 · 2D Heat Transfer — Part 2

10

Answers: (1) None: right and bottom are insulated (q_b = 0), so {b} = 0 for every element; the top and left edges are Dirichlet. (2) The unknown flux multiplies N_i of nodes on those edges; when we impose T on those nodes we discard their equations, and the unknown flux disappears with them (it can be recovered afterwards as a reaction, like a support reaction). (3) 4 equations per element; 9 for the 2 × 2 mesh with 9 nodes; after imposing T on 5 nodes, 4 remain.
