Evaluate [k] and {q} for the text’s element, then check the row sums

Given (text §2.3)

  • a = b = 0.5 m (2 × 2 mesh of a 1 m × 1 m plate), unit thickness.
  • kx = ky = 300 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹, qh = 600 W m⁻³.

Find

  • s1, s2, s3, s4 and hence [k].
  • {q} for the element.
  • Verify that each row of [k] sums to zero.

Check

Units: W K⁻¹ for [k]; W for {q} (per metre of thickness).

8 minutes. Early finishers: what changes in [k] if a is doubled and b kept?

16

Answers: s1 = 100 + 100 = 200; s2 = −100 + 50 = −50; s3 = −50 − 50 = −100; s4 = 50 − 100 = −50. Row sum 200 − 50 − 100 − 50 = 0. {q} = 600 × 0.5 × 0.5 / 4 = 37.5 W each. Early finishers: doubling a halves b k_x/a terms and doubles a k_y/b terms; the element conducts less in x per unit temperature difference because the path is longer.
