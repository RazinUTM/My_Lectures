8

Apply Green’s theorem with F = k_x ∂T/∂x and again with F = k_y ∂T/∂y; the two boundary terms combine into the normal flux k ∂T/∂n = q_b, exactly the quantity of the Neumann condition (2.15). Sign discipline: with q_b defined as inward-positive, the boundary term is +∫N q_b ds on the load side, so an inward flux adds heat, as it must. Lecturer note: the text’s (2.41) has the opposite sign to (2.15); we keep (2.15) throughout, and this also corrects the sign I gave in Heat Transfer Part 1’s Activity 3 answer: an inward flux has q_b > 0.
