Three names to learn: conductance matrix [k], heat-generation vector {q}, boundary-flux vector {b}; {r} is their sum. The “left to right” rule is the book’s convention: after IBP the operator acts on whatever is to its left in {N}ᵀ{∂}. Students who write {∂}{N}ᵀ get a 2 by 1 object and stall.
