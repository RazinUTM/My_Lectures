Plane stress, plane strain, or neither? Decide and give the reason

Six structures

  • Steel gusset plate at a truss joint, 12 mm thick
  • Concrete gravity dam, 300 m long
  • Reinforced-concrete shear wall, 200 mm thick, 6 m high
  • Circular tunnel lining, 2 km long
  • Square slab carrying a lateral floor load (bending)
  • Strip footing of a long wall

For each

  • Plane stress / plane strain / neither (and why).
  • What is zero: σzz or εzz?

Compare with the pair next to you. We reveal in 5 minutes.

SEAA 4243 · Plane Stress — Part 1

10

Answers: gusset plate → plane stress (thin, in-plane loads; σ_zz = 0). Gravity dam → plane strain (long, constant section; ε_zz = 0). Shear wall → plane stress (thin relative to height and length, loaded in its plane). Tunnel lining → plane strain. Slab in bending → neither: loads are normal to the plane, so it is a plate-bending problem (not in this course). Strip footing → plane strain per metre run.
