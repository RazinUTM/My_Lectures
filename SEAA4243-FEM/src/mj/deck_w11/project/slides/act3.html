Evaluate [E] for the cantilever’s material

Given (text Fig. 3.4)

  • E = 200 × 10⁶ N m⁻², ν = 0.33.
  • Plane stress.

Find

  • E/(1 − ν²) and the three distinct entries of [E].
  • Then: G = E/(2(1 + ν)). Does it equal the (3,3) entry?

Check

Keep four significant figures; the stiffness matrix the next session is built from these numbers.

6 minutes. Reveal follows.

SEAA 4243 · Plane Stress — Part 1

21

Answers: 1 − ν² = 0.8911; E/(1 − ν²) = 224.44 × 10⁶; ν × that = 74.07 × 10⁶; (1 − ν)/2 × that = 0.335 × 224.44 × 10⁶ = 75.19 × 10⁶. G = 200 × 10⁶/(2 × 1.33) = 75.19 × 10⁶: yes, equal to the (3,3) entry, as it must be.
