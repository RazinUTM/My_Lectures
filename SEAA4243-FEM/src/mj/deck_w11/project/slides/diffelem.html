Normal stresses, shear stresses and body forces on a rectangle dx by dy

Dependent variables: σxx, σyy (normal) and σxy, σyx (shear). Body forces fx, fy per unit volume (self-weight: fy = −ρg).

Far faces carry the near-face stress plus its first-order change — the same Taylor expansion as for the heat flux.

(3.1), (3.2)

Why

Sign convention: a stress component is positive when it acts in the positive coordinate direction on a face whose outward normal is positive (and vice versa).
