Three questions before you leave

Questions

  • A 10 m long culvert loaded uniformly along its length: plane stress or plane strain, and which quantity is zero?
  • Write the strain–displacement relation in matrix form and name the matrix.
  • For a square element (a = b), what are u and v at the centroid in terms of the nodal values?

hint for Q3

Five minutes. Name and matric number; for feedback, not marks.

SEAA 4243 · Plane Stress — Part 1

37

Answers: (1) plane strain, ε_zz = 0. (2) {ε}ᵀ = [∂]ᵀ{u}ᵀ, the transpose of the differential operator matrix. (3) u = (u1 + u2 + u3 + u4)/4 and v = (v1 + v2 + v3 + v4)/4, since every N_i = 1/4 at the centroid.
