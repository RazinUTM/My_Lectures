Why

The 3 × 2 matrix in (3.14) is [∂]ᵀ, the transpose of the equilibrium operator: equilibrium and compatibility are dual.

Watch out

εxy is the engineering shear strain (sum of the two slopes), not the tensor strain (half of it). The text’s [E] is built for it.
