Block 1 · plane stress in practice

A corner bracket in ANSYS, modelled as plane stress

Why

“Because the bracket is thin in the z direction (½-inch thickness) compared to its x and y dimensions, and because the pressure load acts only in the x–y plane, assume plane stress.” — the tutorial’s own justification, word for word the test of this slide.

Images and quotation: PyMAPDL documentation, “Static analysis of a corner bracket” (adapted from the classic Ansys APDL tutorial; PLANE183 elements), © ANSYS, Inc., MIT licence.

Read the quotation aloud and ask which two conditions it checks: thin compared with the in-plane size, and loads in the plane. Those are exactly the plane-stress conditions.
