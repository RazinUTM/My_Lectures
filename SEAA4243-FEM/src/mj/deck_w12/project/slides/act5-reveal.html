Timoshenko beam: bending + shear

Watch out

Shear adds 0.032 m to the 0.160 m of bending: 17% for a beam this deep (L/h = 2). The 2 × 2 FE answer, 0.131 m, is 32% below the beam estimate.

Check

Both symmetries are exact properties of the discrete system, not approximations: the mesh, the loads and the supports are symmetric about y = 1 m.
