Row 3 of [N]ᵀ[∂] (dof u₂) times [E]

(3.39)

Why

Row 3 of the 8 × 3 matrix [N]ᵀ[∂] belongs to u2: the derivatives of N2 sit in columns 1 and 3 (εxx and εxy). Multiplying by [E] attaches a material constant to each.
