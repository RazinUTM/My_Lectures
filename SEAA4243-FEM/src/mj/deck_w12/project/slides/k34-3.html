The integral of −x(b − y)/(a²b²) over the rectangle is −(a²/2)(b²/2)/(a²b²) = −1/4. Then ν + (1 − ν)/2 = (1 + ν)/2 and (1 + ν)/(1 − ν²) = 1/(1 − ν). The text writes the result as E t/(8(ν − 1)).
