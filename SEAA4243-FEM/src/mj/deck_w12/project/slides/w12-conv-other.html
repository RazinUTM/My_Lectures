Check

Coarse meshes: each halving of h cuts the error about three times. Same lesson as our Table 3.1.

Why

Fine meshes: the error stops near 0.7%. The FE end load is spread uniformly; the exact elasticity solution assumes a parabolic end shear. That is a modelling difference, not a mesh error — as our converged 0.191 against Timoshenko’s 0.192 m.
