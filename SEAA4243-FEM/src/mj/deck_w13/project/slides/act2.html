Which of these stresses would you put in a design report?

Six candidates

  • σxx at the centroid of an element in the middle of a 32 × 16 mesh.
  • Peak von Mises at the fixed corner of the same mesh.
  • σxx at the top-right node of the 2 × 2 hand mesh.
  • Nodal-averaged σxx at mid-span from a mesh-converged model.
  • Stress under a single nodal point load.
  • σxy averaged over the depth at a section 1 m from the support.

For each

  • Trust / use with care / do not report.
  • One reason.

6 minutes. Reveal follows.

SEAA 4243 · Plane Stress — Part 3 · Project

16

Answers: (1) trust — centroid of a converged mesh, away from boundaries. (2) do not report — singularity. (3) do not report — coarse mesh and a nodal value (extrapolated). (4) trust if the mesh study shows convergence. (5) do not report — a point load is a singularity too; distribute it over a realistic area. (6) use with care — averaged shear is fine; near the support it is still within the St-Venant zone.
