Answers: Q4 2×2 −32%; Q8 2×1 −7%; Q8 4×4 −1.6%; Q4 16×16 −1.6%. For 1.6%: Q8 needs 16 elements, Q4 needs 256. Q8 wins because its edges can curve (quadratic), so bending does not generate spurious shear strain. Dofs are the fairer cost measure: Q8 4×4 has 25 corner + 40 mid-side = 65 nodes = 130 dofs, versus 17 × 17 = 289 nodes = 578 dofs for Q4 16×16.
