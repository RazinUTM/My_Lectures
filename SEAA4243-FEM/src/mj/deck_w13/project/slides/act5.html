Activity 5 · groups · planning

Draft your model plan on one page

Write, as a group

  • The structure, with a sketch and dimensions; the source of the geometry and loads.
  • Idealisation: plane stress or plane strain, thickness, material (E, ν, units).
  • Supports and loads, each one justified in a few words.
  • The quantity you will report (deflection? stress where?) and the hand check you will use.
  • Three mesh sizes you will run.

Watch out

Lecturers circulate. A plan with a fixed edge that should be free, or no hand check, is sent back now rather than in the report.

Check

Hand in the plan at the end; it comes back with comments at the ANSYS session.

12 minutes. Hand in before you leave.
