Block 5 · the brief

One structure: ANSYS as the primary analysis, your own tool as the independent check

Task

Model a 2D structure in ANSYS (plane stress or strain). Build and verify a small solver of your own — with generative AI if you wish — and use it to cross-check ANSYS. Interpret the results for design.

Team

Groups of [4–5]. One repository and one report per group, with a line on who did what.

Weight

15% of the course (CLO4), marked by the rubric two slides on.

Deliverables

  • Report, at most 15 pages: idealisation, convergence, Gates 1–3 evidence, interpretation, limits.
  • The tool: GitHub repository and Pages link; README with verification table, limits, licence.
  • GenAI log and one-page reflection; ANSYS files; hand-check sheet.

Dates

  • Today: groups formed, Gate 0 plan handed in.
  • ANSYS demo: Gate 1 shown; ANSYS model started.
  • [date]: Gates 2–5, report, demo and individual viva (≈ 20 min per group).
