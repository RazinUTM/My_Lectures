Every object on this sketch is a line of the Heat Transfer Part 2 hand calculation

Build order (click path in the notes)

  • Steady-State Thermal system → Engineering Data: new material, Isotropic Thermal Conductivity 300.
  • Geometry (2D): sketch a 1 m square; one surface body.
  • Mesh: Face Meshing + Element Size 0.5 m, Element Order Linear → the hand mesh.
  • Temperature 0 °C on top and left edges; Internal Heat Generation 600 W m⁻³ on the body.
  • Solve → Temperature contour; Probe at the bottom-right vertex; Total Heat Flux vectors.

Why

Nothing is applied on the right and bottom edges. In the weak form that is qb = 0 — insulated — exactly as in Heat Transfer Part 1 Activity 3.

SEAA 4243 · ANSYS demo · hand calculation vs software

13

Script: (1) Toolbox → Steady-State Thermal, drag to the schematic. (2) Geometry cell → Properties → Advanced Geometry Options → Analysis Type: 2D. (3) Engineering Data: add material ‘plate’, Isotropic Thermal Conductivity = 300 W/m·K. (4) Geometry: new sketch on XY plane, rectangle 1 × 1 m, Surfaces From Sketches. (5) Model: assign material to the body. Mesh → insert Face Meshing on the face; Sizing → Element Size 0.5 m; Mesh details → Element Order: Linear. Generate: 4 elements, 9 nodes. (6) Steady-State Thermal → Temperature on the top edge and left edge (Ctrl-select), magnitude 0 °C; Internal Heat Generation on the body, 600 W/m³. (7) Solve. Insert Temperature result and a Temperature probe on the bottom-right vertex; insert Total Heat Flux (vector display). Expected nodal temperatures with 4 linear elements (Heat Transfer Part 2, text (2.55)): T2 = 0.4821 at the bottom mid-edge, T3 = 0.6214 at the bottom-right corner, T5 = 0.3857 at the centre, T6 = 0.4821 at the right mid-edge.
