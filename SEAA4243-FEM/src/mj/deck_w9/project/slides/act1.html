Scalar or vector? Classify each field and count the unknowns per node

Six fields — decide: scalar (1 dof) or vector (2 dof in 2D)?

  • Temperature in a concrete slab
  • Pore-water pressure in an embankment
  • Displacement of a shear wall
  • Velocity of water in a pipe network
  • Electric potential in a grounding grid
  • Settlement of a strip footing (2D section)

Then answer

  • How many unknowns does a 4-node element have in each case?
  • Which of the six could be solved with today’s element?

SEAA 4243 · 2D Heat Transfer — Part 1

10

Answers: temperature = scalar (1 dof); pore pressure = scalar (1 dof); displacement = vector (2 dof: u, v); velocity = vector (2 dof: u, v); electric potential = scalar; settlement of a footing = vector (2D plane-strain displacement; even if we only care about the vertical component, the element carries u and v). A 4-node element therefore has 4 unknowns for a scalar field and 8 for a vector field. The first, second and fifth could be solved with today’s element, with the property k reinterpreted (conductivity, permeability, electrical conductivity).
