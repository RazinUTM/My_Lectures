Welcome back after the mid-semester break. The first half of the course was one-dimensional elements: bar, beam, truss, frame, buckling and vibration. From today we move to two dimensions. The heat-transfer element is the simplest continuum element (one unknown per node) and it carries every idea we need for plane stress later: shape functions, Galerkin, integration by parts, assembly, boundary conditions. Today is Part 1: PDE, boundary conditions and shape functions. Next session: the element matrices, assembly, solution and MATLAB. Session is 3 hours with six short activities and a break at about 1h25.
