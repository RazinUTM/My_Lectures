Why two dimensions?

FEM is a tool for solving a differential equation — so every topic starts from the PDE. Today’s PDE has one unknown: temperature.

SEAA 4243 · 2D Heat Transfer — Part 1

5

Five minutes of warm-up before this slide: ask the class to recall the 1D bar pipeline in pairs: what was the shape function, what did Galerkin do, where did the boundary terms come from. Then the key framing: FEM can be everything (it solves most engineering PDEs) and FEM can be nothing (it is just a tool to solve a DE). Because it is nothing, every topic must start from the first principle, the PDE. That is what we do today.
