Learning outcomes · today

By the end of this session you should be able to

1

Derive the steady 2D heat conduction PDE from an energy balance and Fourier’s law, and write it as {∂}[E]{∂}ᵀT = −qh.

2

Classify boundary conditions as Dirichlet (essential) or Neumann (natural) and write them for a rectangular domain.

3

Derive the bilinear shape functions N1…N4 of the four-node element and use them to interpolate T(x, y).

4

Differentiate the approximation to obtain the temperature gradient and the heat flux at any point of an element.

Why

Outcomes 1–3 are the first half of Chapter 2 of the text (§2.1 – §2.2.1). Outcome 4 prepares the next session’s conductance matrix [k].
