Block 4 · what a shape function looks like

A tent: 1 at its own node, 0 at the other three, straight along every edge

Left: N3 as a surface. Right: all four as colour maps (dark = 1). Each Ni is the temperature field you get when node i is at 1 and the others at 0.

Why

Along an edge only the two nodes on that edge matter — the straight line of the bar element. That is what lets two neighbouring elements agree along their common edge.
