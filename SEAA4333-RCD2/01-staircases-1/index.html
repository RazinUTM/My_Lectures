    1. A stair is a slab that climbsTangga ialah slab yang mendaki

    ≈ 5 min

    Structurally, most stairs in buildings are slabs. The sloping slab under the steps is the waist; the steps sit on it as triangles of concrete that add weight but little strength. A stair that spans along its length carries its load from one support to the other like a one-way slab, so its design is the RCD I slab design with three new steps: the geometry, the load on plan and the span on plan.Dari segi struktur, kebanyakan tangga dalam bangunan ialah slab. Slab condong di bawah anak tangga ialah waist; anak tangga duduk di atasnya sebagai segi tiga konkrit yang menambah berat tetapi hampir tidak menambah kekuatan. Tangga yang span sepanjang panjangnya membawa load dari satu support ke support yang lain seperti one-way slab, jadi design-nya ialah design slab RCD I dengan tiga langkah baharu: geometri, load on plan dan span on plan.

    Stair spanning along its lengthTangga yang span sepanjang panjangnyaA stair carried by supports at its two ends: beams, walls or the edges of floor slabs. It is designed as a one-way slab on a strip 1 m wide.Tangga yang disokong oleh support di kedua-dua hujungnya: beam, dinding atau tepi slab lantai. Ia di-design sebagai one-way slab pada strip selebar 1 m.
    • The waist h is measured square to the soffit, not vertically.Waist h diukur tegak lurus dengan soffit, bukan secara menegak.
    • The going G is the horizontal depth of a tread, without the nosing overhang.Going G ialah kedalaman mendatar tread, tanpa unjuran nosing.
    • The rise R is the height of one step; the riser is the vertical face that makes it.Rise R ialah ketinggian satu anak tangga; riser ialah muka menegak yang membentuknya.
    • The effective span L is measured on plan, centre to centre of the supports.Effective span L diukur on plan, dari tengah ke tengah support.

    This week covers flights that span along their length, alone or with a landing at the top or the bottom. Week 2 adds stairs supported by their landings, two flights at right angles, and stairs with their sides built into walls, which span across their width.Minggu ini meliputi flight yang span sepanjang panjangnya, sendirian atau dengan landing di atas atau di bawah. Minggu 2 menambah tangga yang disokong oleh landing-nya, dua flight bersudut tegak, dan tangga yang sisinya terbina dalam dinding, yang span merentasi lebarnya.

    Answer 1Jawapan 1 The flight is thicker on planFlight lebih tebal on plan

    Measured vertically, which is how a load on plan sees it, the flight’s concrete is thicker than h for two reasons. The waist is y = h/cos θ = h√(R² + G²)/G, and each step adds a triangle of average height R/2, so t = y + R/2. With h = 175, R = 175 and G = 260 mm, t = 298.4 mm against a landing of 175 mm, and nd = 17.69 against 13.53 kN/m², a ratio of 1.31.Diukur secara menegak, iaitu cara load on plan melihatnya, konkrit flight lebih tebal daripada h atas dua sebab. Waist ialah y = h/cos θ = h√(R² + G²)/G, dan setiap anak tangga menambah segi tiga dengan purata tinggi R/2, jadi t = y + R/2. Dengan h = 175, R = 175 dan G = 260 mm, t = 298.4 mm berbanding landing 175 mm, dan nd = 17.69 berbanding 13.53 kN/m², nisbah 1.31.

    Answer 2Jawapan 2 FL/10 and K = 1.3FL/10 dan K = 1.3

    M = FL/10, because one end is continuous with the beam: the course method uses FL/10 whenever at least one end is continuous, and FL/8 only when both ends are simply supported. K = 1.3, because the stair is an end span, continuous at one end only (EC2 Table 7.4N).M = FL/10, kerana satu hujung continuous dengan beam: kaedah kursus menggunakan FL/10 apabila sekurang-kurangnya satu hujung continuous, dan FL/8 hanya apabila kedua-dua hujung simply supported. K = 1.3, kerana tangga itu ialah end span, continuous di satu hujung sahaja (EC2 Table 7.4N).

    Answer 3Jawapan 3 The hogging moment shifts the reactionHogging moment mengalihkan reaction

    The beam restrains rotation at A, so a hogging moment MA develops there. Taking moments about B, A’s reaction gains MA/L and B’s loses the same: VA = ΣFi(L − xi)/L + MA/L. In Faded Example 1.2, MA/L = 6.98 kN, so VA = 44.01 kN against VB = 25.84 kN.Beam menahan putaran di A, jadi hogging moment MA terhasil di situ. Dengan mengambil moment terhadap B, reaction A bertambah MA/L dan reaction B berkurang sebanyak yang sama: VA = ΣFi(L − xi)/L + MA/L. Dalam Faded Example 1.2, MA/L = 6.98 kN, jadi VA = 44.01 kN berbanding VB = 25.84 kN.

    Answer 4Jawapan 4 Cross the bars at the kinkSilangkan bar di kink

    Do not bend a bottom bar round the inside corner: in tension, the pulls in its two legs add up to a force pointing out of the concrete, which straightens the bar and spalls the cover. Instead, run each set of bottom bars straight on past the corner into the opposite face, so that the two sets cross, and anchor each in the compression zone. Sebaliknya, teruskan setiap set bottom bar lurus melepasi sudut ke muka bertentangan, supaya kedua-dua set bersilang, dan anchor setiap satu dalam compression zone. Lakaran: Nota §6, Detail B.
