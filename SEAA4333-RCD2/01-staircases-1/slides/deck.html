Block B · from stair to beam

Model the stair as a beam on plan

Span L: centre to centre of the supports, on plan.

F = Σ nd × length, per metre width: the flight part and the landing part.

M = FL/10 with at least one continuous end; FL/8 when both ends are simply supported.

Deflection K (Table 7.4N): 1.5 both ends continuous, 1.3 one end, 1.0 neither.

Block C · checks and detailing

What a stair adds to the slab checks

Shear: the continuous end carries more; take ρl from the top bars there.

Deflection: K = 1.5, 1.3 or 1.0 by the continuous ends.

Spacing where M peaks: main ≤ 2h and 250; distribution ≤ 3h and 400 mm.

Distribution steel: at least 20% of the main steel provided.

Top bars: 0.3L at a continuous end; at least 25% of the span steel over 0.2L at a simple end.
