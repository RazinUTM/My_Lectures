Unit 1 Staircases I

SEAA 4333 Reinforced Concrete Design II, Week 1. Set a stair’s geometry by the by-laws, turn its waist into a load per m² on plan, model it as a beam on plan, then design and detail the waist as a one-way slab. Notes, slides, model answers, exercises with new numbers on every attempt, and the Staircase Lab.SEAA 4333 Reinforced Concrete Design II, Minggu 1. Tetapkan geometri tangga mengikut by-law, tukarkan waist kepada load per m² on plan, modelkan tangga sebagai beam on plan, kemudian design dan detail waist sebagai one-way slab. Nota, slides, jawapan model, latihan dengan nombor baharu pada setiap cubaan, dan Staircase Lab.
