What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

A stair is a slab that climbs. In this Unit you design a reinforced concrete stair that spans along its length, with or without a landing: the geometry first, then the load on plan, then the beam model, then the waist as a one-way slab. Week 2 adds stairs supported by landings, two flights at right angles, and stairs with their sides built into walls.Tangga ialah slab yang mendaki. Dalam Unit ini anda design tangga konkrit bertetulang yang span sepanjang panjangnya, dengan atau tanpa landing: geometri dahulu, kemudian load on plan, kemudian beam model, kemudian waist sebagai one-way slab. Minggu 2 menambah tangga yang disokong oleh landing, dua flight bersudut tegak, dan tangga yang sisinya terbina dalam dinding.

After this Unit you should be able toSelepas Unit ini, anda sepatutnya boleh

  1. set the rise R, the going G and the number of risers for a storey, and check them against UBBL By-laws 106 and 108;menetapkan rise R, going G dan bilangan riser bagi satu tingkat, dan menyemaknya dengan UBBL By-law 106 dan 108;
  2. turn a waist thickness into a design load per m² on plan;menukar ketebalan waist kepada design load per m² on plan;
  3. model a flight, with or without its landing, as a beam on plan, and find the total load F, the design moment M and the reactions;memodelkan flight, dengan atau tanpa landing, sebagai beam on plan, dan mencari total load F, design moment M dan reaction;
  4. design and detail the waist as a one-way slab, and find the slips in someone else’s calculation.design dan detail waist sebagai one-way slab, dan mencari kesilapan dalam pengiraan orang lain.

These outcomes serve CLO1: explain the concepts and methods of analysis for stairs. Texts: Mosley, Bungey and Hulse, Reinforced Concrete Design to Eurocode 2, §8.8; Yassin and Abdullah, Reinforced Concrete Design to Eurocode 2 (2022), staircase chapter; the Design Rules and Formulae (RDF); Uniform Building By-Laws 1984.Hasil pembelajaran ini menyokong CLO1: menerangkan konsep dan kaedah analisis bagi tangga. Teks: Mosley, Bungey dan Hulse, Reinforced Concrete Design to Eurocode 2, §8.8; Yassin dan Abdullah, Reinforced Concrete Design to Eurocode 2 (2022), bab staircase; Design Rules and Formulae (RDF); Uniform Building By-Laws 1984.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

A one-way slab has K = M/(bd²fck) = 0.090. Does it need compression steel?Satu one-way slab mempunyai K = M/(bd²fck) = 0.090. Adakah ia memerlukan compression steel?

K is below Kbal = 0.167, so the section is singly reinforced: z = d[0.5 + √(0.25 − K/1.134)] and As = M/(0.87fykz). A stair waist uses exactly these lines in step 7.K lebih kecil daripada Kbal = 0.167, jadi section itu singly reinforced: z = d[0.5 + √(0.25 − K/1.134)] dan As = M/(0.87fykz). Waist tangga menggunakan baris yang sama dalam langkah 7.

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

Distribution (secondary) bars in a one-way slab must be at leastDistribution bar (tetulang sekunder) dalam one-way slab mestilah sekurang-kurangnya

EC2 9.3.1.1(2): at least 20% of the main reinforcement. This course takes 20% of the main steel provided, which is never less than 20% of the steel required.EC2 9.3.1.1(2): sekurang-kurangnya 20% daripada main reinforcement. Kursus ini mengambil 20% daripada main steel yang disediakan; nilai ini tidak pernah kurang daripada 20% steel yang diperlukan.

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

The design load on a floor carrying gk and qk (EN 1990, Eq. 6.10) isDesign load pada lantai yang membawa gk dan qk (EN 1990, Eq. 6.10) ialah

γG = 1.35 on permanent and γQ = 1.5 on variable actions. A stair uses the same factors, with every load taken per m² on plan. The BS 8110 factors (1.4 and 1.6) are not used.γG = 1.35 bagi permanent action dan γQ = 1.5 bagi variable action. Tangga menggunakan faktor yang sama, dengan setiap load diambil per m² on plan. Faktor BS 8110 (1.4 dan 1.6) tidak digunakan.
