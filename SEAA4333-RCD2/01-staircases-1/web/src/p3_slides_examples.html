Write your four answers first, then open the model answers one by one.Tulis empat jawapan anda dahulu, kemudian buka jawapan model satu demi satu.

Answer 1Jawapan 1 The flight is thicker on planFlight lebih tebal on plan

Measured vertically, which is how a load on plan sees it, the flight’s concrete is thicker than h for two reasons. The waist is y = h/cos θ = h√(R² + G²)/G, and each step adds a triangle of average height R/2, so t = y + R/2. With h = 175, R = 175 and G = 260 mm, t = 298.4 mm against a landing of 175 mm, and nd = 17.69 against 13.53 kN/m², a ratio of 1.31.Diukur secara menegak, iaitu cara load on plan melihatnya, konkrit flight lebih tebal daripada h atas dua sebab. Waist ialah y = h/cos θ = h√(R² + G²)/G, dan setiap anak tangga menambah segi tiga dengan purata tinggi R/2, jadi t = y + R/2. Dengan h = 175, R = 175 dan G = 260 mm, t = 298.4 mm berbanding landing 175 mm, dan nd = 17.69 berbanding 13.53 kN/m², nisbah 1.31.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 FL/10 and K = 1.3FL/10 dan K = 1.3

M = FL/10, because one end is continuous with the beam: the course method uses FL/10 whenever at least one end is continuous, and FL/8 only when both ends are simply supported. K = 1.3, because the stair is an end span, continuous at one end only (EC2 Table 7.4N).M = FL/10, kerana satu hujung continuous dengan beam: kaedah kursus menggunakan FL/10 apabila sekurang-kurangnya satu hujung continuous, dan FL/8 hanya apabila kedua-dua hujung simply supported. K = 1.3, kerana tangga itu ialah end span, continuous di satu hujung sahaja (EC2 Table 7.4N).

Answer 3Jawapan 3 The hogging moment shifts the reactionHogging moment mengalihkan reaction

The beam restrains rotation at A, so a hogging moment MA develops there. Taking moments about B, A’s reaction gains MA/L and B’s loses the same: VA = ΣFi(L − xi)/L + MA/L. In Faded Example 1.2, MA/L = 6.98 kN, so VA = 44.01 kN against VB = 25.84 kN.Beam menahan putaran di A, jadi hogging moment MA terhasil di situ. Dengan mengambil moment terhadap B, reaction A bertambah MA/L dan reaction B berkurang sebanyak yang sama: VA = ΣFi(L − xi)/L + MA/L. Dalam Faded Example 1.2, MA/L = 6.98 kN, jadi VA = 44.01 kN berbanding VB = 25.84 kN.

Answer 4Jawapan 4 Cross the bars at the kinkSilangkan bar di kink

Do not bend a bottom bar round the inside corner: in tension, the pulls in its two legs add up to a force pointing out of the concrete, which straightens the bar and spalls the cover. Instead, run each set of bottom bars straight on past the corner into the opposite face, so that the two sets cross, and anchor each in the compression zone. Sketch: Notes §6, Detail B.Jangan bengkokkan bottom bar mengelilingi sudut dalam: dalam tension, tarikan pada kedua-dua kakinya bergabung menjadi daya yang menghala keluar dari konkrit, yang meluruskan bar itu dan memecahkan cover. Sebaliknya, teruskan setiap set bottom bar lurus melepasi sudut ke muka bertentangan, supaya kedua-dua set bersilang, dan anchor setiap satu dalam compression zone. Lakaran: Nota §6, Detail B.
