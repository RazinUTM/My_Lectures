A1 · 1 markmarkah
The self-weight on plan of a flight is computed from which thickness?Self-weight on plan bagi flight dikira daripada ketebalan yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Self-weight on plan is 25t: the waist measured vertically plus the steps, whose triangles have an average height of R/2. Using h misses both the slope and the steps; using y misses the steps.Self-weight on plan ialah 25t: waist yang diukur secara menegak ditambah anak tangga, yang segi tiganya mempunyai purata tinggi R/2. Menggunakan h terlepas kecondongan dan anak tangga; menggunakan y terlepas anak tangga.

A2 · 1 markmarkah
In nd (EN 1990, Eq. 6.10), which partial factor multiplies the imposed load qk?Dalam nd (EN 1990, Eq. 6.10), partial factor manakah yang mendarab imposed load qk?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

γQ = 1.5 on variable actions; 1.35 is γG, for permanent actions only. Using 1.35 on qk was slip B in Stair S1, and it lowers the flight and landing loads alike.γQ = 1.5 bagi variable action; 1.35 ialah γG, bagi permanent action sahaja. Menggunakan 1.35 pada qk ialah slip B dalam Tangga S1, dan ia merendahkan load flight dan landing secara sama.

A3 · 1 markmarkah
The effective span L of a stair is measuredEffective span L bagi tangga diukur

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

On plan, centre to centre: each flight or landing plus half the width of the support at its end. The load nd is already per m² on plan, so a span along the slope would count the slope twice. The clear span was slip C in Stair S1.On plan, tengah ke tengah: setiap flight atau landing tambah separuh lebar support di hujungnya. Load nd sudah per m² on plan, jadi span di sepanjang cerun akan mengira kecondongan dua kali. Clear span ialah slip C dalam Tangga S1.

A4 · 1 markmarkah
A flight spans between two monolithic beams, so both ends are continuous. Which pair is right under the course method?Satu flight span di antara dua beam monolitik, jadi kedua-dua hujung continuous. Pasangan manakah yang betul mengikut kaedah kursus?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

The course method uses FL/10 whenever at least one end is continuous, including when both are. With both ends continuous the strip is an interior span, so K = 1.5 (EC2 Table 7.4N). This is Worked Example 1.1.Kaedah kursus menggunakan FL/10 apabila sekurang-kurangnya satu hujung continuous, termasuk apabila kedua-duanya continuous. Dengan kedua-dua hujung continuous, strip itu ialah interior span, jadi K = 1.5 (EC2 Table 7.4N). Ini ialah Worked Example 1.1.

A5 · 1 markmarkah
What top steel goes at a simply supported end, such as a wall?Top steel apakah yang diletakkan di hujung simply supported, seperti dinding?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

EC2 9.3.1.2(2): a wall gives some partial fixity even where the design assumes none, so at least 25% of the span steel is placed in the top over 0.2L. Option C is the rule for a continuous end.EC2 9.3.1.2(2): dinding memberi sedikit partial fixity walaupun design menganggap tiada, jadi sekurang-kurangnya 25% steel span diletakkan di atas sepanjang 0.2L. Pilihan C ialah peraturan bagi hujung continuous.

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A flight has h = 150 mm, R = 165 mm and G = 280 mm, with a landing 150 mm thick; finishes 1.2 kN/m² and imposed 4.0 kN/m², both on plan. One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu flight mempunyai h = 150 mm, R = 165 mm dan G = 280 mm, dengan landing setebal 150 mm; finishes 1.2 kN/m² dan imposed 4.0 kN/m², kedua-duanya on plan. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: the self-weight on plan uses t, not h: 25 × 0.2566 = 6.415 kN/m². Then gk = 6.415 + 1.20 = 7.615 kN/m² and nd = 1.35 × 7.615 + 1.5 × 4.0 = 16.28 kN/m². Line 4 only carries the slip forward. Professional Habit 1 flags it at once: lines 4 and 5 give the flight and the landing the same 12.68 kN/m², a ratio of 1.00.Baris 3: self-weight on plan menggunakan t, bukan h: 25 × 0.2566 = 6.415 kN/m². Kemudian gk = 6.415 + 1.20 = 7.615 kN/m² dan nd = 1.35 × 7.615 + 1.5 × 4.0 = 16.28 kN/m². Baris 4 hanya membawa kesilapan itu ke hadapan. Professional Habit 1 mengesannya serta-merta: baris 4 dan 5 memberi flight dan landing nilai 12.68 kN/m² yang sama, nisbah 1.00.

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
A wall 230 mm wide at A (simple) carries a bottom landing of 1400 mm clear, then a flight of 10 risers of 170 mm and 10 goings of 270 mm (2700 mm on plan) up to a beam 250 mm wide at B (continuous). Given nd = 12.60 kN/m² for the landing and 16.40 kN/m² for the flight. One line contains the slip. Which one?Dinding selebar 230 mm di A (simple) membawa landing bawah 1400 mm bersih, kemudian flight 10 riser setinggi 170 mm dan 10 going sepanjang 270 mm (2700 mm on plan) hingga beam selebar 250 mm di B (continuous). Diberi nd = 12.60 kN/m² bagi landing dan 16.40 kN/m² bagi flight. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2: the span is measured on plan, L2 = 2700 + 125 = 2825 mm, so L = 4340 mm. The slope is already inside nd through t, so a length along the slope counts it twice. Corrected: F = 19.09 + 16.40 × 2.825 = 65.42 kN and M = 65.42 × 4.340/10 = 28.39 kNm. Lines 3 to 5 use the right method on the wrong length; line 5 rightly takes FL/10, since B is continuous.Baris 2: span diukur on plan, L2 = 2700 + 125 = 2825 mm, jadi L = 4340 mm. Kecondongan sudah berada dalam nd melalui t, jadi panjang di sepanjang cerun mengiranya dua kali. Pembetulan: F = 19.09 + 16.40 × 2.825 = 65.42 kN dan M = 65.42 × 4.340/10 = 28.39 kNm. Baris 3 hingga 5 menggunakan kaedah yang betul pada panjang yang salah; baris 5 betul mengambil FL/10, kerana B continuous.
