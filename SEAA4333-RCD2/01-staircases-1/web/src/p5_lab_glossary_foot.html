What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

flightAn inclined run of steps between two levels or landings.Satu larian anak tangga yang condong di antara dua aras atau landing.
landingA flat slab at the top or the bottom of a flight, or between two flights.Slab rata di atas atau di bawah flight, atau di antara dua flight.
waist hThe thickness of the inclined slab under the steps, measured square to the soffit.Ketebalan slab condong di bawah anak tangga, diukur tegak lurus dengan soffit.
rise R · riserR is the height of one step; the riser is the vertical face of the step. UBBL By-law 106: R ≤ 180 mm, the same on every step.R ialah ketinggian satu anak tangga; riser ialah muka menegak anak tangga. UBBL By-law 106: R ≤ 180 mm, sama pada setiap anak tangga.
going G · treadG is the horizontal depth of a tread, without the nosing; the tread is the surface you step on. UBBL By-law 106: G ≥ 255 mm.G ialah kedalaman mendatar tread, tanpa nosing; tread ialah permukaan yang dipijak. UBBL By-law 106: G ≥ 255 mm.
nosingThe front edge of a tread, which may overhang the riser below.Tepi hadapan tread, yang boleh terunjur melepasi riser di bawahnya.
soffitThe underside of the waist or of the landing.Permukaan bawah waist atau landing.
pitch θThe slope of the flight: tan θ = R/G.Kecondongan flight: tan θ = R/G.
vertical waist thickness yThe waist measured vertically: y = h/cos θ = h√(R² + G²)/G.Waist yang diukur secara menegak: y = h/cos θ = h√(R² + G²)/G.
average thickness tt = y + R/2: the mean vertical thickness of the waist and the steps, used for the self-weight on plan, 25t kN/m².t = y + R/2: purata ketebalan menegak waist dan anak tangga, digunakan untuk self-weight on plan, 25t kN/m².
load on planA load given per m² of horizontal (plan) area. Stair loads and stair spans are both taken on plan.Load yang diberi per m² luas mendatar (pelan). Load dan span tangga kedua-duanya diambil on plan.
design load ndnd = 1.35gk + 1.5qk per m² on plan (EN 1990, Eq. 6.10).nd = 1.35gk + 1.5qk per m² on plan (EN 1990, Eq. 6.10).
partial factors γG, γQ1.35 on permanent and 1.5 on variable actions at the ultimate limit state.1.35 bagi permanent action dan 1.5 bagi variable action pada ultimate limit state.
effective span LThe span of the beam on plan, centre to centre of the supports, built from segments: each flight or landing plus half the width of its support.Span beam on plan, tengah ke tengah support, dibina daripada segmen: setiap flight atau landing tambah separuh lebar support-nya.
one-way slab · 1 m stripA slab that bends in one direction. A stair is designed on a strip 1 m wide, so its loads are per metre width and its steel per metre.Slab yang melentur dalam satu arah. Tangga di-design pada strip selebar 1 m, jadi load-nya per meter lebar dan steel-nya per meter.
continuous endAn end built monolithically into a beam or a slab that resists rotation, so a hogging moment develops there.Hujung yang dibina secara monolitik ke dalam beam atau slab yang menahan putaran, jadi hogging moment terhasil di situ.
simply supported endAn end free to rotate, such as a slab resting on a brick wall; zero moment is assumed there.Hujung yang bebas berputar, seperti slab yang terletak di atas dinding bata; moment sifar diandaikan di situ.
hogging momentA moment that puts the top face in tension. Over a continuous support of a stair it is taken as FL/10.Moment yang menyebabkan muka atas dalam tension. Di atas support continuous tangga ia diambil sebagai FL/10.
K, two meaningsIn flexure, K = M/(bd²fck), compared with Kbal = 0.167. In deflection, the structural factor K of EC2 Table 7.4N: 1.0 simply supported, 1.3 end span, 1.5 interior span.Dalam flexure, K = M/(bd²fck), dibandingkan dengan Kbal = 0.167. Dalam deflection, structural factor K daripada EC2 Table 7.4N: 1.0 simply supported, 1.3 end span, 1.5 interior span.
distribution barsSecondary bars at right angles to the main bars: at least 20% of the main steel, at a spacing ≤ min(3h, 400) mm where the moment is largest.Bar sekunder yang bersudut tegak dengan main bar: sekurang-kurangnya 20% daripada main steel, dengan jarak ≤ min(3h, 400) mm di tempat moment paling besar.
top barsBars near the top face over a support: the same as the main bars over 0.3L at a continuous end; at least 25% of the span steel over 0.2L at a simple end.Bar berhampiran muka atas di atas support: sama seperti main bar sepanjang 0.3L di hujung continuous; sekurang-kurangnya 25% steel span sepanjang 0.2L di hujung simple.
kinkThe inside (re-entrant) corner of the soffit where a flight meets a landing. The bottom bars must cross there, not bend round it.Sudut dalam (re-entrant) pada soffit di tempat flight bertemu landing. Bottom bar mesti bersilang di situ, bukan dibengkokkan mengelilinginya.
UBBLUniform Building By-Laws 1984. By-law 106: rise, going and landing depth; By-law 108: risers in one flight.Uniform Building By-Laws 1984. By-law 106: rise, going dan kedalaman landing; By-law 108: riser dalam satu flight.
comfort rule 2R + GAbout 600 mm, from 550 to 700 mm (course notes). A comfort test, separate from the by-laws.Lebih kurang 600 mm, daripada 550 hingga 700 mm (nota kursus). Ujian keselesaan, berasingan daripada by-law.
Professional Habit 1Sanity-check the load before you design: the flight’s nd must exceed the landing’s nd. A ratio of 1.0 or less means a slip.Semak kewajaran load sebelum design: nd flight mesti melebihi nd landing. Nisbah 1.0 atau kurang bermakna ada kesilapan.

Short notesNota ringkas

§2 GeometryGeometri

  • R ≤ 180 and G ≥ 255 mm, uniform (By-law 106); landing depth ≥ stair width.R ≤ 180 dan G ≥ 255 mm, seragam (By-law 106); kedalaman landing ≥ lebar tangga.
  • At most 16 risers per flight, not residential (By-law 108).Paling banyak 16 riser setiap flight, bukan kediaman (By-law 108).
  • n = H/180 rounded up; R = H/n; 2R + G ≈ 600 (550 to 700) mm.n = H/180 dibundarkan ke atas; R = H/n; 2R + G ≈ 600 (550 hingga 700) mm.

§3 Load on plan

Landing: 25hL + finishes. Flight nd ÷ landing nd > 1.Landing: 25hL + finishes. nd flight ÷ nd landing > 1.

§4 Beam on plan

  • L = Σ segments, centre to centre on plan; F = Σ nd,i li.L = Σ segmen, tengah ke tengah on plan; F = Σ nd,i li.
  • M = FL/10 with at least one continuous end; FL/8 with none.M = FL/10 dengan sekurang-kurangnya satu hujung continuous; FL/8 tanpanya.
  • VA = ΣFi(L − xi)/L + (MA − MB)/L; VB = F − VA.
  • Deflection K = 1.5 (both continuous), 1.3 (one), 1.0 (none).Deflection K = 1.5 (kedua-dua continuous), 1.3 (satu), 1.0 (tiada).

§5 The waistWaist

  • d = h − cnom − φ/2; K = M/(bd²fck) ≤ 0.167; z ≤ 0.95d; As = M/(0.87fykz).
  • Distribution ≥ 20% of the main steel provided.Distribution ≥ 20% daripada main steel yang disediakan.
  • Shear at the continuous end, with ρl from the top bars there.Shear di hujung continuous, dengan ρl daripada top bar di situ.
  • (l/d)allow = K × basic × As,prov/As,req (≤ 1.5) ≥ L/d.(l/d)allow = K × asas × As,prov/As,req (≤ 1.5) ≥ L/d.
  • Spacing where M peaks: main ≤ min(2h, 250), distribution ≤ min(3h, 400) mm.Jarak di tempat M puncak: main ≤ min(2h, 250), distribution ≤ min(3h, 400) mm.

§6 Detailing

  • Continuous end: top bars as the main bars, over 0.3L from the face.Hujung continuous: top bar sama seperti main bar, sepanjang 0.3L dari muka.
  • Simple end: at least 25% of the span steel, over 0.2L.Hujung simple: sekurang-kurangnya 25% steel span, sepanjang 0.2L.
  • Kink: the bottom bars cross, each anchored in the compression zone.Kink: bottom bar bersilang, setiap satu di-anchor dalam compression zone.
