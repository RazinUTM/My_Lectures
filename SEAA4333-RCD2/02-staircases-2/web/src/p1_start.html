What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Unit 1 designed a flight that spans from one beam to another. In most buildings a flight rests on a landing instead, and the landing is the member that carries it to the walls. This Unit starts with the drawing: a stair plan tells you which way is up, how many risers and goings there are and how long the flight is on plan, and past finals show that it is easy to misread. It then follows the load of a flight that rests on a landing spanning between two walls, the open-well stair of the course notes. The flight’s span runs into the landing, to its centre, and the half of the landing inside that span carries load too: leave it out, as the notes’ own example does, and the flight is under-reinforced. The landing is then designed as a slab between the walls, carrying its own load and the flight’s reaction. Two flights at right angles share the load of their common landing, half each, and a flight built into walls along its sides passes a strip of its load straight to the walls.Unit 1 membuat design flight yang merentang dari satu beam ke beam yang lain. Dalam kebanyakan bangunan, flight sebaliknya bertumpu pada landing, dan landing ialah member yang memikulnya ke dinding. Unit ini bermula dengan lukisan: pelan tangga memberitahu arah naik, bilangan riser dan going serta panjang flight pada pelan, dan peperiksaan akhir yang lalu menunjukkan bahawa ia mudah tersalah baca. Kemudian ia menjejak load flight yang bertumpu pada landing yang merentang di antara dua dinding, iaitu tangga open-well dalam nota kursus. Span flight berlanjut ke dalam landing, hingga ke tengahnya, dan separuh landing di dalam span itu juga memikul load: jika ia ditinggalkan, seperti dalam contoh nota itu sendiri, flight kekurangan reinforcement. Landing kemudian direka bentuk sebagai slab di antara dinding, memikul load sendiri dan reaction flight. Dua flight yang bersudut tepat berkongsi load landing bersama, separuh setiap satu, dan flight yang dibina ke dalam dinding di sepanjang sisinya memindahkan satu jalur load terus ke dinding.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

A landing slab is 150 mm thick, with finishes of 1.2 kN/m² and an imposed load of 3.0 kN/m². What is its design load per m²? Concrete weighs 25 kN/m³.Satu slab landing setebal 150 mm, dengan kemasan 1.2 kN/m² dan imposed load 3.0 kN/m². Berapakah design load per m²? Konkrit seberat 25 kN/m³.

1.35 × (25 × 0.15 + 1.2) + 1.5 × 3.0 = 1.35 × 4.95 + 4.5 = 11.18 kN/m². A landing has no steps, so its own weight is 25h, not 25t: lighter than a flight of the same waist.1.35 × (25 × 0.15 + 1.2) + 1.5 × 3.0 = 1.35 × 4.95 + 4.5 = 11.18 kN/m². Landing tiada anak tangga, jadi berat sendirinya ialah 25h, bukan 25t: lebih ringan daripada flight dengan waist yang sama.

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

A span of 4.0 m carries 10 kN/m. End A is simply supported; end B is continuous with a hogging moment of 16 kN m. What is the reaction at A?Satu span 4.0 m memikul 10 kN/m. Hujung A simply supported; hujung B continuous dengan hogging moment 16 kN m. Berapakah reaction di A?

Moments about B: 4.0VA = 10 × 4.0 × 2.0 − 16, so VA = 16 kN and VB = 24 kN. The hogging moment at B moves load towards B: the reaction at the simple end falls below wL/2.Moment pada B: 4.0VA = 10 × 4.0 × 2.0 − 16, jadi VA = 16 kN dan VB = 24 kN. Hogging moment di B memindahkan load ke arah B: reaction di hujung simple menjadi kurang daripada wL/2.

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A slab spans between two walls and bears on them without continuity. Which structural system factor K of EC2 Table 7.4N do you use for its span/depth check?Satu slab merentang di antara dua dinding dan bertumpu padanya tanpa continuity. Faktor sistem struktur K yang manakah daripada EC2 Table 7.4N digunakan bagi semakan span/depth?

K = 1.0 for a simply supported span; 1.3 for an end span of a continuous slab, 1.5 for an interior span, 0.4 for a cantilever. A landing that spans between two walls is simply supported: K = 1.0, whatever the flight on it uses.K = 1.0 bagi span simply supported; 1.3 bagi span hujung continuous slab, 1.5 bagi span dalaman, 0.4 bagi cantilever. Landing yang merentang di antara dua dinding simply supported: K = 1.0, walau apa pun yang digunakan oleh flight di atasnya.
