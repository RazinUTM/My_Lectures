1. Where a flight’s load goesKe mana load flight pergi

≈ 6 min

Unit 1 put a flight between two beams and designed it as a one-way slab on plan. Real stairs are seldom that simple. A flight usually starts or ends on a landing, two flights often share one landing, and a stair inside a core of walls is cast into those walls. The flight is still a slab, and its design steps do not change; what changes is where its supports are, which loads lie between them, and which member carries what to the ground.Unit 1 meletakkan flight di antara dua beam dan membuat design sebagai one-way slab pada pelan. Tangga sebenar jarang semudah itu. Flight biasanya bermula atau berakhir pada landing, dua flight sering berkongsi satu landing, dan tangga di dalam teras dinding dituang ke dalam dinding tersebut. Flight masih slab, dan langkah design-nya tidak berubah; yang berubah ialah kedudukan support-nya, load mana yang berada di antara support itu, dan member mana yang memikul apa hingga ke tanah.

In an open-well stair the flights run side by side with a gap, the well, between them. Each flight rests at one end on a beam or a floor slab and at the other on a landing. The landing does not run along the flight: it spans across it, from one wall to the other, past the end of the well. So the flight is supported by the landing, and the landing is supported by the walls. This is Example 3 of the course notes, and it is the main case of this Unit.Dalam tangga open-well, flight-flight berjalan sebelah-menyebelah dengan satu ruang, iaitu well, di antaranya. Setiap flight bertumpu di satu hujung pada beam atau slab lantai dan di hujung yang lain pada landing. Landing tidak berjalan di sepanjang flight: ia merentang melintang, dari satu dinding ke dinding yang lain, melepasi hujung well. Jadi flight disokong oleh landing, dan landing disokong oleh dinding. Inilah Contoh 3 dalam nota kursus, dan ia kes utama Unit ini.

When two flights meet at right angles, round two sides of a well, they share a quarter landing. The landing then lies inside the span of both flights at once, and its load is divided between them. This is Example 4 of the course notes. A flight built into walls along its sides hands a strip of its load straight to the walls, which BS 8110 allows the designer to use. Steps can also span across a flight between two stringer beams, or cantilever from a wall; each step is then a small beam (Mosley §8.8.1). Those two are named here and left to the RC Design app (cases ST9 and ST10).Apabila dua flight bertemu pada sudut tepat, mengelilingi dua sisi well, ia berkongsi quarter landing. Landing itu kemudian berada di dalam span kedua-dua flight serentak, dan load-nya dibahagikan di antara keduanya. Inilah Contoh 4 dalam nota kursus. Flight yang dibina ke dalam dinding di sepanjang sisinya memindahkan satu jalur load terus ke dinding, dan BS 8110 membenarkan pereka bentuk menggunakannya. Anak tangga juga boleh merentang melintang flight di antara dua stringer beam, atau menganjur sebagai cantilever dari dinding; setiap anak tangga kemudian menjadi beam kecil (Mosley §8.8.1). Kedua-dua kes itu hanya dinamakan di sini dan diserahkan kepada RC Design app (kes ST9 dan ST10).

Whatever the arrangement, the design of a stair answers three questions in order: where are the supports, which loads lie between them, and which member passes its load to which. The calculation of Unit 1 then runs unchanged. Most mistakes in stairs are made before that calculation starts, in one of those three answers.Walau apa pun susunannya, design tangga menjawab tiga soalan mengikut urutan: di manakah support, load manakah yang berada di antaranya, dan member manakah yang memindahkan load kepada member yang mana. Pengiraan Unit 1 kemudian berjalan tanpa perubahan. Kebanyakan kesilapan dalam tangga dibuat sebelum pengiraan itu bermula, dalam salah satu daripada tiga jawapan tersebut.

The landing is a member in its own right: it carries its own load and the reaction of the flight, and it needs its own design.Flight bertumpu pada landing, dan landing merentang melintang di antara dua dinding. Quick checkSemak cepat

Two flights meet at right angles at one quarter landing. Whose span is the landing part of?Dua flight bertemu pada sudut tepat di satu quarter landing. Landing itu sebahagian daripada span siapa?

Each flight spans through the landing to the support beyond it, so the landing lies inside both spans. That is why its load is shared between them (§5).Setiap flight merentang melalui landing hingga ke support di seberangnya, jadi landing berada di dalam kedua-dua span. Itulah sebabnya load-nya dikongsi di antara keduanya (§5).

  • A flight is still a slab; what changes from Unit 1 is where its supports are and which loads lie on its span.Flight masih slab; yang berubah daripada Unit 1 ialah kedudukan support-nya dan load yang berada pada span-nya.
  • Open well: the flight rests on a landing; the landing spans across between walls.Open well: flight bertumpu pada landing; landing merentang melintang di antara dinding.
  • Right angles: a common landing lies inside two spans; its load is shared.Sudut tepat: landing bersama berada di dalam dua span; load-nya dikongsi.

2. Reading a stair planMembaca pelan tangga

≈ 7 min

Before any load, read the drawing. The course notes end their staircase chapter with the mistakes made in a past final examination, and none of them is a calculation: the dimensions of the stair, the number of steps, which way is up on the plan, and the wrong dimension used for the span. Each one starts a correct calculation on the wrong stair.Sebelum sebarang load, baca lukisan. Nota kursus menutup bab tangga dengan kesilapan yang dibuat dalam peperiksaan akhir yang lalu, dan tiada satu pun ialah pengiraan: dimensi tangga, bilangan anak tangga, arah naik pada pelan, dan dimensi yang salah digunakan bagi span. Setiap satu memulakan pengiraan yang betul pada tangga yang salah.

A stair plan shows the treads as parallel lines, the nosings of the steps. Arrows start at the floor the plan is drawn for: UP runs up the flight that climbs from that floor and DN runs down the flight that descends from it, so a DN arrow ends at the lower end of its flight; follow them to know which end of each flight is the bottom. Where the plan cut passes through the flight that climbs, a break line crosses it: the rest of that flight belongs to the plan of the floor above. Dimension strings give the treads as a count and a size, such as 9 @ 260 = 2340, and the widths of the landings and walls. The section, or a level on the plan, gives the heights.Pelan tangga menunjukkan tread sebagai garisan selari, iaitu nosing anak tangga. Anak panah bermula di lantai yang dilukis pelannya: UP menghala ke atas flight yang mendaki dari lantai itu dan DN menghala ke bawah flight yang menurun darinya, jadi anak panah DN berakhir di hujung bawah flight-nya; ikutinya untuk mengetahui hujung mana bagi setiap flight ialah bahagian bawah. Di tempat potongan pelan melalui flight yang mendaki, satu garisan putus merentasinya: baki flight itu milik pelan lantai di atas. Rentetan dimensi memberi tread sebagai bilangan dan saiz, seperti 9 @ 260 = 2340, serta lebar landing dan dinding. Section, atau aras pada pelan, memberi ketinggian.

Then count. The height a flight climbs is shared equally by its risers, so R = Hf/nR. The plan length of its sloping slab is the number of goings on the slope times G. The two counts are not always equal. A flight that runs from one floor or landing to the next has one going fewer than risers, because its last step is the landing itself; that is the stair above, with 10 risers and 9 treads. The course notes’ Examples 3 and 4 draw 10 risers and 10 goings, because there the top going is drawn as part of the flight. Read the dimension string for the goings and the heights for the risers, and do not assume one from the other.Kemudian kira. Ketinggian yang dinaiki flight dibahagi sama rata oleh riser-nya, jadi R = Hf/nR. Panjang pelan slab cerunnya ialah bilangan going pada cerun didarab G. Dua bilangan itu tidak selalu sama. Flight yang berjalan dari satu lantai atau landing ke yang seterusnya mempunyai satu going kurang daripada riser, kerana anak tangga terakhirnya ialah landing itu sendiri; itulah tangga di atas, dengan 10 riser dan 9 tread. Contoh 3 dan 4 dalam nota kursus melukis 10 riser dan 10 going, kerana di situ going teratas dilukis sebagai sebahagian daripada flight. Baca rentetan dimensi bagi going dan ketinggian bagi riser, dan jangan andaikan satu daripada yang lain.

(2.1)

Last, check the numbers against the rules of Unit 1: UBBL By-law 106 asks for R ≤ 180 mm and G ≥ 255 mm, the same on every step (one slide of the course notes prints “G ≤ 255”; the by-law sets a minimum), and a landing at least as deep as the stair is wide; By-law 108 allows at most 16 risers in one flight of a building other than a residence. 2R + G of about 600 mm (550 to 700) is the comfort rule of the notes, a separate test from the law. For the stair above: R = 170 ≤ 180, G = 260 ≥ 255, 2R + G = 600.Akhir sekali, semak nombor dengan peraturan Unit 1: UBBL By-law 106 memerlukan R ≤ 180 mm dan G ≥ 255 mm, sama pada setiap anak tangga (satu slide nota kursus mencetak “G ≤ 255”; by-law menetapkan had minimum), dan landing sekurang-kurangnya sedalam lebar tangga; By-law 108 membenarkan paling banyak 16 riser dalam satu flight bagi bangunan selain kediaman. 2R + G lebih kurang 600 mm (550 hingga 700) ialah peraturan keselesaan dalam nota, ujian yang berasingan daripada undang-undang. Bagi tangga di atas: R = 170 ≤ 180, G = 260 ≥ 255, 2R + G = 600.

A flight runs from a floor to a landing 1.53 m higher. Its plan shows 9 @ 250 = 2250, and the landing is the last step. How many risers does it have, and what is R?Satu flight berjalan dari lantai ke landing 1.53 m lebih tinggi. Pelannya menunjukkan 9 @ 250 = 2250, dan landing ialah anak tangga terakhir. Berapakah bilangan riser, dan berapakah R?

Nine treads on the slope and the landing as the tenth step: 10 risers, R = 1530/10 = 153 mm. Taking 9 risers would give 170 mm, a stair that does not fit the floor height.Sembilan tread pada cerun dan landing sebagai anak tangga kesepuluh: 10 riser, R = 1530/10 = 153 mm. Mengambil 9 riser memberi 170 mm, iaitu tangga yang tidak sepadan dengan ketinggian lantai.

Quick checkSemak cepat

On a plan seen from the first floor, the arrow on a flight is marked DN. Where is the bottom of that flight?Pada pelan yang dilihat dari tingkat satu, anak panah pada satu flight ditanda DN. Di manakah bahagian bawah flight itu?

DN points down the stair from the floor the plan is drawn for, so the arrow ends at the lower end. UP starts at the lower end. Either way, the arrow is the first thing to read.DN menghala menuruni tangga dari lantai yang dilukis pelannya, jadi anak panah berakhir di hujung bawah. UP bermula di hujung bawah. Walau bagaimanapun, anak panah ialah perkara pertama yang perlu dibaca.

Key pointsPerkara utama
  • Read the arrow first: UP starts at the bottom, DN ends at the bottom.Baca anak panah dahulu: UP bermula di bawah, DN berakhir di bawah.
  • R = Hf/nR; Lf = nGG. Count the goings from the dimension string; they may be one fewer than the risers.R = Hf/nR; Lf = nGG. Kira going daripada rentetan dimensi; ia mungkin satu kurang daripada riser.
  • Check R ≤ 180, G ≥ 255, at most 16 risers, 2R + G near 600 mm.Semak R ≤ 180, G ≥ 255, paling banyak 16 riser, 2R + G sekitar 600 mm.

3. A flight on a landing: the effective span and its loadFlight pada landing: effective span dan load-nya

≈ 8 min

Here is the big question of the Unit. A flight rests on a landing that spans between two walls. The flight is a slab one metre wide spanning from its beam to the landing, but where exactly does its span end, and what load lies on it?Inilah soalan besar Unit ini. Satu flight bertumpu pada landing yang merentang di antara dua dinding. Flight ialah slab selebar satu meter yang merentang dari beam-nya ke landing, tetapi di manakah tepatnya span itu berakhir, dan load apakah yang berada padanya?

Effective span of a flight supported by landings (BS 8110-1, 3.10.1.3; the course notes)Effective span flight yang disokong oleh landing (BS 8110-1, 3.10.1.3; nota kursus)L = La + ½(Lb1 + Lb2), where La is the clear horizontal distance between the supports and Lb1, Lb2 are the widths of the supports along the flight, a beam, a wall or a landing, each taken as not more than 1.8 m. EC2 has no rule of its own for stairs; the course uses this one.L = La + ½(Lb1 + Lb2), dengan La jarak mengufuk bersih di antara support dan Lb1, Lb2 lebar support di sepanjang flight, sama ada beam, dinding atau landing, setiap satu diambil tidak melebihi 1.8 m. EC2 tiada peraturan sendiri bagi tangga; kursus menggunakan peraturan ini.
(2.2)

The support of the flight is therefore taken at the centre of the landing, up to 0.9 m into it. A landing is a wide, flexible support, not a knife edge. Mosley measures the span “between the centres of the supports” (§8.8.2), which for a landing is its centre; BS 8110 and the course notes add the 1.8 m limit for a very deep landing. For the notes’ Example 3: La = 10 × 260 = 2600 mm, Lb1 = 200 mm (the beam), Lb2 = 1500 mm (the landing), so L = 3450 mm.Oleh itu support flight diambil di tengah landing, sehingga 0.9 m ke dalamnya. Landing ialah support yang lebar dan fleksibel, bukan mata pisau. Mosley mengukur span “between the centres of the supports” (§8.8.2), dan bagi landing itulah tengahnya; BS 8110 dan nota kursus menambah had 1.8 m bagi landing yang sangat dalam. Bagi Contoh 3 nota: La = 10 × 260 = 2600 mm, Lb1 = 200 mm (beam), Lb2 = 1500 mm (landing), jadi L = 3450 mm.

Now the step that is easy to miss. The span ends 750 mm inside the landing, so the last 750 mm of the span is landing, and it carries the landing’s load. The total load on the span has two parts: the flight load over the flight, measured to the centre of the beam, and the landing load over the strip of landing inside the span.Sekarang langkah yang mudah terlepas. Span berakhir 750 mm di dalam landing, jadi 750 mm terakhir span itu ialah landing, dan ia memikul load landing. Jumlah load pada span mempunyai dua bahagian: load flight di sepanjang flight, diukur hingga ke tengah beam, dan load landing di sepanjang jalur landing di dalam span.

(2.3)

With nf = 15.0374 and nL = 11.1825 kN/m² for the 150 mm flight and landing of Example 3: F = 15.0374 × 2.70 + 11.1825 × 0.75 = 40.601 + 8.387 = 48.988 kN per metre width. The course notes count the flight only, F = 15.04 × 2.7 = 40.6 kN: the 8.39 kN of landing inside the span, 17 % of the load, is missing (audit T-01 of the RC Design app). Every result that follows, the moment, the steel and the landing’s own load, inherits the shortfall.Dengan nf = 15.0374 dan nL = 11.1825 kN/m² bagi flight dan landing 150 mm dalam Contoh 3: F = 15.0374 × 2.70 + 11.1825 × 0.75 = 40.601 + 8.387 = 48.988 kN per meter lebar. Nota kursus mengira flight sahaja, F = 15.04 × 2.7 = 40.6 kN: 8.39 kN landing di dalam span, 17 % daripada load, tertinggal (audit T-01 RC Design app). Setiap keputusan seterusnya, iaitu moment, steel dan load landing itu sendiri, mewarisi kekurangan tersebut.

The moment follows the end conditions, as in Unit 1: FL/10 when at least one end is continuous, here the beam at the foot, FL/8 when neither is. M = 48.988 × 3.45/10 = 16.901 kN m/m, against the notes’ 14.0. The reactions come from statics on the whole span, with the hogging moment at each continuous end (MA, MB; zero at a simple end). Taking moments about the beam end B, with each load at the centre of its own length and xB measured from B:Moment mengikut keadaan hujung, seperti dalam Unit 1: FL/10 apabila sekurang-kurangnya satu hujung continuous, di sini beam di kaki flight, FL/8 apabila tiada hujung yang continuous. M = 48.988 × 3.45/10 = 16.901 kN m/m, berbanding 14.0 dalam nota. Reaction diperoleh daripada statik pada keseluruhan span, dengan hogging moment di setiap hujung continuous (MA, MB; sifar di hujung simple). With MA = 0 at the landing and MB = 16.901 kN m at the beam: VA = (8.387 × 3.075 + 40.601 × 1.35 − 16.901)/3.45 = (25.790 + 54.811 − 16.901)/3.45 = 18.464 kN/m at the landing end, and VB = 48.988 − 18.464 = 30.524 kN/m at the beam. VA is what each metre width of the flight puts on the landing: the starting point of §4.Dengan MA = 0 di landing dan MB = 16.901 kN m di beam: VA = (8.387 × 3.075 + 40.601 × 1.35 − 16.901)/3.45 = (25.790 + 54.811 − 16.901)/3.45 = 18.464 kN/m di hujung landing, dan VB = 48.988 − 18.464 = 30.524 kN/m di beam. Quick checkSemak cepat

A flight has a clear horizontal length of 2400 mm between a beam 250 mm wide and a landing 2000 mm deep. What is its effective span?Satu flight mempunyai panjang mengufuk bersih 2400 mm di antara beam selebar 250 mm dan landing sedalam 2000 mm. Berapakah effective span-nya?

The landing counts as at most 1.8 m: L = 2400 + ½(250 + 1800) = 3425 mm. Without the limit you would get 3525 mm; stopping at the edge of the landing, 2525 mm, leaves out the part of the span that lies on the landing.Landing dikira paling banyak 1.8 m: L = 2400 + ½(250 + 1800) = 3425 mm. Quick checkSemak cepat

In the same stair, which load lies on the last 900 mm of the span?Dalam tangga yang sama, load manakah yang berada pada 900 mm terakhir span?

The span ends at the centre of the landing, 900 mm in (half of the 1.8 m limit), so its last 900 mm is landing and carries nL, which is lighter than the flight load because a landing has no steps. Only the half inside the span counts.Span berakhir di tengah landing, 900 mm ke dalam (separuh had 1.8 m), jadi 900 mm terakhirnya ialah landing dan memikul nL, yang lebih ringan daripada load flight kerana landing tiada anak tangga. Hanya separuh yang berada di dalam span dikira.

4. The landing: a slab between wallsLanding: slab di antara dinding

≈ 7 min

Follow the load one member further. The flight puts VA on the landing along the line where they meet. The landing spans across, between the two walls, so it is a one-way slab of its own: its breadth is the depth of the landing on plan, its span is the distance between the walls, and its supports are simple. It is designed exactly like a slab of Unit 1, for a load that the flight decides.Jejak load satu member lagi. Flight meletakkan VA pada landing di sepanjang garisan pertemuan keduanya. Landing merentang melintang, di antara dua dinding, jadi ia one-way slab tersendiri: lebarnya ialah kedalaman landing pada pelan, span-nya ialah jarak di antara dinding, dan support-nya simple. Ia direka bentuk tepat seperti slab dalam Unit 1, bagi load yang ditentukan oleh flight.

Per metre of its span the landing carries two things: its own load nL over its whole breadth bL, and the reaction VA of the flights that rest along it. The flight below the landing and the flight above it both bear on it, side by side, so the reaction runs along most of the span; the course notes and the RC Design app take it along the whole span, which ignores the narrow well and is slightly on the safe side.Per meter span-nya landing memikul dua perkara: load sendiri nL di sepanjang seluruh lebarnya bL, dan reaction VA daripada flight yang bertumpu di sepanjangnya. Flight di bawah landing dan flight di atasnya kedua-duanya bertumpu padanya, sebelah-menyebelah, jadi reaction itu berada di sepanjang sebahagian besar span; nota kursus dan RC Design app mengambilnya di sepanjang seluruh span, yang mengabaikan well yang sempit dan sedikit di pihak selamat.

(2.5)

For the notes’ Example 3 with the corrected reaction of §3: wL = 11.1825 × 1.5 + 18.464 = 16.774 + 18.464 = 35.238 kN/m. The notes, which left the landing strip out of the flight’s span, have 28.6 kN/m: the shortfall of §3 has travelled into the landing.Bagi Contoh 3 nota dengan reaction yang dibetulkan dalam §3: wL = 11.1825 × 1.5 + 18.464 = 16.774 + 18.464 = 35.238 kN/m. Nota, yang meninggalkan jalur landing daripada span flight, mendapat 28.6 kN/m: kekurangan dalam §3 telah berpindah ke dalam landing.

(2.6)

The landing is simply supported on the walls, with LL = 3.4 m between their centres: ML = 35.2375 × 3.4²/8 = 50.918 kN m and VL = 35.2375 × 3.4/2 = 59.904 kN, both for the whole 1.5 m breadth (the notes: 41.3 kN m). Bending, shear and deflection are then checked on b = bL = 1500 mm, with K = 1.0 for the span/depth ratio.Landing simply supported pada dinding, dengan LL = 3.4 m di antara pusatnya: ML = 35.2375 × 3.4²/8 = 50.918 kN m dan VL = 35.2375 × 3.4/2 = 59.904 kN, kedua-duanya bagi seluruh lebar 1.5 m (nota: 41.3 kN m). Bending, shear dan deflection kemudian disemak pada b = bL = 1500 mm, dengan K = 1.0 bagi nisbah span/depth.

One detail sets the effective depth. Where the flight meets the landing, the flight’s main bars run into the landing in one direction and the landing’s main bars cross them in the other, so one set must lie on the other. The course places the landing bars in the inner layer, above the flight bars (RC Design app, decision D40), which is on the safe side; the notes take the outer layer, d = hL − c − φL/2. Whichever is chosen, the drawing must show the same.Satu butiran menentukan effective depth. Di tempat flight bertemu landing, bar utama flight masuk ke dalam landing dalam satu arah dan bar utama landing merentasinya dalam arah yang lain, jadi satu set mesti berada di atas set yang lain. Kursus meletakkan bar landing dalam lapisan dalam, di atas bar flight (RC Design app, keputusan D40), yang berada di pihak selamat; nota mengambil lapisan luar, d = hL − c − φL/2. Apa pun pilihannya, lukisan mesti menunjukkan perkara yang sama.

(2.7)

5. Two flights at right anglesDua flight bersudut tepat

≈ 6 min

When two flights turn a corner round a well, the quarter landing at the corner belongs to both. Each flight spans from its beam, through the landing, to the wall on the far side of it. The landing therefore lies inside two spans at once, and both flights carry it.Apabila dua flight membelok di satu sudut mengelilingi well, quarter landing di sudut itu milik kedua-duanya. Setiap flight merentang dari beam-nya, melalui landing, hingga ke dinding di seberang landing. Oleh itu landing berada di dalam dua span serentak, dan kedua-dua flight memikulnya.

Mosley §8.8.2 states the rule that the course notes use: loads common to two spans which intersect at right angles and surround an open well may be assumed to be divided equally between the spans. The landing is supported in two directions, by the two flights, and the equal split is the agreed simplification of that two-way load path; it is not a calculated share. In the span of each flight the landing therefore carries half of its load per m²:Mosley §8.8.2 menyatakan peraturan yang digunakan oleh nota kursus: load yang sepunya bagi dua span yang bersilang pada sudut tepat dan mengelilingi open well boleh dianggap dibahagi sama rata di antara span tersebut. Landing disokong dalam dua arah, oleh dua flight, dan pembahagian sama rata ialah permudahan yang diterima bagi laluan load dua arah itu; ia bukan bahagian yang dikira.
