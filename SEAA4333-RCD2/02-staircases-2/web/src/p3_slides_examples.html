What the numbers sayApa yang nombor ini katakanThe notes’ design looks complete, but it starts from a span that ends 750 mm inside the landing and a load that stops at the landing’s edge. The 8.39 kN left out is 17 % of the load. It reaches the flight steel first (H10-250 is 8 % short), then the landing’s load (35.2 instead of 28.6 kN/m) and its moment (50.9 instead of 41.3 kN m). The landing’s deflection, which the notes passed by 1 %, now fails by a wide margin. Deflection is cured by depth, not by bars. And because the landing is part of the flight’s span, a thicker landing puts 1.27 kN more on the flight: in a stair, every member’s decision reaches the one next to it.Design dalam nota kelihatan lengkap, tetapi ia bermula daripada span yang berakhir 750 mm di dalam landing dan load yang berhenti di tepi landing. 8.39 kN yang tertinggal ialah 17 % daripada load. Ia sampai kepada steel flight dahulu (H10-250 kurang 8 %), kemudian load landing (35.2 dan bukan 28.6 kN/m) dan moment-nya (50.9 dan bukan 41.3 kN m). Deflection landing, yang diluluskan nota dengan lebihan 1 %, kini gagal dengan jauh. Deflection diselesaikan dengan kedalaman, bukan dengan bar. Dan kerana landing sebahagian daripada span flight, landing yang lebih tebal meletakkan 1.27 kN lagi pada flight: dalam tangga, keputusan bagi setiap member sampai kepada member di sebelahnya.

Answer 1Jawapan 1 A wide support, counted up to a pointSupport yang lebar, dikira hingga satu had

A landing is not a knife edge: the flight hands its load to it over a width, and the landing itself bends. Taking the span to the centre of the support is the usual rule for a slab on a wide support; Mosley measures the span of a stair between the centres of its supports. A very deep landing would put that centre far from where the flight meets it, so BS 8110 counts the landing’s width only up to 1.8 m: at most 0.9 m of it lies inside the span. Inside that length the landing strip carries its own load, which belongs in F.Landing bukan mata pisau: flight memindahkan load kepadanya di sepanjang suatu lebar, dan landing itu sendiri melentur. Mengambil span hingga ke tengah support ialah peraturan biasa bagi slab pada support yang lebar; Mosley mengukur span tangga di antara pusat support-nya. Landing yang sangat dalam akan meletakkan pusat itu jauh dari tempat flight bertemu landing, jadi BS 8110 mengira lebar landing hanya hingga 1.8 m: paling banyak 0.9 m daripadanya berada di dalam span. Di sepanjang panjang itu, jalur landing memikul load sendiri, yang mesti dimasukkan dalam F.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 No: the other flight carries itTidak: flight yang satu lagi memikulnya

Nothing is lost. The quarter landing lies inside two spans, one for each flight, so it is supported in two directions. Mosley §8.8.2 lets the load common to two spans at right angles be divided equally between them: each flight takes half, and together they take all of it. The split is an agreed simplification, not a calculated share. It holds only if the second flight is designed for its half; and it does not apply to a landing that one flight carries alone, nor to the open-well landing, which is a support.Tiada apa yang hilang. Quarter landing berada di dalam dua span, satu bagi setiap flight, jadi ia disokong dalam dua arah. Mosley §8.8.2 membenarkan load yang sepunya bagi dua span bersudut tepat dibahagi sama rata di antara keduanya: setiap flight mengambil separuh, dan bersama-sama ia mengambil kesemuanya. Pembahagian itu ialah permudahan yang diterima, bukan bahagian yang dikira. Ia sah hanya jika flight kedua direka bentuk bagi separuhnya; dan ia tidak terpakai bagi landing yang dipikul oleh satu flight sahaja, atau bagi landing open-well, yang merupakan support.

Bar tambahan menaikkan As,prov/As,req, tetapi nisbah itu dihadkan kepada 1.5, dan nisbah asas kekal 16.17 kerana ia bergantung pada steel yang diperlukan, yang sudah melebihi ρ0 = 0.5 %. Answer 4Jawapan 4 Both, on different assumptionsKedua-duanya, dengan andaian yang berbeza

Both designs are safe. Loading the whole width ignores the help of the walls; it is conservative, and it is the RC Design app’s default. The BS 8110 rule, which the course uses, leaves a 150 mm strip next to each wall unloaded and adds 75 mm per wall to the breadth: κw = 900/1350 = ⅔, and H8-225 passes every check. The second answer is right only if its conditions are stated and met: the flight is built at least 110 mm into each wall, the walls can take the load, and the clause is read per wall (read as one strip in all, κw = 0.824 and more steel is needed). A design that uses an allowance must say so.Kedua-dua design selamat. Membebani seluruh lebar mengabaikan bantuan dinding; ia konservatif, dan ia default RC Design app. Peraturan BS 8110, yang digunakan oleh kursus, membiarkan jalur 150 mm bersebelahan setiap dinding tanpa load dan menambah 75 mm bagi setiap dinding kepada lebar: κw = 900/1350 = ⅔, dan H8-225 lulus setiap semakan.
