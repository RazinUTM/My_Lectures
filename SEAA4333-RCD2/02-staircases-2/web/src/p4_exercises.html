A1 · 1 markmarkah
A flight of 12 risers runs from a floor up to a landing, and the landing forms its last step. How many treads are drawn on its slope on the plan?Satu flight dengan 12 riser berjalan dari lantai hingga ke landing, dan landing membentuk anak tangga terakhirnya. Berapakah bilangan tread yang dilukis pada cerunnya dalam pelan?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

The top step is the landing itself, so the slope has one tread fewer than the flight has risers: 11 treads, and the plan length of the slope is 11G. Read the treads from the dimension string and the risers from the heights (Notes §2).Anak tangga teratas ialah landing itu sendiri, jadi cerun mempunyai satu tread kurang daripada bilangan riser flight: 11 tread, dan panjang pelan cerun ialah 11G. Baca tread daripada rentetan dimensi dan riser daripada ketinggian (Nota §2).

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A straight flight (R = 170, G = 255 mm, 2550 mm on plan, waist 150 mm) rises from a 250 mm beam, continuous with it, to a 1200 mm landing at its top that bears on a 200 mm wall. Nothing else spans through the landing. gk = 1.0 and qk = 3.0 kN/m²; C25/30, cover 25 mm, H10. One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu flight lurus (R = 170, G = 255 mm, 2550 mm pada pelan, waist 150 mm) naik dari beam 250 mm, continuous dengannya, hingga ke landing 1200 mm di atasnya yang bertumpu pada dinding 200 mm. Tiada apa-apa lagi yang merentang melalui landing itu. gk = 1.0 dan qk = 3.0 kN/m²; C25/30, cover 25 mm, H10. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: this landing is carried by this flight alone; nothing else spans through it, so it keeps its full load. F = 14.80 × 2.675 + 10.91 × 1.300 = 53.78 kN, M = 21.38 kN m/m and As = 434 mm²/m: H10-200 fails bending (393 mm²/m) and deflection (29.6 < 33.1); H10-175 passes (449 mm²/m, 33.9 ≥ 33.1). Halve only a landing common to two spans at right angles (Notes §5).Baris 3: landing ini dipikul oleh flight ini sahaja; tiada apa-apa lagi yang merentang melaluinya, jadi ia mengekalkan load penuh. F = 14.80 × 2.675 + 10.91 × 1.300 = 53.78 kN, M = 21.38 kN m/m dan As = 434 mm²/m: H10-200 gagal bending (393 mm²/m) dan deflection (29.6 < 33.1); H10-175 lulus (449 mm²/m, 33.9 ≥ 33.1). Bahagi dua hanya landing yang sepunya bagi dua span bersudut tepat (Nota §5).

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
An open-well stair has two flights 1200 mm wide either side of a 150 mm well. The landing at their heads is 1200 mm deep and 150 mm thick and spans between two 200 mm walls; gk = 1.2 and qk = 4.0 kN/m²; C25/30, cover 25 mm, H10 bars in the flights and the landing. One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu tangga open-well mempunyai dua flight selebar 1200 mm di kiri dan kanan well 150 mm. Landing di kepala flight sedalam 1200 mm dan setebal 150 mm serta merentang di antara dua dinding 200 mm; gk = 1.2 dan qk = 4.0 kN/m²; C25/30, cover 25 mm, bar H10 dalam flight dan landing. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: the landing spans across, between the walls: LL = (2 × 1200 + 150 + 200)/1000 = 2.75 m. ML = 35.16 × 2.75²/8 = 33.24 kN m, 5.25 times as much; As = 762 mm² > 471 mm², and deflection fails (actual L/d 25.0). A 175 mm landing with 8H10 passes (626 ≤ 628 mm², 23.3 ≥ 20.4). Draw the landing on plan before you take its span (Notes §4).Baris 3: landing merentang melintang, di antara dinding: LL = (2 × 1200 + 150 + 200)/1000 = 2.75 m. ML = 35.16 × 2.75²/8 = 33.24 kN m, 5.25 kali ganda; As = 762 mm² > 471 mm², dan deflection gagal (L/d sebenar 25.0). Landing 175 mm dengan 8H10 lulus (626 ≤ 628 mm², 23.3 ≥ 20.4). Lukis landing pada pelan sebelum mengambil span-nya (Nota §4).
