GlossaryGlosari

What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Terms stay in English in both languages; the explanation follows the language you chose.Cari istilah; taip dalam kotak untuk menapis. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

open-well stairTwo flights side by side with a gap, the well, between them; each rests at one end on a landing that spans across between walls.Dua flight sebelah-menyebelah dengan ruang, iaitu well, di antaranya; setiap satu bertumpu di satu hujung pada landing yang merentang melintang di antara dinding.
wellThe open gap between the flights of a stair, seen on plan.Ruang terbuka di antara flight tangga, dilihat pada pelan.
dog-leg stairTwo flights that run in opposite directions with a half landing between them, and no well (or a very narrow one).Dua flight yang berjalan dalam arah bertentangan dengan half landing di antaranya, tanpa well (atau well yang sangat sempit).
half landing · quarter landingA landing where the stair turns through 180° (half) or 90° (quarter). A quarter landing is often shared by two flights at right angles.Landing tempat tangga berpusing 180° (half) atau 90° (quarter). Quarter landing sering dikongsi oleh dua flight bersudut tepat.
landing as a supportIn an open-well stair the landing spans across between walls and carries the flight: a one-way slab of its own, simply supported, K = 1.0.Dalam tangga open-well, landing merentang melintang di antara dinding dan memikul flight: one-way slab tersendiri, disokong mudah, K = 1.0.
effective span on a landingL = La + ½(Lb1 + Lb2), each Lb ≤ 1.8 m (BS 8110-1, 3.10.1.3): the span ends at the centre of the landing, at most 0.9 m into it.L = La + ½(Lb1 + Lb2), setiap Lb ≤ 1.8 m (BS 8110-1, 3.10.1.3): span berakhir di tengah landing, paling banyak 0.9 m ke dalamnya.
LaThe clear horizontal distance between the supports of a flight: its goings on plan.Jarak mengufuk bersih di antara sokongan flight: going-nya pada pelan.
landing strip, lLThe part of a landing that lies inside the flight’s effective span, ½Lb2. It carries the landing load nL; the course notes’ Example 3 leaves it out.Bahagian landing yang berada di dalam effective span flight, ½Lb2. Ia memikul load landing nL; Contoh 3 nota kursus meninggalkannya.
reaction on the landing, VAThe reaction of the flight at its landing end, per metre width of flight: the load the flight puts on the landing.Reaction flight di hujung landing-nya, per meter lebar flight: load yang diletakkan oleh flight pada landing.
wLThe load on the open-well landing per metre of its span: nLbL + VA.Load pada landing open-well per meter span-nya: nLbL + VA.
inner layer · outer layerWhere two sets of bars cross, one lies on the other. The course puts the landing bars in the inner layer, above the flight bars: dL = hL − c − φflight − φL/2.Di tempat dua set bar bersilang, satu berada di atas yang lain. Kursus meletakkan bar landing dalam lapisan dalam, di atas bar flight: dL = hL − c − φflight − φL/2.
shared landingA landing common to two flights at right angles round a well. It lies inside both spans, so each flight carries half of its load (Mosley §8.8.2).Landing yang sepunya bagi dua flight bersudut tepat mengelilingi well. Ia berada di dalam kedua-dua span, jadi setiap flight memikul separuh daripada load-nya (Mosley §8.8.2).
built into wallsA flight cast at least 110 mm into the walls along its sides. BS 8110-1, 3.10.1.2, leaves a 150 mm strip at each wall unloaded and adds 75 mm per wall to the breadth.Flight yang dituang sekurang-kurangnya 110 mm ke dalam dinding di sepanjang sisinya. BS 8110-1, 3.10.1.2, membiarkan jalur 150 mm di setiap dinding tanpa load dan menambah 75 mm bagi setiap dinding kepada lebar.
bld, beff, κwLoaded width bF − 150nw; effective breadth bF + 75nw; κw = bld/beff, the share of the load per m² on a 1 m strip of beff.Lebar yang dibebani bF − 150nw; lebar berkesan bF + 75nw; κw = bld/beff, bahagian load per m² pada jalur 1 m daripada beff.
chaseA groove left in a wall to receive the edge of a slab or a flight.Alur yang ditinggalkan dalam dinding untuk menerima tepi slab atau flight.
stair planThe drawing of a stair seen from above: the treads as parallel lines, an arrow from the bottom of a flight (UP) or to it (DN), a break line where the cut passes, dimension strings and levels.Lukisan tangga dilihat dari atas: tread sebagai garisan selari, anak panah dari bawah flight (UP) atau ke arahnya (DN), garisan putus di tempat potongan melalui, rentetan dimensi dan aras.
UP · DNThe arrow on a stair plan. UP starts at the lower end of the flight; DN, seen from the floor above, ends at it.Anak panah pada pelan tangga. UP bermula di hujung bawah flight; DN, dilihat dari lantai atas, berakhir di situ.
break lineA zigzag line across a flight on plan where the horizontal cut passes; the rest of the flight is drawn on the plan of the floor above.Garisan zigzag merentasi flight pada pelan di tempat potongan mengufuk melalui; baki flight itu dilukis pada pelan lantai di atas.
risers and treads, nR, nGA flight that ends on a landing or a floor has one tread fewer on its slope than it has risers; read the treads from the dimension string and the risers from the heights.Flight yang berakhir pada landing atau lantai mempunyai satu tread kurang pada cerunnya berbanding bilangan riser; baca tread daripada rentetan dimensi dan riser daripada ketinggian.
UBBL By-laws 106 and 108R ≤ 180 mm and G ≥ 255 mm, the same on every step; a landing at least as deep as the stair is wide; at most 16 risers in a flight of a building other than a residence.R ≤ 180 mm dan G ≥ 255 mm, sama pada setiap anak tangga; landing sekurang-kurangnya sedalam lebar tangga; paling banyak 16 riser dalam satu flight bagi bangunan selain kediaman.
ρ0 and Eq. 7.16bρ0 = √fck × 10⁻³. Above it the basic span/depth ratio falls to 11 + 1.5√fckρ0/ρ: a heavily reinforced landing needs depth, not more bars.ρ0 = √fck × 10⁻³. Di atasnya nisbah span/depth asas turun kepada 11 + 1.5√fckρ0/ρ: landing dengan tetulang yang banyak memerlukan kedalaman, bukan bar tambahan.
BS 8110-1, 3.10The British code’s clauses for stairs that the course and the notes use where EC2 is silent: the effective span on landings (3.10.1.3) and flights built into walls (3.10.1.2).Klausa kod British bagi tangga yang digunakan oleh kursus dan nota apabila EC2 tidak menyatakannya: effective span pada landing (3.10.1.3) dan flight yang dibina ke dalam dinding (3.10.1.2).
steps spanning acrossSteps carried across the flight between stringer beams or walls, or cantilevered from one wall: each step is a small beam (Mosley §8.8.1; app cases ST9 and ST10).Anak tangga yang dipikul melintang flight di antara stringer beam atau dinding, atau menganjur dari satu dinding: setiap anak tangga ialah beam kecil (Mosley §8.8.1; kes app ST9 dan ST10).
Professional Habit 2Draw the span, then load all of it: mark both supports on the drawing and check that every metre between them carries its load.Lukis span, kemudian bebankan kesemuanya: tanda kedua-dua sokongan pada lukisan dan semak bahawa setiap meter di antaranya memikul load-nya.

Short notesNota ringkas

§1 Where the load goesKe mana load pergi

  • Open well: flight → landing → walls. Right angles: a common landing inside two spans. Built into walls: the walls take a strip along each side.Open well: flight → landing → dinding. Sudut tepat: landing bersama di dalam dua span. Dibina ke dalam dinding: dinding memikul satu jalur di sepanjang setiap sisi.

§2 Reading the planMembaca pelan

  • Arrow first: UP starts at the bottom, DN ends at it. R = Hf/nR; Lf = nGG; nG = nR − 1 when the landing is the last step.Anak panah dahulu: UP bermula di bawah, DN berakhir di situ. R = Hf/nR; Lf = nGG; nG = nR − 1 apabila landing ialah anak tangga terakhir.
  • R ≤ 180, G ≥ 255 mm; at most 16 risers; 2R + G about 600 mm.R ≤ 180, G ≥ 255 mm; paling banyak 16 riser; 2R + G lebih kurang 600 mm.

§3 A flight on a landingFlight pada landing

§4 The landingLanding

  • K = 1.0. Above ρ0, Eq. 7.16b: deepen the landing rather than add bars.K = 1.0. Di atas ρ0, Eq. 7.16b: dalamkan landing dan bukan menambah bar.

§5 Right anglesSudut tepat

  • A landing common to two spans at right angles: ½nL in each. Not for a landing one flight carries alone, nor for the open-well landing.Landing yang sepunya bagi dua span bersudut tepat: ½nL dalam setiap span. Bukan bagi landing yang dipikul oleh satu flight sahaja, atau landing open-well.
