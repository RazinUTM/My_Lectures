    1. Frames and sub-framesFrame dan sub-frame

    ≈ 7 min

    Units 1 and 2 designed one member at a time: a flight, a landing. In a building the beams and columns along each grid line are cast together and work as a frame: a load on one beam bends its neighbours and the columns as well. Frame analysis finds the design moments and shears of the beams and the moments and axial forces of the columns. Units 3 to 6 do it by hand, with the simplified methods of the course.Unit 1 dan 2 membuat design satu member pada satu masa: flight, landing. Dalam bangunan, beam dan column di sepanjang setiap grid line dituang bersama dan bertindak sebagai frame: load pada satu beam turut melenturkan beam di sebelahnya dan column. Frame analysis mencari design moment dan shear bagi beam serta moment dan axial force bagi column. Unit 3 hingga 6 melakukannya dengan tangan, menggunakan kaedah ringkas kursus.

    A building frame is three-dimensional, but it is analysed as a set of plane frames, one along each grid line. Each plane frame carries the load of the slab panels on either side of its beams (§3) and passes it down its columns to the foundations.Frame bangunan adalah tiga dimensi, tetapi ia dianalisis sebagai satu set plane frame, satu di sepanjang setiap grid line. Setiap plane frame memikul load panel slab di kedua-dua sisi beam-nya (§3) dan memindahkannya melalui column ke asas.

    Whether the frame also resists the wind decides the analysis it needs. In a braced frame, shear walls, a lift core or bracing carry all the horizontal load and keep the building stable; the beams and columns carry vertical load only. In an unbraced frame the beams and columns carry both, and the frame sways under the wind. Units 3 and 4 analyse braced frames under vertical load; Units 5 and 6 add the wind to an unbraced frame.Sama ada frame turut menahan angin menentukan analisis yang diperlukannya. Dalam braced frame, shear wall, teras lif atau bracing memikul semua load mengufuk dan menjaga kestabilan bangunan; beam dan column hanya memikul load menegak. Dalam unbraced frame, beam dan column memikul kedua-duanya, dan frame bergoyang di bawah angin. Unit 3 dan 4 menganalisis braced frame di bawah load menegak; Unit 5 dan 6 menambah angin kepada unbraced frame.

    Even a braced plane frame has many joints. The course follows the simplifications of BS 8110, which the faculty’s Design Rules and Formulae (RDF §11.0) keep for design to Eurocode 2: EC2 allows a structure to be idealised into parts (MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.1 and 5.3) but does not say how to cut a frame. There are three sub-frames, each with the far ends of its members fixed:Braced plane frame pun mempunyai banyak joint. Kursus mengikut kaedah ringkas BS 8110, yang dikekalkan oleh Design Rules and Formulae fakulti (RDF §11.0) bagi design mengikut Eurocode 2: EC2 membenarkan struktur diidealkan kepada bahagian-bahagian (MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.1 dan 5.3) tetapi tidak menyatakan cara memotong frame. Terdapat tiga sub-frame, setiap satu dengan hujung jauh member-nya fixed:

    1. the one-level sub-frame: the beams of one floor with the columns just above and below it;one-level sub-frame: beam satu lantai dengan column tepat di atas dan di bawahnya;
    2. the two-free-joint sub-frame: one beam, the columns at its two ends, and the beams on either side at half their stiffness;two-free-joint sub-frame: satu beam, column di kedua-dua hujungnya, dan beam di kedua-dua sisi dengan separuh stiffness-nya;
    3. the continuous beam and one-free-joint sub-frame: the beams of one floor as a continuous beam on supports that do not restrain its rotation (knife-edge supports), for the beam moments; then, at each column, a sub-frame of that one joint with its columns and the beams either side at half stiffness, for the column moments.continuous beam dan one-free-joint sub-frame: beam satu lantai sebagai continuous beam di atas support yang tidak menghalang putarannya (knife-edge support), bagi moment beam; kemudian, di setiap column, sub-frame bagi satu joint itu dengan column-nya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada separuh stiffness, bagi moment column.

    The third method is the quickest by hand, and the course notes’ Example 4 uses it. It splits the work in two. The continuous beam finds the beam moments as if the columns held the beam up without holding it straight. The one-free-joint sub-frame then finds how much of the beam’s unbalanced load each column must take. The two answers are not exactly those of the whole frame, and §6 shows where they differ.Kaedah ketiga paling cepat dengan tangan, dan Contoh 4 nota kursus menggunakannya. Ia membahagikan kerja kepada dua. Continuous beam mencari moment beam seolah-olah column hanya menyokong beam tanpa menahannya daripada berputar. One-free-joint sub-frame kemudian mencari berapa banyak load beam yang tidak seimbang perlu dipikul oleh setiap column. Kedua-dua jawapan tidak tepat sama dengan jawapan seluruh frame, dan §6 menunjukkan di mana ia berbeza.

    2. Load arrangements: which spans carry the variable loadLoad arrangement: span mana yang memikul load berubah

    ≈ 8 min

    The permanent load is always there; the variable load, people, furniture and movable partitions, can be on any span or on none. A beam must be designed for the worst position of the variable load at each of its sections, and the worst position is not the same for every section.Load kekal sentiasa ada; load berubah, iaitu manusia, perabot dan partition boleh alih, boleh berada pada mana-mana span atau tiada langsung. Beam mesti direka bentuk bagi kedudukan load berubah yang paling teruk pada setiap section-nya, dan kedudukan paling teruk itu tidak sama bagi setiap section.

    Each span is given either the maximum design load or the minimum: the same γG = 1.35 on every span, and the variable load on some spans only.Setiap span diberi sama ada design load maksimum atau minimum: γG = 1.35 yang sama pada setiap span, dan load berubah pada sebahagian span sahaja.

    (3.1)

    Which spans to load follows from how a continuous beam deflects. A load on one span bends that span down and lifts the spans next to it, the next ones down again, and so on. So, for the largest sagging in a span, load that span and every second span from it (alternate spans). For the largest hogging at a support, load the two spans either side of it (adjacent spans), then every second span beyond. For the least moment in a span, leave that span light and load its neighbours: the span may then hog along its whole length.Span mana yang perlu dibebani ditentukan oleh cara continuous beam melendut. Load pada satu span melenturkan span itu ke bawah dan mengangkat span di sebelahnya, span seterusnya ke bawah semula, dan begitulah seterusnya. Jadi, bagi sagging terbesar dalam satu span, bebani span itu dan setiap span kedua daripadanya (span berselang, alternate spans). Bagi hogging terbesar di satu support, bebani dua span di kiri dan kanannya (span bersebelahan, adjacent spans), kemudian setiap span kedua di luarnya. Bagi moment terkecil dalam satu span, biarkan span itu ringan dan bebani span di sebelahnya: span itu mungkin hogging di sepanjang panjangnya.

    MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.3, recommends Load Set 1: (a) alternate spans at wmax with the others at wmin, and (b) any two adjacent spans at wmax with the others at wmin. The Malaysian National Annex, like the UK one, gives Load Set 2: (a) all spans at wmax, and (b) alternate spans at wmax (RDF §11.0). For three spans, Load Set 1 is four cases and Load Set 2 is three.     Method (iii) first takes the beams of one floor as a continuous beam on knife-edge supports: the columns hold the beam up but do not stop it turning.     Stiffness. Each span has K = I/L; only the ratios matter. With I in 10⁹ mm⁴ and L in m, 10I/L comes out in 10⁵ mm³, the unit of the notes. Setiap span mempunyai K = I/L; hanya nisbahnya yang penting. Dengan I dalam 10⁹ mm⁴ dan L dalam m, 10I/L diperoleh dalam 10⁵ mm³, unit dalam nota.     Answer 1Jawapan 1 Every pattern that governsSetiap corak yang menguasai

    All spans loaded is the heaviest total load but not the worst case for any section.     Answer 2Jawapan 2 The pin is counted oncePin dikira sekali

    A span pinned at its far end turns more easily than one fixed there: its stiffness is 3EI/L, ¾ of 4EI/L. Using ¾K builds the pin into the distribution factors, so the pin is released at the start and nothing is carried back to it. Balancing the pin again in every cycle with the full K gives the same answer, only more slowly; doing both, ¾K and carrying moments back to the pin, counts the pin twice and leaves a moment at the pin, which is wrong (Frame S3, line 8).Span yang hujung jauhnya pin lebih mudah berputar berbanding yang fixed di situ: stiffness-nya 3EI/L, ¾ daripada 4EI/L. Menggunakan ¾K memasukkan pin ke dalam distribution factor, jadi pin dilepaskan pada permulaan dan tiada apa yang dibawa kembali kepadanya. Mengimbangi pin semula dalam setiap cycle dengan K penuh memberi jawapan yang sama, cuma lebih perlahan; melakukan kedua-duanya, ¾K dan membawa moment kembali ke pin, mengira pin dua kali dan meninggalkan moment di pin, yang salah (Frame S3, baris 8).

    Answer 3Jawapan 3 The knife edge hides itKnife edge menyembunyikannya

    The knife-edge support is an idealisation for the beam moments. The real joint is monolithic with its columns, and the one-free-joint sub-frame gives them 64.0 + 56.0 = 120.1 kN m in Worked Example 3.1. By equilibrium the beam end carries the same moment. It is about four times 0.15Mspan of EC2 9.2.1.2(1), which is a minimum for partial fixity, not an estimate of this moment.Knife-edge support ialah pengidealan bagi moment beam. Joint sebenar adalah monolitik dengan column-nya, dan one-free-joint sub-frame memberi column itu 64.0 + 56.0 = 120.1 kN m dalam Worked Example 3.1. Melalui keseimbangan, hujung beam memikul moment yang sama. Ia lebih kurang empat kali 0.15Mspan dalam EC2 9.2.1.2(1), yang merupakan had minimum bagi partial fixity, bukan anggaran moment ini.

    Answer 4Jawapan 4 No: the interior joint is nearly balancedTidak: joint dalaman hampir seimbang

    At an interior joint the two beams pull in opposite directions, so only the difference between a loaded and an unloaded span is out of balance (85.3 kN m at B against 172.6 at A in Worked Example 3.1); B also has two beams to share it with. So the interior column gets less. But the one-free-joint sub-frame does underestimate it: it fixes the far ends and ignores the load beyond.     frame · plane frameThe beams and columns along one grid line of a building, analysed in their own plane; each carries the slab panels on either side of its beams.Beam dan column di sepanjang satu grid line bangunan, dianalisis dalam satahnya sendiri; setiap satu memikul panel slab di kedua-dua sisi beam-nya.
    braced frameA frame whose horizontal load is carried by shear walls, a core or bracing: its beams and columns carry vertical load only.Frame yang load mengufuknya dipikul oleh shear wall, teras atau bracing: beam dan column-nya memikul load menegak sahaja.
    unbraced frameA frame whose beams and columns carry the horizontal load as well, and sway under it (Units 5 and 6).Frame yang beam dan column-nya turut memikul load mengufuk, dan bergoyang di bawahnya (Unit 5 dan 6).
    sub-frameA part cut from the frame for hand analysis, with the far ends of its members taken as fixed (RDF §11.0, from BS 8110).Bahagian yang dipotong daripada frame bagi analisis tangan, dengan hujung jauh member-nya diambil sebagai fixed (RDF §11.0, daripada BS 8110).
    one-level sub-frameThe beams of one floor with the columns just above and below (Unit 4).Beam satu lantai dengan column tepat di atas dan di bawahnya (Unit 4).
    two-free-joint sub-frameOne beam, the columns at its ends and the beams either side at half stiffness (Unit 4).Satu beam, column di hujungnya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada separuh stiffness (Unit 4).
    continuous beam (knife-edge supports)The beams of one floor on supports that hold them up but do not restrain their rotation; it gives the beam moments of method (iii).Beam satu lantai di atas support yang menyokongnya tetapi tidak menghalang putarannya; ia memberi moment beam bagi kaedah (iii).
    one-free-joint sub-frameOne joint with its columns and the beams either side at half stiffness, far ends fixed, wmax on one side and wmin on the other; it gives the column moments.Satu joint dengan column-nya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada separuh stiffness, hujung jauh fixed, wmax pada satu sisi dan wmin pada sisi lain; ia memberi moment column.
    load arrangement · pattern loadingWhich spans carry the variable load. Each section has its own worst arrangement.Span mana yang memikul load berubah. Setiap section mempunyai susunan paling teruknya sendiri.
    wmax, wmin1.35Gk + 1.5Qk on a loaded span, 1.35Gk on an unloaded one: the same γG on every span.1.35Gk + 1.5Qk pada span yang dibebani, 1.35Gk pada span yang tidak dibebani: γG yang sama pada setiap span.
    Load Set 1EC2 5.1.3: alternate spans at wmax, and any two adjacent spans at wmax, the others at wmin. The course notes and workbook use it.EC2 5.1.3: span berselang pada wmax, dan mana-mana dua span bersebelahan pada wmax, span lain pada wmin. Nota kursus dan workbook menggunakannya.
    Load Set 2Malaysian (and UK) National Annex: all spans at wmax, and alternate spans at wmax. It can miss part of a support moment (7 % and 21 % at B in the course frames).National Annex Malaysia (dan UK): semua span pada wmax, dan span berselang pada wmax. Ia boleh terlepas sebahagian moment support (7 % dan 21 % di B dalam frame kursus).
    shear coefficient βv (Table 3.15)The share of a two-way panel’s load per metre on each supporting beam: v = βv n lx.Bahagian load panel two-way per meter pada setiap beam penyokong: v = βv n lx.
    βvx · βvyβvx for a beam on a long side of the panel, interpolated in ly/lx; βvy for a beam on a short side, one value for the case.βvx bagi beam pada sisi panjang panel, diinterpolasi mengikut ly/lx; βvy bagi beam pada sisi pendek, satu nilai bagi kes itu.
    lx, lyThe short and the long side of a slab panel. The load per metre on any of its beams uses lx.Sisi pendek dan sisi panjang panel slab. Load per meter pada mana-mana beam-nya menggunakan lx.
    panel caseWhich edges of a panel are continuous: case 1 interior, case 2 one short edge discontinuous, and so on (Table 3.15).Tepi panel mana yang continuous: kes 1 dalaman, kes 2 satu tepi pendek discontinuous, dan seterusnya (Table 3.15).
    stiffness KI/L of a member; only the ratios matter. 10I/L with I in 10⁹ mm⁴ and L in m gives 10⁵ mm³.I/L sesuatu member; hanya nisbahnya yang penting. 10I/L dengan I dalam 10⁹ mm⁴ dan L dalam m memberi 10⁵ mm³.
    ¾KThe stiffness of a span whose far end is pinned (3EI/L against 4EI/L). The pin is then released once and receives no carry-over.Stiffness span yang hujung jauhnya pin (3EI/L berbanding 4EI/L). Pin kemudian dilepaskan sekali dan tidak menerima carry-over.
    distribution factor FK/ΣK of a member at a joint: its share of the out-of-balance moment there. The factors at a joint add up to 1.K/ΣK sesuatu member di satu joint: bahagiannya daripada moment tidak seimbang di situ. Faktor di satu joint berjumlah 1.
    fixed-end moment (FEM)The end moment of a span with both ends locked: wL²/12 for a uniform load; −wL²/12 at the left end and +wL²/12 at the right, clockwise positive.Moment hujung span dengan kedua-dua hujung dikunci: wL²/12 bagi load seragam; −wL²/12 di hujung kiri dan +wL²/12 di hujung kanan, positif mengikut arah jam.
    balance · carry-overBalance: give each member at a free joint −F × ΣM. Carry over: half of each balancing moment goes to the far end of its span.Imbangi: beri setiap member di joint bebas −F × ΣM. Carry over: separuh setiap balancing moment pergi ke hujung jauh span-nya.
    cycle · convergenceOne balance and one carry-over. Hand calculation stops after a set number of cycles; to convergence, the carried moments have become negligible.Satu imbangan dan satu carry-over. Pengiraan tangan berhenti selepas bilangan cycle yang ditetapkan; hingga converge, moment yang dibawa sudah boleh diabaikan.
    envelopeThe largest hogging, largest sagging, least span moment and largest shears of every section over all the load arrangements.Hogging terbesar, sagging terbesar, moment span terkecil dan shear terbesar setiap section merentasi semua load arrangement.
    least span momentThe smallest of the span’s sagging peaks over the arrangements. Negative means the span hogs along its whole length and needs top bars across it.Nilai terkecil puncak sagging span merentasi semua susunan. Negatif bermakna span hogging di sepanjang panjangnya dan memerlukan bar atas merentasinya.
    ΔFEMThe out-of-balance moment at a joint in the column sub-frame: FEM of the wmax side minus FEM of the wmin side; at an end column, the whole FEM at wmax.Moment tidak seimbang di joint dalam sub-frame column: FEM sisi wmax tolak FEM sisi wmin; di column hujung, seluruh FEM pada wmax.
    beam moment at an end supportMc,u + Mc,l by equilibrium of the joint. The continuous beam shows zero there; the top bars must take it.Mc,u + Mc,l melalui keseimbangan joint. Continuous beam menunjukkan sifar di situ; bar atas mesti memikulnya.
    Professional Habit 3Check the table before you use it: ΣM = 0 at every joint, M = 0 at a pin, VL + VR = wL in every span.Semak jadual sebelum menggunakannya: ΣM = 0 di setiap joint, M = 0 di pin, VL + VR = wL dalam setiap span.

    §1 Frames and sub-framesFrame dan sub-frame

    • Braced: walls or a core take the wind; the frame carries vertical load. Unbraced: the frame takes both and sways.Braced: dinding atau teras memikul angin; frame memikul load menegak. Unbraced: frame memikul kedua-duanya dan bergoyang.
    • Sub-frames (RDF §11.0): one-level; two-free-joint; continuous beam + one-free-joint sub-frame at each column. Far ends fixed.Sub-frame (RDF §11.0): one-level; two-free-joint; continuous beam + one-free-joint sub-frame di setiap column. Hujung jauh fixed.

    §2 Load arrangementsLoad arrangement

    • Sagging in a span: alternate spans. Hogging at a support: the two adjacent spans. Least span moment: neighbours loaded, the span light.Sagging dalam span: span berselang. Hogging di support: dua span bersebelahan. Moment span terkecil: jiran dibebani, span ringan.
    • Load Set 1 (EC2): alternate + adjacent. Load Set 2 (Malaysian NA): alternate + all.Load Set 1 (EC2): berselang + bersebelahan. Load Set 2 (NA Malaysia): berselang + semua.

    • Long side: βvx (interpolate in ly/lx). Short side: βvy. lx is always the short side.Sisi panjang: βvx (interpolasi mengikut ly/lx). Sisi pendek: βvy. lx sentiasa sisi pendek.
