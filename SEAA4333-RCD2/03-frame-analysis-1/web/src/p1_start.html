What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Units 1 and 2 designed one member at a time. In a building the beams and columns along each grid line are cast together and work as a frame: load one span and its neighbours and the columns bend too. This Unit starts frame analysis by hand, for a braced frame under vertical load, in the order the course notes take it. It begins with the question that decides every design moment: which spans carry the variable load? The worst position is different for each section, and the Malaysian National Annex allows a simpler set of arrangements than EC2 recommends, which can miss part of a support moment. It then works out the load each beam takes from the slab panels with Table 3.15, analyses the beams of one floor as a continuous beam by moment distribution, and turns the support moments into shears, span moments and an envelope. Last, a small sub-frame at each joint gives the column moments, and with them the hogging that the beam must carry at an end support, which the continuous beam does not show.Unit 1 dan 2 membuat design satu member pada satu masa. Dalam bangunan, beam dan column di sepanjang setiap grid line dituang bersama dan bertindak sebagai frame: bebani satu span, dan span di sebelahnya serta column turut melentur. Unit ini memulakan frame analysis dengan tangan, bagi braced frame di bawah load menegak, mengikut urutan nota kursus. Ia bermula dengan soalan yang menentukan setiap design moment: span manakah yang memikul load berubah? Kedudukan paling teruk berbeza bagi setiap section, dan Malaysian National Annex membenarkan set susunan yang lebih ringkas daripada yang disyorkan EC2, yang boleh terlepas sebahagian moment support. Kemudian ia mengira load yang diterima setiap beam daripada panel slab dengan Table 3.15, menganalisis beam satu lantai sebagai continuous beam melalui moment distribution, dan menukar moment support kepada shear, moment span dan envelope. Akhir sekali, sub-frame kecil di setiap joint memberi moment column, dan bersamanya hogging yang mesti dipikul beam di support hujung, yang tidak ditunjukkan oleh continuous beam.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

A beam fixed at both ends spans 6 m and carries 30 kN/m. What is the moment at each fixed end?Satu beam fixed di kedua-dua hujung merentang 6 m dan memikul 30 kN/m. Berapakah moment di setiap hujung fixed?

wL²/12 = 30 × 6²/12 = 90 kN m, hogging at both ends; the sagging at midspan is wL²/24 = 45 kN m. These fixed-end moments start every moment distribution today (Notes §4).wL²/12 = 30 × 6²/12 = 90 kN m, hogging di kedua-dua hujung; sagging di tengah span ialah wL²/24 = 45 kN m. Fixed-end moment ini memulakan setiap moment distribution hari ini (Nota §4).

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

Two members meet at a joint: beam 1 with I/L = 7.5 and beam 2 with I/L = 5.0, both with their far ends fixed. What share of an out-of-balance moment does beam 1 take?Dua member bertemu di satu joint: beam 1 dengan I/L = 7.5 dan beam 2 dengan I/L = 5.0, kedua-duanya dengan hujung jauh fixed. Berapakah bahagian moment tidak seimbang yang diambil oleh beam 1?

The distribution factor is K/ΣK = 7.5/(7.5 + 5.0) = 0.60: the stiffer member takes more. If beam 1’s far end were a pin, its stiffness would be ¾ × 7.5 and the share 0.53 (Notes §4).Distribution factor ialah K/ΣK = 7.5/(7.5 + 5.0) = 0.60: member yang lebih kaku mengambil lebih. Jika hujung jauh beam 1 ialah pin, stiffness-nya ¾ × 7.5 dan bahagiannya 0.53 (Nota §4).

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A two-way slab panel 4 m × 6 m sits on four beams. Which beams carry more load per metre?Satu panel slab two-way 4 m × 6 m bertumpu pada empat beam. Beam manakah yang memikul lebih load per meter?

A rectangular panel carries more of its load in the short direction, onto the long sides. In Table 3.15 the long-side coefficient βvx grows with ly/lx (0.45 at 1.5 for an interior panel) while the short side keeps βvy = 0.33 (Notes §3).Panel segi empat tepat memikul lebih load-nya dalam arah pendek, ke sisi panjang. Dalam Table 3.15, pekali sisi panjang βvx bertambah dengan ly/lx (0.45 pada 1.5 bagi panel dalaman) manakala sisi pendek mengekalkan βvy = 0.33 (Nota §3).
