1. Frames and sub-framesFrame dan sub-frame

≈ 7 min

Units 1 and 2 designed one member at a time: a flight, a landing. In a building the beams and columns along each grid line are cast together and work as a frame: a load on one beam bends its neighbours and the columns as well. Frame analysis finds the design moments and shears of the beams and the moments and axial forces of the columns. Units 3 to 6 do it by hand, with the simplified methods of the course.Unit 1 dan 2 membuat design satu member pada satu masa: flight, landing. Dalam bangunan, beam dan column di sepanjang setiap grid line dituang bersama dan bertindak sebagai frame: load pada satu beam turut melenturkan beam di sebelahnya dan column. Frame analysis mencari design moment dan shear bagi beam serta moment dan axial force bagi column. Unit 3 hingga 6 melakukannya dengan tangan, menggunakan kaedah ringkas kursus.

A building frame is three-dimensional, but it is analysed as a set of plane frames, one along each grid line. Each plane frame carries the load of the slab panels on either side of its beams (§3) and passes it down its columns to the foundations.Frame bangunan adalah tiga dimensi, tetapi ia dianalisis sebagai satu set plane frame, satu di sepanjang setiap grid line. Setiap plane frame memikul load panel slab di kedua-dua sisi beam-nya (§3) dan memindahkannya melalui column ke asas.

Whether the frame also resists the wind decides the analysis it needs. In a braced frame, shear walls, a lift core or bracing carry all the horizontal load and keep the building stable; the beams and columns carry vertical load only. In an unbraced frame the beams and columns carry both, and the frame sways under the wind. Units 3 and 4 analyse braced frames under vertical load; Units 5 and 6 add the wind to an unbraced frame.Sama ada frame turut menahan angin menentukan analisis yang diperlukannya. Dalam braced frame, shear wall, teras lif atau bracing memikul semua load mengufuk dan menjaga kestabilan bangunan; beam dan column hanya memikul load menegak. Dalam unbraced frame, beam dan column memikul kedua-duanya, dan frame bergoyang di bawah angin. Unit 3 dan 4 menganalisis braced frame di bawah load menegak; Unit 5 dan 6 menambah angin kepada unbraced frame.

Even a braced plane frame has many joints. The course follows the simplifications of BS 8110, which the faculty’s Design Rules and Formulae (RDF §11.0) keep for design to Eurocode 2: EC2 allows a structure to be idealised into parts (MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.1 and 5.3) but does not say how to cut a frame. There are three sub-frames, each with the far ends of its members fixed:Braced plane frame pun mempunyai banyak joint. Kursus mengikut kaedah ringkas BS 8110, yang dikekalkan oleh Design Rules and Formulae fakulti (RDF §11.0) bagi design mengikut Eurocode 2: EC2 membenarkan struktur diidealkan kepada bahagian-bahagian (MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.1 dan 5.3) tetapi tidak menyatakan cara memotong frame. Terdapat tiga sub-frame, setiap satu dengan hujung jauh member-nya fixed:

  1. the one-level sub-frame: the beams of one floor with the columns just above and below it;one-level sub-frame: beam satu lantai dengan column tepat di atas dan di bawahnya;
  2. the two-free-joint sub-frame: one beam, the columns at its two ends, and the beams on either side at half their stiffness;two-free-joint sub-frame: satu beam, column di kedua-dua hujungnya, dan beam di kedua-dua sisi dengan separuh stiffness-nya;
  3. the continuous beam and one-free-joint sub-frame: the beams of one floor as a continuous beam on supports that do not restrain its rotation (knife-edge supports), for the beam moments; then, at each column, a sub-frame of that one joint with its columns and the beams either side at half stiffness, for the column moments.continuous beam dan one-free-joint sub-frame: beam satu lantai sebagai continuous beam di atas support yang tidak menghalang putarannya (knife-edge support), bagi moment beam; kemudian, di setiap column, sub-frame bagi satu joint itu dengan column-nya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada separuh stiffness, bagi moment column.

The third method is the quickest by hand, and the course notes’ Example 4 uses it. It splits the work in two. The continuous beam finds the beam moments as if the columns held the beam up without holding it straight. The one-free-joint sub-frame then finds how much of the beam’s unbalanced load each column must take. The two answers are not exactly those of the whole frame, and §6 shows where they differ.Kaedah ketiga paling cepat dengan tangan, dan Contoh 4 nota kursus menggunakannya. Ia membahagikan kerja kepada dua. Continuous beam mencari moment beam seolah-olah column hanya menyokong beam tanpa menahannya daripada berputar. One-free-joint sub-frame kemudian mencari berapa banyak load beam yang tidak seimbang perlu dipikul oleh setiap column. Kedua-dua jawapan tidak tepat sama dengan jawapan seluruh frame, dan §6 menunjukkan di mana ia berbeza.

2. Load arrangements: which spans carry the variable loadLoad arrangement: span mana yang memikul load berubah

≈ 8 min

The permanent load is always there; the variable load, people, furniture and movable partitions, can be on any span or on none. A beam must be designed for the worst position of the variable load at each of its sections, and the worst position is not the same for every section.Load kekal sentiasa ada; load berubah, iaitu manusia, perabot dan partition boleh alih, boleh berada pada mana-mana span atau tiada langsung. Beam mesti direka bentuk bagi kedudukan load berubah yang paling teruk pada setiap section-nya, dan kedudukan paling teruk itu tidak sama bagi setiap section.

Each span is given either the maximum design load or the minimum: the same γG = 1.35 on every span, and the variable load on some spans only.Setiap span diberi sama ada design load maksimum atau minimum: γG = 1.35 yang sama pada setiap span, dan load berubah pada sebahagian span sahaja.

(3.1)

Which spans to load follows from how a continuous beam deflects. A load on one span bends that span down and lifts the spans next to it, the next ones down again, and so on. So, for the largest sagging in a span, load that span and every second span from it (alternate spans). For the largest hogging at a support, load the two spans either side of it (adjacent spans), then every second span beyond. For the least moment in a span, leave that span light and load its neighbours: the span may then hog along its whole length.Span mana yang perlu dibebani ditentukan oleh cara continuous beam melendut. Load pada satu span melenturkan span itu ke bawah dan mengangkat span di sebelahnya, span seterusnya ke bawah semula, dan begitulah seterusnya. Jadi, bagi sagging terbesar dalam satu span, bebani span itu dan setiap span kedua daripadanya (span berselang, alternate spans). Bagi hogging terbesar di satu support, bebani dua span di kiri dan kanannya (span bersebelahan, adjacent spans), kemudian setiap span kedua di luarnya. Bagi moment terkecil dalam satu span, biarkan span itu ringan dan bebani span di sebelahnya: span itu mungkin hogging di sepanjang panjangnya.

MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.3, recommends Load Set 1: (a) alternate spans at wmax with the others at wmin, and (b) any two adjacent spans at wmax with the others at wmin. The Malaysian National Annex, like the UK one, gives Load Set 2: (a) all spans at wmax, and (b) alternate spans at wmax (RDF §11.0). For three spans, Load Set 1 is four cases and Load Set 2 is three. The course notes and the course workbook use Load Set 1, and so do the examples of this Unit; the RC Design app uses Load Set 2 unless you choose otherwise.MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.3, mengesyorkan Load Set 1: (a) span berselang pada wmax dengan span lain pada wmin, dan (b) mana-mana dua span bersebelahan pada wmax dengan span lain pada wmin. Malaysian National Annex, seperti UK National Annex, memberi Load Set 2: (a) semua span pada wmax, dan (b) span berselang pada wmax (RDF §11.0). Bagi tiga span, Load Set 1 ialah empat kes dan Load Set 2 ialah tiga. Nota kursus dan workbook kursus menggunakan Load Set 1, begitu juga contoh dalam Unit ini; RC Design app menggunakan Load Set 2 melainkan anda memilih yang lain.

4. The continuous beam by moment distributionContinuous beam melalui moment distribution

≈ 9 min

Method (iii) first takes the beams of one floor as a continuous beam on knife-edge supports: the columns hold the beam up but do not stop it turning. Its moments come from moment distribution, the hand method of Theory of Structures. This section recalls the steps in the form the course notes and the course workbook use.Kaedah (iii) mula-mula mengambil beam satu lantai sebagai continuous beam di atas knife-edge support: column menyokong beam tetapi tidak menghalangnya berputar. Moment-nya diperoleh melalui moment distribution, kaedah tangan dalam Theory of Structures. Seksyen ini mengingat semula langkah-langkahnya dalam bentuk yang digunakan oleh nota kursus dan workbook kursus.

Stiffness. Each span has K = I/L; only the ratios matter. With I in 10⁹ mm⁴ and L in m, 10I/L comes out in 10⁵ mm³, the unit of the notes. An end span whose far end is a pin is ¾ as stiff as one whose far end is fixed (3EI/L against 4EI/L): use ¾K for it, release the pin once at the start, and carry nothing back to it afterwards.Stiffness. Setiap span mempunyai K = I/L; hanya nisbahnya yang penting. Dengan I dalam 10⁹ mm⁴ dan L dalam m, 10I/L diperoleh dalam 10⁵ mm³, unit dalam nota. Span hujung yang hujung jauhnya pin mempunyai ¾ stiffness span yang hujung jauhnya fixed (3EI/L berbanding 4EI/L): gunakan ¾K baginya, lepaskan pin sekali pada permulaan, dan jangan bawa apa-apa kembali kepadanya selepas itu.

(3.4)

Distribution factors. At each interior joint, F = K/ΣK for each member meeting there; the factors at a joint add up to 1. At a pinned end the beam is the only member, so F = 1.Distribution factor. Di setiap joint dalaman, F = K/ΣK bagi setiap member yang bertemu di situ; faktor di satu joint berjumlah 1. Di hujung pin, beam ialah satu-satunya member, jadi F = 1.

Fixed-end moments. Lock every joint and load each span on its own: a uniform load w on a span L gives wL²/12 at both ends. In the table the end moments are clockwise positive: −wL²/12 at the left end of a span and +wL²/12 at the right end.Fixed-end moment. Kunci setiap joint dan bebani setiap span secara berasingan: load seragam w pada span L memberi wL²/12 di kedua-dua hujung. Dalam jadual, moment hujung positif mengikut arah jam: −wL²/12 di hujung kiri span dan +wL²/12 di hujung kanan.

(3.5)

Balance and carry over. At each free joint, add the end moments there and give each member −F × (sum): the joint is now in equilibrium. Then carry half of each balancing moment to the far end of its span, except to a pin already released. The carried moments unbalance the joints again, so repeat.
