Answer 1Jawapan 1 Every pattern that governsSetiap corak yang menguasai

All spans loaded is the heaviest total load but not the worst case for any section. In the course notes’ frame it gives 167.9 kN m of sagging in AB against 186.8 with alternate spans, and 201.5 kN m at B against 217.6 with the adjacent spans; it also hides the hogging along a light middle span (−25.0 kN m in BC). Each section has its own worst arrangement, which is why EC2 5.1.3 asks for alternate and adjacent patterns.Semua span dibebani ialah jumlah load paling berat tetapi bukan kes paling teruk bagi mana-mana keratan. Dalam frame nota kursus, ia memberi sagging 167.9 kN m dalam AB berbanding 186.8 dengan span berselang, dan 201.5 kN m di B berbanding 217.6 dengan span bersebelahan; ia juga menyembunyikan hogging di sepanjang span tengah yang ringan (−25.0 kN m dalam BC). Setiap keratan mempunyai susunan paling teruknya sendiri, sebab itulah EC2 5.1.3 meminta corak berselang dan bersebelahan.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 The pin is counted oncePin dikira sekali

A span pinned at its far end turns more easily than one fixed there: its stiffness is 3EI/L, ¾ of 4EI/L. Using ¾K builds the pin into the distribution factors, so the pin is released at the start and nothing is carried back to it. Balancing the pin again in every cycle with the full K gives the same answer, only more slowly; doing both, ¾K and carrying moments back to the pin, counts the pin twice and leaves a moment at the pin, which is wrong (Frame S3, line 8).Span yang hujung jauhnya pin lebih mudah berputar berbanding yang fixed di situ: stiffness-nya 3EI/L, ¾ daripada 4EI/L. Menggunakan ¾K memasukkan pin ke dalam distribution factor, jadi pin dilepaskan pada permulaan dan tiada apa yang dibawa kembali kepadanya. Mengimbangi pin semula dalam setiap cycle dengan K penuh memberi jawapan yang sama, cuma lebih perlahan; melakukan kedua-duanya, ¾K dan membawa moment kembali ke pin, mengira pin dua kali dan meninggalkan moment di pin, yang salah (Frame S3, baris 8).

Answer 3Jawapan 3 The knife edge hides itKnife edge menyembunyikannya

The knife-edge support is an idealisation for the beam moments. The real joint is monolithic with its columns, and the one-free-joint sub-frame gives them 64.0 + 56.0 = 120.1 kN m in Worked Example 3.1. By equilibrium the beam end carries the same moment. It is about four times 0.15Mspan of EC2 9.2.1.2(1), which is a minimum for partial fixity, not an estimate of this moment.Knife-edge support ialah pengidealan bagi moment beam. Joint sebenar adalah monolitik dengan column-nya, dan one-free-joint sub-frame memberi column itu 64.0 + 56.0 = 120.1 kN m dalam Worked Example 3.1. Melalui keseimbangan, hujung beam memikul moment yang sama. Ia lebih kurang empat kali 0.15Mspan dalam EC2 9.2.1.2(1), yang merupakan had minimum bagi partial fixity, bukan anggaran moment ini.

Answer 4Jawapan 4 No: the interior joint is nearly balancedTidak: joint dalaman hampir seimbang

At an interior joint the two beams pull in opposite directions, so only the difference between a loaded and an unloaded span is out of balance (85.3 kN m at B against 172.6 at A in Worked Example 3.1); B also has two beams to share it with. So the interior column gets less. But the one-free-joint sub-frame does underestimate it: it fixes the far ends and ignores the load beyond. The one-level sub-frame of Unit 4 gives B 30.3 kN m instead of 24.3, which is why the RC Design app designs for the larger of the two.Di joint dalaman, dua beam menarik dalam arah bertentangan, jadi hanya perbezaan antara span yang dibebani dan yang tidak dibebani yang tidak seimbang (85.3 kN m di B berbanding 172.6 di A dalam Worked Example 3.1); B juga mempunyai dua beam untuk berkongsi. Jadi column dalaman mendapat kurang. Tetapi one-free-joint sub-frame memang memberi anggaran yang lebih rendah: ia fixed-kan hujung jauh dan mengabaikan load di luarnya. One-level sub-frame dalam Unit 4 memberi B 30.3 kN m dan bukan 24.3, sebab itulah RC Design app membuat design bagi yang lebih besar antara keduanya.
