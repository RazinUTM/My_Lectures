A1 · 1 markmarkah
Which statement describes a braced frame?Pernyataan manakah yang menerangkan braced frame?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Bracing is whatever takes the horizontal load and keeps the building stable: shear walls, a lift core or braces. The frame itself is then analysed for vertical load only, by the sub-frames of Units 3 and 4 (Notes §1).Bracing ialah apa sahaja yang memikul load mengufuk dan menjaga kestabilan bangunan: shear wall, teras lif atau brace. Frame itu sendiri kemudian dianalisis bagi load menegak sahaja, melalui sub-frame Unit 3 dan 4 (Nota §1).

A2 · 1 markmarkah
A beam has four spans AB, BC, CD and DE. For the largest sagging moment in CD, which spans carry wmax?Satu beam mempunyai empat span AB, BC, CD dan DE. Bagi sagging moment terbesar dalam CD, span manakah yang memikul wmax?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Load the span and every second span from it: AB and CD, with BC and DE light. A load on BC or DE would lift CD and reduce its sagging (Notes §2).Bebani span itu dan setiap span kedua daripadanya: AB dan CD, dengan BC dan DE ringan. Load pada BC atau DE akan mengangkat CD dan mengurangkan sagging-nya (Nota §2).

A5 · 1 markmarkah
In the continuous beam, the end span AB is pinned at A. With ¾K for AB, what happens at A after the first row?Dalam continuous beam, span hujung AB adalah pin di A. Dengan ¾K bagi AB, apakah yang berlaku di A selepas baris pertama?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

¾K (3EI/L) already allows for the pin, so A is released once and left alone; its final moment is zero. Carrying moments back to A as well would count the pin twice (Notes §4; Frame S3).¾K (3EI/L) sudah mengambil kira pin, jadi A dilepaskan sekali dan dibiarkan; moment akhirnya sifar. Membawa moment kembali ke A juga bermakna pin dikira dua kali (Nota §4; Frame S3).

A6 · 1 markmarkah
By the one-free-joint sub-frame, the column moments at an end column A are 60 kN m above and 50 kN m below. What hogging moment must the beam carry at A?Melalui one-free-joint sub-frame, moment column di column hujung A ialah 60 kN m di atas dan 50 kN m di bawah. Berapakah hogging moment yang mesti dipikul beam di A?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Only one beam meets the columns at A, so by equilibrium of the joint it carries 60 + 50 = 110 kN m. The knife-edge support of the continuous beam hid it; 15 % of the span moment is the EC2 minimum for partial fixity, not this moment (Notes §6).Hanya satu beam bertemu column di A, jadi melalui keseimbangan joint ia memikul 60 + 50 = 110 kN m. Knife-edge support continuous beam menyembunyikannya; 15 % daripada moment span ialah had minimum EC2 bagi partial fixity, bukan moment ini (Nota §6).

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
An interior frame beam spans 7.0 m in an interior bay (case 1); the panels either side are 4.5 m and 5.0 m wide. Slab 175 mm, finishes 1.5 kN/m², imposed 3.0 and partitions 1.0 kN/m², beam 300 × 600. Which line is wrong?Satu beam frame dalaman merentang 7.0 m dalam bay dalaman (kes 1); panel di kedua-dua sisinya selebar 4.5 m dan 5.0 m. Slab 175 mm, kemasan 1.5 kN/m², imposed 3.0 dan partition 1.0 kN/m², beam 300 × 600. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: lx is the short side of the panel, 4.5 m, not the span: vg = 0.457 × 5.88 × 4.5 = 12.08 and vq = 8.22 kN/m. Then Gk = 27.90, Qk = 16.82 and wmax = 62.90 kN/m: the sheet overloads the beam by 25 %, on the safe side but wasteful. Line 4 is right because there the short side is 5.0 m.Baris 3: lx ialah sisi pendek panel, 4.5 m, bukan span: vg = 0.457 × 5.88 × 4.5 = 12.08 dan vq = 8.22 kN/m. Maka Gk = 27.90, Qk = 16.82 dan wmax = 62.90 kN/m: helaian membebani beam 25 % lebih, di pihak yang selamat tetapi membazir. Baris 4 betul kerana di situ sisi pendeknya 5.0 m.

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
At the interior column B, AB spans 6.0 m (wmax = 55.0, wmin = 30.0 kN/m) and BC 7.0 m (wmax = 58.0, wmin = 32.0 kN/m); beams 300 × 600; column 300 × 450, 3.5 m above the floor and 4.0 m below. Which line is wrong?Di column dalaman B, AB merentang 6.0 m (wmax = 55.0, wmin = 30.0 kN/m) dan BC 7.0 m (wmax = 58.0, wmin = 32.0 kN/m); beam 300 × 600; column 300 × 450, 3.5 m di atas lantai dan 4.0 m di bawah. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2: the column moment is largest with wmax on one side and wmin on the other. ΔFEM = max(236.83 − 30.0 × 36/12, 165.00 − 32.0 × 49/12) = max(146.83, 34.33) = 146.83 kN m, with BC loaded and AB light. Then Mc,u = 46.49 and Mc,l = 40.68 kN m, twice the sheet’s.Baris 2: moment column terbesar dengan wmax pada satu sisi dan wmin pada sisi lain. ΔFEM = max(236.83 − 30.0 × 36/12, 165.00 − 32.0 × 49/12) = max(146.83, 34.33) = 146.83 kN m, dengan BC dibebani dan AB ringan. Maka Mc,u = 46.49 dan Mc,l = 40.68 kN m, dua kali ganda nilai dalam helaian.
