GlossaryGlosari

What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Terms stay in English in both languages; the explanation follows the language you chose.Cari istilah; taip dalam kotak untuk menapis. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

frame · plane frameThe beams and columns along one grid line of a building, analysed in their own plane; each carries the slab panels on either side of its beams.Beam dan column di sepanjang satu grid line bangunan, dianalisis dalam satahnya sendiri; setiap satu memikul panel slab di kedua-dua sisi beam-nya.
braced frameA frame whose horizontal load is carried by shear walls, a core or bracing: its beams and columns carry vertical load only.Frame yang load mengufuknya dipikul oleh shear wall, teras atau bracing: beam dan column-nya memikul load menegak sahaja.
unbraced frameA frame whose beams and columns carry the horizontal load as well, and sway under it (Units 5 and 6).Frame yang beam dan column-nya turut memikul load mengufuk, dan bergoyang di bawahnya (Unit 5 dan 6).
sub-frameA part cut from the frame for hand analysis, with the far ends of its members taken as fixed (RDF §11.0, from BS 8110).Bahagian yang dipotong daripada frame bagi analisis tangan, dengan hujung jauh anggotanya diambil sebagai fixed (RDF §11.0, daripada BS 8110).
one-level sub-frameThe beams of one floor with the columns just above and below (Unit 4).Beam satu lantai dengan column tepat di atas dan di bawahnya (Unit 4).
two-free-joint sub-frameOne beam, the columns at its ends and the beams either side at half stiffness (Unit 4).Satu beam, column di hujungnya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada separuh stiffness (Unit 4).
continuous beam (knife-edge supports)The beams of one floor on supports that hold them up but do not restrain their rotation; it gives the beam moments of method (iii).Beam satu lantai di atas support yang menyokongnya tetapi tidak menghalang putarannya; ia memberi moment beam bagi kaedah (iii).
one-free-joint sub-frameOne joint with its columns and the beams either side at half stiffness, far ends fixed, wmax on one side and wmin on the other; it gives the column moments.Satu joint dengan column-nya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada separuh stiffness, hujung jauh fixed, wmax pada satu sisi dan wmin pada sisi lain; ia memberi moment column.
load arrangement · pattern loadingWhich spans carry the variable load. Each section has its own worst arrangement.Span mana yang memikul load berubah. Setiap keratan mempunyai susunan paling teruknya sendiri.
wmax, wmin1.35Gk + 1.5Qk on a loaded span, 1.35Gk on an unloaded one: the same γG on every span.1.35Gk + 1.5Qk pada span yang dibebani, 1.35Gk pada span yang tidak dibebani: γG yang sama pada setiap span.
Load Set 1EC2 5.1.3: alternate spans at wmax, and any two adjacent spans at wmax, the others at wmin. The course notes and workbook use it.EC2 5.1.3: span berselang pada wmax, dan mana-mana dua span bersebelahan pada wmax, span lain pada wmin. Nota kursus dan workbook menggunakannya.
Load Set 2Malaysian (and UK) National Annex: all spans at wmax, and alternate spans at wmax. It can miss part of a support moment (7 % and 21 % at B in the course frames).National Annex Malaysia (dan UK): semua span pada wmax, dan span berselang pada wmax. Ia boleh terlepas sebahagian moment support (7 % dan 21 % di B dalam frame kursus).
shear coefficient βv (Table 3.15)The share of a two-way panel’s load per metre on each supporting beam: v = βv n lx.Bahagian load panel two-way per meter pada setiap beam penyokong: v = βv n lx.
βvx · βvyβvx for a beam on a long side of the panel, interpolated in ly/lx; βvy for a beam on a short side, one value for the case.βvx bagi beam pada sisi panjang panel, diinterpolasi mengikut ly/lx; βvy bagi beam pada sisi pendek, satu nilai bagi kes itu.
lx, lyThe short and the long side of a slab panel. The load per metre on any of its beams uses lx.Sisi pendek dan sisi panjang panel slab. Load per meter pada mana-mana beam-nya menggunakan lx.
panel caseWhich edges of a panel are continuous: case 1 interior, case 2 one short edge discontinuous, and so on (Table 3.15).Tepi panel mana yang selanjar: kes 1 dalaman, kes 2 satu tepi pendek tak selanjar, dan seterusnya (Table 3.15).
stiffness KI/L of a member; only the ratios matter. 10I/L with I in 10⁹ mm⁴ and L in m gives 10⁵ mm³.I/L sesuatu anggota; hanya nisbahnya yang penting. 10I/L dengan I dalam 10⁹ mm⁴ dan L dalam m memberi 10⁵ mm³.
¾KThe stiffness of a span whose far end is pinned (3EI/L against 4EI/L). The pin is then released once and receives no carry-over.Stiffness span yang hujung jauhnya pin (3EI/L berbanding 4EI/L). Pin kemudian dilepaskan sekali dan tidak menerima carry-over.
distribution factor FK/ΣK of a member at a joint: its share of the out-of-balance moment there. The factors at a joint add up to 1.K/ΣK sesuatu anggota di satu joint: bahagiannya daripada moment tidak seimbang di situ. Faktor di satu joint berjumlah 1.
fixed-end moment (FEM)The end moment of a span with both ends locked: wL²/12 for a uniform load; −wL²/12 at the left end and +wL²/12 at the right, clockwise positive.Moment hujung span dengan kedua-dua hujung dikunci: wL²/12 bagi load seragam; −wL²/12 di hujung kiri dan +wL²/12 di hujung kanan, positif mengikut arah jam.
balance · carry-overBalance: give each member at a free joint −F × ΣM. Carry over: half of each balancing moment goes to the far end of its span.Imbangi: beri setiap anggota di joint bebas −F × ΣM. Carry over: separuh setiap balancing moment pergi ke hujung jauh span-nya.
cycle · convergenceOne balance and one carry-over. Hand calculation stops after a set number of cycles; to convergence, the carried moments have become negligible.Satu imbangan dan satu carry-over. Pengiraan tangan berhenti selepas bilangan cycle yang ditetapkan; hingga converge, moment yang dibawa sudah boleh diabaikan.
envelopeThe largest hogging, largest sagging, least span moment and largest shears of every section over all the load arrangements.Hogging terbesar, sagging terbesar, moment span terkecil dan shear terbesar setiap keratan merentasi semua load arrangement.
least span momentThe smallest of the span’s sagging peaks over the arrangements. Negative means the span hogs along its whole length and needs top bars across it.Nilai terkecil puncak sagging span merentasi semua susunan. Negatif bermakna span hogging di sepanjang panjangnya dan memerlukan bar atas merentasinya.
ΔFEMThe out-of-balance moment at a joint in the column sub-frame: FEM of the wmax side minus FEM of the wmin side; at an end column, the whole FEM at wmax.Moment tidak seimbang di joint dalam sub-frame column: FEM sisi wmax tolak FEM sisi wmin; di column hujung, seluruh FEM pada wmax.
beam moment at an end supportMc,u + Mc,l by equilibrium of the joint. The continuous beam shows zero there; the top bars must take it.Mc,u + Mc,l melalui keseimbangan joint. Continuous beam menunjukkan sifar di situ; bar atas mesti memikulnya.
Professional Habit 3Check the table before you use it: ΣM = 0 at every joint, M = 0 at a pin, VL + VR = wL in every span.Semak jadual sebelum menggunakannya: ΣM = 0 di setiap joint, M = 0 di pin, VL + VR = wL dalam setiap span.

Short notesNota ringkas

§1 Frames and sub-framesFrame dan sub-frame

  • Braced: walls or a core take the wind; the frame carries vertical load. Unbraced: the frame takes both and sways.Braced: dinding atau teras memikul angin; frame memikul load menegak. Unbraced: frame memikul kedua-duanya dan bergoyang.
  • Sub-frames (RDF §11.0): one-level; two-free-joint; continuous beam + one-free-joint sub-frame at each column. Far ends fixed.Sub-frame (RDF §11.0): one-level; two-free-joint; continuous beam + one-free-joint sub-frame di setiap column. Hujung jauh fixed.

§2 Load arrangementsLoad arrangement

  • Sagging in a span: alternate spans. Hogging at a support: the two adjacent spans. Least span moment: neighbours loaded, the span light.Sagging dalam span: span berselang. Hogging di support: dua span bersebelahan. Moment span terkecil: jiran dibebani, span ringan.
  • Load Set 1 (EC2): alternate + adjacent. Load Set 2 (Malaysian NA): alternate + all.Load Set 1 (EC2): berselang + bersebelahan. Load Set 2 (NA Malaysia): berselang + semua.

§3 Loads from the slabsLoad daripada slab

  • Long side: βvx (interpolate in ly/lx). Short side: βvy. lx is always the short side.Sisi panjang: βvx (interpolasi mengikut ly/lx). Sisi pendek: βvy. lx sentiasa sisi pendek.

§5 Shears and the envelopeShear dan envelope

  • Check: ΣM = 0 at each joint, M = 0 at a pin, VL + VR = wL.Semak: ΣM = 0 di setiap joint, M = 0 di pin, VL + VR = wL.
