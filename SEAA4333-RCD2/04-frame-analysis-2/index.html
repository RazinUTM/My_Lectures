    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 3 cut the frame into a continuous beam on knife edges and a small sub-frame at each column. That is quick, but the knife edges show no moment at an end support and let the end spans turn freely. This Unit keeps more of the frame together, with the two other sub-frames of the course. The one-level sub-frame takes the beams of a floor with the columns above and below, their far ends fixed; one moment distribution, with the columns balanced in every joint, gives the beam moments and the column moments together. The two-free-joint sub-frame takes one beam at a time, with the columns at its two ends and its neighbours at half stiffness; it needs fewer balancing operations and fewer load cases for each beam. The Unit then sets the three methods side by side on the same frame, finds where they agree and where they differ by half, and decides which values go into the design of the beams and the columns.Unit 3 memotong frame kepada continuous beam di atas knife edge dan sub-frame kecil di setiap column. Kaedah itu cepat, tetapi knife edge tidak menunjukkan moment di support hujung dan membenarkan span hujung berputar bebas. Unit ini mengekalkan lebih banyak bahagian frame bersama, dengan dua lagi sub-frame kursus. One-level sub-frame mengambil beam satu lantai bersama column di atas dan di bawahnya, dengan hujung jauhnya fixed; satu moment distribution, dengan column diimbangi di setiap joint, memberi moment beam dan moment column serentak. Two-free-joint sub-frame mengambil satu beam pada satu masa, bersama column di kedua-dua hujungnya dan beam di sebelahnya pada separuh stiffness; ia memerlukan kurang operasi imbangan dan kurang kes load bagi setiap beam. Unit ini kemudian meletakkan tiga kaedah bersebelahan pada frame yang sama, mencari di mana ia bersetuju dan di mana ia berbeza sehingga separuh, dan menentukan nilai yang digunakan dalam design beam dan column.

    1. Why cut the frame, and what each cut keepsMengapa frame dipotong, dan apa yang dikekalkan setiap potongan

    ≈ 7 min

    Unit 3 analysed the floor in two pieces: a continuous beam on knife edges for the beams, and a small sub-frame at each joint for the columns. Both pieces are cuts of the same frame. This Unit uses the two other sub-frames of RDF §11.0, which keep more of the frame in one analysis, and asks what each cut gains and what it loses.Unit 3 menganalisis lantai dalam dua bahagian: continuous beam di atas knife edge bagi beam, dan sub-frame kecil di setiap joint bagi column. Kedua-dua bahagian ialah potongan daripada frame yang sama. Unit ini menggunakan dua lagi sub-frame dalam RDF §11.0, yang mengekalkan lebih banyak bahagian frame dalam satu analisis, dan bertanya apa yang diperoleh dan apa yang hilang bagi setiap potongan.

    The frame of the course notes has seven storeys and three bays on each grid line: 28 joints that can turn. By hand that is too many unknowns, so the frame is cut into sub-frames small enough for moment distribution. Every cut replaces the rest of the frame by an assumption at the cut: a fixed end, a pin, or a member at reduced stiffness. RDF §11.0 (from BS 8110 3.2.1.2) gives three sub-frames for a braced frame under vertical load:Frame dalam nota kursus mempunyai tujuh tingkat dan tiga bay pada setiap grid line: 28 joint yang boleh berputar. Dengan tangan, itu terlalu banyak anu, jadi frame dipotong kepada sub-frame yang cukup kecil untuk moment distribution. Setiap potongan menggantikan bahagian frame yang lain dengan satu andaian di tempat potongan: hujung fixed, pin, atau member dengan stiffness dikurangkan.     Why fix the far ends of the columns? The floors above and below are loaded too, so the far ends of the columns turn much less than the joints of the floor being analysed. RDF §11.0 takes them as fixed, unless a pin is clearly more reasonable, for example at a foundation that cannot resist moment.Mengapa hujung jauh column dianggap fixed? Lantai di atas dan di bawah turut dibebani, jadi hujung jauh column berputar jauh kurang daripada joint lantai yang dianalisis. RDF §11.0 mengambilnya sebagai fixed, kecuali jika pin jelas lebih munasabah, contohnya di asas yang tidak dapat menahan moment.

    Why half the stiffness of the beams beyond? A beam fixed at its far end is stiffer than the same beam in the frame, where the far joint can turn. Taking ½K for it allows for that flexibility, for the same reason as the ½K beams of the one-free-joint sub-frame in Unit 3.Mengapa separuh stiffness bagi beam di luar? Beam yang fixed di hujung jauhnya lebih kaku daripada beam yang sama dalam frame, di mana joint jauhnya boleh berputar. Mengambil ½K baginya mengambil kira flexibility itu, atas sebab yang sama seperti beam ½K dalam one-free-joint sub-frame Unit 3.

    2. The one-level sub-frameOne-level sub-frame

    ≈ 9 min

    The members. The beams of the floor, each at its full stiffness K = I/L; the columns just above and just below the floor, each with Kc = Ic/h for its own storey height, their far ends fixed. Every joint of the floor is free to turn, the end joints included. The ¾K of the continuous beam does not apply: A and D are no longer pins but joints held by their columns.Member. Beam lantai itu, setiap satu pada stiffness penuh K = I/L; column tepat di atas dan tepat di bawah lantai, setiap satu dengan Kc = Ic/h bagi ketinggian tingkatnya sendiri, dengan hujung jauh fixed. Setiap joint lantai bebas berputar, termasuk joint hujung. Faktor ¾K continuous beam tidak digunakan: A dan D bukan lagi pin tetapi joint yang ditahan oleh column-nya.

    Balance and carry over. At each joint add the beam end moments there (the out-of-balance moment U) and give every member −F × U: the beam ends get their share, and so do the columns. Then carry half of each beam’s balancing moment to the far end of that beam, at A and D too, since they are free joints now. Nothing is carried from a column back into the floor: the half that reaches its fixed far end stays there.Imbangi dan carry over. Di setiap joint, jumlahkan moment hujung beam di situ (moment tidak seimbang U) dan beri setiap member −F × U: hujung beam mendapat bahagiannya, begitu juga column. Kemudian bawa separuh balancing moment setiap beam ke hujung jauh beam itu, termasuk ke A dan D, kerana kedua-duanya kini joint bebas. Tiada apa-apa dibawa dari column kembali ke lantai: separuh yang sampai di hujung jauhnya yang fixed kekal di situ.

    Short notesNota ringkas

    §1 Why cut the frameMengapa frame dipotong

    • One-level: the floor and its columns, full K. Two-free-joint: one beam, its columns, neighbours at ½K. Continuous beam + one-free-joint: Unit 3.One-level: lantai dan column-nya, K penuh. Two-free-joint: satu beam, column-nya, jiran pada ½K. Continuous beam + one-free-joint: Unit 3.
    • Far ends fixed unless a pin is clearly more reasonable.Hujung jauh fixed kecuali jika pin jelas lebih munasabah.

    §2 The one-level sub-frameOne-level sub-frame

    • Every joint, A and D included, is balanced in every row; carry half along the beams only.Setiap joint, termasuk A dan D, diimbangi dalam setiap baris; bawa separuh di sepanjang beam sahaja.

    §3 A worked case and the envelopeKes contoh dan envelope

    • Check ΣM = 0 at every joint, beams and columns together.Semak ΣM = 0 di setiap joint, beam dan column bersama.

    §4 The two-free-joint sub-frameTwo-free-joint sub-frame

    • Only the beam’s two joints are balanced; nothing is carried to the fixed ends. Keep only the beam’s own results; a column takes the larger of its two sub-frames.Hanya dua joint beam diimbangi; tiada apa-apa dibawa ke hujung fixed. Kekalkan hasil beam itu sahaja; column mengambil yang lebih besar daripada dua sub-frame-nya.

    §5 How much do we lose?Berapa banyak yang hilang?

    • Continuous beam: 0 at an end support, sagging in the end span about 50 % high, one moment at an interior support.Continuous beam: 0 di support hujung, sagging dalam span hujung lebih kurang 50 % tinggi, satu moment di support dalaman.
    • One-free-joint columns: high at an end column, low at an interior one.Column one-free-joint: tinggi di column hujung, rendah di column dalaman.

    §6 Design valuesNilai design

    • Use one model throughout. End support: the sub-frame’s envelope value, or Mc,u + Mc,l with the continuous beam; at least 0.15Mspan.Gunakan satu model sepanjang masa. Support hujung: nilai envelope sub-frame, atau Mc,u + Mc,l dengan continuous beam; sekurang-kurangnya 0.15Mspan.
    • Columns: the larger of the one-free-joint and one-level values.Column: yang lebih besar antara nilai one-free-joint dan one-level.
