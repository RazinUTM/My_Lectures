What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Unit 3 cut the frame into a continuous beam on knife edges and a small sub-frame at each column. That is quick, but the knife edges show no moment at an end support and let the end spans turn freely. This Unit keeps more of the frame together, with the two other sub-frames of the course. The one-level sub-frame takes the beams of a floor with the columns above and below, their far ends fixed; one moment distribution, with the columns balanced in every joint, gives the beam moments and the column moments together. The two-free-joint sub-frame takes one beam at a time, with the columns at its two ends and its neighbours at half stiffness; it needs fewer balancing operations and fewer load cases for each beam. The Unit then sets the three methods side by side on the same frame, finds where they agree and where they differ by half, and decides which values go into the design of the beams and the columns.Unit 3 memotong frame kepada continuous beam di atas knife edge dan sub-frame kecil di setiap column. Kaedah itu cepat, tetapi knife edge tidak menunjukkan moment di support hujung dan membenarkan span hujung berputar bebas. Unit ini mengekalkan lebih banyak bahagian frame bersama, dengan dua lagi sub-frame kursus. One-level sub-frame mengambil beam satu lantai bersama column di atas dan di bawahnya, dengan hujung jauhnya fixed; satu moment distribution, dengan column diimbangi di setiap joint, memberi moment beam dan moment column serentak. Two-free-joint sub-frame mengambil satu beam pada satu masa, bersama column di kedua-dua hujungnya dan beam di sebelahnya pada separuh stiffness; ia memerlukan kurang operasi imbangan dan kurang kes load bagi setiap beam. Unit ini kemudian meletakkan tiga kaedah bersebelahan pada frame yang sama, mencari di mana ia bersetuju dan di mana ia berbeza sehingga separuh, dan menentukan nilai yang digunakan dalam design beam dan column.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

In the continuous beam of Unit 3, the end span AB (pinned at A) and the interior span BC both have I/L = 7.5. What is FBA at B?Dalam continuous beam Unit 3, span hujung AB (pin di A) dan span dalaman BC kedua-duanya mempunyai I/L = 7.5. Berapakah FBA di B?

The pinned far end makes AB ¾ as stiff: K′ = 5.63, so FBA = 5.63/(5.63 + 7.5) = 0.43. Today the end joints keep their columns and are no longer pins, so the ¾ goes away (Notes §2).Hujung jauh pin menjadikan AB ¾ kaku: K′ = 5.63, jadi FBA = 5.63/(5.63 + 7.5) = 0.43. Hari ini joint hujung mengekalkan column-nya dan bukan lagi pin, jadi faktor ¾ tidak digunakan (Nota §2).

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

A balancing moment of −20 kN m is put on the near end of a member whose far end is fixed. What moment arrives at the far end?Satu balancing moment −20 kN m dikenakan pada hujung dekat satu member yang hujung jauhnya fixed. Berapakah moment yang sampai di hujung jauh?

Half of it, with the same sign: −10 kN m (the carry-over factor ½). Today this tells you the moment at the far end of a column: half of the column’s moment at the floor (Notes §2).Separuh daripadanya, dengan tanda yang sama: −10 kN m (carry-over factor ½). Hari ini ia memberi moment di hujung jauh column: separuh daripada moment column di lantai (Nota §2).

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A 6 m span carries 50 kN/m, with hogging moments of 100 kN m at its left end and 150 kN m at its right end. What is its largest sagging moment?Satu span 6 m memikul 50 kN/m, dengan hogging moment 100 kN m di hujung kiri dan 150 kN m di hujung kanan. Berapakah sagging moment terbesarnya?

VL = 50 × 3 + (100 − 150)/6 = 141.67 kN; M = VL²/2w − ML = 141.67²/100 − 100 = 100.7 kN m. 200.7 forgets ML; in the sub-frames of today both ends hog, so ML is never zero (Notes §3).VL = 50 × 3 + (100 − 150)/6 = 141.67 kN; M = VL²/2w − ML = 141.67²/100 − 100 = 100.7 kN m. 200.7 terlupa ML; dalam sub-frame hari ini kedua-dua hujung hogging, jadi ML tidak pernah sifar (Nota §3).
