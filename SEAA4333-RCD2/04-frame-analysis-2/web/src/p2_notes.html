1. Why cut the frame, and what each cut keepsMengapa frame dipotong, dan apa yang dikekalkan setiap potongan

≈ 7 min

Unit 3 analysed the floor in two pieces: a continuous beam on knife edges for the beams, and a small sub-frame at each joint for the columns. Both pieces are cuts of the same frame. This Unit uses the two other sub-frames of RDF §11.0, which keep more of the frame in one analysis, and asks what each cut gains and what it loses.Unit 3 menganalisis lantai dalam dua bahagian: continuous beam di atas knife edge bagi beam, dan sub-frame kecil di setiap joint bagi column. Kedua-dua bahagian ialah potongan daripada frame yang sama. Unit ini menggunakan dua lagi sub-frame dalam RDF §11.0, yang mengekalkan lebih banyak bahagian frame dalam satu analisis, dan bertanya apa yang diperoleh dan apa yang hilang bagi setiap potongan.

The frame of the course notes has seven storeys and three bays on each grid line: 28 joints that can turn. By hand that is too many unknowns, so the frame is cut into sub-frames small enough for moment distribution. Every cut replaces the rest of the frame by an assumption at the cut: a fixed end, a pin, or a member at reduced stiffness. RDF §11.0 (from BS 8110 3.2.1.2) gives three sub-frames for a braced frame under vertical load:Frame dalam nota kursus mempunyai tujuh tingkat dan tiga bay pada setiap grid line: 28 joint yang boleh berputar. Dengan tangan, itu terlalu banyak anu, jadi frame dipotong kepada sub-frame yang cukup kecil untuk moment distribution. Setiap potongan menggantikan bahagian frame yang lain dengan satu andaian di tempat potongan: hujung fixed, pin, atau anggota dengan stiffness dikurangkan. RDF §11.0 (daripada BS 8110 3.2.1.2) memberi tiga sub-frame bagi braced frame di bawah load menegak:

Why fix the far ends of the columns? The floors above and below are loaded too, so the far ends of the columns turn much less than the joints of the floor being analysed. RDF §11.0 takes them as fixed, unless a pin is clearly more reasonable, for example at a foundation that cannot resist moment.Mengapa hujung jauh column dianggap fixed? Lantai di atas dan di bawah turut dibebani, jadi hujung jauh column berputar jauh kurang daripada joint lantai yang dianalisis. RDF §11.0 mengambilnya sebagai fixed, kecuali jika pin jelas lebih munasabah, contohnya di asas yang tidak dapat menahan moment.

Why half the stiffness of the beams beyond? A beam fixed at its far end is stiffer than the same beam in the frame, where the far joint can turn. Taking ½K for it allows for that flexibility, for the same reason as the ½K beams of the one-free-joint sub-frame in Unit 3.Mengapa separuh stiffness bagi beam di luar? Beam yang fixed di hujung jauhnya lebih kaku daripada beam yang sama dalam frame, di mana joint jauhnya boleh berputar. Mengambil ½K baginya mengambil kira kelenturan itu, atas sebab yang sama seperti beam ½K dalam one-free-joint sub-frame Unit 3.

2. The one-level sub-frameOne-level sub-frame

≈ 9 min

The members. The beams of the floor, each at its full stiffness K = I/L; the columns just above and just below the floor, each with Kc = Ic/h for its own storey height, their far ends fixed. Every joint of the floor is free to turn, the end joints included. The ¾K of the continuous beam does not apply: A and D are no longer pins but joints held by their columns.Anggota. Beam lantai itu, setiap satu pada stiffness penuh K = I/L; column tepat di atas dan tepat di bawah lantai, setiap satu dengan Kc = Ic/h bagi ketinggian tingkatnya sendiri, dengan hujung jauh fixed. Setiap joint lantai bebas berputar, termasuk joint hujung. Faktor ¾K continuous beam tidak digunakan: A dan D bukan lagi pin tetapi joint yang ditahan oleh column-nya.

Balance and carry over. At each joint add the beam end moments there (the out-of-balance moment U) and give every member −F × U: the beam ends get their share, and so do the columns. Then carry half of each beam’s balancing moment to the far end of that beam, at A and D too, since they are free joints now. Nothing is carried from a column back into the floor: the half that reaches its fixed far end stays there.Imbangi dan carry over. Di setiap joint, jumlahkan moment hujung beam di situ (moment tidak seimbang U) dan beri setiap anggota −F × U: hujung beam mendapat bahagiannya, begitu juga column. Kemudian bawa separuh balancing moment setiap beam ke hujung jauh beam itu, termasuk ke A dan D, kerana kedua-duanya kini joint bebas. Tiada apa-apa dibawa dari column kembali ke lantai: separuh yang sampai di hujung jauhnya yang fixed kekal di situ.

Column moments. Each balance at a joint gives the two columns shares in the ratio Kc,u : Kc,l, and nothing else enters the columns, so the column moments keep that ratio. With the joint in equilibrium they follow from the final beam moments alone, and the far end of each column takes half, with the same sign:Moment column. Setiap imbangan di satu joint memberi dua column bahagian dalam nisbah Kc,u : Kc,l, dan tiada yang lain memasuki column, jadi moment column mengekalkan nisbah itu. Dengan joint dalam keseimbangan, ia boleh diperoleh daripada moment akhir beam sahaja, dan hujung jauh setiap column mengambil separuh, dengan tanda yang sama:

4. The two-free-joint sub-frameTwo-free-joint sub-frame

≈ 8 min

The one-level sub-frame balances every joint of the floor in every row. RDF §11.0 also allows a smaller sub-frame for the moments of one beam: the beam, the columns at its two ends, and the beams on either side. Only the beam’s two end joints are free, hence the name.One-level sub-frame mengimbangi setiap joint lantai dalam setiap baris. RDF §11.0 juga membenarkan sub-frame yang lebih kecil bagi moment satu beam: beam itu, column di kedua-dua hujungnya, dan beam di kedua-dua sisinya. Hanya dua joint hujung beam itu yang bebas, maka namanya.

The members. The beam considered at its full K; at each of its two end joints the columns above and below, their far ends fixed; the beam on either side, if there is one, at ½K with its far end fixed. An end beam has one neighbour and an interior beam two. Each beam of the floor gets its own sub-frame.Anggota. Beam yang dipertimbangkan pada K penuh; di setiap dua joint hujungnya, column di atas dan di bawah, dengan hujung jauh fixed; beam di setiap sisi, jika ada, pada ½K dengan hujung jauh fixed. Beam hujung mempunyai satu jiran dan beam dalaman dua. Setiap beam lantai mendapat sub-frame-nya sendiri.

For the interior beam BC, the four Load Set 1 cases are all distinct on AB, BC and CD.Kes load. Hanya span di dalam sub-frame yang penting. Bagi beam AB, nota menjalankan dua: AB dan BC kedua-duanya dibebani, bagi hogging di B; AB dibebani dan BC ringan, bagi sagging dalam AB dan moment column. Bebanan berbeza yang ketiga, AB ringan dan BC dibebani, memberi moment span terkecil AB, dan app serta Frame Lab turut menjalankannya. Bagi beam dalaman BC, empat kes Load Set 1 kesemuanya berbeza pada AB, BC dan CD.

What is kept. From each sub-frame keep only its own beam: its end moments, its shears and its span moment, and the moments of the columns at its ends. Beams. End supports. With a sub-frame, the hogging at an end support is part of the envelope (104.84 kN m at A, one-level).
