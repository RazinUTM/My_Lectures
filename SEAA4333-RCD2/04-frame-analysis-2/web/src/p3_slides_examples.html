Answer 1Jawapan 1 A is a joint, not a pinA ialah joint, bukan pin

¾K models a far end that is free to turn with no moment, a pin. In the one-level sub-frame A carries a column above and a column below, so it turns only as much as the columns let it and carries a moment. It is a free joint that is balanced in every row and receives carry-over like any other; the beam keeps its full K and the columns enter ΣK.¾K memodelkan hujung jauh yang bebas berputar tanpa moment, iaitu pin. Dalam one-level sub-frame, A mempunyai column di atas dan di bawah, jadi ia hanya berputar setakat yang dibenarkan oleh column dan memikul moment. Ia ialah joint bebas yang diimbangi dalam setiap baris dan menerima carry-over seperti joint lain; beam mengekalkan K penuhnya dan column dimasukkan dalam ΣK.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 No: the columns take the differenceTidak: column mengambil perbezaannya

Joint B is in equilibrium with four members: MBA + MBC + Mc,u + Mc,l = 0. In case 1, 200.39 − 184.52 = 15.87 kN m is held by the columns (−8.46 and −7.40, to rounding). Only on knife edges, with no columns, are the two moments equal. Each beam is designed with its own value at the face of B.Joint B dalam keseimbangan dengan empat member: MBA + MBC + Mc,u + Mc,l = 0. Dalam kes 1, 200.39 − 184.52 = 15.87 kN m dipegang oleh column (−8.46 dan −7.40, dengan pembundaran). Hanya di atas knife edge, tanpa column, kedua-dua moment itu sama. Setiap beam direka bentuk dengan nilainya sendiri di muka B.

Answer 3Jawapan 3 Only its own spans matterHanya span-nya sendiri yang penting

The sub-frame of AB contains only AB and BC, and Load Set 1 loads two spans in three different ways (both, AB alone, BC alone); the load on CD is outside it. It gives up the effect of the spans beyond its neighbours and the agreement between the beams at a joint, since each beam is solved with its own ½K guess for the rest of the frame. In the notes’ frame the price is small: within 8 % of the one-level sub-frame on every beam moment.Sub-frame AB hanya mengandungi AB dan BC, dan Load Set 1 membebani dua span dalam tiga cara berbeza (kedua-duanya, AB sahaja, BC sahaja); load pada CD berada di luarnya. Ia mengorbankan kesan span di luar jirannya dan persetujuan antara beam di satu joint, kerana setiap beam diselesaikan dengan andaian ½K-nya sendiri bagi bahagian frame yang lain. Dalam frame nota, harganya kecil: dalam lingkungan 8 % daripada one-level sub-frame bagi setiap moment beam.

Answer 4Jawapan 4 Two models mixedDua model dicampur

The 123.0 kN m is low because the one-level sub-frame puts 104.84 kN m of hogging at A; the top bars at A must take that, not 0.15 × 123.0 = 18.46. Taking the smaller span moment from one model and the smaller support moment from another is unsafe. Use one model throughout: the one-level envelope (123.0 bottom, 104.84 top at A), or the continuous beam (186.6 bottom, and Mc,u + Mc,l = 120.06 kN m at the top of A).Nilai 123.0 kN m rendah kerana one-level sub-frame meletakkan hogging 104.84 kN m di A; bar atas di A mesti memikulnya, bukan 0.15 × 123.0 = 18.46. Mengambil moment span yang lebih kecil daripada satu model dan moment support yang lebih kecil daripada model lain adalah tidak selamat. Gunakan satu model sepanjang masa: envelope one-level (123.0 bawah, 104.84 atas di A), atau continuous beam (186.6 bawah, dan Mc,u + Mc,l = 120.06 kN m di atas A).
