A1 · 1 markmarkah
In the one-level sub-frame, an end span AB whose end A has a column above and a column below is taken atDalam one-level sub-frame, span hujung AB yang hujung A-nya mempunyai column di atas dan di bawah diambil pada

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Full K: A is a joint held by its columns, balanced in every row, not a pin. ¾K is for a pinned far end in the continuous beam; ½K is for the neighbours in a two-free-joint sub-frame (Notes §2).K penuh: A ialah joint yang ditahan oleh column-nya, diimbangi dalam setiap baris, bukan pin. ¾K adalah bagi hujung jauh pin dalam continuous beam; ½K adalah bagi jiran dalam two-free-joint sub-frame (Nota §2).

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

By (4.2): Mc,u = −(180 − 150) × 4.0/(4.0 + 4.0) = −15 kN m; the two columns hold −30 together. −7.5 is the far end (4.3) (Notes §2).Melalui (4.2): Mc,u = −(180 − 150) × 4.0/(4.0 + 4.0) = −15 kN m; dua column memegang −30 bersama. −7.5 ialah hujung jauh (4.3) (Nota §2).

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
One-level sub-frame, interior joint B: AB = 6.0 m and BC = 7.0 m, beams 300 × 600 (Ib = 5.4 × 10⁹ mm⁴); columns 300 × 450 (Ic = 2.28 × 10⁹ mm⁴), 3.5 m above the floor and 4.0 m below. In this case AB carries 55.0 and BC 58.0 kN/m. Which line is wrong?One-level sub-frame, joint dalaman B: AB = 6.0 m dan BC = 7.0 m, beam 300 × 600 (Ib = 5.4 × 10⁹ mm⁴); column 300 × 450 (Ic = 2.28 × 10⁹ mm⁴), 3.5 m di atas lantai dan 4.0 m di bawah. Dalam kes ini AB memikul 55.0 dan BC 58.0 kN/m. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2: in a one-level sub-frame the columns are members of the joint: ΣK = 9.00 + 7.71 + 6.51 + 5.70 = 28.92. Then FBA = 0.311, FBC = 0.267, Fc,u = 0.225, Fc,l = 0.197, and the first balance gives BA 22.36, BC 19.16, the upper column 16.17 and the lower 14.15 kN m. The sheet gives the beams all of the 71.83 and the columns nothing.Baris 2: dalam one-level sub-frame, column ialah anggota joint: ΣK = 9.00 + 7.71 + 6.51 + 5.70 = 28.92. Maka FBA = 0.311, FBC = 0.267, Fc,u = 0.225, Fc,l = 0.197, dan imbangan pertama memberi BA 22.36, BC 19.16, column atas 16.17 dan column bawah 14.15 kN m. Helaian memberi beam kesemua 71.83 dan column tiada apa-apa.

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
One-level sub-frame after four cycles, joint B: MBA = +188.40 and MBC = −161.20 kN m; KAB = KBC = 7.50, Kc,u = 4.57 and Kc,l = 4.00. Which line is wrong?One-level sub-frame selepas empat cycle, joint B: MBA = +188.40 dan MBC = −161.20 kN m; KAB = KBC = 7.50, Kc,u = 4.57 dan Kc,l = 4.00. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: the −27.20 kN m is shared between the two columns only, in the ratio of their stiffnesses (4.2): Mc,u = −27.20 × 4.57/8.57 = −14.50 and Mc,l = −12.70 kN m, with far ends −7.25 and −6.35. Dividing by ΣK applies one balance’s factor to the final moments; the sheet’s columns add up to −9.89, so joint B would not be in equilibrium.Baris 3: −27.20 kN m dibahagi antara dua column sahaja, mengikut nisbah stiffness-nya (4.2): Mc,u = −27.20 × 4.57/8.57 = −14.50 dan Mc,l = −12.70 kN m, dengan hujung jauh −7.25 dan −6.35. Membahagi dengan ΣK menggunakan faktor satu imbangan pada moment akhir; column dalam helaian berjumlah −9.89, jadi joint B tidak akan berada dalam keseimbangan.
