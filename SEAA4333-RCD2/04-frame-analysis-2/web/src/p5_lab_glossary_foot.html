GlossaryGlosari

What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Terms stay in English in both languages; the explanation follows the language you chose.Cari istilah; taip dalam kotak untuk menapis. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

sub-frameA part cut from the frame for hand analysis; the cut replaces the rest of the frame by fixed ends, pins or members at reduced stiffness (RDF §11.0, from BS 8110).Bahagian yang dipotong daripada frame bagi analisis tangan; potongan menggantikan bahagian frame yang lain dengan hujung fixed, pin atau anggota dengan stiffness dikurangkan (RDF §11.0, daripada BS 8110).
one-level sub-frameThe beams of one floor at full K with the columns just above and below, their far ends fixed; every joint free. It gives the beam and the column moments in one analysis per load case.Beam satu lantai pada K penuh bersama column tepat di atas dan di bawah, dengan hujung jauh fixed; setiap joint bebas. Ia memberi moment beam dan column dalam satu analisis bagi setiap kes load.
two-free-joint sub-frameOne beam at full K, the columns at its two ends and the beams either side at ½K, all far ends fixed; only the beam’s two end joints are balanced.Satu beam pada K penuh, column di kedua-dua hujungnya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada ½K, semua hujung jauh fixed; hanya dua joint hujung beam itu diimbangi.
one-free-joint sub-frameOne joint with its columns and the beams either side at ½K, far ends fixed: the column moments of the continuous-beam method (Unit 3).Satu joint bersama column-nya dan beam di kedua-dua sisi pada ½K, hujung jauh fixed: moment column bagi kaedah continuous beam (Unit 3).
continuous beam (knife-edge supports)The beams of one floor on supports that do not restrain rotation; end spans at ¾K. It shows no moment at an end support (Unit 3).Beam satu lantai di atas support yang tidak menghalang putaran; span hujung pada ¾K. Ia tidak menunjukkan moment di support hujung (Unit 3).
free jointA joint that turns until it is in equilibrium; it is balanced in every row of the distribution and receives carry-over.Joint yang berputar sehingga berada dalam keseimbangan; ia diimbangi dalam setiap baris distribution dan menerima carry-over.
fixed far endThe end of a column or of a ½K neighbour that the sub-frame holds still. It is never balanced; in the two-free-joint sub-frame nothing is carried to it.Hujung column atau jiran ½K yang ditahan tidak bergerak oleh sub-frame. Ia tidak pernah diimbangi; dalam two-free-joint sub-frame tiada apa-apa dibawa kepadanya.
full K · ¾K · ½KThe beam stiffness each model uses: full I/L in the one-level sub-frame and for the beam of a two-free-joint sub-frame; ¾K for a pinned end span of the continuous beam; ½K for the neighbours fixed at their far ends.Stiffness beam yang digunakan setiap model: I/L penuh dalam one-level sub-frame dan bagi beam two-free-joint sub-frame; ¾K bagi span hujung yang pin dalam continuous beam; ½K bagi jiran yang fixed di hujung jauhnya.
Kc = Ic/hThe stiffness of a column, with h the height of its own storey: Kc,u for the storey above the floor, Kc,l for the storey below.Stiffness column, dengan h ketinggian tingkatnya sendiri: Kc,u bagi tingkat di atas lantai, Kc,l bagi tingkat di bawah.
distribution factor with columnsF = K/ΣK at a joint, ΣK = Kb,L + Kb,R + Kc,u + Kc,l; the factors at a joint, columns included, add up to 1.F = K/ΣK di satu joint, ΣK = Kb,L + Kb,R + Kc,u + Kc,l; faktor di satu joint, termasuk column, berjumlah 1.
column momentThe sum of a column’s balancing shares at its joint; from the final beam moments, Mc = −(ML + MR) Kc/(Kc,u + Kc,l).Jumlah bahagian imbangan column di joint-nya; daripada moment akhir beam, Mc = −(ML + MR) Kc/(Kc,u + Kc,l).
far-end momentHalf of a column’s moment at the floor, with the same sign, at its fixed far end: the carry-over factor ½. At a fixed foundation it is the moment at the base.Separuh moment column di lantai, dengan tanda yang sama, di hujung jauhnya yang fixed: carry-over factor ½. Di asas yang fixed, ia ialah moment di dasar.
local load casesThe distinct loadings of the spans inside a two-free-joint sub-frame: three for an end beam and four for an interior beam with Load Set 1.Bebanan berbeza bagi span di dalam two-free-joint sub-frame: tiga bagi beam hujung dan empat bagi beam dalaman dengan Load Set 1.
two moments at an interior supportIn a frame the beams on the two sides of a joint carry different moments; the columns take the difference. Each beam is designed for its own face value.Dalam frame, beam di dua sisi satu joint memikul moment yang berbeza; column mengambil perbezaannya. Setiap beam direka bentuk bagi nilai di mukanya sendiri.
end-support momentThe hogging of a beam at an end column. A sub-frame gives it in its envelope; with the continuous beam the course takes Mc,u + Mc,l of the one-free-joint sub-frame. Never less than 0.15Mspan (EC2 9.2.1.2(1)).Hogging beam di column hujung. Sub-frame memberinya dalam envelope; dengan continuous beam, kursus mengambil Mc,u + Mc,l one-free-joint sub-frame. Tidak pernah kurang daripada 0.15Mspan (EC2 9.2.1.2(1)).
envelopeThe worst value of every effect over the load cases: hogging at each end, sagging, least span moment, shears and column moments.Nilai paling teruk bagi setiap kesan merentasi kes load: hogging di setiap hujung, sagging, moment span terkecil, shear dan moment column.
four cyclesThe number of balancing cycles the notes use for the sub-frames; the columns absorb part of every balance, so the table settles faster than the continuous beam’s five.Bilangan balancing cycle yang digunakan nota bagi sub-frame; column menyerap sebahagian setiap imbangan, jadi jadual stabil lebih cepat daripada lima cycle continuous beam.
Load Set 1 · Load Set 2EC2’s alternate and adjacent spans (the examples), against the Malaysian National Annex’s all and alternate spans, which can miss part of a support moment (up to 20 % at B in the workbook’s frame, one-level).Span berselang dan bersebelahan EC2 (contoh), berbanding semua span dan span berselang Malaysian National Annex, yang boleh terlepas sebahagian moment support (sehingga 20 % di B dalam frame workbook, one-level).
design column momentIn this course, the larger of the one-free-joint and the one-level values at each column; checked with the largest and with the smallest axial force that can come with it.Dalam kursus ini, yang lebih besar antara nilai one-free-joint dan one-level di setiap column; disemak dengan daya paksi terbesar dan terkecil yang boleh hadir bersamanya.
Professional Habit 4Know which model you are in: write it at the top of the sheet, with its stiffnesses, its far ends and the joints it balances, before the first row.Ketahui model mana yang anda gunakan: tulis di bahagian atas helaian, bersama stiffness-nya, hujung jauhnya dan joint yang diimbanginya, sebelum baris pertama.

Short notesNota ringkas

§1 Why cut the frameMengapa frame dipotong

  • One-level: the floor and its columns, full K. Two-free-joint: one beam, its columns, neighbours at ½K. Continuous beam + one-free-joint: Unit 3.One-level: lantai dan column-nya, K penuh. Two-free-joint: satu beam, column-nya, jiran pada ½K. Continuous beam + one-free-joint: Unit 3.
  • Far ends fixed unless a pin is clearly more reasonable.Hujung jauh fixed kecuali jika pin jelas lebih munasabah.

§2 The one-level sub-frameOne-level sub-frame

  • Every joint, A and D included, is balanced in every row; carry half along the beams only.Setiap joint, termasuk A dan D, diimbangi dalam setiap baris; bawa separuh di sepanjang beam sahaja.

§3 A worked case and the envelopeKes contoh dan envelope

  • Check ΣM = 0 at every joint, beams and columns together.Semak ΣM = 0 di setiap joint, beam dan column bersama.

§4 The two-free-joint sub-frameTwo-free-joint sub-frame

  • Only the beam’s two joints are balanced; nothing is carried to the fixed ends. Keep only the beam’s own results; a column takes the larger of its two sub-frames.Hanya dua joint beam diimbangi; tiada apa-apa dibawa ke hujung fixed. Kekalkan hasil beam itu sahaja; column mengambil yang lebih besar daripada dua sub-frame-nya.

§5 How much do we lose?Berapa banyak yang hilang?

  • Continuous beam: 0 at an end support, sagging in the end span about 50 % high, one moment at an interior support.Continuous beam: 0 di support hujung, sagging dalam span hujung lebih kurang 50 % tinggi, satu moment di support dalaman.
  • One-free-joint columns: high at an end column, low at an interior one.Column one-free-joint: tinggi di column hujung, rendah di column dalaman.

§6 Design valuesNilai design

  • Use one model throughout. End support: the sub-frame’s envelope value, or Mc,u + Mc,l with the continuous beam; at least 0.15Mspan.Gunakan satu model sepanjang masa. Support hujung: nilai envelope sub-frame, atau Mc,u + Mc,l dengan continuous beam; sekurang-kurangnya 0.15Mspan.
  • Columns: the larger of the one-free-joint and one-level values.Column: yang lebih besar antara nilai one-free-joint dan one-level.
