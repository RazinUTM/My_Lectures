Floors

The design wind speed, level by level

Vsit = Vs Md Mz,cat Ms Mh
Vdes = Vsit × I

Block B · talk 3 · 6 minutes

From speed to pressure

p = 0.613 V² Cfig Cdyn (Pa)
Cfig = Cp,e Ka Kc Kl Kp

Windward 0.8 with Vdes,z; leeward from d/b with Vdes at h.

Cdyn = 1.0 if na > 1 Hz; 6.2.1 for 0.2–1 Hz.

Net = pW − pL: both push downwind.
