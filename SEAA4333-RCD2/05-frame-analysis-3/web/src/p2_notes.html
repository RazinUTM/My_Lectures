1. Unbraced frames and the lateral loadUnbraced frame dan lateral load

≈ 7 min

Units 3 and 4 analysed a braced frame. The wind on the building went to shear walls or a core, and the frame carried the vertical load alone. Without that bracing the frame itself must resist the wind, and the beams and columns take moments from it at every floor. This Unit finds the wind; Unit 6 analyses the frame under it.Unit 3 dan 4 menganalisis braced frame. Angin pada bangunan dihantar ke shear wall atau core, dan frame hanya menanggung load menegak. Tanpa bracing itu, frame sendiri perlu menahan angin, dan beam serta column menerima moment daripadanya pada setiap lantai. Unit ini mencari angin tersebut; Unit 6 menganalisis frame di bawahnya.

Braced and unbraced framesBraced frame dan unbraced frameA braced frame does not contribute to the overall stability of the building: the lateral actions go to bracing members (shear walls, a lift or stair core), and the frame supports the vertical actions only. An unbraced frame contributes to the stability: all lateral actions are carried by its beams and columns, which support both the vertical and the lateral actions (course notes, Types of Frame).Braced frame tidak menyumbang kepada kestabilan keseluruhan bangunan: lateral action dihantar ke bracing member (shear wall, core lif atau tangga), dan frame hanya menyokong vertical action. Unbraced frame menyumbang kepada kestabilan: semua lateral action ditanggung oleh beam dan column-nya, yang menyokong vertical action dan lateral action (nota kursus, Types of Frame).

A frame with pinned beam-to-column connections and pinned bases is not stable under a lateral force: it folds sideways like a parallelogram (Figure 1a). It becomes stable in two ways. Rigid joints, or fixed bases, let the frame resist the sway by bending its members (b): this is the unbraced frame of this Unit. Or a stiff core or wall holds the frame up, and the frame can then have pinned joints (c).Frame dengan sambungan beam-ke-column pin dan tapak pin tidak stabil di bawah daya lateral: ia terlipat ke sisi seperti segi empat selari (Rajah 1a). Ia menjadi stabil dengan dua cara. Joint tegar, atau tapak fixed, membolehkan frame menahan sway dengan melenturkan member-nya (b): inilah unbraced frame dalam Unit ini. Atau core atau dinding yang kaku menahan frame, dan frame itu kemudian boleh mempunyai joint pin (c).

Two lateral actions act on an unbraced frame. The first is the wind, from MS 1553:2002, the subject of §2 to §5. The second comes from the building itself: no frame is built exactly plumb, and EC2 5.2 asks for the effect of a small out-of-plumb, applied as horizontal forces at each floor (§6).Dua lateral action bertindak pada unbraced frame. Yang pertama ialah angin, daripada MS 1553:2002, tajuk §2 hingga §5. Yang kedua datang daripada bangunan itu sendiri: tiada frame dibina tegak sepenuhnya, dan EC2 5.2 meminta kesan sedikit ketidaktegakan, dikenakan sebagai daya mengufuk pada setiap lantai (§6).

How the wind reaches a frame. The wind presses on the walls. The walls span between the floors, so each floor slab collects the pressure on half a storey above and half a storey below. The slab is very stiff in its own plane and passes that force to the frames, each frame taking the share of its own width. A plane frame therefore receives one horizontal force at each floor level, and the analysis of Unit 6 starts from those forces.Bagaimana angin sampai ke frame. Angin menekan dinding. Dinding merentang di antara lantai, jadi setiap slab lantai mengumpul tekanan pada separuh tingkat di atas dan separuh tingkat di bawah. Slab sangat kaku dalam satahnya sendiri dan menghantar daya itu kepada frame, setiap frame menerima bahagian pada lebarnya sendiri. Oleh itu plane frame menerima satu daya mengufuk pada setiap aras lantai, dan analisis Unit 6 bermula daripada daya tersebut.

2. Wind on a building, and the procedures of MS 1553Angin pada bangunan, dan prosedur MS 1553

≈ 7 min

Wind flowing round a building pushes on the face it meets and pulls on the others. On the windward wall the air slows down against the building and the pressure rises (+). It separates at the corners, so the side walls, the roof and the leeward wall at the back are in suction (−). For the frame, the windward push and the leeward pull act in the same direction: both move the building downwind.Angin yang mengalir mengelilingi bangunan menolak muka yang ditemuinya dan menarik muka yang lain. Pada dinding windward, udara diperlahankan oleh bangunan dan tekanan meningkat (+). Aliran terpisah di sudut bangunan, jadi dinding sisi, bumbung dan dinding leeward di belakang berada dalam suction (−). Bagi frame, tolakan windward dan tarikan leeward bertindak dalam arah yang sama: kedua-duanya menolak bangunan ke arah angin bertiup.

The intensity of the wind action grows with the square of the wind speed, and the speed depends on the site: its region, the height above ground, the topography and the roughness of the terrain around it. A building answers the gusts in two parts: a background part, the static deflection under the pressure, and a resonant part, a vibration that matters only for flexible buildings. MS 1553 treats both with a static pressure, raised by a dynamic response factor when the building is flexible (§4).Keamatan wind action meningkat dengan kuasa dua laju angin, dan laju itu bergantung pada tapak: kawasannya, ketinggian dari tanah, topografi dan kekasaran terrain di sekelilingnya. Bangunan bertindak balas terhadap tiupan angin dalam dua bahagian: bahagian background, iaitu static deflection di bawah tekanan, dan bahagian resonant, iaitu getaran yang penting hanya bagi bangunan fleksibel. MS 1553 merawat kedua-duanya dengan tekanan statik, yang dinaikkan oleh dynamic response factor apabila bangunan itu fleksibel (§4).
