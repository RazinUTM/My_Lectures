A1 · 1 markmarkah
Which frame carries the wind on a building by bending in its own beams and columns?Frame yang manakah menanggung angin pada bangunan melalui lenturan beam dan column-nya sendiri?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Without bracing, the rigid joints let the beams and columns resist the sway in bending: an unbraced frame. With pinned joints and bases the frame is a mechanism.Tanpa bracing, joint tegar membolehkan beam dan column menahan sway melalui lenturan: unbraced frame. Dengan joint dan tapak pin, frame ialah mekanisme.

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A building is 40 m long and 15 m wide; its frames span the 15 m width. At the roof Vdes = 36.0 m/s and na = 1.5 Hz. A student works out the leeward suction on these frames. Which line is wrong?Sebuah bangunan sepanjang 40 m dan selebar 15 m; frame-nya merentang lebar 15 m. Di bumbung Vdes = 36.0 m/s dan na = 1.5 Hz. Seorang pelajar mengira suction leeward pada frame ini. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2: the wind blows onto the 40 m face, so b = 40 m and d = 15 m: d/b = 15/40 = 0.375 ≤ 1 and Cp,e = −0.5. Then pL = 0.613 × 36.0² × (−0.5)/1000 = −0.397 kN/m², not −0.215: swapping b and d lost 46 % of the suction. Line 4 follows from line 2.Baris 2: angin bertiup ke atas muka 40 m, jadi b = 40 m dan d = 15 m: d/b = 15/40 = 0.375 ≤ 1 dan Cp,e = −0.5. Maka pL = 0.613 × 36.0² × (−0.5)/1000 = −0.397 kN/m², bukan −0.215: menukar b dan d menghilangkan 46 % suction. Baris 4 mengikut baris 2.

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
A frame stands between bays of 6 m; every storey above the ground storey is 3.5 m. The net pressure is 1.05 kN/m² at the roof and 0.98 kN/m² at level 4, the floor below it. A student works out the forces. Which line is wrong?Sebuah frame terletak di antara bay 6 m; setiap tingkat di atas tingkat bawah setinggi 3.5 m. Net pressure ialah 1.05 kN/m² di bumbung dan 0.98 kN/m² pada aras 4, lantai di bawahnya. Seorang pelajar mengira daya. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2: the roof collects only the upper half of the top storey, htrib = 3.5/2 = 1.75 m (the lower half goes to level 4). Wk,roof = 1.05 × 6.0 × 1.75 = 11.03 kN, half of 22.05. Line 3 follows; lines 4 and 5 are right.Baris 2: bumbung hanya mengumpul separuh atas tingkat teratas, htrib = 3.5/2 = 1.75 m (separuh bawah dihantar ke aras 4). Wk,roof = 1.05 × 6.0 × 1.75 = 11.03 kN, separuh daripada 22.05. Baris 3 mengikutinya; baris 4 dan 5 betul.
