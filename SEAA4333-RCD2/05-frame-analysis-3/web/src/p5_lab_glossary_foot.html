Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

unbraced frameA frame that gives the building its stability: rigid joints or fixed bases let its beams and columns carry the lateral actions as well as the vertical ones.Frame yang memberi bangunan kestabilannya: joint tegar atau tapak fixed membolehkan beam dan column-nya menanggung lateral action dan juga vertical action.
braced frameA frame whose lateral actions go to bracing members (shear walls, a core); it carries the vertical actions only.Frame yang lateral action-nya dihantar ke bracing member (shear wall, core); ia hanya menanggung vertical action.
basic wind speed VsThe wind speed of the region from the map of MS 1553 (Figure A1): 33.5 m/s in Zone I, 32.5 m/s in Zone II.Laju angin kawasan daripada peta MS 1553 (Figure A1): 33.5 m/s di Zone I, 32.5 m/s di Zone II.
site wind speed VsitVsMdMz,catMsMh: the basic speed adjusted for direction, terrain and height, shielding and hills (Eq. 1).VsMdMz,catMsMh: laju asas yang dilaraskan bagi arah, terrain dan ketinggian, perlindungan dan bukit (Eq. 1).
design wind speed VdesVsit × I, at the roof; at a lower level Vdes,z = VdesMz,cat(z)/Mz,cat(h).Vsit × I, di bumbung; pada aras yang lebih rendah Vdes,z = VdesMz,cat(z)/Mz,cat(h).
terrain categoryThe roughness of the ground upwind, from 1 (open water, flat open land) to 4 (city centres); it sets Mz,cat.Kekasaran tanah di hulu angin, daripada 1 (permukaan air, tanah terbuka rata) hingga 4 (pusat bandar); ia menentukan Mz,cat.
Mz,catThe terrain/height multiplier of Table 4.1 (Table A1 in Appendix A), interpolated linearly in height.Terrain/height multiplier dalam Table 4.1 (Table A1 dalam Appendix A), diinterpolasi secara linear mengikut ketinggian.
importance factor ITable 3.2: 0.87 (category I), 1.0 (II: offices, flats), 1.15 (III: more than 300 people in one area; IV: essential buildings). Not the turbulence intensity Ih.Table 3.2: 0.87 (category I), 1.0 (II: pejabat, rumah pangsa), 1.15 (III: lebih daripada 300 orang di satu kawasan; IV: bangunan penting). Bukan turbulence intensity Ih.
windward, leewardThe wall the wind blows on (pressure, +) and the wall at the back (suction, −). Both push the frame downwind.Dinding yang ditiup angin (tekanan, +) dan dinding di belakang (suction, −). Kedua-duanya menolak frame ke arah angin.
b, dMS 1553 Figure 5.2: b is the breadth of the building normal to the wind, d its depth along the wind. A plane frame carries the wind in its own plane.MS 1553 Figure 5.2: b ialah lebar bangunan yang normal kepada angin, d dalamnya sepanjang arah angin. Plane frame menanggung angin dalam satahnya sendiri.
Cp,eExternal pressure coefficient: 0.8 on the windward wall (Table 5.2(a)); on the leeward wall from d/b (Table 5.2(b)): −0.5 for d/b ≤ 1, −0.3 at 2, −0.2 from 4.External pressure coefficient: 0.8 pada dinding windward (Table 5.2(a)); pada dinding leeward daripada d/b (Table 5.2(b)): −0.5 bagi d/b ≤ 1, −0.3 pada 2, −0.2 mulai 4.
CfigAerodynamic shape factor Cp,eKaKcKlKp; for the walls of a frame the K factors are 1.0.Aerodynamic shape factor Cp,eKaKcKlKp; bagi dinding frame, faktor K bernilai 1.0.
CdynDynamic response factor: 1.0 when na > 1 Hz; from 6.2.1 between 0.2 and 1 Hz (Eq. 10).Dynamic response factor: 1.0 apabila na > 1 Hz; daripada 6.2.1 di antara 0.2 dan 1 Hz (Eq. 10).
naThe first natural frequency of the building in the direction of the wind; it decides whether the building is wind-sensitive (6.1).Frekuensi tabii pertama bangunan dalam arah angin; ia menentukan sama ada bangunan itu wind-sensitive (6.1).
net pressurepW − pL at a level: the windward pressure minus the (negative) leeward suction.pW − pL pada satu aras: tekanan windward tolak suction leeward (negatif).
Bw, htribThe width of building a frame carries (half the bay on each side) and the height a floor carries (half the storey above and below).Lebar bangunan yang ditanggung frame (separuh bay pada setiap sisi) dan ketinggian yang ditanggung lantai (separuh tingkat di atas dan di bawah).
minimum design wind load0.65 kN/m² times the projected area for the main structure (2.4.2, A2.2): scale the forces by 0.65/pavg when the average net pressure is lower.0.65 kN/m² didarab dengan luas unjuran bagi struktur utama (2.4.2, A2.2): skalakan daya dengan 0.65/pavg apabila purata net pressure lebih rendah.
Cp,iInternal pressure coefficient, +0.6 or −0.3 for an enclosed building; it cancels in the force on the frame.Internal pressure coefficient, +0.6 atau −0.3 bagi bangunan tertutup; ia terbatal dalam daya pada frame.
geometric imperfectionThe out-of-plumb of EC2 5.2: θi = θ0αhαm, applied as Hi = θiNi at each floor.Ketidaktegakan EC2 5.2: θi = θ0αhαm, dikenakan sebagai Hi = θiNi pada setiap lantai.

  • Braced: the bracing takes the lateral load. Unbraced: the frame does, by bending at rigid joints.Braced: bracing menanggung lateral load. Unbraced: frame menanggungnya, melalui bending di joint tegar.
