    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 5 turned the wind on a building into one force per floor on frame ABCD. This Unit carries those forces through the frame. An unbraced frame bends in double curvature, so its columns and beams have points of contraflexure near mid-height and mid-span; with the moment zero there, each storey becomes statically determinate once one more assumption shares the load between the columns. The cantilever method treats the frame as one vertical cantilever: the column axial forces grow with the distance from the centroid. The portal method treats each bay as a portal: the storey shear is shared by the spans. Both give the column shears, the beam shears and the moments storey by storey. The course method then adds the wind moments at a level to those of the vertical load (BS 8110 load combination 3, as the course notes’ Example 7): the beam ends hog more on one side and less on the other, and a column that the vertical load hardly bends takes one of the largest moments of the floor. The EN 1990 combinations with the EC2 imperfections, the app’s default route, are shown beside it.Unit 5 menukar angin pada bangunan kepada satu daya bagi setiap lantai pada frame ABCD. Unit ini membawa daya tersebut melalui frame. Unbraced frame melentur dalam double curvature, jadi column dan beam-nya mempunyai point of contraflexure berhampiran tengah ketinggian dan tengah span; dengan moment sifar di situ, setiap tingkat menjadi statically determinate apabila satu lagi andaian mengagihkan load kepada column. Cantilever method menganggap frame sebagai satu cantilever menegak: axial force column bertambah dengan jarak dari centroid. Portal method menganggap setiap bay sebagai portal: storey shear diagihkan mengikut span. Kedua-duanya memberi shear column, shear beam dan moment tingkat demi tingkat. Course method kemudian menambah moment angin pada satu aras kepada moment load menegak (BS 8110 load combination 3, seperti Contoh 7 nota kursus): hujung beam hogging lebih pada satu sisi dan kurang pada sisi lain, dan column yang hampir tidak dilenturkan oleh load menegak menanggung antara moment terbesar di lantai itu. Kombinasi EN 1990 dengan imperfection EC2, laluan lalai app, ditunjukkan di sebelahnya.

    1. The frame under lateral loadFrame di bawah lateral load

    ≈ 7 min

    Unit 5 ended with one force per floor on frame ABCD: the wind of Worked Example 5.1, from 11.63 kN at the roof to 18.75 kN at level 1. This Unit takes those forces down through the frame to the foundations, finds the moments they cause in every beam and column, and adds them to the moments of the vertical load.Unit 5 berakhir dengan satu daya bagi setiap lantai pada frame ABCD: angin Worked Example 5.1, daripada 11.63 kN di bumbung hingga 18.75 kN pada aras 1. Unit ini membawa daya tersebut turun melalui frame ke asas, mencari moment yang terhasil dalam setiap beam dan column, dan menambahnya kepada moment load menegak.

    Under horizontal forces at the floors, an unbraced frame sways. The floors move sideways almost as rigid bodies, and the rigid joints force each column and each beam to bend in double curvature: the moment at one end is opposite to the moment at the other (Figure 1). Somewhere in between the moment is zero, at a point of contraflexure. In a regular frame it lies close to the mid-height of every column and the mid-span of every beam.Di bawah daya mengufuk pada lantai, unbraced frame bergoyang. Lantai bergerak ke sisi hampir seperti jasad tegar, dan joint tegar memaksa setiap column dan setiap beam melentur dalam double curvature: moment di satu hujung berlawanan dengan moment di hujung yang lain (Rajah 1). Di antara keduanya moment adalah sifar, pada point of contraflexure. Dalam frame yang sekata, titik ini terletak hampir di tengah ketinggian setiap column dan tengah span setiap beam.

    A frame with rigid joints is highly indeterminate, and its exact moments come from a stiffness analysis, by computer. By hand, the points of contraflexure open a short cut: a hinge at each one removes an unknown moment. A frame of several bays is still indeterminate after that, and one more assumption about how each storey shares its load closes the problem. The two classic choices give the two methods of the course:Frame dengan joint tegar adalah sangat indeterminate, dan moment tepatnya diperoleh daripada analisis stiffness, menggunakan komputer. Dengan tangan, point of contraflexure membuka jalan pintas: satu hinge pada setiap titik itu menghapuskan satu moment yang tidak diketahui. Frame dengan beberapa bay masih indeterminate selepas itu, dan satu lagi andaian tentang cara setiap tingkat mengagihkan load-nya menyelesaikan masalah. Dua pilihan klasik memberi dua kaedah kursus:

    Answer 1Jawapan 1 Double curvature, if the members are alikeDouble curvature, jika member serupa

    With rigid joints and floors moving sideways, each column and beam bends in double curvature; when the beams and columns at both ends of a member are alike, its end moments are nearly equal and opposite, so the zero falls near the middle. It is poorest in the ground storey with fixed bases, where the point moves up from mid-height, and wherever a stiff member meets a flexible one.Dengan joint tegar dan lantai bergerak ke sisi, setiap column dan beam melentur dalam double curvature; apabila beam dan column di kedua-dua hujung member serupa, moment hujungnya hampir sama besar dan berlawanan, jadi sifar jatuh berhampiran tengah. Ia paling lemah dalam tingkat bawah dengan tapak fixed, di mana titik itu bergerak ke atas dari tengah ketinggian, dan di mana-mana member kaku bertemu member fleksibel.

    Answer 2Jawapan 2 The shares are half spansBahagiannya separuh span

    Each column takes the half spans beside it, so the joint balance gives each beam a moment proportional to its span: Mb = (Vabovehabove + V h)L/(4ΣL). Then F = 2Mb/L is the same for every span, and an interior column receives equal and opposite shears from its two beams: no axial force.Setiap column menanggung separuh span di sebelahnya, jadi imbangan joint memberi setiap beam moment yang berkadar dengan span-nya: Mb = (Vabovehabove + V h)L/(4ΣL). Maka F = 2Mb/L sama bagi setiap span, dan column dalam menerima shear yang sama besar dan berlawanan daripada dua beam-nya: tiada axial force.

    Answer 3Jawapan 3 Balanced against antisymmetricSeimbang berbanding antisimetri

    Under the vertical load the beams on both sides of B hog almost equally, so the joint is nearly balanced and the columns take only the small difference, 8.72 kN m below B. The wind bends the frame antisymmetrically: every beam end and every column at the joint turns the same way, and the column takes its full share, 112.44 kN m. The hogging was large already (146.91 kN m), so the same wind adds less in proportion.Di bawah load menegak, beam di kedua-dua sisi B hogging hampir sama, jadi joint hampir seimbang dan column hanya menanggung perbezaan kecil, 8.72 kN m di bawah B. Angin melenturkan frame secara antisimetri: setiap hujung beam dan setiap column di joint berputar ke arah yang sama, dan column menanggung bahagian penuhnya, 112.44 kN m. Hogging sudah besar (146.91 kN m), jadi angin yang sama menambah kurang secara perkadaran.

    Answer 4Jawapan 4 Sagging at a supportSagging di support

    At the end the wind relieves, the beam-end moment is Mw − Mv; when the wind moment is the larger, the end sags: 2.28 kN m at A of frame ABCD by the course method. The section then needs bottom bars carried into the support and anchored there, not stopped short as for a hogging end. On the EN 1990 route the check is 1.0Gk + 1.5Wk, which finds much larger reversals.Di hujung yang dilegakan oleh angin, moment hujung beam ialah Mw − Mv; apabila moment angin lebih besar, hujung itu sagging: 2.28 kN m di A frame ABCD mengikut course method. Section itu kemudian memerlukan bar bawah yang dibawa masuk ke support dan di-anchor di situ, bukan dihentikan awal seperti hujung hogging.     A3 · 1 markmarkah
    In a frame of equal spans, the portal method gives an interior columnDalam frame dengan span yang sama, portal method memberi column dalaman

    Short notesNota ringkas

    §1 The frame under lateral loadFrame di bawah lateral load

    • Under sway the columns and beams bend in double curvature: points of contraflexure at mid-height and mid-span (assumed).Di bawah sway, column dan beam melentur dalam double curvature: point of contraflexure di tengah ketinggian dan tengah span (diandaikan).
    • Cut each storey at mid-height: the forces above give V and Mo.Potong setiap tingkat di tengah ketinggian: daya di atas memberi V dan Mo.

    §2 The cantilever methodCantilever method

    • Tension on the windward side. Beam shear at mid-span: the change of axial force to its left.Tension di sisi windward. Shear beam di tengah span: perubahan axial force di sebelah kirinya.
    • Column shears from moments about each beam cut; the last column from ΣH = V.Shear column daripada moment terhadap setiap beam section; column terakhir daripada ΣH = V.

    §3 Storey by storeyTingkat demi tingkat

    • Below the top storey use ΔN = N − Nabove, and include the column shears of the storey above in the moments about the beam cut.Di bawah tingkat teratas gunakan ΔN = N − Nabove, dan masukkan shear column tingkat di atas dalam moment terhadap beam section.
    • Mcol = H h/2; Mbeam = F L/2. Check ΣH = V (Professional Habit 6).Mcol = H h/2; Mbeam = F L/2. Semak ΣH = V (Professional Habit 6).

    §4 The portal methodPortal method

    • Every beam at a level carries the same shear, so only the outer columns carry axial force.Setiap beam pada satu aras membawa shear yang sama, jadi hanya column luar membawa axial force.

    §5 Wind plus vertical loadAngin dan load menegak

    • At level n: the beams take the Mbeam of the storey below the level, the upper columns the Mcol of the storey above, the lower columns that of the storey below.Pada aras n: beam mengambil Mbeam tingkat di bawah aras, column atas Mcol tingkat di atas, column bawah Mcol tingkat di bawah.
    • Span: the largest Mv(x) ± Mw(1 − 2x/L).Span: nilai terbesar Mv(x) ± Mw(1 − 2x/L).

    §6 EN 1990 and which methodEN 1990 dan kaedah mana

    • On the EN 1990 route add the imperfections Hi = θiNi. Portal for low, wide frames; cantilever for tall, slender ones; both check a stiffness analysis.Dalam laluan EN 1990, tambah imperfection Hi = θiNi. Portal bagi frame rendah dan lebar; cantilever bagi frame tinggi dan slender; kedua-duanya menyemak stiffness analysis.
