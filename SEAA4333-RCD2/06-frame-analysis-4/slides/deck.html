Block A · talk 2 · 7 minutes

Two assumptions, two methods

Portal

the storey shear: each bay’s share by its span, half to each leg

Cantilever

the overturning moment: axial force ∝ distance from the centroid (equal areas)

When

portal for low, wide frames; cantilever for tall, slender ones

The portal method, joint by joint

ki = (Lleft + Lright)/(2ΣL) · Hi = ki V
Mcol = H h/2
Mb,right = Mc,above + Mc,below − Mb,left
F = 2Mb/L

Start at the windward joint and walk along the level.

Every beam of a level carries the same shear: only the outer columns take axial force.

Professional Habit 6

Check every storey’s equilibrium before using its moments

Shear

ΣH = V in every storey

Sway moment

Σ(Mtop + Mbottom) of the columns = V h

Joints

Σ Mbeam = Σ Mcol at every joint; a negative beam moment is a warning

Overturning

cantilever method: ΣNi di = Mo

Frame S6: lines 4 and 6 fail these checks; line 8 tests only ΣH, which equal shares still pass.
