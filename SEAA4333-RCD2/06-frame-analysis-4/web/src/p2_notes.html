1. The frame under lateral loadFrame di bawah lateral load

≈ 7 min

Unit 5 ended with one force per floor on frame ABCD: the wind of Worked Example 5.1, from 11.63 kN at the roof to 18.75 kN at level 1. This Unit takes those forces down through the frame to the foundations, finds the moments they cause in every beam and column, and adds them to the moments of the vertical load.Unit 5 berakhir dengan satu daya bagi setiap lantai pada frame ABCD: angin Worked Example 5.1, daripada 11.63 kN di bumbung hingga 18.75 kN pada aras 1. Unit ini membawa daya tersebut turun melalui frame ke asas, mencari moment yang terhasil dalam setiap beam dan column, dan menambahnya kepada moment load menegak.

Under horizontal forces at the floors, an unbraced frame sways. The floors move sideways almost as rigid bodies, and the rigid joints force each column and each beam to bend in double curvature: the moment at one end is opposite to the moment at the other (Figure 1). Somewhere in between the moment is zero, at a point of contraflexure. In a regular frame it lies close to the mid-height of every column and the mid-span of every beam.Di bawah daya mengufuk pada lantai, unbraced frame bergoyang. Lantai bergerak ke sisi hampir seperti jasad tegar, dan joint tegar memaksa setiap column dan setiap beam melentur dalam double curvature: moment di satu hujung berlawanan dengan moment di hujung yang lain (Rajah 1). Di antara keduanya moment adalah sifar, pada point of contraflexure. Dalam frame yang sekata, titik ini terletak hampir di tengah ketinggian setiap column dan tengah span setiap beam.

A frame with rigid joints is highly indeterminate, and its exact moments come from a stiffness analysis, by computer. By hand, the points of contraflexure open a short cut: a hinge at each one removes an unknown moment. A frame of several bays is still indeterminate after that, and one more assumption about how each storey shares its load closes the problem. The two classic choices give the two methods of the course:Frame dengan joint tegar adalah sangat indeterminate, dan moment tepatnya diperoleh daripada analisis stiffness, menggunakan komputer. Dengan tangan, point of contraflexure membuka jalan pintas: satu hinge pada setiap titik itu menghapuskan satu moment yang tidak diketahui. Frame dengan beberapa bay masih indeterminate selepas itu, dan satu lagi andaian tentang cara setiap tingkat mengagihkan load-nya menyelesaikan masalah. Dua pilihan klasik memberi dua kaedah kursus:

Portal method: the assumptionsPortal method: andaian(1) The points of contraflexure are at the mid-height of every column and the mid-span of every beam. (2) The shear of each storey is shared between the bays in proportion to their spans. (3) The shear of each bay is shared equally by its two columns, so an interior column takes half of each bay beside it (course notes; Mosley, Bungey and Hulse §3.4.2).(1) Point of contraflexure terletak di tengah ketinggian setiap column dan tengah span setiap beam. (2) Shear setiap tingkat diagihkan kepada bay mengikut span-nya. (3) Shear setiap bay dibahagi sama rata kepada dua column-nya, jadi column dalam menanggung separuh daripada setiap bay di sebelahnya (nota kursus; Mosley, Bungey dan Hulse §3.4.2).
(6.5)

The column moments follow at once, H h/2 at the top and bottom of the storey. The beam moments come from the equilibrium of each joint, working from the windward side: the beam on the right of a joint balances the columns above and below it, less the beam on its left. The beam shear follows from the moment zero at mid-span, and each beam shear passes to the columns as axial force:Moment column diperoleh serta-merta, H h/2 di atas dan bawah tingkat. Moment beam datang daripada keseimbangan setiap joint, bermula dari sisi windward: beam di sebelah kanan joint mengimbangi column di atas dan di bawahnya, tolak beam di sebelah kirinya. Shear beam diperoleh daripada moment sifar di tengah span, dan setiap shear beam dihantar kepada column sebagai axial force:

Two results are worth knowing. Because every column takes the half spans beside it, every beam at a level carries the same shear: at the roof of Faded Example 6.2, F = 0.62 kN in all three beams, although their spans are 6, 5 and 8 m. So the interior columns receive equal and opposite beam shears and only the two outer columns carry axial force from the wind. The joint balance also closes by itself at the leeward column: the last beam moment equals the column moments there, a free check of the arithmetic.Dua hasil wajar diketahui. Oleh sebab setiap column menanggung separuh span di sebelahnya, setiap beam pada satu aras membawa shear yang sama: di bumbung Faded Example 6.2, F = 0.62 kN dalam ketiga-tiga beam, walaupun span-nya 6, 5 dan 8 m. Jadi column dalam menerima shear beam yang sama besar dan berlawanan dan hanya dua column luar menanggung axial force daripada angin. Imbangan joint juga tertutup dengan sendirinya di column leeward: moment beam terakhir sama dengan moment column di situ, semakan percuma bagi aritmetik.

Portal or cantilever? On frame ABCD the two methods give column moments within about 10 % (Figure 5): 53.54 against 48.19 kN m in an outer column of the ground storey, 107.08 against 112.44 in an interior one. The beams differ more: the portal method gives every beam of level 1 the same 93.83 kN m, the cantilever method 84.44 in the outer beams and 112.59 in the middle one, 20 % more. Neither is exact. The portal method suits low, wide frames whose columns sway in shear; the cantilever method suits tall, slender frames that bend as a whole. The course notes use the cantilever method for the seven-storey frame (Example 6).Portal atau cantilever? Pada frame ABCD kedua-dua kaedah memberi moment column dalam lingkungan 10 % (Rajah 5): 53.54 berbanding 48.19 kN m dalam column luar tingkat bawah, 107.08 berbanding 112.44 dalam column dalam. Beam berbeza lebih banyak: portal method memberi setiap beam aras 1 nilai yang sama 93.83 kN m, cantilever method 84.44 dalam beam luar dan 112.59 dalam beam tengah, 20 % lebih. Kedua-duanya tidak tepat. Portal method sesuai bagi frame yang rendah dan lebar yang column-nya bergoyang secara shear; cantilever method sesuai bagi frame yang tinggi dan slender yang melentur secara keseluruhan. Nota kursus menggunakan cantilever method bagi frame tujuh tingkat (Contoh 6).

Which method, and how far to trust it. Both approximate methods assume a regular frame: equal storey heights within a storey, columns of equal area (cantilever), points of contraflexure at the middle. They ignore the relative stiffness of the beams and the columns, which decides where the points of contraflexure really are; in the ground storey of a frame with fixed bases the point moves up, and a stiff beam over flexible columns changes the shares. The course uses them to understand the load path and to check a computer analysis by its order of magnitude and its equilibrium; the design values of a real frame come from a stiffness analysis.Kaedah mana, dan sejauh mana boleh dipercayai. Kedua-dua kaedah anggaran menganggap frame yang sekata: ketinggian tingkat yang sama dalam satu tingkat, column dengan luas yang sama (cantilever), point of contraflexure di tengah. Ia mengabaikan relative stiffness beam dan column, yang menentukan di mana point of contraflexure sebenarnya berada; dalam tingkat bawah frame dengan tapak fixed titik itu bergerak ke atas, dan beam yang kaku di atas column yang fleksibel mengubah agihan. Kursus menggunakannya untuk memahami laluan load dan menyemak analisis komputer melalui magnitud dan keseimbangannya; design value bagi frame sebenar datang daripada analisis stiffness.
