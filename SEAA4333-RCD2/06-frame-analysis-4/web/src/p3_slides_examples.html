Answer 1Jawapan 1 Double curvature, if the members are alikeDouble curvature, jika member serupa

With rigid joints and floors moving sideways, each column and beam bends in double curvature; when the beams and columns at both ends of a member are alike, its end moments are nearly equal and opposite, so the zero falls near the middle. It is poorest in the ground storey with fixed bases, where the point moves up from mid-height, and wherever a stiff member meets a flexible one.Dengan joint tegar dan lantai bergerak ke sisi, setiap column dan beam melentur dalam double curvature; apabila beam dan column di kedua-dua hujung member serupa, moment hujungnya hampir sama besar dan berlawanan, jadi sifar jatuh berhampiran tengah. Ia paling lemah dalam tingkat bawah dengan tapak fixed, di mana titik itu bergerak ke atas dari tengah ketinggian, dan di mana-mana member kaku bertemu member fleksibel.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 The shares are half spansBahagiannya separuh span

Each column takes the half spans beside it, so the joint balance gives each beam a moment proportional to its span: Mb = (Vabovehabove + V h)L/(4ΣL). Then F = 2Mb/L is the same for every span, and an interior column receives equal and opposite shears from its two beams: no axial force.Setiap column menanggung separuh span di sebelahnya, jadi imbangan joint memberi setiap beam moment yang berkadar dengan span-nya: Mb = (Vabovehabove + V h)L/(4ΣL). Maka F = 2Mb/L sama bagi setiap span, dan column dalam menerima shear yang sama besar dan berlawanan daripada dua beam-nya: tiada axial force.

Answer 3Jawapan 3 Balanced against antisymmetricSeimbang berbanding antisimetri

Under the vertical load the beams on both sides of B hog almost equally, so the joint is nearly balanced and the columns take only the small difference, 8.72 kN m below B. The wind bends the frame antisymmetrically: every beam end and every column at the joint turns the same way, and the column takes its full share, 112.44 kN m. The hogging was large already (146.91 kN m), so the same wind adds less in proportion.Di bawah load menegak, beam di kedua-dua sisi B hogging hampir sama, jadi joint hampir seimbang dan column hanya menanggung perbezaan kecil, 8.72 kN m di bawah B. Angin melenturkan frame secara antisimetri: setiap hujung beam dan setiap column di joint berputar ke arah yang sama, dan column menanggung bahagian penuhnya, 112.44 kN m. Hogging sudah besar (146.91 kN m), jadi angin yang sama menambah kurang secara perkadaran.

Answer 4Jawapan 4 Sagging at a supportSagging di support

At the end the wind relieves, the beam-end moment is Mw − Mv; when the wind moment is the larger, the end sags: 2.28 kN m at A of frame ABCD by the course method. The section then needs bottom bars carried into the support and anchored there, not stopped short as for a hogging end. On the EN 1990 route the check is 1.0Gk + 1.5Wk, which finds much larger reversals.Di hujung yang dilegakan oleh angin, moment hujung beam ialah Mw − Mv; apabila moment angin lebih besar, hujung itu sagging: 2.28 kN m di A frame ABCD mengikut course method. Keratan itu kemudian memerlukan bar bawah yang dibawa masuk ke support dan dijangkar di situ, bukan dihentikan awal seperti hujung hogging. Pada laluan EN 1990, semakannya ialah 1.0Gk + 1.5Wk, yang mendapat reversal yang jauh lebih besar.
