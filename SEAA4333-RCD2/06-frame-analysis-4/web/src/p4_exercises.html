A1 · 1 markmarkah
Both approximate methods for a frame under lateral load place a point of contraflexureKedua-dua kaedah anggaran bagi frame di bawah beban sisi meletakkan point of contraflexure

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Under sway, every member of a regular frame bends in double curvature, so the moment changes sign near its middle. Putting the zero there makes each storey statically determinate.Di bawah sway, setiap anggota frame yang sekata melentur dalam double curvature, jadi moment bertukar tanda berhampiran tengahnya. Meletakkan sifar di situ menjadikan setiap tingkat statically determinate.

A2 · 1 markmarkah
The cantilever method takes the axial force in each column of a storey (columns of equal area) asCantilever method mengambil axial force dalam setiap column satu tingkat (column dengan luas yang sama) sebagai

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

The frame is treated as one cantilever bending about the centroid of the column areas, so the axial stress, and with equal areas the force, grows linearly with the distance: tension on the windward side, compression on the leeward side.Frame dianggap sebagai satu cantilever yang melentur terhadap centroid luas column, jadi axial stress, dan dengan luas sama daya itu, bertambah secara linear dengan jarak: tegangan di sisi windward, mampatan di sisi leeward.

A3 · 1 markmarkah
In a frame of equal spans, the portal method gives an interior columnDalam frame dengan span yang sama, portal method memberi column dalaman

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Each bay acts as a portal whose two legs share its part of the storey shear equally; an interior column is a leg of two bays. With equal spans: H = V/(2n) outside and V/n inside. Equal shares in every column is the slip of the S6 sheet.Setiap bay bertindak sebagai portal yang dua kakinya berkongsi sama rata bahagian storey shear-nya; column dalaman ialah kaki bagi dua bay. Dengan span sama: H = V/(2n) di luar dan V/n di dalam. Bahagian sama bagi setiap column ialah kesilapan helaian S6.

A4 · 1 markmarkah
By the portal method, which columns of a storey carry an axial force from the wind?Dengan portal method, column manakah dalam satu tingkat membawa axial force daripada angin?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

The portal method gives every beam at a level the same shear, so at an interior joint the beam shears from the left and the right cancel. Only the outer columns, with one beam each, collect axial force. (a) is the cantilever method.Portal method memberi setiap beam pada satu aras shear yang sama, jadi pada joint dalaman shear beam dari kiri dan kanan saling membatalkan. Hanya column luar, dengan satu beam setiap satu, mengumpul axial force. (a) ialah cantilever method.

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A two-bay frame ABC has spans AB = 6 m and BC = 8 m. Its top storey, 3.5 m high, carries the roof force of 20 kN. A student works out the moment in the roof beam AB by the portal method. Which line is wrong?Sebuah frame dua bay ABC mempunyai span AB = 6 m dan BC = 8 m. Tingkat teratasnya, setinggi 3.5 m, membawa daya bumbung 20 kN. Seorang pelajar mengira moment dalam beam bumbung AB dengan portal method. Baris manakah yang salah?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: the point of contraflexure is at mid-height, so the column bends over half its height: Mcol,A = HA × h/2 = 4.286 × 1.75 = 7.50 kN m. M = H h doubles every moment, as on the S6 sheet. Lines 4 and 5 follow from line 3: MAB = 7.50 kN m and FAB = 2.50 kN.Baris 3: point of contraflexure berada di tengah ketinggian, jadi column melentur sepanjang separuh ketinggiannya: Mcol,A = HA × h/2 = 4.286 × 1.75 = 7.50 kN m. M = H h menggandakan setiap moment, seperti dalam helaian S6. Baris 4 dan 5 mengikut baris 3: MAB = 7.50 kN m dan FAB = 2.50 kN.
