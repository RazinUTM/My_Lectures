GlossaryGlosari

What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Terms stay in English in both languages; the explanation follows the language you chose.Cari istilah; taip dalam kotak untuk menapis. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

lateral loadA horizontal action on the frame: the wind (Unit 5) and, by EC2, the equivalent forces of the geometric imperfections.Horizontal action pada frame: angin (Unit 5) dan, mengikut EC2, daya setara geometric imperfection.
swayThe sideways movement of the floors of an unbraced frame under lateral load; each storey drifts relative to the one below.Pergerakan ke sisi lantai unbraced frame di bawah lateral load; setiap tingkat beranjak relatif kepada tingkat di bawahnya.
double curvatureA member bent by end moments that turn the same way, as a column of a swaying storey: the moment changes sign between the ends.Member yang dilenturkan oleh moment hujung yang berputar ke arah yang sama, seperti column tingkat yang bergoyang: moment bertukar tanda di antara hujung.
point of contraflexureWhere the bending moment changes sign. Both approximate methods put one at the mid-height of every column and the mid-span of every beam.Titik di mana bending moment bertukar tanda. Kedua-dua kaedah anggaran meletakkan satu di tengah ketinggian setiap column dan tengah span setiap beam.
storey shear VThe sum of the lateral forces above a cut through a storey; the column shears of the storey add up to it.Jumlah daya sisi di atas section melalui satu tingkat; shear column tingkat itu berjumlah kepadanya.
overturning moment MoThe moment of the lateral forces above the cut, about the cut at the mid-height of the storey: ΣF(z − zcut).Moment daya sisi di atas section, terhadap section di tengah ketinggian tingkat: ΣF(z − zcut).
cantilever methodThe frame bends as one cantilever about the centroid of its columns: column axial forces in proportion to their distance from the centroid (equal areas). Suits tall, slender frames.Frame melentur sebagai satu cantilever terhadap centroid column-nya: axial force column berkadar dengan jaraknya dari centroid (luas sama). Sesuai bagi frame tinggi dan slender.
portal methodEach bay acts as a portal: the storey shear is shared by the bays in proportion to their spans, half to each leg. Suits low, wide frames.Setiap bay bertindak sebagai portal: storey shear diagihkan kepada bay mengikut span-nya, separuh kepada setiap kaki. Sesuai bagi frame rendah dan lebar.
centroid xΣxiAi/ΣAi of the columns of a storey; with equal areas, the average column position.ΣxiAi/ΣAi bagi column satu tingkat; dengan luas sama, purata kedudukan column.
ri, Pri = di/dmin, the column’s distance from the centroid over the smallest one; P = Mo/Σ|r||d|, the axial force at dmin; Ni = riP.ri = di/dmin, jarak column dari centroid dibahagi jarak terkecil; P = Mo/Σ|r||d|, axial force pada dmin; Ni = riP.
column shear HThe horizontal force in a column at its point of contraflexure; Mcol = H h/2 at each end.Daya mendatar dalam column pada point of contraflexure-nya; Mcol = H h/2 di setiap hujung.
beam shear FThe vertical force in a beam at its mid-span: the change of axial force to the left (cantilever) or 2Mb/L (portal); Mbeam = F L/2.Daya menegak dalam beam di tengah span: perubahan axial force di sebelah kiri (cantilever) atau 2Mb/L (portal); Mbeam = F L/2.
kiThe portal share of column i, (Lleft + Lright)/(2ΣL); Hi = kiV.Bahagian portal column i, (Lleft + Lright)/(2ΣL); Hi = kiV.
joint balanceAt a joint the beam moments balance the column moments: Mb,right = Mc,above + Mc,below − Mb,left, joint by joint from the windward side.Pada joint, moment beam mengimbangi moment column: Mb,right = Mc,above + Mc,below − Mb,left, joint demi joint dari sisi windward.
BS 8110 load combination 3Dead, imposed and wind together at 1.2(Gk + Qk + Wk): the course method, with every span loaded.Dead, imposed dan angin bersama pada 1.2(Gk + Qk + Wk): course method, dengan setiap span dibebani.
moment reversalA beam end whose hogging moment the wind turns into sagging, when Mw > Mv; it needs bottom steel at the support, anchored into the column.Hujung beam yang moment hogging-nya ditukar oleh angin menjadi sagging, apabila Mw > Mv; ia memerlukan bottom steel di support, di-anchor ke dalam column.
EN 1990 Eq. 6.10The fundamental combination: one leading variable action at 1.5, the others at 1.5ψ0; G at 1.35, or 1.0 where it helps.Kombinasi asas: satu variable action utama pada 1.5, yang lain pada 1.5ψ0; G pada 1.35, atau 1.0 apabila ia membantu.
ψ0The combination factor of an accompanying variable action; the app takes 0.7 for the imposed load and 0.5 for the wind.Combination factor bagi variable action pengiring; aplikasi mengambil 0.7 bagi imposed load dan 0.5 bagi angin.
stiffness analysisA full analysis of the frame (the stiffness method in a computer program), which finds the points of contraflexure from the member stiffnesses; the approximate methods check its order of magnitude.Analisis penuh frame (stiffness method dalam program komputer), yang mencari point of contraflexure daripada member stiffness; kaedah anggaran menyemak magnitud keputusannya.
Professional Habit 6Check every storey’s equilibrium before using its moments: ΣH = V and, for the cantilever method, ΣNixi about the centroid = Mo.Semak keseimbangan setiap tingkat sebelum menggunakan moment-nya: ΣH = V dan, bagi cantilever method, ΣNixi terhadap centroid = Mo.

Short notesNota ringkas

§1 The frame under lateral loadFrame di bawah lateral load

  • Under sway the columns and beams bend in double curvature: points of contraflexure at mid-height and mid-span (assumed).Di bawah sway, column dan beam melentur dalam double curvature: point of contraflexure di tengah ketinggian dan tengah span (diandaikan).
  • Cut each storey at mid-height: the forces above give V and Mo.Potong setiap tingkat di tengah ketinggian: daya di atas memberi V dan Mo.

§2 The cantilever methodCantilever method

  • Tension on the windward side. Beam shear at mid-span: the change of axial force to its left.Tension di sisi windward. Shear beam di tengah span: perubahan axial force di sebelah kirinya.
  • Column shears from moments about each beam cut; the last column from ΣH = V.Shear column daripada moment terhadap setiap beam section; column terakhir daripada ΣH = V.

§3 Storey by storeyTingkat demi tingkat

  • Below the top storey use ΔN = N − Nabove, and include the column shears of the storey above in the moments about the beam cut.Di bawah tingkat teratas gunakan ΔN = N − Nabove, dan masukkan shear column tingkat di atas dalam moment terhadap beam section.
  • Mcol = H h/2; Mbeam = F L/2. Check ΣH = V (Professional Habit 6).Mcol = H h/2; Mbeam = F L/2. Semak ΣH = V (Professional Habit 6).

§4 The portal methodPortal method

  • Every beam at a level carries the same shear, so only the outer columns carry axial force.Setiap beam pada satu aras membawa shear yang sama, jadi hanya column luar membawa axial force.

§5 Wind plus vertical loadAngin dan load menegak

  • At level n: the beams take the Mbeam of the storey below the level, the upper columns the Mcol of the storey above, the lower columns that of the storey below.Pada aras n: beam mengambil Mbeam tingkat di bawah aras, column atas Mcol tingkat di atas, column bawah Mcol tingkat di bawah.
  • Span: the largest Mv(x) ± Mw(1 − 2x/L).Span: nilai terbesar Mv(x) ± Mw(1 − 2x/L).

§6 EN 1990 and which methodEN 1990 dan kaedah mana

  • On the EN 1990 route add the imperfections Hi = θiNi. Portal for low, wide frames; cantilever for tall, slender ones; both check a stiffness analysis.Dalam laluan EN 1990, tambah imperfection Hi = θiNi. Portal bagi frame rendah dan lebar; cantilever bagi frame tinggi dan slender; kedua-duanya menyemak stiffness analysis.
