    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    A column carries the building down to its foundation. This Unit answers the first question of every column design: is the column short or slender? You find its effective length from the beams at its ends, its slenderness ratio about each axis, and the EC2 limit, which depends on how hard the column is pushed and on the shape of its moment diagram. Unit 8 then designs short columns for axial load and moment, and Unit 9 adds the second-order moment of slender and biaxially bent columns.Column membawa bangunan turun ke foundation. Unit ini menjawab soalan pertama dalam setiap design column: adakah column itu short atau slender? Anda mencari effective length-nya daripada beam di hujungnya, slenderness ratio-nya pada setiap paksi, dan had EC2, yang bergantung pada betapa kuat column itu ditekan dan pada bentuk moment diagram-nya. Unit 8 kemudian membuat design column pendek untuk axial load dan moment, dan Unit 9 menambah second-order moment bagi column slender dan column yang melentur biaxial.

    1. Columns in a frameColumn dalam frame

    ≈ 6 min

    A column carries the floors above it down to the foundation. Its axial load grows storey by storey from the roof down, and it also takes moments: from its continuity with the beams when the spans or the loads on either side differ, from sway when wind pushes on a frame without bracing, and from the small eccentricity that EC2 assumes for every compression member. Every column design starts with one question: is the column short (non-slender) or slender? The answer decides whether the design for N and M in Unit 8 is enough, or whether the second-order moment of Unit 9 must be added.Column membawa lantai di atasnya turun ke foundation. Setiap design column bermula dengan satu soalan: adakah column itu short (non-slender) atau slender? Jawapannya menentukan sama ada design untuk N dan M dalam Unit 8 sudah mencukupi, atau second-order moment dalam Unit 9 perlu ditambah.

    2. Braced or unbraced, short or slenderBraced atau unbraced, short atau slender

    ≈ 6 min

    A column is braced when walls, a lift core or bracing carry all the horizontal load of the building, so its ends cannot move sideways relative to each other. It is unbraced when the frame itself resists the horizontal load by bending of its columns and beams: the column sways. The project frame you analysed for wind in Units 5 and 6 has no walls or cores, so its columns are unbraced.Column itu braced apabila wall, lift core atau bracing membawa semua horizontal load bangunan, jadi hujung-hujungnya tidak boleh bergerak ke sisi relatif antara satu sama lain. Column itu unbraced apabila frame itu sendiri menahan horizontal load melalui lenturan column dan beam-nya: column itu sway. Frame projek yang anda analisis untuk angin dalam Unit 5 dan 6 tidak mempunyai wall atau core, jadi column-nya unbraced.

    Braced and bracing members (EC2 5.8.1)Braced dan bracing member (EC2 5.8.1)Bracing members (walls, cores) are assumed to contribute to the overall horizontal stability of the structure. Braced members are assumed not to contribute to it, so they are designed without sway.Bracing member (wall, core) diandaikan menyumbang kepada kestabilan mendatar keseluruhan struktur. Braced member diandaikan tidak menyumbang kepadanya, jadi ia di-design tanpa sway.

    Any column deflects a little under load, and the axial load acting on that deflection adds a moment: the second-order effect. EC2 5.8.2(6) lets you ignore it when it is less than 10% of the first-order effects, and 5.8.3.1 turns that into a simple test: compare the slenderness λ with a limit λlim (§5 and §6). Below the limit the column is non-slender (short); above it the column is slender.Setiap column melentur sedikit di bawah load, dan axial load yang bertindak pada deflection itu menambah moment: second-order effect. EC2 5.8.2(6) membenarkan anda mengabaikannya jika ia kurang daripada 10% first-order effect, dan 5.8.3.1 menukarkannya kepada ujian mudah: bandingkan slenderness λ dengan had λlim (§5 dan §6). Di bawah had itu column ialah non-slender (pendek); di atasnya column ialah slender.

    3. Effective lengthEffective length

    ≈ 7 min

    Press the ends of a long ruler together and it bows sideways at a small force; a ruler half as long needs about four times the force. Euler’s buckling load explains why: it falls with the square of the length.Tekan kedua-dua hujung sebatang pembaris panjang dan ia melengkung ke sisi pada daya kecil; pembaris separuh panjang memerlukan lebih kurang empat kali daya itu.     (7.1)
    Effective length l0 (EC2 5.8.1)Effective length l0 (EC2 5.8.1)The length of a pin-ended column with the same section and the same buckling load as the real one.     • Beams give partial restraint: neither fixed nor pinned. §4 measures how much.Beam memberi restraint separa: bukan fixed dan bukan pinned. §4 mengukur sebanyak mana.

    Answer 1Jawapan 1 Stiff beams hold the endsBeam yang kaku memegang hujung

    Deep beams are stiff, so they resist the rotation of the column ends: k = (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam is small. The buckled shape then curves back near each end and the points of contraflexure move into the column, which shortens the effective length: l0 tends to 0.5l, and to 0.59l at the floor k = 0.1. Shallow beams let the ends rotate, k grows, and l0 tends to l (braced).Beam yang dalam adalah kaku, jadi ia menahan putaran hujung column: k = (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam kecil.     Two effects act together. Its ends can move sideways, so l0 ≥ l, at least 1.22l with k ≥ 0.1 (Eq. 7.3), which makes λ large: a 300 mm column with l = 3.0 m has λ ≥ 1.22 × 3000/86.6 = 42.     Answer 3Jawapan 3 An S-shape bulges lessBentuk S kurang membonjol

    In double curvature the end moments bend the column into an S-shape with a point of contraflexure in its height, so the sideways deflection stays small and N times that deflection adds little to the moment at the critical end. In single curvature the column bows to one side, with the largest deflection at mid-height, where the first-order moment is also large. Dalam single curvature column melengkung ke satu sisi, dengan deflection terbesar di tengah ketinggian, tempat first-order moment juga besar. EC2 menggambarkan perkara ini melalui rm: negatif dalam double curvature, jadi C = 1.7 − rm > 1.7 dan λlim lebih tinggi.

    Answer 4Jawapan 4 Imperfections bend it in single curvatureImperfection melenturkannya dalam single curvature

    No column is perfectly straight or perfectly loaded: EC2 assumes an imperfection ei = l0/400, which bows the column to one side, so its only first-order moment is in single curvature. Oleh itu EC2 5.8.3.1(1) mengambil rm = 1 dan C = 0.7. Menggunakan C = 1.7 akan menaikkan λlim sebanyak 2.4 kali dan boleh meluluskan column slender sebagai column pendek.

    unbraced member · swayA member of a frame that resists the horizontal load by bending, so its ends move sideways relative to each other (sway).Member dalam frame yang menahan horizontal load melalui lenturan, jadi hujung-hujungnya bergerak ke sisi relatif antara satu sama lain (sway).
    non-slender (short) columnA column with λ ≤ λlim about the axis considered: second-order effects may be ignored, and it fails by crushing of the section.Column dengan λ ≤ λlim pada paksi yang dipertimbangkan: second-order effect boleh diabaikan, dan ia gagal apabila section-nya hancur.
    slender columnA column with λ > λlim: the second-order moment must be added to the design moment (Unit 9).Column dengan λ > λlim: second-order moment mesti ditambah kepada design moment (Unit 9).
    first-order effectsAction effects calculated without the deformation of the structure, but including geometric imperfections (EC2 5.8.1).Kesan tindakan yang dikira tanpa mengambil kira ubah bentuk struktur, tetapi termasuk geometric imperfection (EC2 5.8.1).
    second-order effects · P–ΔAdditional effects caused by the deformation of the structure: the axial load acting on the lateral deflection adds moment.Kesan tambahan akibat ubah bentuk struktur: axial load yang bertindak pada lateral deflection menambah moment.
    buckling · Euler load NcrFailure by instability of a member under axial compression. For an elastic pin-ended member Ncr = π²EI/l0².Kegagalan akibat ketidakstabilan member di bawah mampatan paksi. Bagi member elastik pin-ended Ncr = π²EI/l0².
    effective length l0The length of a pin-ended column with the same section and the same buckling load as the real one; also called the buckling length.Panjang column pin-ended dengan section dan buckling load yang sama seperti column sebenar; juga dipanggil buckling length.
    clear height lThe height of the column between its end restraints. In this course: the storey height minus the depth of the beams in the plane considered.Ketinggian column di antara end restraint-nya. Dalam kursus ini: ketinggian tingkat tolak kedalaman beam dalam satah yang dipertimbangkan.
    point of contraflexureA point of zero moment where the curvature changes sign. The effective length is the distance between two such points on the buckled shape.Titik moment sifar tempat curvature bertukar tanda. Effective length ialah jarak antara dua titik sedemikian pada bentuk buckled.
    end restraintThe resistance to rotation at a column end given by the beams or the foundation.Rintangan terhadap putaran di hujung column yang diberi oleh beam atau foundation.
    relative flexibility kk = (θ/M)(EI/l) at a column end; in this course (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam, never below 0.1. Small k: stiff restraint.k = (θ/M)(EI/l) di hujung column; dalam kursus ini (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam, tidak pernah di bawah 0.1. k kecil: restraint yang kaku.
    stiffness K = I/lThe relative stiffness of a member, used in k and in moment distribution; E cancels when all members have the same concrete.Stiffness relatif bagi member, digunakan dalam k dan dalam moment distribution; E terbatal apabila semua member menggunakan konkrit yang sama.
    fixed base · pinned endA base fixed into a footing designed for the moment takes k1 = 0.1 (the EC2 floor); a pinned end takes k = ∞.Base yang fixed ke dalam footing yang di-design untuk moment mengambil k1 = 0.1 (nilai minimum EC2); hujung pinned mengambil k = ∞.
    radius of gyration ii = √(I/A) of the uncracked concrete section about the axis of bending; h/√12 = 0.289h for a rectangle, with h in the plane of bending.i = √(I/A) bagi section konkrit tidak retak pada paksi lenturan; h/√12 = 0.289h bagi segi empat tepat, dengan h dalam satah lenturan.
    slenderness ratio λλ = l0/i, about each axis (EC2 Eq. 5.14).λ = l0/i, pada setiap paksi (EC2 Eq. 5.14).
    slenderness limit λlimλlim = 20ABC/√n (EC2 Eq. 5.13N); below it, second-order effects may be ignored.λlim = 20ABC/√n (EC2 Eq. 5.13N); di bawahnya, second-order effect boleh diabaikan.
    relative axial force nn = NEd/(Acfcd): how hard the column is pushed compared with its concrete strength.n = NEd/(Acfcd): betapa kuat column ditekan berbanding kekuatan konkritnya.
    moment ratio rmrm = M01/M02 with |M02| ≥ |M01|; negative in double curvature; taken as 1 for unbraced columns and when imperfections dominate.rm = M01/M02 dengan |M02| ≥ |M01|; negatif dalam double curvature; diambil sebagai 1 bagi column unbraced dan apabila imperfection mendominasi.
    single curvature · double curvatureSingle: the end moments give tension on the same face and the column bows to one side. Double: tension on opposite faces, an S-shape with a point of contraflexure.Single: end moment memberi tegangan pada muka yang sama dan column melengkung ke satu sisi. Double: tegangan pada muka bertentangan, bentuk S dengan point of contraflexure.
    geometric imperfection eiAn assumed eccentricity for out-of-straightness and misplaced loads; ei = l0/400 for isolated braced columns (EC2 5.2(9)).Eccentricity yang diandaikan bagi ketidaklurusan dan load yang tersasar; ei = l0/400 bagi column braced terpencil (EC2 5.2(9)).
    minimum eccentricity e0e0 = max(h/30, 20 mm): every column is designed for at least NEde0 (EC2 6.1(4), Unit 8).e0 = max(h/30, 20 mm): setiap column di-design untuk sekurang-kurangnya NEde0 (EC2 6.1(4), Unit 8).
    effective creep ratio φefCreep under the quasi-permanent load, scaled by the moment ratio; it enters A = 1/(1 + 0.2φef), taken as 0.7 when unknown.Creep di bawah quasi-permanent load, didarab dengan nisbah moment; ia masuk ke dalam A = 1/(1 + 0.2φef), diambil sebagai 0.7 jika tidak diketahui.
    mechanical reinforcement ratio ωω = Asfyd/(Acfcd); it enters B = √(1 + 2ω), taken as 1.1 when unknown.ω = Asfyd/(Acfcd); ia masuk ke dalam B = √(1 + 2ω), diambil sebagai 1.1 jika tidak diketahui.
    major axis · minor axisThe stiffer and the weaker axes of a section; in the course notes z (Iz = bh³/12) and y (Iy = hb³/12).Paksi yang lebih kaku dan yang lebih lemah bagi section; dalam nota kursus z (Iz = bh³/12) dan y (Iy = hb³/12).
    nominal curvature methodThe EC2 5.8.8 method that adds a nominal second-order moment M2 = NEde2 to a slender column (Unit 9).Kaedah EC2 5.8.8 yang menambah nominal second-order moment M2 = NEde2 kepada column slender (Unit 9).
