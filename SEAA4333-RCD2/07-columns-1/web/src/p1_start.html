What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

A column carries the building down to its foundation. This Unit answers the first question of every column design: is the column short or slender? You find its effective length from the beams at its ends, its slenderness ratio about each axis, and the EC2 limit, which depends on how hard the column is pushed and on the shape of its moment diagram. Unit 8 then designs short columns for axial load and moment, and Unit 9 adds the second-order moment of slender and biaxially bent columns.Column membawa bangunan turun ke foundation. Unit ini menjawab soalan pertama dalam setiap design column: adakah column itu short atau slender? Anda mencari effective length-nya daripada beam di hujungnya, slenderness ratio-nya pada setiap paksi, dan had EC2, yang bergantung pada betapa kuat column itu ditekan dan pada bentuk moment diagram-nya. Unit 8 kemudian membuat design column pendek untuk axial load dan moment, dan Unit 9 menambah second-order moment bagi column slender dan column yang melentur biaxial.

  2. find the clear height and the relative flexibilities k1 and k2 at the ends of a column, and its effective length l0 by EC2 Eq. 5.15 or 5.16;mencari clear height dan relative flexibility k1 dan k2 di hujung column, dan effective length l0-nya dengan EC2 Eq. 5.15 atau 5.16;
  3. calculate the slenderness ratio λ about each axis and the limit λlim, with the right n and rm;mengira slenderness ratio λ pada setiap paksi dan had λlim, dengan n dan rm yang betul;
  4. classify a column as non-slender or slender about each axis, and find the slips in someone else’s check.mengklasifikasikan column sebagai non-slender atau slender pada setiap paksi, dan mencari kesilapan dalam semakan orang lain.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

The second moment of area of a 300 × 500 mm section about its minor axis isSecond moment of area bagi section 300 × 500 mm pada minor axis-nya ialah

About the minor axis the 300 mm side is the depth: I = 500 × 300³/12 = 1.125 × 10⁹ mm⁴. 3.125 × 10⁹ is the major-axis value, 300 × 500³/12. In this Unit each axis has its own I, and so its own λ.Pada minor axis, sisi 300 mm ialah kedalaman: I = 500 × 300³/12 = 1.125 × 10⁹ mm⁴. 3.125 × 10⁹ ialah nilai major axis, 300 × 500³/12. Dalam Unit ini setiap paksi mempunyai I sendiri, dan oleh itu λ sendiri.

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

The design compressive strength fcd of C30/37 concrete, with αcc = 0.85 and γc = 1.5, isDesign compressive strength fcd bagi konkrit C30/37, dengan αcc = 0.85 dan γc = 1.5, ialah

fcd = αccfck/γc = 0.85 × 30/1.5 = 17.0 N/mm² (0.567fck). It enters the relative axial force n = NEd/(Acfcd), which sets the slenderness limit.fcd = αccfck/γc = 0.85 × 30/1.5 = 17.0 N/mm² (0.567fck). Ia masuk ke dalam relative axial force n = NEd/(Acfcd), yang menetapkan slenderness limit.

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A pin-ended strut is cut to half its length. Its Euler buckling loadSatu strut pin-ended dipotong kepada separuh panjangnya. Euler buckling load-nya

Ncr = π²EI/l2, so half the length gives four times the load. The same law makes a column between stiff beams (a shorter effective length) far stronger against buckling than one that can sway.Ncr = π²EI/l2, jadi separuh panjang memberi empat kali load. Hukum yang sama menjadikan column di antara beam yang kaku (effective length yang lebih pendek) jauh lebih kuat terhadap buckling berbanding column yang boleh sway.
