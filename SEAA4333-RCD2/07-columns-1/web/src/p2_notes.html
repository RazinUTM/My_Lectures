1. Columns in a frameColumn dalam frame

≈ 6 min

A column carries the floors above it down to the foundation. Its axial load grows storey by storey from the roof down, and it also takes moments: from its continuity with the beams when the spans or the loads on either side differ, from sway when wind pushes on a frame without bracing, and from the small eccentricity that EC2 assumes for every compression member. Every column design starts with one question: is the column short (non-slender) or slender? The answer decides whether the design for N and M in Unit 8 is enough, or whether the second-order moment of Unit 9 must be added.Column membawa lantai di atasnya turun ke foundation. Axial load-nya bertambah setingkat demi setingkat dari bumbung ke bawah, dan ia juga menerima moment: daripada continuity dengan beam apabila span atau load pada kedua-dua belah berbeza, daripada sway apabila angin menolak frame tanpa bracing, dan daripada eccentricity kecil yang diandaikan oleh EC2 bagi setiap compression member. Setiap design column bermula dengan satu soalan: adakah column itu short (non-slender) atau slender? Jawapannya menentukan sama ada design untuk N dan M dalam Unit 8 sudah mencukupi, atau second-order moment dalam Unit 9 perlu ditambah.

Column (EC2 5.3.1(7))Column (EC2 5.3.1(7))A member whose section depth does not exceed 4 times its width (h ≤ 4b) and whose height is at least 3 times its section depth. A longer, thinner section is designed as a wall.Member yang section depth-nya tidak melebihi 4 kali lebarnya (h ≤ 4b) dan ketinggiannya sekurang-kurangnya 3 kali section depth. Section yang lebih panjang dan nipis di-design sebagai wall.

The design of a column follows nine steps (course notes). This Unit is step 6; Unit 8 covers steps 7 to 9 for a short column, and Unit 9 adds the second-order moment for a slender one.Design column mengikut sembilan langkah (nota kursus). Unit ini ialah langkah 6; Unit 8 meliputi langkah 7 hingga 9 bagi column pendek, dan Unit 9 menambah second-order moment bagi column slender.

2. Braced or unbraced, short or slenderBraced atau unbraced, short atau slender

≈ 6 min

A column is braced when walls, a lift core or bracing carry all the horizontal load of the building, so its ends cannot move sideways relative to each other. It is unbraced when the frame itself resists the horizontal load by bending of its columns and beams: the column sways. The project frame you analysed for wind in Units 5 and 6 has no walls or cores, so its columns are unbraced.Column itu braced apabila wall, lift core atau bracing membawa semua horizontal load bangunan, jadi hujung-hujungnya tidak boleh bergerak ke sisi relatif antara satu sama lain. Column itu unbraced apabila frame itu sendiri menahan horizontal load melalui lenturan column dan beam-nya: column itu sway. Frame projek yang anda analisis untuk angin dalam Unit 5 dan 6 tidak mempunyai wall atau core, jadi column-nya unbraced.

Braced and bracing members (EC2 5.8.1)Braced dan bracing member (EC2 5.8.1)Bracing members (walls, cores) are assumed to contribute to the overall horizontal stability of the structure. Braced members are assumed not to contribute to it, so they are designed without sway.Bracing member (wall, core) diandaikan menyumbang kepada kestabilan mendatar keseluruhan struktur. Braced member diandaikan tidak menyumbang kepadanya, jadi ia di-design tanpa sway.

Any column deflects a little under load, and the axial load acting on that deflection adds a moment: the second-order effect. EC2 5.8.2(6) lets you ignore it when it is less than 10% of the first-order effects, and 5.8.3.1 turns that into a simple test: compare the slenderness λ with a limit λlim (§5 and §6). Below the limit the column is non-slender (short); above it the column is slender.Setiap column melentur sedikit di bawah load, dan axial load yang bertindak pada deflection itu menambah moment: second-order effect. EC2 5.8.2(6) membenarkan anda mengabaikannya jika ia kurang daripada 10% first-order effect, dan 5.8.3.1 menukarkannya kepada ujian mudah: bandingkan slenderness λ dengan had λlim (§5 dan §6). Di bawah had itu column ialah non-slender (pendek); di atasnya column ialah slender.

3. Effective lengthEffective length

≈ 7 min

Press the ends of a long ruler together and it bows sideways at a small force; a ruler half as long needs about four times the force. Euler’s buckling load explains why: it falls with the square of the length.Tekan kedua-dua hujung sebatang pembaris panjang dan ia melengkung ke sisi pada daya kecil; pembaris separuh panjang memerlukan lebih kurang empat kali daya itu. Euler buckling load menerangkan sebabnya: ia berkurang dengan kuasa dua panjang.

(7.1)
Effective length l0 (EC2 5.8.1)Effective length l0 (EC2 5.8.1)The length of a pin-ended column with the same section and the same buckling load as the real one. On the buckled shape it is the distance between two points of zero moment (points of contraflexure).Panjang column yang pin di kedua-dua hujung dengan section dan buckling load yang sama seperti column sebenar. Pada bentuk buckled, ia ialah jarak antara dua titik moment sifar (point of contraflexure).
  • l is the clear height between the end restraints: in this course, the storey height minus the depth of the beams framing in the plane considered (the deeper one where two differ).l ialah clear height di antara end restraint: dalam kursus ini, ketinggian tingkat tolak kedalaman beam dalam satah yang dipertimbangkan (yang lebih dalam jika dua berbeza).
  • Fixing an end against rotation shortens l0: the buckled shape curves back, and the points of contraflexure move inside the column.Mengikat hujung daripada berputar memendekkan l0: bentuk buckled melengkung balik, dan point of contraflexure bergerak ke dalam column.
  • Letting an end sway lengthens l0: a column fixed at the base and free at the top buckles like half of a pin-ended column twice as long.Membenarkan hujung sway memanjangkan l0: column yang fixed di dasar dan bebas di atas mengalami buckling seperti separuh column pin-ended yang dua kali lebih panjang.
  • Beams give partial restraint: neither fixed nor pinned. §4 measures how much.Beam memberi restraint separa: bukan fixed dan bukan pinned. §4 mengukur sebanyak mana.
Key pointsPerkara utama
  • Ncr = π²EI/l0²: half the effective length, four times the buckling load.Ncr = π²EI/l0²: separuh effective length, empat kali buckling load.
  • Ideal cases: l, 2l, 0.7l, 0.5l, and l for fixed ends that sway.Kes unggul: l, 2l, 0.7l, 0.5l, dan l bagi hujung fixed yang sway.
  • l is the clear height; braced 0.5l ≤ l0 ≤ l, unbraced l0 ≥ l.l ialah clear height; braced 0.5l ≤ l0 ≤ l, unbraced l0 ≥ l.
Quick checkSemak cepat

Four columns have the same section and the same clear height l. Which has the lowest buckling load?Empat column mempunyai section dan clear height l yang sama. Yang manakah mempunyai buckling load paling rendah?

The free top lets it sway: l0 = 2l, the longest of the four, so Ncr is a quarter of the pin-ended value and one sixteenth of the value for fixed ends (l0 = 0.5l).Hujung atas yang bebas membenarkannya sway: l0 = 2l, yang paling panjang antara keempat-empat, jadi Ncr ialah satu perempat nilai pin-ended dan satu per enam belas nilai bagi hujung fixed (l0 = 0.5l).

Quick checkSemak cepat

A braced column has end moments of 60 kN m at the top and 35 kN m at the bottom, giving tension on opposite faces. NEdl0/400 = 9.6 kN m. What is C?Satu column braced mempunyai end moment 60 kN m di atas dan 35 kN m di bawah, yang memberi tegangan pada muka bertentangan. NEdl0/400 = 9.6 kN m. Berapakah C?

Double curvature, so rm = −35/60 = −0.583 and C = 1.7 + 0.583 = 2.28. Taking rm positive gives 1.12; 0.7 applies only when unbraced or when the end moments do not exceed the imperfection moment (60 > 9.6 here).Double curvature, jadi rm = −35/60 = −0.583 dan C = 1.7 + 0.583 = 2.28.
