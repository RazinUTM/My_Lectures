Example 3 · Explain-type questionsContoh 3 · Soalan jenis terangkan

Exam typeJenis soalan short written answersjawapan bertulis pendekTimeMasa ≈ 3 min eachsetiap satu
QuestionsSoalan
  1. Explain why a column between deep beams has a shorter effective length than the same column between shallow beams.Terangkan mengapa column di antara beam yang dalam mempunyai effective length yang lebih pendek berbanding column yang sama di antara beam yang cetek.
  2. Explain why an unbraced column is almost always slender by EC2.Terangkan mengapa column unbraced hampir selalu slender mengikut EC2.
  3. A braced column bends in double curvature. Why is its slenderness limit higher than in single curvature?Satu column braced melentur dalam double curvature. Mengapa slenderness limit-nya lebih tinggi berbanding dalam single curvature?
  4. The analysis gives no end moments for an interior braced column. Why may you not take rm = 0 and C = 1.7?Analisis tidak memberi sebarang end moment bagi satu column braced dalaman. Mengapa anda tidak boleh mengambil rm = 0 dan C = 1.7?

Write your four answers first, then open the model answers one by one.Tulis empat jawapan anda dahulu, kemudian buka jawapan model satu demi satu.

Answer 1Jawapan 1 Stiff beams hold the endsBeam yang kaku memegang hujung

Deep beams are stiff, so they resist the rotation of the column ends: k = (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam is small. The buckled shape then curves back near each end and the points of contraflexure move into the column, which shortens the effective length: l0 tends to 0.5l, and to 0.59l at the floor k = 0.1. Shallow beams let the ends rotate, k grows, and l0 tends to l (braced).Beam yang dalam adalah kaku, jadi ia menahan putaran hujung column: k = (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam kecil. Bentuk buckled kemudian melengkung balik berhampiran setiap hujung dan point of contraflexure bergerak ke dalam column, yang memendekkan effective length: l0 menghampiri 0.5l, dan 0.59l pada nilai minimum k = 0.1. Beam yang cetek membenarkan hujung berputar, k bertambah, dan l0 menghampiri l (braced).

Answer 2Jawapan 2 Long l0, low limitl0 panjang, had rendah

Two effects act together. Its ends can move sideways, so l0 ≥ l, at least 1.22l with k ≥ 0.1 (Eq. 7.3), which makes λ large: a 300 mm column with l = 3.0 m has λ ≥ 1.22 × 3000/86.6 = 42. And EC2 takes rm = 1, C = 0.7, for unbraced members whatever the end moments, so λlim = 10.8/√n, only 11 to 15 for n between 1.0 and 0.5.Dua kesan bertindak bersama. Hujungnya boleh bergerak ke sisi, jadi l0 ≥ l, sekurang-kurangnya 1.22l dengan k ≥ 0.1 (Eq. 7.3), yang menjadikan λ besar: column 300 mm dengan l = 3.0 m mempunyai λ ≥ 1.22 × 3000/86.6 = 42. Dan EC2 mengambil rm = 1, C = 0.7, bagi member unbraced walau apa pun end moment-nya, jadi λlim = 10.8/√n, hanya 11 hingga 15 bagi n antara 1.0 dan 0.5.

Answer 3Jawapan 3 An S-shape bulges lessBentuk S kurang membonjol

In double curvature the end moments bend the column into an S-shape with a point of contraflexure in its height, so the sideways deflection stays small and N times that deflection adds little to the moment at the critical end. In single curvature the column bows to one side, with the largest deflection at mid-height, where the first-order moment is also large. EC2 reflects this through rm: negative in double curvature, so C = 1.7 − rm > 1.7 and λlim is higher.Dalam double curvature end moment melenturkan column menjadi bentuk S dengan point of contraflexure pada ketinggiannya, jadi deflection ke sisi kekal kecil dan N darab deflection itu hanya menambah sedikit kepada moment di hujung kritikal. Dalam single curvature column melengkung ke satu sisi, dengan deflection terbesar di tengah ketinggian, tempat first-order moment juga besar. EC2 menggambarkan perkara ini melalui rm: negatif dalam double curvature, jadi C = 1.7 − rm > 1.7 dan λlim lebih tinggi.

Answer 4Jawapan 4 Imperfections bend it in single curvatureImperfection melenturkannya dalam single curvature

No column is perfectly straight or perfectly loaded: EC2 assumes an imperfection ei = l0/400, which bows the column to one side, so its only first-order moment is in single curvature. EC2 5.8.3.1(1) therefore takes rm = 1 and C = 0.7. Using C = 1.7 would raise λlim by a factor of 2.4 and could pass a slender column as short.Tiada column yang lurus sempurna atau dibebankan dengan sempurna: EC2 mengandaikan imperfection ei = l0/400, yang melengkungkan column ke satu sisi, jadi satu-satunya first-order moment adalah dalam single curvature. Oleh itu EC2 5.8.3.1(1) mengambil rm = 1 dan C = 0.7. Menggunakan C = 1.7 akan menaikkan λlim sebanyak 2.4 kali dan boleh meluluskan column slender sebagai column pendek.
