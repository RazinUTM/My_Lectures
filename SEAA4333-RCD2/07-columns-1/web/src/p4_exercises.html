A1 · 1 markmarkah
The effective length l0 of a column isEffective length l0 bagi column ialah

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

EC2 5.8.1 defines l0 as a buckling length: the length of a pin-ended column with the same buckling load. It equals the clear height l only when both ends are effectively pinned; restraint from the beams makes it shorter (braced) and sway makes it longer.EC2 5.8.1 mentakrifkan l0 sebagai buckling length: panjang column pin-ended dengan buckling load yang sama. Ia sama dengan clear height l hanya apabila kedua-dua hujung berkesan pinned; restraint daripada beam memendekkannya (braced) dan sway memanjangkannya.

Which column is braced?Column manakah yang braced?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Braced means the bracing members (cores, walls) carry the horizontal load, so the column ends do not sway. Beams and a fixed base restrain rotation, not sway; option D is the unbraced case.Braced bermaksud bracing member (core, wall) membawa horizontal load, jadi hujung column tidak sway. Beam dan fixed base mengekang putaran, bukan sway; pilihan D ialah kes unbraced.

Which one?Satu column tingkat bawah unbraced 300 × 300 mempunyai clear height 3000 mm, k1 = 0.1 (fixed base) dan k2 = 0.25; NEd = 1100 kN; C30/37. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2 uses the braced formula (7.2) for an unbraced column. Eq. (7.3): √(1 + 10 × 0.1 × 0.25/0.35) = 1.309 and (1 + 0.1/1.1)(1 + 0.25/1.25) = 1.309, so l0 = 3928 mm and λ = 45.4. The verdict (slender) survives, but l0 is half the true value, and the second-order moment of Unit 9 grows with l0²: it would come out four times too small.Baris 2 menggunakan formula braced (7.2) bagi column unbraced. Eq. (7.3): √(1 + 10 × 0.1 × 0.25/0.35) = 1.309 dan (1 + 0.1/1.1)(1 + 0.25/1.25) = 1.309, jadi l0 = 3928 mm dan λ = 45.4. Keputusan (slender) kekal, tetapi l0 hanya separuh nilai sebenar, dan second-order moment dalam Unit 9 bertambah dengan l0²: ia akan menjadi empat kali terlalu kecil.
