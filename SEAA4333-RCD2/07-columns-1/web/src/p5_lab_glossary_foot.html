What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

columnA compression member whose section depth is at most 4 times its width and whose height is at least 3 times its depth (EC2 5.3.1(7)); otherwise it is a wall.Compression member yang section depth-nya paling banyak 4 kali lebarnya dan ketinggiannya sekurang-kurangnya 3 kali kedalamannya (EC2 5.3.1(7)); jika tidak, ia ialah wall.
braced memberA member assumed not to contribute to the horizontal stability of the structure: walls or cores carry the horizontal load, so its ends do not sway (EC2 5.8.1).Member yang diandaikan tidak menyumbang kepada kestabilan mendatar struktur: wall atau core membawa horizontal load, jadi hujungnya tidak sway (EC2 5.8.1).
bracing member · bracing systemWalls, lift cores or cross-bracing that carry the horizontal load of the building and keep the frame from swaying.Wall, lift core atau cross-bracing yang membawa horizontal load bangunan dan menghalang frame daripada sway.
unbraced member · swayA member of a frame that resists the horizontal load by bending, so its ends move sideways relative to each other (sway).Member dalam frame yang menahan horizontal load melalui lenturan, jadi hujung-hujungnya bergerak ke sisi relatif antara satu sama lain (sway).
non-slender (short) columnA column with λ ≤ λlim about the axis considered: second-order effects may be ignored, and it fails by crushing of the section.Column dengan λ ≤ λlim pada paksi yang dipertimbangkan: second-order effect boleh diabaikan, dan ia gagal apabila section-nya hancur.
slender columnA column with λ > λlim: the second-order moment must be added to the design moment (Unit 9).Column dengan λ > λlim: second-order moment mesti ditambah kepada design moment (Unit 9).
first-order effectsAction effects calculated without the deformation of the structure, but including geometric imperfections (EC2 5.8.1).Kesan tindakan yang dikira tanpa mengambil kira ubah bentuk struktur, tetapi termasuk geometric imperfection (EC2 5.8.1).
second-order effects · P–ΔAdditional effects caused by the deformation of the structure: the axial load acting on the lateral deflection adds moment.Kesan tambahan akibat ubah bentuk struktur: axial load yang bertindak pada lateral deflection menambah moment.
buckling · Euler load NcrFailure by instability of a member under axial compression. For an elastic pin-ended member Ncr = π²EI/l0².Kegagalan akibat ketidakstabilan member di bawah mampatan paksi. Bagi member elastik pin-ended Ncr = π²EI/l0².
effective length l0The length of a pin-ended column with the same section and the same buckling load as the real one; also called the buckling length.Panjang column pin-ended dengan section dan buckling load yang sama seperti column sebenar; juga dipanggil buckling length.
clear height lThe height of the column between its end restraints. In this course: the storey height minus the depth of the beams in the plane considered.Ketinggian column di antara end restraint-nya. Dalam kursus ini: ketinggian tingkat tolak kedalaman beam dalam satah yang dipertimbangkan.
point of contraflexureA point of zero moment where the curvature changes sign. The effective length is the distance between two such points on the buckled shape.Titik moment sifar tempat curvature bertukar tanda. Effective length ialah jarak antara dua titik sedemikian pada bentuk buckled.
end restraintThe resistance to rotation at a column end given by the beams or the foundation.Rintangan terhadap putaran di hujung column yang diberi oleh beam atau foundation.
relative flexibility kk = (θ/M)(EI/l) at a column end; in this course (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam, never below 0.1. Small k: stiff restraint.k = (θ/M)(EI/l) di hujung column; dalam kursus ini (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam, tidak pernah di bawah 0.1. k kecil: restraint yang kaku.
stiffness K = I/lThe relative stiffness of a member, used in k and in moment distribution; E cancels when all members have the same concrete.Stiffness relatif bagi member, digunakan dalam k dan dalam moment distribution; E terbatal apabila semua member menggunakan konkrit yang sama.
fixed base · pinned endA base fixed into a footing designed for the moment takes k1 = 0.1 (the EC2 floor); a pinned end takes k = ∞.Base yang fixed ke dalam footing yang di-design untuk moment mengambil k1 = 0.1 (nilai minimum EC2); hujung pinned mengambil k = ∞.
radius of gyration ii = √(I/A) of the uncracked concrete section about the axis of bending; h/√12 = 0.289h for a rectangle, with h in the plane of bending.i = √(I/A) bagi section konkrit tidak retak pada paksi lenturan; h/√12 = 0.289h bagi segi empat tepat, dengan h dalam satah lenturan.
slenderness ratio λλ = l0/i, about each axis (EC2 Eq. 5.14).λ = l0/i, pada setiap paksi (EC2 Eq. 5.14).
slenderness limit λlimλlim = 20ABC/√n (EC2 Eq. 5.13N); below it, second-order effects may be ignored.λlim = 20ABC/√n (EC2 Eq. 5.13N); di bawahnya, second-order effect boleh diabaikan.
relative axial force nn = NEd/(Acfcd): how hard the column is pushed compared with its concrete strength.n = NEd/(Acfcd): betapa kuat column ditekan berbanding kekuatan konkritnya.
moment ratio rmrm = M01/M02 with |M02| ≥ |M01|; negative in double curvature; taken as 1 for unbraced columns and when imperfections dominate.rm = M01/M02 dengan |M02| ≥ |M01|; negatif dalam double curvature; diambil sebagai 1 bagi column unbraced dan apabila imperfection mendominasi.
single curvature · double curvatureSingle: the end moments give tension on the same face and the column bows to one side. Double: tension on opposite faces, an S-shape with a point of contraflexure.Single: end moment memberi tegangan pada muka yang sama dan column melengkung ke satu sisi. Double: tegangan pada muka bertentangan, bentuk S dengan point of contraflexure.
geometric imperfection eiAn assumed eccentricity for out-of-straightness and misplaced loads; ei = l0/400 for isolated braced columns (EC2 5.2(9)).Eccentricity yang diandaikan bagi ketidaklurusan dan load yang tersasar; ei = l0/400 bagi column braced terpencil (EC2 5.2(9)).
minimum eccentricity e0e0 = max(h/30, 20 mm): every column is designed for at least NEde0 (EC2 6.1(4), Unit 8).e0 = max(h/30, 20 mm): setiap column di-design untuk sekurang-kurangnya NEde0 (EC2 6.1(4), Unit 8).
effective creep ratio φefCreep under the quasi-permanent load, scaled by the moment ratio; it enters A = 1/(1 + 0.2φef), taken as 0.7 when unknown.Creep di bawah quasi-permanent load, didarab dengan nisbah moment; ia masuk ke dalam A = 1/(1 + 0.2φef), diambil sebagai 0.7 jika tidak diketahui.
mechanical reinforcement ratio ωω = Asfyd/(Acfcd); it enters B = √(1 + 2ω), taken as 1.1 when unknown.ω = Asfyd/(Acfcd); ia masuk ke dalam B = √(1 + 2ω), diambil sebagai 1.1 jika tidak diketahui.
major axis · minor axisThe stiffer and the weaker axes of a section; in the course notes z (Iz = bh³/12) and y (Iy = hb³/12).Paksi yang lebih kaku dan yang lebih lemah bagi section; dalam nota kursus z (Iz = bh³/12) dan y (Iy = hb³/12).
nominal curvature methodThe EC2 5.8.8 method that adds a nominal second-order moment M2 = NEde2 to a slender column (Unit 9).Kaedah EC2 5.8.8 yang menambah nominal second-order moment M2 = NEde2 kepada column slender (Unit 9).

Short notesNota ringkas

§1–§2 Columns and classificationColumn dan klasifikasi

  • Column: h ≤ 4b and height ≥ 3h (EC2 5.3.1(7)); otherwise a wall.Column: h ≤ 4b dan ketinggian ≥ 3h (EC2 5.3.1(7)); jika tidak, wall.
  • Braced: cores or walls carry the horizontal load, no sway. Unbraced: the frame sways.Braced: core atau wall membawa horizontal load, tiada sway. Unbraced: frame sway.
  • λ ≤ λlim: non-slender (Unit 8). λ > λlim: slender, add M2 (Unit 9). Each axis separately.λ ≤ λlim: non-slender (Unit 8). λ > λlim: slender, tambah M2 (Unit 9). Setiap paksi secara berasingan.

§3–§4 Effective length

  • l = storey height − beam depth in the plane; k = (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam ≥ 0.1.l = ketinggian tingkat − kedalaman beam dalam satah; k = (I/l)col/Σ2(I/l)beam ≥ 0.1.
  • Fixed base k1 = 0.1; pinned end k = ∞ (bracket = 2).Fixed base k1 = 0.1; hujung pinned k = ∞ (kurungan = 2).
  • With k = 0.1: braced 0.59l, unbraced 1.22l.Dengan k = 0.1: braced 0.59l, unbraced 1.22l.
