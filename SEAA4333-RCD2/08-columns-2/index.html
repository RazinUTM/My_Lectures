    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 7 told you whether a column is short or slender. This Unit designs the short one. You find the design moment, with the imperfection and the minimum eccentricity that EC2 adds to every column; you see how a section resists axial load and moment together, through strain compatibility and the N–M interaction diagram; you read the bars from a design chart and check them directly; and you finish with the minimum and maximum steel, the cover and the links. On the way you answer the big question of the week: does more axial load raise or lower a column’s moment capacity?     2. The section under N and MSection di bawah N dan M

    ≈ 8 min

    A column section is analysed with the same assumptions as a beam in RCD I (EC2 6.1(2)): plane sections remain plane; a bar has the strain of the concrete around it; concrete in tension is ignored; the concrete stress is a rectangular block of 0.567fck over a depth s = 0.8x (fck ≤ 50 MPa); the steel is elastic with Es = 200 kN/mm² up to fyd = 0.87fyk. Three things are new in a column.Section column dianalisis dengan andaian yang sama seperti beam dalam RCD I (EC2 6.1(2)): plane section kekal satah; bar mempunyai strain yang sama dengan konkrit di sekelilingnya; konkrit dalam tegangan diabaikan; concrete stress ialah rectangular block 0.567fck sedalam s = 0.8x (fck ≤ 50 MPa); steel adalah elastik dengan Es = 200 kN/mm² sehingga fyd = 0.87fyk. Tiga perkara adalah baharu dalam column.

    • N is not zero. In a beam the compression equals the tension; in a column the forces add up to the axial load: N = Fc + Fsc − Fs.N bukan sifar. Dalam beam, mampatan sama dengan tegangan; dalam column, daya-daya itu dijumlahkan menjadi axial load: N = Fc + Fsc − Fs.

The strain at the most compressed face can no longer reach 0.0035, so EC2 Figure 6.1 pivots the strain diagram on 0.002 at 3h/7 from that face.     Answer 1Jawapan 1 Real columns are not straightColumn sebenar tidak lurus

    The analysis assumes a perfectly straight and plumb column loaded along its axis.     Answer 2Jawapan 2 A deeper block with a good lever armBlock yang lebih dalam dengan lever arm yang baik

    Below balance the tension bars have yielded, so their force stays at fydAs/2. From N = Fc + Fsc − Fs, extra axial load must then be carried mostly by a larger concrete force (the compression bars add a little as their strain grows): the neutral axis moves down and the block deepens. The block’s moment about the centre is Fc(h − s)/2 = 0.567fckb·s(h − s)/2, which grows with s as long as s < h/2. So more N gives more M, until the tension bars stop yielding at the balance point, where s = 0.8 × 0.617d ≈ 0.43h, close to the h/2 that gives the block its largest moment.
