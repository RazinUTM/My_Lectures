The design moment: the analysis, plus two allowances

eᵢ = l₀/400

Imperfection (EC2 5.2): out of line, out of plumb. Always added: M₀₂ = |M|max + NEd·eᵢ.

e₀ = max(h/30; 20 mm)

Minimum eccentricity (EC2 6.1(4)): a floor, never added to M₀₂.

MEd = max(M₀₂; NEd·e₀)

h is the depth in the plane of bending.

Block A · the big question, answered

Why more axial load can mean more moment

below balance · tension controls

Tension bars already yield: Fs is fixed. Extra N deepens the block; its moment 0.567fck·b·s(h − s)/2 grows while s < h/2.

above balance · compression controls

The concrete crushes before the tension bars yield. The axial load uses the concrete; each extra kN leaves less for bending.

For design: check the largest N with its M, the largest M with its N, and the smallest N with its M.
