What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Unit 7 told you whether a column is short or slender. This Unit designs the short one. You find the design moment, with the imperfection and the minimum eccentricity that EC2 adds to every column; you see how a section resists axial load and moment together, through strain compatibility and the N–M interaction diagram; you read the bars from a design chart and check them directly; and you finish with the minimum and maximum steel, the cover and the links. On the way you answer the big question of the week: does more axial load raise or lower a column’s moment capacity? Unit 9 then adds the second-order moment of slender columns and bending about both axes.Unit 7 memberitahu anda sama ada column itu short atau slender. Unit ini membuat design column yang pendek. Anda mencari design moment, dengan imperfection dan minimum eccentricity yang ditambah oleh EC2 kepada setiap column; anda melihat bagaimana section menahan axial load dan moment bersama-sama, melalui strain compatibility dan N–M interaction diagram; anda membaca bar daripada design chart dan menyemaknya secara terus; dan anda menamatkannya dengan steel minimum dan maksimum, cover dan link. Dalam perjalanan itu anda menjawab soalan besar minggu ini: adakah axial load yang lebih besar menaikkan atau menurunkan moment capacity column? Unit 9 kemudian menambah second-order moment bagi column slender dan bending pada kedua-dua paksi.

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

B500 steel has fyd = 0.87 × 500 = 435 N/mm² and Es = 200 kN/mm². It yields at a strain ofSteel B500 mempunyai fyd = 0.87 × 500 = 435 N/mm² dan Es = 200 kN/mm². Ia yield pada strain

εyd = fyd/Es = 435/200 000 = 0.00217. Below this strain a bar carries Esε; above it, 435 N/mm². In a column some bars do not reach it, so their stress has to be calculated from the strain. 0.0035 is the crushing strain of concrete.εyd = fyd/Es = 435/200 000 = 0.00217. Di bawah strain ini sebatang bar membawa Esε; di atasnya, 435 N/mm². Dalam column sesetengah bar tidak mencapainya, jadi stress-nya perlu dikira daripada strain. 0.0035 ialah crushing strain konkrit.

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A braced column has λ = 25 and λlim = 38 about its only axis of bending. What does its design need?Satu column braced mempunyai λ = 25 dan λlim = 38 pada satu-satunya paksi bending. Apakah yang diperlukan oleh design-nya?

λ ≤ λlim, so the column is non-slender: second-order effects may be ignored and it is designed for its first-order moments plus NEdei, but never for less than NEde0. That is this Unit. Even an axially loaded column carries a moment.λ ≤ λlim, jadi column itu non-slender: second-order effect boleh diabaikan dan ia di-design untuk first-order moment ditambah NEdei, tetapi tidak pernah kurang daripada NEde0. Itulah Unit ini. Column yang hanya membawa axial load pun menerima moment.
