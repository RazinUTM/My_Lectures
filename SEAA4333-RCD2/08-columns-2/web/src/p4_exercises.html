A3 · 1 markmarkah
A column works below its balance point. Its axial load increases while its moment stays the same. Its moment capacitySatu column bekerja di bawah balance point-nya. Axial load-nya bertambah manakala moment-nya kekal sama. Moment capacity-nya

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Below balance the tension bars yield first; extra compression deepens the concrete block, whose moment about the centre grows, so MRd rises until the balance point. Above balance the opposite holds.Di bawah balance, tension bar yield dahulu; compression tambahan mendalamkan concrete block, yang moment-nya pada pusat bertambah, jadi MRd meningkat sehingga balance point. Di atas balance, sebaliknya berlaku.

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A braced, non-slender column 300 × 300 has l0 = 3.0 m and NEd = 1200 kN; its end moments are 40 and 20 kN m, giving tension on opposite faces. One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu column braced dan non-slender 300 × 300 mempunyai l0 = 3.0 m dan NEd = 1200 kN; end moment-nya ialah 40 dan 20 kN m, yang memberi tension pada muka bertentangan. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 2: ei acts on every column. M02 = 40 + 1200 × 0.0075 = 49.0 kN m, so MEd = 49.0 kN m and μ = 0.060: the design moment was 18% too small. Lines 4 and 5 only carry the slip forward.Baris 2: ei bertindak pada setiap column. M02 = 40 + 1200 × 0.0075 = 49.0 kN m, jadi MEd = 49.0 kN m dan μ = 0.060: design moment itu 18% terlalu kecil. Baris 4 dan 5 hanya membawa kesilapan itu ke hadapan.
