Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

axial load ratio νNEd/(bhfck): the vertical axis of the column design charts.NEd/(bhfck): paksi menegak column design chart.
axis distance aThe distance from the concrete face to the centre of a main bar, a = cnom + φlink + φ/2; checked against EN 1992-1-2 Table 5.2a for fire.Jarak dari muka konkrit ke pusat bar utama, a = cnom + φlink + φ/2; disemak dengan EN 1992-1-2 Table 5.2a bagi fire.
balance pointThe state in which the concrete reaches 0.0035 just as the tension bars yield, at x = 0.617d for B500; the largest moment on the N–M curve.Keadaan apabila konkrit mencapai 0.0035 tepat ketika bar tegangan yield, pada x = 0.617d bagi B500; moment terbesar pada N–M curve.
compression controlsAbove the balance point: the concrete crushes before the tension bars yield, and more axial load lowers MRd.Di atas balance point: konkrit hancur sebelum bar tegangan yield, dan axial load yang lebih besar menurunkan MRd.
d2The depth of the compression bars below the compression face, cnom + φlink + φ/2; d2/h selects the design chart.Kedalaman bar mampatan di bawah muka mampatan, cnom + φlink + φ/2; d2/h memilih design chart.
design chartA family of N–M curves in the normalised axes ν and μ, one curve per steel ratio ω, for one value of d2/h; valid for rectangular sections with symmetric bars, fck ≤ 50 MPa and B500.Satu keluarga N–M curve dalam paksi ternormal ν dan μ, satu lengkung bagi setiap steel ratio ω, bagi satu nilai d2/h; sah bagi section segi empat tepat dengan bar simetri, fck ≤ 50 MPa dan B500.
design moment MEdFor a non-slender column, max(M02, NEde0).Bagi column non-slender, max(M02, NEde0).
design yield strain εydfyd/Es = 435/200 000 = 0.00217 for B500: below it a bar’s stress is Esε.fyd/Es = 435/200 000 = 0.00217 bagi B500: di bawahnya stress bar ialah Esε.
direct checkFinding MRd at NEd for the bars actually chosen, by strain compatibility, and confirming MRd ≥ MEd.Mencari MRd pada NEd bagi bar yang benar-benar dipilih, melalui strain compatibility, dan mengesahkan MRd ≥ MEd.
imperfection eccentricity eiThe eccentricity that represents a column built out of line or out of plumb; l0/400 for a braced column (EC2 5.2(9)).Eccentricity yang mewakili column yang dibina tidak sebaris atau tidak tegak; l0/400 bagi column braced (EC2 5.2(9)).
lapA joint in which two bars overlap so that one passes its force to the other through the concrete; at laps of bars over 14 mm the link spacing is reduced by 0.6.Sambungan di mana dua bar bertindih supaya satu memindahkan dayanya kepada yang lain melalui konkrit; pada lap bagi bar melebihi 14 mm, jarak link dikurangkan dengan faktor 0.6.
linkTransverse reinforcement round the main bars: it holds them against buckling and confines the core. φ ≥ max(6 mm, φmax/4) (EC2 9.5.3).Tetulang melintang di sekeliling bar utama: ia memegangnya daripada buckle dan mengurung teras. φ ≥ max(6 mm, φmax/4) (EC2 9.5.3).
load cases for a columnThe largest NEd with its moment, the largest moment with its NEd, and the smallest NEd with its moment; below balance the last can govern.NEd terbesar dengan moment-nya, moment terbesar dengan NEd-nya, dan NEd terkecil dengan moment-nya; di bawah balance yang terakhir boleh menentukan.
M02The larger first-order end moment plus NEdei.First-order end moment yang lebih besar ditambah NEdei.
maximum steel As,max0.04Ac outside laps and 0.08Ac at laps (EC2 9.5.2(3)).0.04Ac di luar lap dan 0.08Ac pada lap (EC2 9.5.2(3)).
mechanical steel ratio ωAsfyk/(bhfck): the label of each curve on a design chart; As = ωbhfck/fyk.Asfyk/(bhfck): label setiap lengkung pada design chart; As = ωbhfck/fyk.
minimum eccentricity e0h/30 but not less than 20 mm (EC2 6.1(4)): the design moment is never less than NEde0.h/30 tetapi tidak kurang daripada 20 mm (EC2 6.1(4)): design moment tidak pernah kurang daripada NEde0.
minimum steel As,minmax(0.10NEd/fyd, 0.002Ac) (EC2 9.5.2(2)); with at least four bars of 12 mm or more in a rectangular column.max(0.10NEd/fyd, 0.002Ac) (EC2 9.5.2(2)); dengan sekurang-kurangnya empat bar 12 mm atau lebih dalam column segi empat tepat.
moment ratio μMEd/(bh²fck), with the full depth h: the horizontal axis of the column design charts.MEd/(bh²fck), dengan kedalaman penuh h: paksi mendatar column design chart.
μfiThe ratio of the axial load in a fire to the design resistance at normal temperature; 0.7 may be taken on the safe side in Table 5.2a.Nisbah axial load dalam fire kepada design resistance pada suhu biasa; 0.7 boleh diambil di pihak selamat dalam Table 5.2a.
N–M interaction diagramThe closed curve of the pairs (M, N) that a section can just resist, found by strain compatibility for every neutral axis depth; points inside it are safe.Lengkung tertutup bagi pasangan (M, N) yang hanya boleh ditahan oleh section, dicari melalui strain compatibility bagi setiap neutral axis depth; titik di dalamnya selamat.
pivotFor x > h EC2 Figure 6.1 rotates the strain diagram about a strain of 0.002 at 3h/7 from the more compressed face; in pure compression the strain is 0.002 everywhere.Bagi x > h, EC2 Figure 6.1 memutarkan strain diagram pada strain 0.002 di 3h/7 dari muka yang lebih termampat; dalam pure compression strain ialah 0.002 di semua tempat.
scl,maxThe largest link spacing: the least of 20 times the smallest main bar, the smaller side of the column and 400 mm (EC2 9.5.3(3)).Jarak link terbesar: yang terkecil antara 20 kali bar utama terkecil, sisi column yang lebih kecil dan 400 mm (EC2 9.5.3(3)).
squash load N0The axial capacity with no moment: 0.567fckbh + 400As with B500 bars at a strain of 0.002.Kapasiti axial tanpa moment: 0.567fckbh + 400As dengan bar B500 pada strain 0.002.
strain compatibilitySection analysis in which plane sections remain plane and every bar has the strain of the concrete around it: strains give stresses, stresses give forces, forces give N and M.Analisis section di mana plane section kekal satah dan setiap bar mempunyai strain konkrit di sekelilingnya: strain memberi stress, stress memberi daya, daya memberi N dan M.
tension controlsBelow the balance point: the tension bars yield before the concrete crushes, and more axial load raises MRd.Di bawah balance point: bar tegangan yield sebelum konkrit hancur, dan axial load yang lebih besar menaikkan MRd.

§1 Design momentDesign moment

  • Non-slender about the axis of bending (Unit 7); h in the plane of bending.Non-slender pada paksi lenturan (Unit 7); h dalam satah lenturan.
  • Load cases: largest N with its M; largest M with its N; smallest N with its M.Load case: N terbesar dengan M-nya; M terbesar dengan N-nya; N terkecil dengan M-nya.

§2–§3 Strain compatibility and the N–M curveStrain compatibility dan N–M curve

  • x ≤ h: 0.0035 at the face; x > h: 0.002 at 3h/7. Bars: σ = 200 000ε, |σ| ≤ 435 N/mm².x ≤ h: 0.0035 pada muka; x > h: 0.002 di 3h/7. Bar: σ = 200 000ε, |σ| ≤ 435 N/mm².
  • Squash N0 = 0.567fckbh + 400As; balance x = 0.617d, largest M.Squash N0 = 0.567fckbh + 400As; balance x = 0.617d, M terbesar.
  • Below balance more N raises MRd; above it, more N lowers MRd.Di bawah balance N yang lebih besar menaikkan MRd; di atasnya, N yang lebih besar menurunkan MRd.

§4 Design chartsDesign chart

  • d2 = cnom + φlink + φ/2; d2/h to two decimals: that chart, or the next larger.d2 = cnom + φlink + φ/2; d2/h kepada dua tempat perpuluhan: chart itu, atau yang lebih besar berikutnya.
  • Finish with the direct check MRd ≥ MEd.Akhiri dengan semakan terus MRd ≥ MEd.
