    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 7 told you whether a column is slender; Unit 8 designed the short ones. This Unit designs the slender ones. A slender column deflects under its own load, and the deflection adds a moment: you find that second-order moment by nominal curvature, add it where EC2 says, and redo the bars. Then you look at both axes together. A column with no analysed moment about one axis is often slender about it, because only the imperfection acts there; EC2 5.8.9 tells you when the two axes must be checked together, and Eq. 5.39 is the check. Worked Example 8.1 of last week is finished here.Unit 7 memberitahu sama ada column itu slender; Unit 8 membuat design column pendek. Unit ini membuat design column slender.     Second-order effect (EC2 5.8.1)Second-order effect (EC2 5.8.1)The additional moment caused by the deformation of the structure. In an isolated column, the axial load NEd acts on the deflected shape, so at the critical section it has a lever arm e2, the deflection, on top of the first-order eccentricity e1 (the analysed moment and the imperfection, divided by NEd).Moment tambahan yang disebabkan oleh perubahan bentuk struktur. Dalam column terpencil, axial load NEd bertindak pada bentuk yang telah melentur, jadi pada section kritikal ia mempunyai lever arm e2, iaitu deflection, di atas first-order eccentricity e1 (moment analisis dan imperfection, dibahagi dengan NEd).
    (9.1)

    Why “by itself”: the extra moment NEde2 bends the column further, which increases e2, which increases the moment again. In a column that is strong enough the sequence settles at a larger, finite deflection; near the buckling load it never settles. For a short column the extra moment is small, under about 10% of the first-order moment, and EC2 lets you ignore it: that is what λ ≤ λlim means (EC2 5.8.2(6), 5.8.3.1).Mengapa “dengan sendirinya”: moment tambahan NEde2 melenturkan column lagi, yang menambah e2, yang menambah moment sekali lagi. Dalam column yang cukup kuat, urutan ini stabil pada deflection yang lebih besar tetapi terhingga; berhampiran buckling load ia tidak pernah stabil. Bagi column pendek, moment tambahan itu kecil, kurang daripada lebih kurang 10% first-order moment, dan EC2 membenarkan anda mengabaikannya: itulah maksud λ ≤ λlim (EC2 5.8.2(6), 5.8.3.1).

    Three methods (EC2 5.8.5)Tiga kaedah (EC2 5.8.5)The general method (5.8.6) follows the non-linear behaviour step by step. Two simplified methods follow: nominal stiffness (5.8.7) reduces the stiffness and magnifies the moment; nominal curvature (5.8.8) assumes a curvature at the critical section and turns it into a deflection. This course uses nominal curvature, as the RDF, the course notes and the RC Design app do. It suits an isolated column with a constant axial force and a known l0.General method (5.8.6) mengikut kelakuan tidak linear langkah demi langkah. Dua kaedah ringkas menyusul: nominal stiffness (5.8.7) mengurangkan stiffness dan membesarkan moment; nominal curvature (5.8.8) mengandaikan curvature pada section kritikal dan menukarkannya kepada deflection. Kursus ini menggunakan nominal curvature, seperti RDF, nota kursus dan app RC Design. Ia sesuai bagi column terpencil dengan axial force yang malar dan l0 yang diketahui.

    2. Nominal curvatureNominal curvature

    ≈ 9 min

    Nominal curvature does not compute the deflection from a stiffness. It assumes the critical section is close to failure, with both layers of bars about to yield, which fixes a curvature; it corrects that curvature for a high axial load (Kr) and for creep (Kφ); and a sine-shaped deflected shape turns the curvature into a deflection.Nominal curvature tidak mengira deflection daripada stiffness. Ia mengandaikan section kritikal hampir gagal, dengan kedua-dua lapisan bar hampir yield, yang menetapkan curvature; ia membetulkan curvature itu bagi axial load yang tinggi (Kr) dan bagi creep (Kφ); dan bentuk deflection berbentuk sinus menukar curvature kepada deflection.

    (9.2)

    d is the effective depth in the plane of bending: d = h − d2 for bars on two opposite faces (EC2 5.8.8.3(2)). The strain changes by 2εyd over about 0.9d, hence 0.45d.d ialah effective depth dalam satah bending: d = h − d2 bagi bar pada dua muka bertentangan (EC2 5.8.8.3(2)). Strain berubah sebanyak 2εyd merentasi lebih kurang 0.9d, maka 0.45d.

    Kr: the axial-load correction (EC2 5.8.8.3(3))Kr: pembetulan axial load (EC2 5.8.8.3(3))A heavily loaded column crushes before its tension bars yield, so its curvature at failure is smaller: Kr falls as n rises. ω here is Asfyd/(Acfcd), not the chart’s Asfyk/(bhfck).     The axial load acts on the deflected column, so at the critical section its eccentricity is e1 + e2. The extra moment NEde2 bends the column further, which increases e2 and the moment again; a strong enough column settles at a larger deflection, a column near its buckling load does not.
