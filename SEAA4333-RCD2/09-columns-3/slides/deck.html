Block A · talk 1 · 10 minutes

The second-order moment: N times the deflection

M = N (e₁ + e₂)

e₁: first-order eccentricity, from the analysis and eᵢ. e₂: the deflection the load itself causes.

λ > λlim → add M₂

Below λlim the extra moment is under 10%, so EC2 lets you ignore it (5.8.2(6)).

nominal curvature (EC2 5.8.8)

Assume the critical section reaches a curvature 1/r; e₂ follows from the deflected shape. The course method, and the RDF’s.

Block A · talk 2 · 8 minutes

Nominal curvature in six steps

1

First-order moments

eᵢ = l₀/400 · M₀₂ · M₀₁ · M₀e = 0.6M₀₂ + 0.4M₀₁ ≥ 0.4M₀₂

2

Creep

φef = φ(∞,t₀)·M₀Eqp/M₀Ed · β = 0.35 + fck/200 − λ/150 · Kφ = 1 + βφef ≥ 1

3

Basic curvature

1/r₀ = εyd/(0.45d), εyd = 435/200 000 = 0.00217

4

Curvature

1/r = Kr Kφ (1/r₀) · Kr = (nu − n)/(nu − 0.4) ≤ 1, nu = 1 + ω

5

Deflection, moment

e₂ = (1/r) l₀²/10 · M₂ = NEd e₂

6

Design moment

braced: max(M₀₂; M₀e + M₂; M₀₁ + 0.5M₂; NEd e₀) · unbraced: M₀₂ + M₂
