What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Unit 7 told you whether a column is slender; Unit 8 designed the short ones. This Unit designs the slender ones. A slender column deflects under its own load, and the deflection adds a moment: you find that second-order moment by nominal curvature, add it where EC2 says, and redo the bars. Then you look at both axes together. A column with no analysed moment about one axis is often slender about it, because only the imperfection acts there; EC2 5.8.9 tells you when the two axes must be checked together, and Eq. 5.39 is the check. Worked Example 8.1 of last week is finished here.Unit 7 memberitahu sama ada column itu slender; Unit 8 membuat design column pendek. Unit ini membuat design column slender. Column slender melentur di bawah load-nya sendiri, dan deflection itu menambah moment: anda mencari second-order moment itu melalui nominal curvature, menambahkannya di tempat yang ditetapkan oleh EC2, dan mengira semula bar. Kemudian anda melihat kedua-dua paksi bersama. Column tanpa moment analisis pada satu paksi sering slender pada paksi itu, kerana hanya imperfection yang bertindak di situ; EC2 5.8.9 memberitahu bila kedua-dua paksi mesti disemak bersama, dan Eq. 5.39 ialah semakannya. Worked Example 8.1 minggu lepas dilengkapkan di sini.

  2. find the design moment of a braced slender column from its four candidates, or of an unbraced one from M02 + M2, and iterate Kr with the steel;mencari design moment bagi column slender braced daripada empat calonnya, atau bagi column unbraced daripada M02 + M2, dan melelar Kr dengan steel;
  3. decide with the screening of EC2 5.8.9(3) whether a column needs a biaxial check, and carry it out with the enhanced moment and Eq. 5.39, taking the imperfection in one direction at a time;memutuskan melalui saringan EC2 5.8.9(3) sama ada column memerlukan biaxial check, dan melaksanakannya dengan enhanced moment dan Eq. 5.39, dengan imperfection dalam satu arah pada satu masa;
  4. complete the design of a column about both axes, and find the slips in someone else’s nominal-curvature calculation.melengkapkan design column pada kedua-dua paksi, dan mencari kesilapan dalam pengiraan nominal curvature orang lain.
