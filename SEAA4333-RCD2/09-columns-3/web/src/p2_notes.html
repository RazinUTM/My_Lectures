Nothing from Unit 8 is thrown away: the cover, the charts, the direct check, the bars and the links all stay. Two things are new: the second-order moment, and the second axis.Unit 7 mengasingkan column dengan λlim; Unit 8 membuat design column pendek, untuk first-order moment dan imperfection. Unit ini membuat design column slender. Tiada apa daripada Unit 8 yang dibuang: cover, chart, semakan terus, bar dan link semuanya kekal. Dua perkara baharu: second-order moment, dan paksi kedua.

Second-order effect (EC2 5.8.1)Second-order effect (EC2 5.8.1)The additional moment caused by the deformation of the structure. In an isolated column, the axial load NEd acts on the deflected shape, so at the critical section it has a lever arm e2, the deflection, on top of the first-order eccentricity e1 (the analysed moment and the imperfection, divided by NEd).Moment tambahan yang disebabkan oleh perubahan bentuk struktur. Dalam column terpencil, axial load NEd bertindak pada bentuk yang telah melentur, jadi pada section kritikal ia mempunyai lever arm e2, iaitu deflection, di atas first-order eccentricity e1 (moment analisis dan imperfection, dibahagi dengan NEd).
(9.1)

Why “by itself”: the extra moment NEde2 bends the column further, which increases e2, which increases the moment again. In a column that is strong enough the sequence settles at a larger, finite deflection; near the buckling load it never settles. For a short column the extra moment is small, under about 10% of the first-order moment, and EC2 lets you ignore it: that is what λ ≤ λlim means (EC2 5.8.2(6), 5.8.3.1).Mengapa “dengan sendirinya”: moment tambahan NEde2 melenturkan column lagi, yang menambah e2, yang menambah moment sekali lagi. Dalam column yang cukup kuat, urutan ini stabil pada deflection yang lebih besar tetapi terhingga; berhampiran buckling load ia tidak pernah stabil. Bagi column pendek, moment tambahan itu kecil, kurang daripada lebih kurang 10% first-order moment, dan EC2 membenarkan anda mengabaikannya: itulah maksud λ ≤ λlim (EC2 5.8.2(6), 5.8.3.1).

Three methods (EC2 5.8.5)Tiga kaedah (EC2 5.8.5)The general method (5.8.6) follows the non-linear behaviour step by step. Two simplified methods follow: nominal stiffness (5.8.7) reduces the stiffness and magnifies the moment; nominal curvature (5.8.8) assumes a curvature at the critical section and turns it into a deflection. This course uses nominal curvature, as the RDF, the course notes and the RC Design app do. It suits an isolated column with a constant axial force and a known l0.General method (5.8.6) mengikut kelakuan tak linear langkah demi langkah. Dua kaedah ringkas menyusul: nominal stiffness (5.8.7) mengurangkan stiffness dan membesarkan moment; nominal curvature (5.8.8) mengandaikan curvature pada section kritikal dan menukarkannya kepada deflection. Kursus ini menggunakan nominal curvature, seperti RDF, nota kursus dan app RC Design. Ia sesuai bagi column terpencil dengan axial force yang malar dan l0 yang diketahui.

2. Nominal curvatureNominal curvature

≈ 9 min

Nominal curvature does not compute the deflection from a stiffness. It assumes the critical section is close to failure, with both layers of bars about to yield, which fixes a curvature; it corrects that curvature for a high axial load (Kr) and for creep (Kφ); and a sine-shaped deflected shape turns the curvature into a deflection.Nominal curvature tidak mengira deflection daripada stiffness. Ia mengandaikan section kritikal hampir gagal, dengan kedua-dua lapisan bar hampir yield, yang menetapkan curvature; ia membetulkan curvature itu bagi axial load yang tinggi (Kr) dan bagi creep (Kφ); dan bentuk deflection berbentuk sinus menukar curvature kepada deflection.

d is the effective depth in the plane of bending: d = h − d2 for bars on two opposite faces (EC2 5.8.8.3(2)). The strain changes by 2εyd over about 0.9d, hence 0.45d.d ialah effective depth dalam satah lenturan: d = h − d2 bagi bar pada dua muka bertentangan (EC2 5.8.8.3(2)). Strain berubah sebanyak 2εyd merentasi lebih kurang 0.9d, maka 0.45d.

4. The Kr loop and the barsGelung Kr dan bar

≈ 7 min

Kr depends on the steel, and the steel on the design moment, which depends on Kr. The loop: take Kr = 1, find M2 and MEd, design As (chart or Lab), compute ω and a new Kr, and repeat until Kr stops changing. Two to four passes are enough; Kr = 1 alone is safe but wasteful. A column has two axes, and Unit 7 classified both. About an axis with no analysed moment only the imperfection acts, so rm = 1, C = 0.7 and λlim is low, about 10 to 15: that axis is usually slender. Worked Example 8.1 non-slender pada paksi z (λlim = 38.8) tetapi slender pada paksi y (λlim = 11.5 < 15.6).

Imperfection in one direction (EC2 5.8.9(2))Imperfection dalam satu arah (EC2 5.8.9(2))Design each direction separately as a first step.
