biaxial bendingMoments about both axes at once. Design each axis first; check together with Eq. 5.39 unless the screening says one axis dominates (EC2 5.8.9).Moment pada kedua-dua paksi serentak. creep coefficient φ(∞,t0)The final creep strain over the elastic strain, from EC2 Figure 3.1 or Annex B (RH, t0, h0, cement class).Strain creep akhir berbanding strain elastik, daripada EC2 Figure 3.1 atau Annex B (RH, t0, h0, cement class).
curvature 1/rKrKφ(1/r0): the nominal curvature of the critical section.KrKφ(1/r0): nominal curvature section kritikal.

first-order eccentricity e1The eccentricity of NEd before the column deflects: the analysed moment plus the imperfection, over NEd.Eccentricity NEd sebelum column melentur: moment analisis campur imperfection, dibahagi NEd.
imperfection eil0/400 for a braced column; in biaxial bending taken about one axis at a time, the more unfavourable (EC2 5.2, 5.8.9(2)).l0/400 bagi column braced; dalam biaxial bending diambil pada satu paksi pada satu masa, yang lebih tidak menguntungkan (EC2 5.2, 5.8.9(2)).
interaction check, Eq. 5.39(MEd,z/MRd,z)a + (MEd,y/MRd,y)a ≤ 1 for the bars provided (EC2 5.8.9(4)).(MEd,z/MRd,z)a + (MEd,y/MRd,y)a ≤ 1 bagi bar yang disediakan (EC2 5.8.9(4)).
Kr(nu − n)/(nu − nbal) ≤ 1: lowers the curvature of a heavily loaded column; iterated with the steel.(nu − n)/(nu − nbal) ≤ 1: merendahkan curvature column yang dibebankan berat; dilelar dengan steel.
Kφ1 + βφef ≥ 1: raises the curvature for creep.1 + βφef ≥ 1: menaikkan curvature bagi creep.
minimum eccentricity e0max(h/30, 20 mm): a floor on the design moment; in biaxial bending it goes with the imperfection.max(h/30, 20 mm): lantai bagi design moment; dalam biaxial bending ia bersama imperfection.
nbal, nunbal = 0.4, the axial load ratio at maximum moment resistance; nu = 1 + ω, with ω = Asfyd/(Acfcd).nbal = 0.4, nisbah axial load pada moment resistance maksimum; nu = 1 + ω, dengan ω = Asfyd/(Acfcd).
NRdAcfcd + Asfyd: the axial resistance used for the exponent a.Acfcd + Asfyd: axial resistance yang digunakan untuk eksponen a.
nominal curvature methodEC2 5.8.8: M2 from an assumed curvature at the critical section; the course method.EC2 5.8.8: M2 daripada curvature yang diandaikan pada section kritikal; kaedah kursus.
nominal stiffness methodEC2 5.8.7: the other simplified method, which reduces the stiffness and magnifies the first-order moment. Not used in this course.EC2 5.8.7: kaedah ringkas yang lain, yang mengurangkan stiffness dan membesarkan first-order moment. Tidak digunakan dalam kursus ini.
quasi-permanent ratio M0Eqp/M0EdThe share of the first-order moment that acts for a long time; 0.67 in the course unless the analysis gives it.Bahagian first-order moment yang bertindak untuk tempoh lama; 0.67 dalam kursus melainkan analisis memberikannya.
