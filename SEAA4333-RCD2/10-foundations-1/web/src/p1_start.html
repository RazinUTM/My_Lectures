What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

The columns of Units 7 to 9 have to stand on something. This Unit starts the foundations: what a foundation must do and which type suits which ground, then the pad footing, the strip footing under a wall and the combined footing under two columns. Two checks run side by side. The soil is checked with the service loads against the allowable bearing pressure; the concrete is designed with the ultimate loads, at the sections where a base bends, shears and punches. The big question is what the soil pressure does when the load moves off-centre: it tilts, then it becomes a triangle, then part of the base lifts off the ground. Strap footings and piles follow in Unit 11, pile caps in Unit 12.Column dalam Unit 7 hingga 9 mesti berdiri di atas sesuatu. Unit ini memulakan foundation: apa yang mesti dilakukan oleh foundation dan jenis manakah sesuai dengan tanah yang mana, kemudian pad footing, strip footing di bawah dinding dan combined footing di bawah dua column. Dua semakan berjalan seiring. Tanah disemak dengan service load berbanding allowable bearing pressure; konkrit dibuat design dengan ultimate load, pada section tempat tapak melentur, menerima shear dan punching. Soalan besarnya ialah apa yang berlaku kepada tekanan tanah apabila load beralih dari tengah: ia menjadi condong, kemudian menjadi segi tiga, kemudian sebahagian tapak terangkat dari tanah. Strap footing dan pile menyusul dalam Unit 11, pile cap dalam Unit 12.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

A footing section 3000 mm wide with d = 442 mm carries MEd = 428 kN m in C25/30, so K = MEd/(fckbd²) = 0.029. Which lever arm do you use for the bars?Satu section footing selebar 3000 mm dengan d = 442 mm membawa MEd = 428 kN m dalam C25/30, jadi K = MEd/(fckbd²) = 0.029. Lever arm manakah yang anda gunakan bagi bar?

z = d[0.5 + √(0.25 − K/1.134)] = 0.974d, but RCD I caps it at 0.95d = 420 mm. Footings are lightly stressed, so the cap nearly always governs: As = 428 × 10⁶/(0.87 × 500 × 420) = 2345 mm². 0.82d is the lever arm at Kbal = 0.167.z = d[0.5 + √(0.25 − K/1.134)] = 0.974d, tetapi RCD I menghadkannya kepada 0.95d = 420 mm. Footing mempunyai stress yang rendah, jadi had ini hampir selalu menentukan: As = 428 × 10⁶/(0.87 × 500 × 420) = 2345 mm². 0.82d ialah lever arm pada Kbal = 0.167.

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A column carries Gk = 600 kN and Qk = 400 kN. Which pair is right: the load that sizes its footing against an allowable bearing pressure, and the load that designs the footing’s bars?Satu column membawa Gk = 600 kN dan Qk = 400 kN. Pasangan manakah yang betul: load yang menentukan saiz footing-nya berbanding allowable bearing pressure, dan load yang membuat design bar footing itu?

The allowable bearing pressure already holds the geotechnical factor of safety, so the base is sized with the service load Gk + Qk = 1000 kN. The concrete and the bars are designed at the ultimate limit state, 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk = 1410 kN (Notes §2).Allowable bearing pressure sudah mengandungi faktor keselamatan geoteknik, jadi saiz tapak ditentukan dengan service load Gk + Qk = 1000 kN. Konkrit dan bar dibuat design pada ultimate limit state, 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk = 1410 kN (Nota §2).
