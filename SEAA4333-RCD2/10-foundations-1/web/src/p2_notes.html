1. Foundations: types and requirementsFoundation: jenis dan keperluan

≈ 7 min

A foundation carries the loads of the building into the ground. Every soil compresses under load, so every building settles. The design keeps the total settlement small, and the difference between neighbouring columns smaller still, by bearing on a stratum that is strong enough and by spreading the load over a large enough area (course notes).Foundation membawa load bangunan ke dalam tanah. Setiap tanah termampat di bawah load, jadi setiap bangunan mendap. Design memastikan jumlah settlement kecil, dan perbezaan antara column yang bersebelahan lebih kecil lagi, dengan menumpu pada lapisan tanah yang cukup kuat dan dengan menyebarkan load ke atas luas yang cukup besar (nota kursus).

Two checks at two load levelsDua semakan pada dua aras loadThe soil. Under the service loads, Gk + Qk, the bearing pressure must not exceed the allowable bearing pressure qall. The geotechnical engineer sets qall with a factor of safety against bearing failure already inside it (typically 2.5 to 3) and low enough to keep the settlement acceptable, so the loads are not factored again. The concrete. The base is designed at the ultimate limit state, 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk, for bending, shear and punching, like any other member (EC2).Tanah. Di bawah service load, Gk + Qk, bearing pressure tidak boleh melebihi allowable bearing pressure qall. Jurutera geoteknik menetapkan qall dengan faktor keselamatan terhadap kegagalan galas sudah di dalamnya (biasanya 2.5 hingga 3) dan cukup rendah untuk mengekalkan settlement yang boleh diterima, jadi load tidak difaktorkan sekali lagi. Konkrit. Tapak dibuat design pada ultimate limit state, 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk, bagi lenturan, ricih dan punching, seperti mana-mana anggota lain (EC2).

2. Sizing the baseMenentukan saiz tapak

≈ 7 min

Sizing comes first and uses the service loads; the depth and the bars come next and use the ultimate loads. Keep the two apart and most footing slips disappear.Penentuan saiz didahulukan dan menggunakan service load; kedalaman dan bar menyusul dan menggunakan ultimate load. Asingkan kedua-duanya dan kebanyakan kesilapan footing akan hilang.

(10.1)

Choose the plan to the nearest 0.1 m above Areq, then recompute W with the chosen size and check p = (Gk + Qk + W)/A ≤ qall. If only design loads are given, the course divides them by γF ≈ 1.4 to get service loads (course notes, Examples 2 and 3). Mosley does the same sum the other way round: the net allowable pressure qall − 25h carries Gk + Qk alone.Pilih pelan hingga 0.1 m terdekat di atas Areq, kemudian kira semula W dengan saiz yang dipilih dan semak p = (Gk + Qk + W)/A ≤ qall. Jika hanya design load diberi, kursus membahagikannya dengan γF ≈ 1.4 untuk mendapatkan service load (nota kursus, Contoh 2 dan 3). Mosley membuat hasil tambah yang sama secara terbalik: net allowable pressure qall − 25h membawa Gk + Qk sahaja.

(10.2)

The ultimate pressure leaves the self-weight out. The weight of the base rests on the soil directly below it, so it bends nothing and shears nothing; only the column load, spread by the base, does (Mosley §10.1). Adding 1.35W to NEd overstates every moment and shear in the base.Ultimate pressure tidak mengambil kira self-weight. Berat tapak terletak pada tanah betul-betul di bawahnya, jadi ia tidak melenturkan apa-apa dan tidak menghasilkan ricih; hanya load column, yang disebarkan oleh tapak, yang berbuat demikian (Mosley §10.1). Menambah 1.35W kepada NEd melebihkan setiap moment dan ricih dalam tapak.

The depthKedalamanBending rarely decides the depth of a footing; shear does, by punching or by vertical shear at d. Choose a trial h, design, and deepen the base if a shear check fails. The column face sets a floor, d ≥ NEd/(vRd,maxu0) (course notes), and the starter bars need their compression anchorage within h (Mosley §10.1). For Worked Example 10.1 the floor is 1410 × 10³/(3.60 × 1200) = 326 mm; the punching check needs more.Lenturan jarang menentukan kedalaman footing; ricih yang menentukan, melalui punching atau ricih menegak pada d. Pilih h percubaan, buat design, dan dalamkan tapak jika semakan ricih gagal. Muka column menetapkan had bawah, d ≥ NEd/(vRd,maxu0) (nota kursus), dan starter bar memerlukan anchorage mampatan di dalam h (Mosley §10.1). Bagi Worked Example 10.1 had bawah ini ialah 1410 × 10³/(3.60 × 1200) = 326 mm; semakan punching memerlukan lebih.

3. The pressure when the load is off-centreTekanan apabila load di luar tengah

≈ 9 min

Today’s big question. Press a foam block through a stiff plate: at the centre it sinks evenly; move your finger off-centre and one edge sinks more; move it far enough and the far edge of the plate lifts off the foam. The soil under a footing does the same, and the numbers follow from equilibrium alone.Soalan besar hari ini. Tekan blok span melalui plat yang keras: di tengah ia tenggelam sama rata; alihkan jari anda dari tengah dan satu tepi tenggelam lebih; alihkan cukup jauh dan tepi yang jauh terangkat dari span. Tanah di bawah footing berkelakuan sama, dan nombornya terhasil daripada keseimbangan sahaja.

(10.3)

A moment on the base moves the resultant of the load off the centre by e. As long as the whole base stays in contact, the pressure is that of a short column, axial load plus bending, with the plan of the base as the section (Mosley Eq. 10.2 and 10.3):Moment pada tapak mengalihkan paduan load dari tengah sejauh e. Selagi seluruh tapak kekal bersentuhan, tekanannya sama seperti column pendek, axial load campur lenturan, dengan pelan tapak sebagai section (Mosley Eq. 10.2 dan 10.3):

(10.4)

At e = L/6 the minimum pressure reaches zero and the diagram is a triangle with pmax = 2N/A: the resultant sits on the edge of the middle third, the kern. Beyond L/6 the formula would give a tension under the low edge, which soil cannot give. That part of the base lifts off, and the pressure becomes a triangle over a contact length Lc whose centroid lies under the load: Lc/3 = L/2 − e.Pada e = L/6 tekanan minimum mencapai sifar dan diagram menjadi segi tiga dengan pmax = 2N/A: paduan berada di tepi middle third, iaitu kern. Melepasi L/6, formula itu akan memberi tegangan di bawah tepi yang rendah, yang tidak boleh diberikan oleh tanah. Bahagian tapak itu terangkat, dan tekanan menjadi segi tiga sepanjang contact length Lc yang centroid-nya berada di bawah load: Lc/3 = L/2 − e.

A pad footing is a short, thick cantilever slab turned upside down: the soil pushes up, the column holds it down.
