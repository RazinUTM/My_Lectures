A1 · 1 markmarkah
Which load sizes the plan of a pad footing against the allowable bearing pressure?Load manakah yang menentukan saiz pelan pad footing berbanding allowable bearing pressure?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

The allowable bearing pressure already contains the geotechnical factor of safety, so the plan is sized with the service loads, and the soil feels the base as well as the column. The ultimate loads design the concrete (Notes §1–§2).Allowable bearing pressure sudah mengandungi faktor keselamatan geoteknik, jadi saiz pelan ditentukan dengan service load, dan tanah menerima tapak serta column. Ultimate load membuat design konkrit (Nota §1–§2).

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A 2.4 × 2.0 m base under a 400 mm square column carries NEd = 600 kN and MEd = 300 kN m along its length. One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu tapak 2.4 × 2.0 m di bawah column segi empat sama 400 mm membawa NEd = 600 kN dan MEd = 300 kN m sepanjang panjangnya. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: e = 0.50 m > L/6, so the base cannot stay in contact and the soil cannot give −31 kN/m². The pressure is a triangle over Lc = 3(1.2 − 0.5) = 2.10 m with pmax = 2 × 600/(2.0 × 2.10) = 286 kN/m²; at the face 149.7 kN/m², and Mx = 240.4 kN m (case F3). Lines 4 and 5 carry the wrong diagram forward. The moment happens to be close, but the pressure is wrong in kind: the last 0.30 m of the base is off the ground, which matters for the top face and the bearing check.Baris 3: e = 0.50 m > L/6, jadi tapak tidak boleh kekal bersentuhan dan tanah tidak boleh memberi −31 kN/m². Tekanannya ialah segi tiga sepanjang Lc = 3(1.2 − 0.5) = 2.10 m dengan pmax = 2 × 600/(2.0 × 2.10) = 286 kN/m²; di muka 149.7 kN/m², dan Mx = 240.4 kN m (kes F3). Baris 4 dan 5 membawa diagram yang salah ke hadapan. Moment-nya kebetulan hampir sama, tetapi jenis tekanannya salah: 0.30 m terakhir tapak terangkat dari tanah, yang penting bagi muka atas dan semakan galas.

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
Worked Example 10.1 (d = 448 mm, pEd = 156.7 kN/m², vRd,c = 0.377 N/mm²) is checked for punching on the perimeter at a = d. One line contains the slip. Which one?Worked Example 10.1 (d = 448 mm, pEd = 156.7 kN/m², vRd,c = 0.377 N/mm²) disemak bagi punching pada perimeter a = d. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 4: inside 2d the resistance rises as vRd = vRd,c · 2d/a, so at a = d, vRd = 0.754 N/mm² and the ratio is 0.674/0.754 = 0.89: the base passes (EC2 Eq. 6.50). The slip turns a sound base into a failing one and would cost depth for nothing. Lines 1 to 3 are right; line 5 only carries the slip forward.Baris 4: di dalam 2d, rintangan naik sebagai vRd = vRd,c · 2d/a, jadi pada a = d, vRd = 0.754 N/mm² dan nisbahnya 0.674/0.754 = 0.89: tapak lulus (EC2 Eq. 6.50). Kesilapan ini menukar tapak yang selamat menjadi gagal dan akan membazirkan kedalaman. Baris 1 hingga 3 betul; baris 5 hanya membawa kesilapan itu ke hadapan.
