Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

allowable bearing pressure qallThe pressure the soil may carry under service loads, set by the geotechnical engineer with a factor of safety against bearing failure (typically 2.5 to 3) and a limit on settlement.Tekanan yang boleh dibawa oleh tanah di bawah service load, ditetapkan oleh jurutera geoteknik dengan faktor keselamatan terhadap kegagalan galas (biasanya 2.5 hingga 3) dan had settlement.
basic control perimeter u1The punching perimeter at 2d from the column face, with rounded corners: 2(c1 + c2) + 4πd (EC2 6.4.2).Punching perimeter pada 2d dari muka column, dengan bucu dibundarkan: 2(c1 + c2) + 4πd (EC2 6.4.2).
bearing pressureThe pressure between the base and the soil; taken as linear under a rigid base.Tekanan antara tapak dan tanah; diambil sebagai linear di bawah tapak tegar.
blindingA thin layer of lean concrete laid on the excavated ground so that the base can be cast clean and level; on blinding cnom ≥ 40 mm (EC2 4.4.1.3(4)).Lapisan nipis konkrit lemah yang diletakkan di atas tanah yang digali supaya tapak boleh dituang dengan bersih dan rata; di atas blinding cnom ≥ 40 mm (EC2 4.4.1.3(4)).
column base (punching)A footing or pile cap under a column; EC2 6.4.4(2) checks it at every perimeter a ≤ 2d with vRd = vRd,c·2d/a.Footing atau pile cap di bawah column; EC2 6.4.4(2) menyemaknya pada setiap perimeter a ≤ 2d dengan vRd = vRd,c·2d/a.
combined footingOne base under two columns, placed so that its centroid lies under the resultant of their loads.Satu tapak di bawah dua column, diletakkan supaya centroid-nya berada di bawah paduan load-nya.
contact length LcThe length of base still pressing on the soil when e > L/6: 3(L/2 − e).Panjang tapak yang masih menekan tanah apabila e > L/6: 3(L/2 − e).
critical sectionA section where a base is checked: the column face (bending), d from the face (shear), the perimeters up to 2d (punching).Section tempat tapak disemak: muka column (lenturan), d dari muka (ricih), perimeter hingga 2d (punching).
differential settlementThe difference in settlement between parts of a building, which cracks it; limited by sound bearing and by tying the base together.Perbezaan settlement antara bahagian bangunan, yang menyebabkannya retak; dihadkan oleh galas yang kukuh dan dengan mengikat tapak bersama.
eccentricity eM/N: how far the resultant of the load lies from the centroid of the base.M/N: sejauh mana paduan load terletak dari centroid tapak.
kern (middle third)The zone round the centroid inside which the resultant keeps the whole base in contact: e ≤ L/6 on one axis; the rhombus 6ex/Lx + 6ey/Ly ≤ 1 for two.Zon di sekeliling centroid yang di dalamnya paduan memastikan seluruh tapak bersentuhan: e ≤ L/6 pada satu paksi; rombus 6ex/Lx + 6ey/Ly ≤ 1 bagi dua.
kickerA short length of column, about 75 mm, cast with the base to hold the column formwork; the starter-bar lap is measured from its top.Bahagian column yang pendek, lebih kurang 75 mm, dituang bersama tapak untuk memegang acuan column; lap starter bar diukur dari atasnya.
pad footingAn isolated base, square or rectangular, under one column.Tapak terpencil, segi empat sama atau segi empat tepat, di bawah satu column.
partial contact (lift-off)When e > L/6, part of the base leaves the soil; the pressure is a triangle over Lc. Special precautions once e > L/3 (EC7 6.5.4).Apabila e > L/6, sebahagian tapak meninggalkan tanah; tekanan menjadi segi tiga sepanjang Lc. Langkah berjaga-jaga khas sebaik e > L/3 (EC7 6.5.4).
pile foundationPiles carry the load down to firm ground by end bearing, friction or both, through a pile cap (Units 11–12).Pile membawa load ke bawah ke tanah keras melalui end bearing, geseran atau kedua-duanya, melalui pile cap (Unit 11–12).
punching shearFailure of a slab or base round a concentrated load, on a truncated cone; checked on control perimeters (EC2 6.4).Kegagalan slab atau tapak di sekeliling load tertumpu, pada kon terpenggal; disemak pada control perimeter (EC2 6.4).
raft foundationOne slab under the whole building, for heavy loads, weak ground or variable soil.Satu slab di bawah seluruh bangunan, bagi load berat, tanah lemah atau tanah yang berubah-ubah.
reduced punching force VEd,redNEd − pEdA: the column load less the soil pressure inside the perimeter (EC2 Eq. 6.48).NEd − pEdA: load column tolak tekanan tanah di dalam perimeter (EC2 Eq. 6.48).
self-weight WThe weight of the base, 25 kN/m³ × volume: in the bearing check, not in the bending and shear pressure.Berat tapak, 25 kN/m³ × isi padu: dalam semakan galas, bukan dalam tekanan lenturan dan ricih.
service and ultimate loadsGk + Qk for the soil (against qall); 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk for the concrete.Gk + Qk bagi tanah (berbanding qall); 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk bagi konkrit.
settlementThe downward movement of a foundation as the soil below it compresses.Pergerakan ke bawah foundation apabila tanah di bawahnya termampat.
starter barsBars cast into the base with a 90° bend tied to the bottom mat, lapped with the column bars above the kicker.Bar yang dituang ke dalam tapak dengan bengkokan 90° diikat pada jaringan bawah, di-lap dengan bar column di atas kicker.
strap footingA boundary pad tied by a strap beam to an inner footing so that the pair resists the overturning of the eccentric pad (Unit 11).Pad sempadan yang diikat oleh strap beam kepada footing dalam supaya pasangan itu menahan terbalik pad yang eksentrik (Unit 11).
strip footingA continuous base under a wall or a row of columns, designed per metre run.Tapak berterusan di bawah dinding atau barisan column, dibuat design per meter panjang.
vmin0.035k3/2fck1/2: the shear resistance of concrete when no bars count; often the whole resistance of a footing.0.035k3/2fck1/2: shear resistance konkrit apabila tiada bar dikira; sering keseluruhan rintangan footing.
vRd,max0.4νfcd, ν = 0.6(1 − fck/250): the strut limit at the column face (EC2 6.4.5(3), 2014 amendment; the course notes use 0.5νfcd).0.4νfcd, ν = 0.6(1 − fck/250): had strut di muka column (EC2 6.4.5(3), pindaan 2014; nota kursus menggunakan 0.5νfcd).
β (punching)1 + k(MEd/VEd)(u/W): raises the punching stress for an unbalanced moment (EC2 6.4.3(3)).1 + k(MEd/VEd)(u/W): menaikkan tegasan punching bagi moment tidak seimbang (EC2 6.4.3(3)).

Short notesNota ringkas

§1–§2 Two checks; sizingDua semakan; penentuan saiz

  • Soil: service loads against qall. Concrete: 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk. cnom ≥ 40 mm on blinding, ≥ 75 mm on soil.Tanah: service load berbanding qall. Konkrit: 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk. cnom ≥ 40 mm di atas blinding, ≥ 75 mm di atas tanah.

§3 Pressure off-centreTekanan di luar tengah

  • Two moments: corner p = N/A ± Mx/Zx ± My/Zy; kern 6ex/Lx + 6ey/Ly ≤ 1. e > L/3: special precautions (EC7 6.5.4).Dua moment: bucu p = N/A ± Mx/Zx ± My/Zy; kern 6ex/Lx + 6ey/Ly ≤ 1. e > L/3: langkah berjaga-jaga khas (EC7 6.5.4).

§4 Critical sectionsSection kritikal

  • u(a) = 2(c1 + c2) + 2πa; A(a) = c1c2 + 2a(c1 + c2) + πa². Worst usually near a = d.u(a) = 2(c1 + c2) + 2πa; A(a) = c1c2 + 2a(c1 + c2) + πa². Terburuk biasanya berhampiran a = d.
  • ρl only for bars anchored lbd + d beyond the section, else vmin. Face: βNEd/(u0d) ≤ 0.4νfcd.ρl hanya bagi bar yang di-anchor lbd + d melepasi section, jika tidak vmin. Muka: βNEd/(u0d) ≤ 0.4νfcd.

§5–§6 Combined and strip footingsCombined dan strip footing

  • Combined: top bars between the columns, bottom bars under them; transverse cantilevers under each column. Strip: per metre; masonry wall, bending halfway to the centre line; no punching.Combined: bar atas di antara column, bar bawah di bawahnya; julur melintang di bawah setiap column. Strip: per meter; dinding batu bata, lenturan separuh jalan ke garis tengah; tiada punching.
