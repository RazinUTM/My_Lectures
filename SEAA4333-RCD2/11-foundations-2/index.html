    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 10 put each column at the centre of its own base. A column on the site boundary cannot do that: its base would have to cross the boundary. Its pad alone would carry the load at one edge and rotate. This Unit ties it back to the next column with a strap beam, a lever that keeps the boundary footing level. The reactions come from statics, the strap is designed as a beam, and the two footings as one-way and two-way slabs. The trapezoidal combined footing is the other way out. The second half turns to piles, for ground too weak near the surface: when to use them, which type, what a pile can carry, how many piles a column needs and how far apart, how big the cap is, and how a moment shares the load among them. Pile caps themselves follow in Unit 12.Unit 10 meletakkan setiap column di tengah tapaknya sendiri. Column di sempadan tapak tidak boleh berbuat demikian: tapaknya terpaksa melintasi sempadan. Pad-nya sahaja akan memikul load di satu tepi dan berputar. Unit ini mengikatnya ke column bersebelahan dengan strap beam, iaitu tuil yang memastikan footing di sempadan kekal rata. Reaction diperoleh daripada statik, strap dibuat design sebagai beam, dan dua footing sebagai slab satu arah dan dua arah. Trapezoidal combined footing ialah jalan keluar yang lain. Separuh kedua beralih kepada pile, bagi tanah yang terlalu lemah berhampiran permukaan: bila menggunakannya, jenis yang mana, apa yang boleh dipikul oleh satu pile, berapa banyak pile yang diperlukan oleh column dan sejauh mana jaraknya, berapa besar pile cap, dan bagaimana moment membahagikan load antara pile. Pile cap itu sendiri menyusul dalam Unit 12.

    1. Columns on the site boundaryColumn di sempadan tapak

    ≈ 6 min

    A column on the site boundary cannot stand at the centre of a pad: the pad would have to cross the boundary into the neighbour’s land. Built flush with the boundary, the pad takes the column load at one edge, and Unit 10’s rule gives the result: the pressure becomes a short, very high triangle and the pad rotates.Column di sempadan tapak tidak boleh berdiri di tengah pad: pad itu terpaksa melintasi sempadan ke tanah jiran. Jika dibina rata dengan sempadan, pad menerima load column di satu tepi, dan peraturan Unit 10 memberi hasilnya: tekanan menjadi segi tiga yang pendek dan sangat tinggi, dan pad berputar.

    With the numbers of Worked Example 11.1: column A carries 1075 kN (service) and stands 0.15 m from the boundary on a pad 2.0 m long and 3.5 m wide. Then e = 1.0 − 0.15 = 0.85 m, beyond L/6 = 0.33 m and even beyond L/3 = 0.67 m, where EC7 6.5.4 asks for special precautions. The contact length is Lc = 3(1.0 − 0.85) = 0.45 m and pmax = 2 × 1075/(3.5 × 0.45) = 1365 kN/m², almost seven times an allowable 200 kN/m². The pad would settle at the boundary and tilt the column.Dengan nombor Worked Example 11.1: column A membawa 1075 kN (service) dan berdiri 0.15 m dari sempadan di atas pad sepanjang 2.0 m dan selebar 3.5 m.     2. The strap footing: staticsStrap footing: statik

    ≈ 8 min

    The strap and its two footings act as one rigid body. Take moments about column B: column A’s load, standing at the boundary, tries to tip the strap about the centre of footing A, and column B’s load holds the far end down. Like a seesaw, the reaction under footing A comes out larger than column A’s own load.Strap dan dua footing-nya bertindak sebagai satu jasad tegar. Ambil moment pada column B: load column A, yang berdiri di sempadan, cuba menterbalikkan strap pada pusat footing A, dan load column B menahan hujung yang jauh ke bawah. Seperti jongkang-jongket, reaction di bawah footing A menjadi lebih besar daripada load column A itu sendiri.

    Let L be the distance between the column centres, LA the length of footing A along the strap (it starts at the boundary) and cA the width of column A. Column A stands cA/2 from the boundary and the centre of footing A is LA/2 from it, so the lever arm is e = LA/2 − cA/2.     Answer 1Jawapan 1 The leverTuil

    Column A stands at the boundary, e = 0.85 m from the centre of footing A. Moments about column B give RA(L − e) = NAL, so RA = NAL/(L − e): 1252 kN for NA = 1075 kN, leaving the weights out. The extra 177 kN, NAe/(L − e), comes off column B: RB = NB − 177 kN. The two 177 kN forces, 5.15 m apart, make the couple that balances NAe, the moment of the eccentric column.Column A berdiri di sempadan, e = 0.85 m dari pusat footing A. Moment pada column B memberi RA(L − e) = NAL, jadi RA = NAL/(L − e): 1252 kN bagi NA = 1075 kN, tanpa berat. Tambahan 177 kN, NAe/(L − e), diambil daripada column B: RB = NB − 177 kN. Dua daya 177 kN, berjarak 5.15 m, membentuk gandingan yang mengimbangi NAe, iaitu moment column yang sipi.

    Answer 2Jawapan 2 A strap that bears takes loadStrap yang menumpu mengambil load

    The statics assume that all the soil reaction comes up through the two footings. A strap resting on the soil would be pushed up between the footings: the reactions would change, the strap moments with them, and the strap would settle with the soil it rests on. So the ground under it is loosened and left uncompacted (Mosley §10.3), and where compaction machinery can act on the strap EC2 9.8.3 asks for a downward load of 10 kN/m as well.Statik mengandaikan semua reaction tanah datang melalui dua footing. Strap yang terletak di atas tanah akan ditolak ke atas di antara footing: reaction akan berubah, moment strap bersamanya, dan strap akan mendap bersama tanah yang didudukinya. Oleh itu tanah di bawahnya dilonggarkan dan dibiarkan tidak dipadatkan (Mosley §10.3), dan jika jentera pemadat boleh bertindak pada strap, EC2 9.8.3 meminta load ke bawah 10 kN/m juga.

    Answer 3Jawapan 3 Hogging, and a shear peakHogging, dan puncak ricih

    Column A pushes the strap down at its end and footing A pushes it up just inside, so the strap hogs from column A to near column B: the tension is at the top. The shear is the soil reaction on footing A coming back to column A, so it is largest at the face of A (1227 kN; 518 kN at d), while between the footings only NAe/(L − e) and the weight are left (261 kN). The links follow the shear: H8 @ 150 over footing A, H6 @ 150 beyond.Column A menolak strap ke bawah di hujungnya dan footing A menolaknya ke atas sedikit ke dalam, jadi strap mengalami hogging dari column A hingga berhampiran column B: tegangan berada di bahagian atas. Ricih ialah reaction tanah pada footing A yang kembali ke column A, jadi ia paling besar di muka A (1227 kN; 518 kN pada d), manakala di antara footing hanya NAe/(L − e) dan berat yang tinggal (261 kN). Link mengikut ricih: H8 @ 150 di atas footing A, H6 @ 150 di luarnya.

    Answer 4Jawapan 4 A working load, and shared soilWorking load, dan tanah yang dikongsi

    A pile’s capacity is a safe working load: its ultimate geotechnical resistance divided by a factor of 2 to 3, the same idea as qall. So it is compared with the service loads, (N + W)/n ± Mx/Σx², not with 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk. In a group of friction piles the shafts load the same soil and their stressed zones overlap: a large, closely spaced group can carry about a third less than n single piles, and it settles more because its bulb of pressure reaches deeper (Mosley §10.6). End-bearing piles on rock carry nearly the full sum.Kapasiti pile ialah working load yang selamat: rintangan geoteknik ultimate-nya dibahagi dengan faktor 2 hingga 3, idea yang sama seperti qall. Oleh itu ia dibandingkan dengan service load, (N + W)/n ± Mx/Σx², bukan dengan 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk. Dalam kumpulan friction pile, aci-aci membebani tanah yang sama dan zon bertegasannya bertindih: kumpulan yang besar dan rapat boleh memikul lebih kurang satu pertiga kurang daripada n pile tunggal, dan ia mendap lebih kerana bulb tekanannya lebih dalam (Mosley §10.6). End-bearing pile di atas batuan memikul hampir jumlah penuh.
