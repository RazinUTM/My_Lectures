What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Unit 10 put each column at the centre of its own base. A column on the site boundary cannot do that: its base would have to cross the boundary. Its pad alone would carry the load at one edge and rotate. This Unit ties it back to the next column with a strap beam, a lever that keeps the boundary footing level. The reactions come from statics, the strap is designed as a beam, and the two footings as one-way and two-way slabs. The trapezoidal combined footing is the other way out. The second half turns to piles, for ground too weak near the surface: when to use them, which type, what a pile can carry, how many piles a column needs and how far apart, how big the cap is, and how a moment shares the load among them. Pile caps themselves follow in Unit 12.Unit 10 meletakkan setiap column di tengah tapaknya sendiri. Column di sempadan tapak tidak boleh berbuat demikian: tapaknya terpaksa melintasi sempadan. Pad-nya sahaja akan memikul load di satu tepi dan berputar. Unit ini mengikatnya ke column bersebelahan dengan strap beam, iaitu tuil yang memastikan footing di sempadan kekal rata. Reaction diperoleh daripada statik, strap dibuat design sebagai beam, dan dua footing sebagai slab satu arah dan dua arah. Trapezoidal combined footing ialah jalan keluar yang lain. Separuh kedua beralih kepada pile, bagi tanah yang terlalu lemah berhampiran permukaan: bila menggunakannya, jenis yang mana, apa yang boleh dipikul oleh satu pile, berapa banyak pile yang diperlukan oleh column dan sejauh mana jaraknya, berapa besar pile cap, dan bagaimana moment membahagikan load antara pile. Pile cap itu sendiri menyusul dalam Unit 12.

Recall 1Ingat semula 1

A rigid beam 6.0 m long carries 100 kN at its left end. It rests on a support 1.0 m from that end and on a second support at the right end. What is the reaction at the first support?Satu beam tegar sepanjang 6.0 m membawa 100 kN di hujung kirinya. Ia terletak di atas satu support 1.0 m dari hujung itu dan support kedua di hujung kanan. Berapakah reaction pada support pertama?

Moments about the right support: R₁ × 5.0 = 100 × 6.0, so R₁ = 120 kN, more than the load itself; the right support then pulls down with 20 kN. A strap footing works the same way: footing A carries more than column A (Notes §2).Moment pada support kanan: R₁ × 5.0 = 100 × 6.0, jadi R₁ = 120 kN, lebih besar daripada load itu sendiri; support kanan pula menarik ke bawah dengan 20 kN. Strap footing berfungsi dengan cara yang sama: footing A memikul lebih daripada column A (Nota §2).

Recall 2Ingat semula 2

A beam section gives K = MEd/(fckbd²) = 0.177, above Kbal = 0.167. What does RCD I tell you to do, if the section cannot be deepened?Satu section beam memberi K = MEd/(fckbd²) = 0.177, melebihi Kbal = 0.167. Apakah yang disuruh oleh RCD I, jika section itu tidak boleh didalamkan?

Above Kbal the concrete in compression is not enough on its own, so bars are added in the compression face; the tension bars then carry the Kbal part at z = 0.82d plus As2. The strap of Worked Example 11.1 has K = 0.177 and needs 174 mm² of compression bars (Notes §3).Di atas Kbal, konkrit dalam mampatan tidak mencukupi dengan sendirinya, jadi bar ditambah pada muka mampatan; bar tegangan pula memikul bahagian Kbal pada z = 0.82d campur As2. Strap dalam Worked Example 11.1 mempunyai K = 0.177 dan memerlukan 174 mm² bar mampatan (Nota §3).

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

A beam with d = 810 mm carries VEd = 520 kN, with the strut at θ = 22° (cot θ = 2.5) and B500 links. The links needed, Asw/s = VEd/(0.78fykd cot θ), are aboutSatu beam dengan d = 810 mm membawa VEd = 520 kN, dengan strut pada θ = 22° (cot θ = 2.5) dan link B500. Link yang diperlukan, Asw/s = VEd/(0.78fykd cot θ), ialah lebih kurang

520 × 10³/(0.78 × 500 × 810 × 2.5) = 0.66 mm²/mm, met by two legs of H8 at 150 mm (0.67). This is the strap of Worked Example 11.1 just inside footing A, where its shear is largest (Notes §3).520 × 10³/(0.78 × 500 × 810 × 2.5) = 0.66 mm²/mm, dipenuhi oleh dua kaki H8 pada 150 mm (0.67). Inilah strap dalam Worked Example 11.1 di dalam footing A, tempat ricihnya paling besar (Nota §3).
