1. Columns on the site boundaryColumn di sempadan tapak

≈ 6 min

A column on the site boundary cannot stand at the centre of a pad: the pad would have to cross the boundary into the neighbour’s land. Built flush with the boundary, the pad takes the column load at one edge, and Unit 10’s rule gives the result: the pressure becomes a short, very high triangle and the pad rotates.Column di sempadan tapak tidak boleh berdiri di tengah pad: pad itu terpaksa melintasi sempadan ke tanah jiran. Jika dibina rata dengan sempadan, pad menerima load column di satu tepi, dan peraturan Unit 10 memberi hasilnya: tekanan menjadi segi tiga yang pendek dan sangat tinggi, dan pad berputar.

With the numbers of Worked Example 11.1: column A carries 1075 kN (service) and stands 0.15 m from the boundary on a pad 2.0 m long and 3.5 m wide. Then e = 1.0 − 0.15 = 0.85 m, beyond L/6 = 0.33 m and even beyond L/3 = 0.67 m, where EC7 6.5.4 asks for special precautions. The contact length is Lc = 3(1.0 − 0.85) = 0.45 m and pmax = 2 × 1075/(3.5 × 0.45) = 1365 kN/m², almost seven times an allowable 200 kN/m². The pad would settle at the boundary and tilt the column.Dengan nombor Worked Example 11.1: column A membawa 1075 kN (service) dan berdiri 0.15 m dari sempadan di atas pad sepanjang 2.0 m dan selebar 3.5 m. Maka e = 1.0 − 0.15 = 0.85 m, melepasi L/6 = 0.33 m malah melepasi L/3 = 0.67 m, tempat EC7 6.5.4 meminta langkah berjaga-jaga khas. Panjang sentuhan ialah Lc = 3(1.0 − 0.85) = 0.45 m dan pmax = 2 × 1075/(3.5 × 0.45) = 1365 kN/m², hampir tujuh kali allowable 200 kN/m². Pad itu akan mendap di sempadan dan mencondongkan column.

The strap is what EC2 calls a tie beam (9.8.3): a beam that removes the eccentricity of the loading of the foundations, designed for the bending moments and shear forces that result. 2. The strap footing: staticsStrap footing: statik

≈ 8 min

The strap and its two footings act as one rigid body. Take moments about column B: column A’s load, standing at the boundary, tries to tip the strap about the centre of footing A, and column B’s load holds the far end down. Like a seesaw, the reaction under footing A comes out larger than column A’s own load.Strap dan dua footing-nya bertindak sebagai satu jasad tegar. Ambil moment pada column B: load column A, yang berdiri di sempadan, cuba menterbalikkan strap pada pusat footing A, dan load column B menahan hujung yang jauh ke bawah. Seperti jongkang-jongket, reaction di bawah footing A menjadi lebih besar daripada load column A itu sendiri.

Let L be the distance between the column centres, LA the length of footing A along the strap (it starts at the boundary) and cA the width of column A. Column A stands cA/2 from the boundary and the centre of footing A is LA/2 from it, so the lever arm is e = LA/2 − cA/2. Two things follow from Eq. (11.1). A longer footing A moves its centre away from the column, so e grows and RA grows with it, while footing A gets narrower. A column B closer to A shortens L − e and also raises RA. Column B pays for the lever: RB = NB − NAe/(L − e). If column B is light, RB could fall towards zero and the strap would lift it, so RB must stay well positive with the smallest load column B can carry.Dua perkara terhasil daripada Eq. (11.1). Footing A yang lebih panjang menjauhkan pusatnya dari column, jadi e bertambah dan RA bertambah bersamanya, manakala footing A menjadi lebih sempit. Column B yang lebih dekat dengan A memendekkan L − e dan juga menaikkan RA. Column B membayar untuk tuil itu: RB = NB − NAe/(L − e). Jika column B ringan, RB boleh menurun ke arah sifar dan strap akan mengangkatnya, jadi RB mesti kekal positif dengan selesa di bawah load paling kecil yang boleh dibawa oleh column B.

Mosley takes one pressure for both footings at the ultimate limit state when the dead-to-imposed load ratios of the two columns are similar. The course method keeps the pressure of each footing from statics: in Worked Example 11.1, 252.7 and 254.3 kN/m² against one value of 253.6 kN/m². 3. Designing a strap footingDesign strap footing

≈ 10 min

Three members come out of the statics: the strap beam, footing A and footing B. The strap is loaded by the columns at their centres, pushed up by each footing over its length, and pulled down by its own weight between the column faces. Its shear force and bending moment follow as for any beam, and they decide where its bars and links go.Tiga member terhasil daripada statik: strap beam, footing A dan footing B. Strap dibebani oleh column pada pusatnya, ditolak ke atas oleh setiap footing sepanjang panjangnya, dan ditarik ke bawah oleh beratnya sendiri di antara muka column. Shear force dan bending moment-nya diperoleh seperti mana-mana beam, dan kedua-duanya menentukan di mana bar dan link-nya diletakkan.

Over footing A the upward pressure wA = pABA works against column A: the shear is largest at the face of column A and falls to zero at x0 from the boundary, where the moment is largest and hogging (top in tension). Between the footings the shear is nearly constant and the moment falls in a straight line. Round column B the moment changes sign: that is footing B bending as a pad under its own column, designed with footing B.Di atas footing A, tekanan ke atas wA = pABA bertindak melawan column A: shear paling besar di muka column A dan menurun ke sifar pada x0 dari sempadan, tempat moment paling besar dan hogging (bahagian atas tegang). Di antara footing, shear hampir malar dan moment menurun secara garis lurus. Di sekitar column B, moment bertukar tanda: itulah footing B melentur sebagai pad di bawah column-nya sendiri, dibuat design bersama footing B.

4. The trapezoidal combined footingTrapezoidal combined footing

≈ 6 min

A combined footing works by putting the centroid of its plan on the resultant of the column loads, so that the pressure is uniform. A rectangle has its centroid at mid-length. When the length is fixed at both ends and the resultant is not at mid-length, a trapezoid moves the centroid towards its wide end, which goes under the heavier column.Combined footing berfungsi dengan meletakkan centroid pelannya pada paduan load column, supaya tekanan seragam. Segi empat tepat mempunyai centroid di tengah panjang. Apabila panjang ditetapkan di kedua-dua hujung dan paduan tidak berada di tengah panjang, trapezoid mengalihkan centroid ke arah hujungnya yang lebar, yang diletakkan di bawah column yang lebih berat.

The load a group can carry is not always n times the load of one pile. Closely spaced friction piles share the same soil, and a large group can carry about a third less; end-bearing piles on rock or dense gravel carry nearly the full sum. 6. Pile loads under a momentLoad pile di bawah moment

≈ 6 min

A pile cap is stiff. Under a moment it turns about the centroid of the pile group, so the load in each pile changes in proportion to its distance from that centroid, the same idea as the pressure under an eccentric pad. Piles on the axis of the moment add nothing to it.Pile cap adalah tegar. Di bawah moment ia berputar pada centroid kumpulan pile, jadi load dalam setiap pile berubah mengikut jaraknya dari centroid itu, idea yang sama seperti tekanan di bawah pad yang sipi. Pile pada paksi moment tidak menyumbang kepadanya.

(11.10)

Here xi and yi are the distances of pile i from the centroidal axes of the group and the sums run over every pile (Mosley §10.6; course notes). For a group symmetrical about both axes that is all; Mosley gives the general form for a group that is not. The largest service load must not exceed the working load Fp, and the smallest must stay positive unless the piles are designed for tension.Di sini xi dan yi ialah jarak pile i dari paksi centroid kumpulan dan jumlah diambil bagi setiap pile (Mosley §10.6; nota kursus). Bagi kumpulan yang simetri pada kedua-dua paksi, itu sahaja; Mosley memberi bentuk umum bagi kumpulan yang tidak simetri. Service load terbesar tidak boleh melebihi working load Fp, dan yang terkecil mesti kekal positif melainkan pile itu dibuat design bagi tension.

Two load levels again. The check against Fp uses the service loads, with the weight of the cap. The design of the cap in Unit 12 uses the ultimate pile loads, NEd/n ± MEdx/Σx², without the cap weight: like the weight of a footing, it goes straight down into the piles and does not bend the cap. Semakan terhadap Fp menggunakan service load, dengan berat pile cap. The way the group is turned matters. A 2 × 3 group with the moment along its rows of three has Σx² = 4 × 0.9² = 3.24 m² and an outer pile 0.9 m out; turned through 90°, the same moment acts along rows of two, with Σx² = 6 × 0.45² = 1.215 m² and the outer piles 0.45 m out. The ratio x/Σx² rises from 0.28 to 0.37 m⁻¹, so the outer piles carry more, although their lever arm is half. Horizontal loads larger than the passive resistance of the soil need raking piles (Mosley §10.6).Cara kumpulan itu dipusingkan adalah penting. Kumpulan 2 × 3 dengan moment sepanjang baris tiga mempunyai Σx² = 4 × 0.9² = 3.24 m² dan pile luar 0.9 m dari pusat; apabila dipusingkan 90°, moment yang sama bertindak sepanjang baris dua, dengan Σx² = 6 × 0.45² = 1.215 m² dan pile luar 0.45 m dari pusat. Nisbah x/Σx² naik daripada 0.28 kepada 0.37 m⁻¹, jadi pile luar memikul lebih, walaupun lever arm-nya separuh. Load mendatar yang lebih besar daripada rintangan pasif tanah memerlukan raking pile (Mosley §10.6).
