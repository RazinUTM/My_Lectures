Write your four answers first, then open the model answers one by one.Tulis empat jawapan anda dahulu, kemudian buka jawapan model satu demi satu.

Answer 1Jawapan 1 The leverTuil

Column A stands at the boundary, e = 0.85 m from the centre of footing A. Moments about column B give RA(L − e) = NAL, so RA = NAL/(L − e): 1252 kN for NA = 1075 kN, leaving the weights out. The extra 177 kN, NAe/(L − e), comes off column B: RB = NB − 177 kN. The two 177 kN forces, 5.15 m apart, make the couple that balances NAe, the moment of the eccentric column.Column A berdiri di sempadan, e = 0.85 m dari pusat footing A. Moment pada column B memberi RA(L − e) = NAL, jadi RA = NAL/(L − e): 1252 kN bagi NA = 1075 kN, tanpa berat. Tambahan 177 kN, NAe/(L − e), diambil daripada column B: RB = NB − 177 kN. Dua daya 177 kN, berjarak 5.15 m, membentuk gandingan yang mengimbangi NAe, iaitu moment column yang sipi.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 A strap that bears takes loadStrap yang menumpu mengambil load

The statics assume that all the soil reaction comes up through the two footings. A strap resting on the soil would be pushed up between the footings: the reactions would change, the strap moments with them, and the strap would settle with the soil it rests on. So the ground under it is loosened and left uncompacted (Mosley §10.3), and where compaction machinery can act on the strap EC2 9.8.3 asks for a downward load of 10 kN/m as well.Statik mengandaikan semua reaction tanah datang melalui dua footing. Strap yang terletak di atas tanah akan ditolak ke atas di antara footing: reaction akan berubah, moment strap bersamanya, dan strap akan mendap bersama tanah yang didudukinya. Oleh itu tanah di bawahnya dilonggarkan dan dibiarkan tidak dipadatkan (Mosley §10.3), dan jika jentera pemadat boleh bertindak pada strap, EC2 9.8.3 meminta load ke bawah 10 kN/m juga.

Answer 3Jawapan 3 Hogging, and a shear peakHogging, dan puncak shear

Column A pushes the strap down at its end and footing A pushes it up just inside, so the strap hogs from column A to near column B: the tension is at the top. The shear is the soil reaction on footing A coming back to column A, so it is largest at the face of A (1227 kN; 518 kN at d), while between the footings only NAe/(L − e) and the weight are left (261 kN). The links follow the shear: H8 @ 150 over footing A, H6 @ 150 beyond.Column A menolak strap ke bawah di hujungnya dan footing A menolaknya ke atas sedikit ke dalam, jadi strap mengalami hogging dari column A hingga berhampiran column B: tension berada di bahagian atas. Shear ialah reaction tanah pada footing A yang kembali ke column A, jadi ia paling besar di muka A (1227 kN; 518 kN pada d), manakala di antara footing hanya NAe/(L − e) dan berat yang tinggal (261 kN). Link mengikut shear: H8 @ 150 di atas footing A, H6 @ 150 di luarnya.

Answer 4Jawapan 4 A working load, and shared soilWorking load, dan tanah yang dikongsi

A pile’s capacity is a safe working load: its ultimate geotechnical resistance divided by a factor of 2 to 3, the same idea as qall. So it is compared with the service loads, (N + W)/n ± Mx/Σx², not with 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk. In a group of friction piles the shafts load the same soil and their stressed zones overlap: a large, closely spaced group can carry about a third less than n single piles, and it settles more because its bulb of pressure reaches deeper (Mosley §10.6). End-bearing piles on rock carry nearly the full sum.Kapasiti pile ialah working load yang selamat: rintangan geoteknik ultimate-nya dibahagi dengan faktor 2 hingga 3, idea yang sama seperti qall. Oleh itu ia dibandingkan dengan service load, (N + W)/n ± Mx/Σx², bukan dengan 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk. Dalam kumpulan friction pile, aci-aci membebani tanah yang sama dan stressed zone-nya bertindih: kumpulan yang besar dan rapat boleh memikul lebih kurang satu pertiga kurang daripada n pile tunggal, dan ia mendap lebih kerana bulb tekanannya lebih dalam (Mosley §10.6). End-bearing pile di atas batuan memikul hampir jumlah penuh.
