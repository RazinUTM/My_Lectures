A1 · 1 markmarkah
A 300 mm column stands on the site boundary. On a 2.0 m pad of its own, the load would act 0.85 m from the centre of the pad, beyond L/6 = 0.33 m. What happens to the pressure under the pad?Satu column 300 mm berdiri di sempadan tapak. Di atas pad 2.0 m miliknya sendiri, load akan bertindak 0.85 m dari pusat pad, melepasi L/6 = 0.33 m. Apakah yang berlaku kepada tekanan di bawah pad itu?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

With e > L/6 part of the base lifts off (Unit 10): Lc = 3(1.0 − 0.85) = 0.45 m, and pmax = 2N/(BLc) is nearly nine times the average N/(BL). The strap removes this by tying the footing to column B (Notes §1).Dengan e > L/6 sebahagian tapak terangkat (Unit 10): Lc = 3(1.0 − 0.85) = 0.45 m, dan pmax = 2N/(BLc) hampir sembilan kali ganda purata N/(BL). Strap menghapuskannya dengan mengikat footing kepada column B (Notes §1).

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

The statics put all the soil reaction under the footings. A strap bearing on the soil would take load, change the reactions and its own moments, and settle with the soil (Mosley §10.3). Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

With both ends fixed, a rectangle cannot move its centroid under the resultant. A trapezoid can: x̄ = (L/3)(b1 + 2b2)/(b1 + b2) shifts towards the wider end, so the pressure is uniform (Notes §4).Dengan kedua-dua hujung tetap, segi empat tepat tidak dapat mengalihkan centroid-nya ke bawah paduan. Trapezium boleh: x̄ = (L/3)(b1 + 2b2)/(b1 + b2) beralih ke arah hujung yang lebih lebar, jadi tekanannya seragam (Notes §4).

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
Worked Example 11.1 at service: NA = 1075 kN at the boundary, NB = 1725 kN, L = 6.0 m, LA = 2.0 m, cA = 0.3 m, qall = 200 kN/m². One line contains the slip. Which one?Worked Example 11.1 pada service: NA = 1075 kN di sempadan, NB = 1725 kN, L = 6.0 m, LA = 2.0 m, cA = 0.3 m, qall = 200 kN/m². Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: RA acts at the centre of footing A, L − e = 5.15 m from column B, not 6.0 m. Dividing by L drops the lever: RA = 1382.3 kN, so BA = 3.46 → 3.5 m. On the 3.0 m footing the soil would see 230 kN/m², 15 % over qall: unsafe. Footing B, 3.10 m instead of 2.95 m, would be too big. Lines 4 and 5 follow from line 3.Baris 3: RA bertindak di pusat footing A, L − e = 5.15 m dari column B, bukan 6.0 m. Membahagi dengan L menghilangkan tuil: RA = 1382.3 kN, jadi BA = 3.46 → 3.5 m. Di atas footing 3.0 m, tanah akan menerima 230 kN/m², 15 % melebihi qall: tidak selamat. Footing B, 3.10 m dan bukannya 2.95 m, akan terlalu besar. Baris 4 dan 5 adalah akibat baris 3.
