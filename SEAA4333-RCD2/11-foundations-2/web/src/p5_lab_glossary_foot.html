Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

allowable bearing pressure qallThe pressure the soil may carry under service loads, set by the geotechnical engineer with a factor of safety against bearing failure and a limit on settlement; both footings of a strap footing are sized against it.Tekanan yang boleh dibawa oleh tanah di bawah service load, ditetapkan oleh jurutera geoteknik dengan faktor keselamatan terhadap bearing failure dan had settlement; kedua-dua footing dalam strap footing ditentukan saiznya berbanding nilai ini.
bakau pileA mangrove timber pile, used in Malaysia for light buildings on soft ground; it lasts only where it stays below the water table.Pile kayu bakau, digunakan di Malaysia bagi bangunan ringan di atas tanah lembut; ia tahan lama hanya jika kekal di bawah paras air bumi.
blindingA thin layer of lean concrete on the excavated ground so that a footing can be cast clean and level; on blinding cnom ≥ 40 mm (EC2 4.4.1.3(4)).Lapisan nipis konkrit lemah di atas tanah yang digali supaya footing boleh dituang dengan bersih dan rata; di atas blinding cnom ≥ 40 mm (EC2 4.4.1.3(4)).
bored pileA pile cast in a hole drilled in the ground (cast in situ); EC2 9.8.5 and Table 9.6N set its minimum longitudinal steel.Pile yang dituang dalam lubang yang digerudi di dalam tanah (cast in situ); EC2 9.8.5 dan Table 9.6N menetapkan steel memanjang minimumnya.
boundary columnA column on the edge of the site: its footing may not project beyond the line, so a pad under it alone would carry the load far off-centre.Column di tepi tapak: footing-nya tidak boleh terkeluar melepasi garis sempadan, jadi pad di bawahnya sahaja akan membawa load jauh dari tengah.
bulb of pressureThe zone of soil stressed by a foundation. A pile group’s bulb reaches deeper than a single pile’s, so the group settles more (Mosley Figure 10.17).Zon tanah yang ditegaskan oleh foundation. Bulb kumpulan pile mencapai lebih dalam daripada bulb satu pile, jadi kumpulan itu mendap lebih banyak (Mosley Figure 10.17).
eccentricity of footing A, eThe distance from column A to the centre of footing A: e = LA/2 − cA/2 for a column on the boundary.Jarak dari column A ke pusat footing A: e = LA/2 − cA/2 bagi column di sempadan.
end-bearing pileA pile that carries its load mainly on its toe, on rock or dense soil; a group of them carries nearly n times one pile.Pile yang membawa load-nya terutamanya pada hujungnya, di atas batuan atau tanah padat; kumpulannya memikul hampir n kali satu pile.
friction pileA pile that carries its load mainly by skin friction along its shaft. A large, close group can carry about a third less than n single piles (Mosley §10.6).Pile yang membawa load-nya terutamanya melalui geseran sepanjang acinya. Kumpulan yang besar dan rapat boleh memikul lebih kurang satu pertiga kurang daripada n pile tunggal (Mosley §10.6).
lever of the strap, L − eThe distance from column B to the centre of footing A. Moments about column B give RA = NAL/(L − e) without the weights: more than NA.Jarak dari column B ke pusat footing A. Moment pada column B memberi RA = NAL/(L − e) tanpa berat: lebih daripada NA.
net design reactionThe ultimate reaction of a footing without its own weight (RA,Ed, RB,Ed); the strap weight stays in, at 1.35WR. It designs the bars.Reaction ultimate footing tanpa beratnya sendiri (RA,Ed, RB,Ed); berat strap kekal, pada 1.35WR. Ia digunakan untuk design bar.
pile capThe block that spreads a column load to its piles; sized from the spacing, its edge 150 mm beyond the pile faces in the course rule; designed in Unit 12.Blok yang menyebarkan load column kepada pile-nya; saiznya daripada jarak pile, tepinya 150 mm melepasi muka pile mengikut peraturan kursus; dibuat design dalam Unit 12.
pile foundationPiles carry the load through weak soil down to firm ground, by end bearing, friction or both; used where a spread footing would be uneconomical or impossible.Pile membawa load melalui tanah lemah ke tanah keras, melalui end bearing, geseran atau kedua-duanya; digunakan apabila spread footing tidak ekonomik atau tidak mungkin.
pile spacingCentre to centre; the course uses 3φ. Mosley §10.6: not less than the pile perimeter for friction piles, and twice the least width for end-bearing piles.Pusat ke pusat; kursus menggunakan 3φ. Mosley §10.6: tidak kurang daripada perimeter pile bagi friction pile, dan dua kali lebar terkecil bagi end-bearing pile.
precast pileA pile made in a yard and driven; it is designed for lifting and stacking as well as for its load, and its head for the hammer (Mosley §10.6).Pile yang dibuat di limbungan dan dipacu; ia dibuat design bagi pengangkatan dan penyusunan serta load-nya, dan kepalanya bagi tukul pemacu (Mosley §10.6).
punching shearFailure of a footing round a column on a truncated cone; footing B of a strap footing is checked like a pad, at every perimeter up to 2d (EC2 6.4.4(2)).Kegagalan footing di sekeliling column pada kon terpenggal; footing B dalam strap footing disemak seperti pad, pada setiap perimeter hingga 2d (EC2 6.4.4(2)).
safe (working) load of a pile, FpThe ultimate resistance of a pile divided by a factor of 2 to 3, from a load test or a driving formula; it is compared with service loads.Rintangan ultimate pile dibahagi dengan faktor 2 hingga 3, daripada load test atau formula pemacuan; ia dibandingkan dengan service load.
service and ultimate loadsGk + Qk for the soil and the piles (against qall and Fp); 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk for the concrete.Gk + Qk bagi tanah dan pile (berbanding qall dan Fp); 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk bagi konkrit.
strap beamThe beam that ties a boundary footing to an inner footing. It hogs, so its main bars are at the top, and it must not bear on the soil.Beam yang mengikat footing sempadan kepada footing dalam. Ia mengalami hogging, jadi bar utamanya di atas, dan ia tidak boleh menumpu pada tanah.
strap footingA boundary footing tied by a strap beam to an inner footing, so that the pair resists the overturning of the eccentric column (Mosley §10.3).Footing sempadan yang diikat oleh strap beam kepada footing dalam, supaya pasangan itu menahan terbalik column yang sipi (Mosley §10.3).
Σx² of a pile groupThe sum of x² over every pile, x measured from the centroid of the group; it plays the part of the section modulus in P = (N + W)/n ± Mx/Σx².Jumlah x² bagi setiap pile, x diukur dari centroid kumpulan; ia berperanan seperti section modulus dalam P = (N + W)/n ± Mx/Σx².
tension pileA pile whose load comes out negative under a large moment: it pulls on the cap and must be designed and anchored for tension. The layout is usually changed instead.Pile yang load-nya menjadi negatif di bawah moment yang besar: ia menarik pile cap dan mesti dibuat design serta di-anchor bagi tension. Susun atur biasanya diubah sebagai ganti.
tie beamA beam between footings or pile caps that holds them together or takes an eccentricity; bars at least 8 mm, and a downward load of 10 kN/m where compaction machinery can act on it (EC2 9.8.3).Beam di antara footing atau pile cap yang mengikatnya bersama atau mengambil eccentricity; bar sekurang-kurangnya 8 mm, dan load ke bawah 10 kN/m jika jentera pemadat boleh bertindak padanya (EC2 9.8.3).
trapezoidal combined footingA combined footing whose length is fixed at both ends: making it wider at the heavier column moves its centroid under the resultant, x̄ = (L/3)(b1 + 2b2)/(b1 + b2).Combined footing yang panjangnya tetap di kedua-dua hujung: menjadikannya lebih lebar di column yang lebih berat mengalihkan centroid-nya ke bawah paduan, x̄ = (L/3)(b1 + 2b2)/(b1 + b2).
zero shear x0The section of the strap where V = 0, over footing A, measured from the boundary; the largest (hogging) moment of the strap is there.Section strap tempat V = 0, di atas footing A, diukur dari sempadan; moment terbesar (hogging) strap berada di situ.

§1–§2 The strap: statics at serviceStrap: statik pada service

  • WA, WB = 10 % at first, then the real weights and the pressures again. The strap weight acts between the column faces.WA, WB = 10 % pada mulanya, kemudian berat sebenar dan tekanan sekali lagi. Berat strap bertindak di antara muka column.

§3 Designing the strap footingDesign strap footing

  • No footing weights at ULS. Strap: top bars (hogging); links for V at d from column A over footing A, the smaller V elsewhere. Footing A: one-way cantilever from the strap. Footing B: a pad.Tiada berat footing pada ULS. Strap: bar atas (hogging); link bagi V pada d dari column A di atas footing A, V yang lebih kecil di tempat lain. Footing A: cantilever satu arah dari strap. Footing B: pad.
  • The strap must not bear on the soil; 10 kN/m downward where compaction machinery acts (EC2 9.8.3).Strap tidak boleh menumpu pada tanah; 10 kN/m ke bawah jika jentera pemadat bertindak (EC2 9.8.3).
