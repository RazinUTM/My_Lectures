    What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 11 left a group of piles with the load on each one; this Unit designs the cap that joins them to the column. A pile cap is short and deep. It does not bend like a slab: the load runs down from the column to each pile head in concrete struts, and a steel tie along the bottom holds the pile heads together. That is the truss analogy, used for two-, three- and four-pile caps. A longer cap, or one under a moment, is designed as a wide beam at the faces of the column. Shear is checked a fifth of a pile diameter inside the piles, where loads close to the column may be reduced by a factor β, but by no more than four times under EC2. The course notes’ four-pile cap passes by 1 % without that limit and fails with it; finding out why, and what depth works instead, is the thread of the Unit. Punching, the column face, crack control and the detailing of a cap complete the foundations.Unit 11 meninggalkan satu kumpulan pile dengan load pada setiap satu; Unit ini membuat design pile cap yang menyambungnya kepada column. Pile cap pendek dan dalam. Ia tidak melentur seperti slab: load mengalir dari column ke setiap kepala pile dalam strut konkrit, dan tie keluli di bahagian bawah menahan kepala pile bersama. Itulah truss analogy, yang digunakan bagi pile cap dua, tiga dan empat pile. Pile cap yang lebih panjang, atau yang menerima moment, dibuat design sebagai beam lebar di muka column. Ricih disemak satu perlima diameter pile ke dalam dari muka pile, tempat load yang dekat dengan column boleh dikurangkan dengan faktor β, tetapi tidak lebih daripada empat kali mengikut EC2. Pile cap empat pile dalam nota kursus lulus dengan margin 1 % tanpa had itu dan gagal dengannya; mencari sebabnya, dan kedalaman yang berfungsi sebagai ganti, ialah benang merah Unit ini. Punching, muka column, kawalan retak dan perincian pile cap melengkapkan topik foundation.

    ≈ 6 min

It is short and deep, so it does not bend like a slab: most of the load runs straight down from the column to each pile head in compression, and the bottom of the cap is pulled apart as the pile heads try to spread.     2. The truss analogyTruss analogy

    ≈ 8 min

    Think of the cap as a triangle. Concrete struts run from the column down to each pile head; a steel tie along the bottom joins the pile heads and stops them spreading. The column pushes the apex down, the piles push the corners up, and the tie carries the horizontal force that holds the triangle closed.Bayangkan pile cap sebagai segi tiga. Strut konkrit mengalir dari column ke setiap kepala pile; tie keluli di bahagian bawah menyambung kepala pile dan menghalangnya daripada merenggang. Column menolak puncak ke bawah, pile menolak bucu ke atas, dan tie membawa daya mendatar yang memastikan segi tiga itu kekal tertutup.

    At a pile node three forces meet: the pile reaction N/2 upwards, the strut C along the line to the column, and the tie T.     A common slip with four piles is to stop at T: the bars across the cap in one direction serve both trusses of that direction, so they carry 2T. The bars go in both directions of the bottom face. With s ≤ 3φ they may be spread evenly across the width, as in all the course examples; with wider spacing only the bars within 1.5φ of a pile centre count, so they are gathered in bands over the piles. They continue past each pile and are bent up to anchor beyond the pile centre lines (§6). The effective depth follows the layers: the tie of a two-pile cap is the bottom layer, a four-pile cap uses the upper layer for both directions (on the safe side, as the notes), and the three bands of a three-pile cap lie in three layers.Kesilapan biasa bagi empat pile ialah berhenti pada T: bar merentas pile cap dalam satu arah berkhidmat kepada kedua-dua truss arah itu, jadi ia membawa 2T.
