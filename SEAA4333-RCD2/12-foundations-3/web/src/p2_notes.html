1. The pile cap: its job, plan and depthPile cap: tugasnya, pelan dan kedalaman

≈ 6 min

A pile cap gathers the column load and shares it among the piles. It is short and deep, so it does not bend like a slab: most of the load runs straight down from the column to each pile head in compression, and the bottom of the cap is pulled apart as the pile heads try to spread. Two models design it: the truss analogy for compact caps, and beam theory for longer ones.Pile cap menghimpunkan load column dan membahagikannya kepada pile. Ia pendek dan dalam, jadi ia tidak melentur seperti slab: kebanyakan load mengalir terus dari column ke setiap kepala pile dalam compression, dan bahagian bawah pile cap ditarik apabila kepala pile cuba merenggang. Dua model digunakan untuk design-nya: truss analogy bagi pile cap yang padat, dan beam theory bagi yang lebih panjang.

The plan follows from Unit 11: piles at s = kφ with k = 3, and the edge of the cap 150 mm beyond the pile faces, so a square four-pile cap is (k + 1)φ + 300 each way and a six-pile cap (k + 1)φ + 300 by (2k + 1)φ + 300. The depth must give room for the anchorage of the pile bars and the column starter bars, and enough shear resistance. Mosley Table 10.5 gives a first depth for groups of up to six piles; the course notes’ 2φ + 100 gives the same values up to 550 mm piles.Pelan diperoleh daripada Unit 11: pile pada s = kφ dengan k = 3, dan tepi pile cap 150 mm melepasi muka pile, jadi pile cap empat pile segi empat sama ialah (k + 1)φ + 300 setiap arah dan pile cap enam pile (k + 1)φ + 300 kali (2k + 1)φ + 300. Kedalamannya mesti memberi ruang bagi anchorage bar pile dan starter bar column, serta shear resistance yang mencukupi. Mosley Table 10.5 memberi kedalaman awal bagi kumpulan sehingga enam pile; 2φ + 100 dalam nota kursus memberi nilai yang sama sehingga pile 550 mm.

2. The truss analogyTruss analogy

≈ 8 min

Think of the cap as a triangle. Concrete struts run from the column down to each pile head; a steel tie along the bottom joins the pile heads and stops them spreading. The column pushes the apex down, the piles push the corners up, and the tie carries the horizontal force that holds the triangle closed.Bayangkan pile cap sebagai segi tiga. Strut konkrit mengalir dari column ke setiap kepala pile; steel tie di bahagian bawah menyambung kepala pile dan menghalangnya daripada merenggang. Column menolak puncak ke bawah, pile menolak bucu ke atas, dan tie membawa daya mendatar yang memastikan segi tiga itu kekal tertutup.

At a pile node three forces meet: the pile reaction N/2 upwards, the strut C along the line to the column, and the tie T. Their triangle is similar to the triangle of the truss, so T/(N/2) = (s/2)/d. A common slip with four piles is to stop at T: the bars across the cap in one direction serve both trusses of that direction, so they carry 2T. The bars go in both directions of the bottom face. With s ≤ 3φ they may be spread evenly across the width, as in all the course examples; with wider spacing only the bars within 1.5φ of a pile centre count, so they are gathered in bands over the piles. They continue past each pile and are bent up to anchor beyond the pile centre lines (§6). Bar itu diteruskan melepasi setiap pile dan dibengkokkan ke atas untuk anchorage melepasi garis tengah pile (§6). 3. Beam theoryBeam theory

≈ 7 min

When the cap is longer, or a moment makes the pile loads unequal, treat it as a wide beam turned upside down: the piles push up, the column holds it down, and the critical section for bending is at the face of the column, as for a pad footing.Apabila pile cap lebih panjang, atau moment menjadikan load pile tidak sama, anggap ia sebagai beam lebar yang terbalik: pile menolak ke atas, column menahannya ke bawah, dan section kritikal bagi bending berada di muka column, seperti pad footing.

The moment at a column face is the sum, over the piles beyond that face, of each pile load times its distance to the face:Moment di muka column ialah jumlah, bagi pile di luar muka itu, setiap load pile darab jaraknya ke muka:

(12.5)

In a 2 × 3 group with the moment about y, the two end piles carry the largest load, so Mxx = 2Fmax(xmax − c/2) designs the bars along the length. Across, one long row of three piles sums to three times the mean load whatever the moment about y, so Myy = 3Fmean(s/2 − c/2). The larger moment takes the outer layer, dx = h − c − φ/2; the other uses dy = h − c − 1.5φ. Then K, z ≤ 0.95d and As as for a beam, with As,min over the full width, which often governs the short direction.Dalam kumpulan 2 × 3 dengan moment pada paksi y, dua pile hujung memikul load terbesar, jadi Mxx = 2Fmax(xmax − c/2) digunakan untuk design bar sepanjang panjang pile cap. Merentas, satu baris panjang tiga pile berjumlah tiga kali load purata walau apa pun moment pada paksi y, jadi Myy = 3Fmean(s/2 − c/2). Moment yang lebih besar mengambil lapisan luar, dx = h − c − φ/2; yang satu lagi menggunakan dy = h − c − 1.5φ. Kemudian K, z ≤ 0.95d dan As seperti beam, dengan As,min merentas seluruh lebar, yang sering menentukan arah pendek.

4. Shear: the section at φ/5 and the enhancementShear: section pada φ/5 dan enhancement

≈ 9 min

A pile cap does not fail in shear like a long beam. Near the column the load reaches the piles along direct struts, which carry far more shear than the beam formula allows. EC2 lets loads close to a support be reduced by a factor β = av/2d before checking VRd,c, but it stops the reduction at av = 0.5d.Pile cap tidak gagal dalam shear seperti beam panjang. Berhampiran column, load sampai ke pile melalui strut terus, yang membawa shear jauh lebih besar daripada yang dibenarkan oleh formula beam. EC2 membenarkan load yang dekat dengan support dikurangkan dengan faktor β = av/2d sebelum menyemak VRd,c, tetapi ia menghentikan pengurangan itu pada av = 0.5d.

The critical section lies 20 % of the pile diameter inside the face of the pile (Mosley §10.7.2, from BS 8110). VEd is the sum of the loads of the piles beyond it: in a four-pile cap the section crosses the full width, so two piles stand beyond it, not one. The section is av from the column face. For a square group:Section kritikal terletak 20 % diameter pile ke dalam dari muka pile (Mosley §10.7.2, daripada BS 8110). VEd ialah jumlah load pile di luarnya: dalam pile cap empat pile, section merentas seluruh lebar, jadi dua pile berada di luarnya, bukan satu. Section itu berada av dari muka column. Three conditions come with β. It needs the main bars fully anchored at the support, here bent up beyond each pile centre line (§6). The unreduced shear must still satisfy VEd ≤ 0.5bdνfcd. And for av < 0.5d, EC2 takes av = 0.5d, so β never drops below 0.25: the shear can be divided by four at most. In VRd,c the bars count in ρl only if they run lbd + d beyond the section (EC2 Figure 6.3, the course convention from Unit 10); in every course example they do not, and vmin governs. With s ≤ 3φ the enhancement applies across the whole section; with wider spacing only to strips 3φ wide over the piles.Tiga syarat datang bersama β. Ia memerlukan bar utama di-anchor sepenuhnya di support, di sini dibengkokkan ke atas melepasi setiap garis tengah pile (§6). Shear yang tidak dikurangkan masih mesti memenuhi VEd ≤ 0.5bdνfcd. Dan bagi av < 0.5d, EC2 mengambil av = 0.5d, jadi β tidak pernah turun di bawah 0.25: shear boleh dibahagi empat paling banyak. 6. Crack control and detailingCrack control dan detailing

≈ 6 min

A pile cap is a deep, heavily loaded block. Most of its detailing makes sure that the tie is anchored where the struts meet it, over the piles, and that the corners and faces of the block hold together.Pile cap ialah blok yang dalam dan dibebani berat. Kebanyakan detailing-nya memastikan tie di-anchor tempat strut bertemu dengannya, di atas pile, dan bucu serta muka blok kekal bersatu.

Anchorage. The main bars continue past each pile and are bent up to give a full anchorage length beyond the pile centre line: available length (edge distance − c) + (d − c) ≥ lbd, with lbd = (φ/4)(0.87fyk/fbd) = 894 mm for H25 in C30/37 (fbd = 3.04 N/mm², good bond). For the notes’ cap (400 − 75) + (987.5 − 75) = 1237.5 mm ≥ 894 mm, which makes β valid. The same bars run only 1387.5 mm past the shear section, less than lbd + d = 1881 mm, so they do not count in ρl: the two conditions differ, and both are checked.Anchorage. Bar utama diteruskan melepasi setiap pile dan dibengkokkan ke atas untuk memberi panjang anchorage penuh melepasi garis tengah pile: panjang tersedia (jarak tepi − c) + (d − c) ≥ lbd, dengan lbd = (φ/4)(0.87fyk/fbd) = 894 mm bagi H25 dalam C30/37 (fbd = 3.04 N/mm², good bond). Bagi pile cap dalam nota (400 − 75) + (987.5 − 75) = 1237.5 mm ≥ 894 mm, yang menjadikan β sah. Bar yang sama melepasi shear section hanya 1387.5 mm, kurang daripada lbd + d = 1881 mm, jadi ia tidak dikira dalam ρl: kedua-dua syarat berbeza, dan kedua-duanya disemak.

The rest of the cage. As,min = 0.26(fctm/fyk)bd in both directions of the bottom face, and Mosley puts the same in the top face; EC2 9.8.1(3) lets the top and the sides stay unreinforced where no tension can develop, so the top mat is the course’s choice, not a code demand. Fully lapped horizontal links of at least H12 at no more than 250 mm run round the sides. The column starter bars are tied with links inside the cap. Link mendatar yang bertindih penuh sekurang-kurangnya H12 pada tidak lebih daripada 250 mm mengelilingi sisi. Starter bar column diikat dengan link di dalam pile cap. Why are the tie bars of a pile cap bent up at both ends?Mengapakah bar tie pile cap dibengkokkan ke atas di kedua-dua hujung?

The tie force is greatest at the nodes over the piles, so it must be anchored beyond them, and a straight bar has too little room (400 − 75 = 325 mm against 894 mm). EC2 6.2.2(6) allows β only with the bars fully anchored at the support.Daya tie paling besar di nod di atas pile, jadi ia mesti di-anchor melepasinya, dan bar lurus tidak mempunyai ruang yang cukup (400 − 75 = 325 mm berbanding 894 mm). EC2 6.2.2(6) membenarkan β hanya dengan bar di-anchor sepenuhnya di support.
