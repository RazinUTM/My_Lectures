Write your four answers first, then open the model answers one by one.Tulis empat jawapan anda dahulu, kemudian buka jawapan model satu demi satu.

Answer 1Jawapan 1 Centre line against faceGaris tengah berbanding muka

In one direction the truss carries 2T = Ns/4d, which is the moment at the column centre line, (N/2)(s/2) = Ns/4, divided by d. Beam theory takes the moment at the column face, (N/2)(s/2 − c/2), smaller by the half-width of the column, over z = 0.95d. For the notes’ four-pile cap that is 3178 kN m over 987.5 mm against 2119 kN m over 938 mm: 7399 against 5192 mm² each way. The truss is the safer model where the struts are steep and the cap compact; beam theory suits longer caps.Dalam satu arah truss membawa 2T = Ns/4d, iaitu moment di garis tengah column, (N/2)(s/2) = Ns/4, dibahagi d. Beam theory mengambil moment di muka column, (N/2)(s/2 − c/2), lebih kecil sebanyak separuh lebar column, dibahagi z = 0.95d. Bagi pile cap empat pile dalam nota, ia ialah 3178 kN m dibahagi 987.5 mm berbanding 2119 kN m dibahagi 938 mm: 7399 berbanding 5192 mm² setiap arah. Truss ialah model yang lebih selamat apabila strut curam dan pile cap padat; beam theory sesuai bagi pile cap yang lebih panjang.

Answer 2Jawapan 2 The floor of βLantai β

av = 350 mm is fixed by the plan; 0.5d is 494 mm at 1100 mm depth and grows with d, so av stays below 0.5d and β stays at its floor, 0.25. VEd,red = 0.25 × 4237.5 = 1059.4 kN whatever the depth.
