A1 · 1 markmarkah
Mosley designs a pile cap by the truss analogy rather than beam theory whenMosley membuat design pile cap melalui truss analogy dan bukannya beam theory apabila

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

A compact cap carries its load in direct struts, so a truss models it better than a beam (Mosley §10.7). Longer caps, and the course’s six-pile cap under a moment, go by beam theory (Notes §1, §3).Pile cap yang padat membawa load-nya dalam strut terus, jadi truss memodelkannya lebih baik daripada beam (Mosley §10.7). Pile cap yang lebih panjang, dan pile cap enam pile kursus di bawah moment, menggunakan beam theory (Nota §1, §3).

C1 · 2 marksmarkah
The course notes’ four-pile cap: NEd = 8475 kN, s = 1500 mm, h = 1100 mm, cover 75 mm, H25. One line contains the slip. Which one?Pile cap empat pile dalam nota kursus: NEd = 8475 kN, s = 1500 mm, h = 1100 mm, cover 75 mm, H25. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 4: the bars in one direction serve both trusses of that direction, so they carry 2T: As = 2 × 3699 = 7399 mm², 16H25 each way. 8H25 is half the tie: unsafe. Lines 1 to 3 are right; line 5 follows from line 4.Baris 4: bar dalam satu arah berkhidmat kepada kedua-dua truss arah itu, jadi ia membawa 2T: As = 2 × 3699 = 7399 mm², 16H25 setiap arah. 8H25 ialah separuh tie: tidak selamat. Baris 1 hingga 3 betul; baris 5 adalah akibat baris 4.
