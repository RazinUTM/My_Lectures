Warm-up · answers

C, A, B, and what each means today

W1

C: part of the base lifts

Soil cannot pull. Today the base of a wall is the same strip: keep the resultant in the middle third.

W2

A: N/BL ± 6M/BL²

Per metre of wall, with M = Ne: q = N/B ± 6Ne/B². The largest pressure is at the toe.

W3

B: 0.25

A reduction with its limit. Today: a factor with its direction, by whether the load helps or harms.

Professional Habit 13

Ask of every load: does it help or harm this check?

The weight of the soil

0.9 against overturning, 1.0 against sliding: it helps

The thrust

1.1, 1.35 or 1.5: it harms, in every check

The surcharge

1.5 on its thrust, 0 on its weight on the heel: one load, two effects

The soil’s strength

factor it too: a weaker soil pushes harder and grips less
