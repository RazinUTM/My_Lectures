1. Retaining walls: what they do and how they failRetaining wall: apa yang dilakukannya dan bagaimana ia gagal

≈ 7 min

A retaining wall holds soil at a steeper face than the soil would keep by itself, wherever the ground level changes abruptly: a road in cutting, a basement, a platform on a slope. The soil it holds pushes on it sideways. The wall must send that push into the ground without turning over, sliding or overloading the ground, and each of its parts must carry its bending and shear. This Unit takes the stability; Unit 14 takes the parts.Retaining wall menahan tanah pada muka yang lebih curam daripada yang tanah itu sendiri boleh kekalkan, di mana-mana aras tanah berubah secara mendadak: jalan dalam potongan, basement, pelantar di cerun. Tanah yang ditahan menolak wall ke sisi. Wall mesti menyalurkan tolakan itu ke tanah tanpa terbalik, menggelongsor atau membebankan tanah secara berlebihan, dan setiap bahagiannya mesti memikul bending dan shear-nya. Unit ini mengambil kestabilan; Unit 14 mengambil bahagian-bahagian wall.

The course notes list three ways a wall fails the ground. It overturns when the moment of the thrust about the toe exceeds the moment of the weights. It slides when the thrust exceeds the friction under the base. It settles, or fails in bearing, when the pressure under the toe exceeds what the ground can carry. The three checks use the same table of loads; they differ in the partial factors and in what they compare.Nota kursus menyenaraikan tiga cara wall menggagalkan tanah. Ia terbalik apabila moment thrust pada toe melebihi moment berat. Ia menggelongsor apabila thrust melebihi friction di bawah base. Ia mendap, atau gagal dalam bearing, apabila tekanan di bawah toe melebihi apa yang tanah boleh pikul. Ketiga-tiga semakan menggunakan jadual load yang sama; yang berbeza ialah partial factor dan perkara yang dibandingkan.

2. Active pressure and the two thrustsActive pressure dan dua thrust

≈ 7 min

Soil is not a liquid: it has shearing strength, so it pushes sideways with only a fraction of its vertical pressure. When the wall yields a little, the soil behind it relaxes to its smallest sideways pressure, the active pressure. Rankine’s theory gives that fraction, Ka, from the angle of shearing resistance φ alone.Tanah bukan cecair: ia mempunyai shear strength, jadi ia menolak ke sisi dengan hanya sebahagian daripada tekanan menegaknya. Apabila wall beralah sedikit, tanah di belakangnya mengendur kepada tekanan sisi yang terkecil, iaitu active pressure. Teori Rankine memberi pecahan itu, Ka, daripada sudut shear resistance φ sahaja.

3. The table of loads and moments about the toeJadual load dan moment pada toe

≈ 6 min

Every stability check starts from one table: each load on a metre length of wall, its lever arm about the toe A, and its moment. Build the table once, with characteristic values and no factors; the three checks then pick from its sums.Setiap semakan kestabilan bermula daripada satu jadual: setiap load pada satu meter panjang wall, lever arm-nya pada toe A, dan moment-nya. Bina jadual itu sekali, dengan characteristic value dan tanpa faktor; tiga semakan itu kemudiannya memilih daripada jumlahnya.

The vertical loads are weights: the stem, split into a triangle and a rectangle when it tapers, the base, and the soil standing on the heel (concrete 25 kN/m³, the soil at its own γ). Each acts at its centroid; the arm is measured from A, the end of the toe, because that is the point the wall would turn about. The horizontal loads are the two thrusts of §2, with arms H/3 and H/2 measured up from the underside of the base. Moments of the weights turn the wall back onto its heel: they are restoring. Moments of the thrusts turn it over the toe: they are overturning.Load menegak ialah berat: stem, dibahagi kepada segi tiga dan segi empat tepat apabila ia menirus, base, dan tanah di atas heel (konkrit 25 kN/m³, tanah pada γ-nya sendiri). Setiap satu bertindak di centroid-nya; arm diukur dari A, hujung toe, kerana itulah titik wall akan berputar. Load mendatar ialah dua thrust §2, dengan arm H/3 dan H/2 diukur ke atas dari bahagian bawah base. Moment berat memutar wall kembali ke heel-nya: ia restoring. Moment thrust memutarnya melepasi toe: ia overturning.

The surcharge appears twiceSurcharge muncul dua kaliThe same surcharge pushes the wall over (its thrust, 13.3 kN) and holds it down (its weight on the heel, 20 kN). The two effects are listed apart, because the stability checks treat them in opposite ways: the thrust is an unfavourable variable action and is always counted; the weight on the heel is a favourable variable action, and a favourable variable action is taken as zero (§4). The surcharge might be standing only beyond the heel.Surcharge yang sama menolak wall supaya terbalik (thrust-nya, 13.3 kN) dan menahannya ke bawah (beratnya di atas heel, 20 kN). Dua kesan itu disenaraikan berasingan, kerana semakan kestabilan melayannya secara bertentangan: thrust ialah variable action yang unfavourable dan sentiasa dikira; berat di atas heel ialah variable action yang favourable, dan variable action yang favourable diambil sebagai sifar (§4). Surcharge itu mungkin hanya berada di luar heel.

Two things are left out on purpose. The soil in front of the wall could resist sliding by its passive pressure, but it is often dug out or backfilled loosely later, so the course ignores it, as the notes and Mosley do. The soil over the toe is ignored as well. Both omissions are on the safe side.Dua perkara sengaja ditinggalkan. Tanah di atas toe juga diabaikan. Kedua-dua pengabaian ini berada di pihak yang selamat.

Why a second combination?Mengapa combination kedua?Eurocode 7 (MS EN 1997-1, Design Approach 1) asks for two combinations, and the design must satisfy both. Combination 1 puts the uncertainty on the loads: 1.35 and 1.5 on the actions, the soil at its characteristic strength. Combination 2 puts it on the ground: the actions take 1.0 and 1.3, and the strength of the soil is divided by 1.25, as tan φ. A weaker soil pushes harder (Ka rises) and grips less (μ falls), so both sides of the sliding check move the wrong way. The course notes and Mosley’s sliding check use the load factors only; the RC Design app checks both combinations, and so does this course.Eurocode 7 (MS EN 1997-1, Design Approach 1) meminta dua combination, dan design mesti memenuhi kedua-duanya. 6. Sizing the base: the heel holds, the toe spreadsMenentukan saiz base: heel menahan, toe menyebarkan

≈ 6 min

When a check fails, the base must change. Which end? The heel and the toe do different jobs, and a metre of one is not worth a metre of the other.Apabila satu semakan gagal, base mesti berubah. Hujung yang mana? Heel dan toe melakukan kerja yang berbeza, dan satu meter bagi satu hujung tidak sama nilainya dengan satu meter hujung yang lain.

The heel holds. Every metre of heel carries a column of backfill: 19 × 4.5 = 85.5 kN in the notes’ wall, against 10 kN for the metre of base slab under it. That weight is what the friction and most of the restoring moment come from. If the wall slides, lengthen the heel.Heel menahan. Setiap meter heel memikul satu turus backfill: 19 × 4.5 = 85.5 kN bagi wall dalam nota, berbanding 10 kN bagi satu meter base slab di bawahnya. Berat itulah punca friction dan sebahagian besar restoring moment. Jika wall menggelongsor, panjangkan heel.

The toe spreads. A longer toe adds almost no weight, but it moves the edge A forward: the resultant then meets the base nearer its centre, the eccentricity falls, and so does the toe pressure. If the toe pressure is too high or the resultant leaves the middle third, lengthen the toe.Toe menyebarkan. Toe yang lebih panjang hampir tidak menambah berat, tetapi ia menggerakkan tepi A ke hadapan: resultant kemudiannya bertemu base lebih dekat dengan pusatnya, eccentricity menurun, dan begitu juga tekanan di toe. Jika tekanan di toe terlalu tinggi atau resultant keluar dari middle third, panjangkan toe.

The two jobs pull against each other on a base of fixed width: moving the stem forward lengthens the heel and shortens the toe. For the notes’ 3.2 m base both the sliding check and the toe pressure pass with the toe between 0.32 and 0.66 m. The notes’ 0.8 m toe lies just outside: the wall slides in Combination 2. Bagi base 3.2 m dalam nota, semakan sliding dan tekanan di toe kedua-duanya lulus dengan toe antara 0.32 dan 0.66 m. Toe 0.8 m dalam nota berada sedikit di luar: wall menggelongsor dalam Combination 2. Toe 0.6 m dengan heel 2.2 m lulus setiap semakan tanpa konkrit tambahan berbanding wall yang gagal; memanjangkan heel sahaja (base 3.4 m, Worked Example 13.1) lulus dengan 3 % lebih.
