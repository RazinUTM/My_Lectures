QuestionSoalanA cantilever wall retains 4.0 m of well-drained sand above the ground in front and is founded 0.5 m below it: stem 4.5 m high, 400 mm thick at its root and 240 mm at the top; base 3.2 m wide and 400 mm thick, with a toe of 0.8 m and a heel of 2.0 m. Sand: γ = 19 kN/m³, φ = 35°, c = 0; surcharge 10 kN/m² on the backfill; μ = 0.5; allowable bearing pressure 200 kN/m²; concrete 25 kN/m³. Check the stability of the wall for overturning, sliding and bearing; revise the base if a check fails.Satu cantilever wall menahan 4.0 m pasir bersaliran baik di atas tanah di hadapannya dan diasaskan 0.5 m di bawahnya: stem setinggi 4.5 m, setebal 400 mm di pangkal dan 240 mm di atas; base selebar 3.2 m dan setebal 400 mm, dengan toe 0.8 m dan heel 2.0 m. Pasir: γ = 19 kN/m³, φ = 35°, c = 0; surcharge 10 kN/m² di atas backfill; μ = 0.5; allowable bearing pressure 200 kN/m²; konkrit 25 kN/m³. Semak kestabilan wall bagi overturning, sliding dan bearing; ubah base jika satu semakan gagal.
In the app: cases RW1 (the course notes’ Example 1; RetaningWall.xlsx Ex 12.1) and RW1R (the heel lengthened) of the RC Design Worked Solutions app.Dalam app: kes RW1 (Contoh 1 nota kursus; RetaningWall.xlsx Ex 12.1) dan RW1R (heel dipanjangkan) dalam app RC Design Worked Solutions.

Physical picture.Gambaran fizikal. The sand pushes the wall forward with about 75 kN on every metre of its length. What holds the wall is mostly not concrete: it is the 171 kN of sand standing on the heel, which presses the base onto the ground and gives it friction. The question is whether that weight is enough, once the push is factored up and the grip is factored down.Pasir menolak wall ke hadapan dengan kira-kira 75 kN pada setiap meter panjangnya. Yang menahan wall kebanyakannya bukan konkrit: ia ialah 171 kN pasir yang berada di atas heel, yang menekan base ke tanah dan memberinya friction. Persoalannya ialah sama ada berat itu mencukupi, setelah tolakan difaktorkan ke atas dan cengkaman difaktorkan ke bawah.

Plan.Pelan. Answer 2Jawapan 2 Favourable and unfavourableFavourable dan unfavourable

A partial factor is put where it makes the check harder to pass. In a column the load is what must be resisted, so it takes 1.35. In a stability check the weight is what resists: overstating it would be unsafe, so it takes 0.9 against overturning (EQU) and 1.0 against sliding, while the thrust, which harms, takes 1.1 or 1.35. Dalam semakan kestabilan, berat ialah perkara yang menentang: melebihkannya tidak selamat, jadi ia menerima 0.9 terhadap overturning (EQU) dan 1.0 terhadap sliding, manakala thrust, yang memudaratkan, menerima 1.1 atau 1.35.
