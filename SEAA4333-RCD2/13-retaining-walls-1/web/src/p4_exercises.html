C1 · 2 marksmarkah
A wall 4.4 m high to the underside of its base retains fill with γ = 18 kN/m³ and Ka = 0.2948 under a surcharge of 10 kN/m². One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu wall setinggi 4.4 m hingga bahagian bawah base menahan tambakan dengan γ = 18 kN/m³ dan Ka = 0.2948 di bawah surcharge 10 kN/m². Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 3: the surcharge pressure is a rectangle, so its resultant acts at mid-height, 4.4/2 = 2.2 m, not at a third. Then Mo,Q = 12.97 × 2.2 = 28.53 kN m and the overturning moment is 1.1 × 75.34 + 1.5 × 28.53 = 125.7 kN m: the slip understates it by 11 %. Lines 1 and 2 are right; lines 4 and 5 follow from line 3.Baris 3: tekanan surcharge berbentuk segi empat tepat, jadi resultant-nya bertindak pada separuh tinggi, 4.4/2 = 2.2 m, bukan pada satu pertiga. Maka Mo,Q = 12.97 × 2.2 = 28.53 kN m dan overturning moment ialah 1.1 × 75.34 + 1.5 × 28.53 = 125.7 kN m: kesilapan ini mengurangkannya sebanyak 11 %. Baris 1 dan 2 betul; baris 4 dan 5 adalah akibat baris 3.

C2 · 2 marksmarkah
A wall on a base 3.6 m wide: under service loads the vertical load is 300 kN, the restoring moments about the toe sum to 660 kN m and the overturning moments to 180 kN m. One line contains the slip. Which one?Satu wall di atas base selebar 3.6 m: di bawah service load, load menegak ialah 300 kN, restoring moment pada toe berjumlah 660 kN m dan overturning moment 180 kN m. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 4: x is measured from the toe, but the eccentricity is measured from the centre of the base: e = 3.6/2 − 1.6 = 0.2 m ≤ 0.6 m, inside the middle third. Then q = 300/3.6 ± 6 × 300 × 0.2/3.6² = 83.33 ± 27.78: 111.1 and 55.6 kN/m². A check would have caught it: the triangle of line 5 would need a contact length of 3 × 1.6 = 4.8 m on a 3.6 m base. Lines 1 to 3 are right; line 5 follows from line 4.Baris 4: x diukur dari toe, tetapi eccentricity diukur dari pusat base: e = 3.6/2 − 1.6 = 0.2 m ≤ 0.6 m, di dalam middle third. Maka q = 300/3.6 ± 6 × 300 × 0.2/3.6² = 83.33 ± 27.78: 111.1 dan 55.6 kN/m². Satu semakan boleh mengesannya: segi tiga baris 5 memerlukan panjang sentuhan 3 × 1.6 = 4.8 m di atas base 3.6 m. Baris 1 hingga 3 betul; baris 5 adalah akibat baris 4.
