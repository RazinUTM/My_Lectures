GlossaryGlosari

What to doApa perlu dibuat Look up a term; type in the box to filter. Terms stay in English in both languages; the explanation follows the language you chose.Cari istilah; taip dalam kotak untuk menapis. Istilah kekal dalam bahasa Inggeris dalam kedua-dua bahasa; penjelasan mengikut bahasa yang anda pilih.

Not counted toward progress.Tidak dikira dalam kemajuan.

active pressureThe smallest sideways pressure of a soil, reached when the wall yields a little away from it: p = Ka(γz + w).Tekanan sisi terkecil bagi tanah, dicapai apabila wall beralah sedikit menjauhinya: p = Ka(γz + w).
allowable bearing pressure, qaThe pressure the ground may carry under service loads; the largest pressure under the base, at the toe, is compared with it.Tekanan yang boleh dipikul oleh tanah di bawah service load; tekanan terbesar di bawah base, di toe, dibandingkan dengannya.
angle of shearing resistance, φThe friction angle of the soil; with c = 0 it alone fixes Ka. EC7 Combination 2 uses φd = tan⁻¹(tan φ/1.25).Sudut geseran tanah; dengan c = 0 ia sahaja menentukan Ka. EC7 Combination 2 menggunakan φd = tan⁻¹(tan φ/1.25).
backfillThe soil placed behind the wall after it is built; granular and drained in this course.Tanah yang diletakkan di belakang wall selepas ia dibina; berbutir dan bersaliran dalam kursus ini.
baseThe slab under a cantilever wall: the toe in front of the stem and the heel behind it; width B, thickness h.Slab di bawah cantilever wall: toe di hadapan stem dan heel di belakangnya; lebar B, tebal h.
bearing pressureThe pressure between the base and the ground: q = N/B ± 6Ne/B² with service loads, largest at the toe.Tekanan antara base dengan tanah: q = N/B ± 6Ne/B² dengan service load, terbesar di toe.
buttress wallA wall whose stem is braced by walls in front of it, in compression.Wall yang stem-nya disokong oleh dinding di hadapannya, dalam mampatan.
cantilever wallA stem fixed to a base slab; it stands mainly by the weight of the backfill on its heel. Economical to about 8 m.Stem yang terikat kepada base slab; ia berdiri terutamanya dengan berat backfill di atas heel-nya. Ekonomi hingga kira-kira 8 m.
coefficient of friction, μμ = tan δ between the base and the ground; the sliding resistance is μ times the vertical load. Divided by 1.25 in EC7 Combination 2.μ = tan δ antara base dengan tanah; rintangan sliding ialah μ kali load menegak. Dibahagi dengan 1.25 dalam EC7 Combination 2.
combination 1The load-factor combination of the stability checks: thrusts × 1.35 and 1.5, weights × 1.0, soil at its characteristic strength.Combination load factor bagi semakan kestabilan: thrust × 1.35 dan 1.5, berat × 1.0, tanah pada kekuatan cirinya.
Combination 2 (EC7 DA1)The second combination of MS EN 1997-1 Design Approach 1: actions × 1.0 and 1.3, tan φ and μ divided by 1.25. It often governs sliding.Combination kedua MS EN 1997-1 Design Approach 1: action × 1.0 dan 1.3, tan φ dan μ dibahagi dengan 1.25. Ia sering menentukan sliding.
counterfort wallA wall whose stem and base are tied together by bracing walls behind the stem, in tension; for heights above 6 to 7 m.Wall yang stem dan base-nya diikat bersama oleh dinding penyokong di belakang stem, dalam tegangan; bagi tinggi melebihi 6 hingga 7 m.
eccentricity, eThe distance of the resultant from the centre of the base: e = B/2 − x, with x measured from the toe.Jarak resultant dari pusat base: e = B/2 − x, dengan x diukur dari toe.
EQUThe limit state of static equilibrium, used for overturning: 1.1 and 1.5 on what overturns, 0.9 on what restores.Limit state keseimbangan statik, digunakan bagi overturning: 1.1 dan 1.5 pada perkara yang menterbalikkan, 0.9 pada perkara yang memulihkan.
favourable actionA load that helps the check being made. A favourable permanent action takes 0.9 or 1.0; a favourable variable action counts as zero.Load yang membantu semakan yang dibuat. Permanent action yang favourable menerima 0.9 atau 1.0; variable action yang favourable dikira sifar.
gravity wallA wall of plain concrete or masonry that stands by its own weight, with the thrust line kept in the middle third; for low walls.Wall konkrit biasa atau masonry yang berdiri dengan berat sendiri, dengan garis thrust dikekalkan dalam middle third; bagi wall rendah.
heelThe part of the base behind the stem. The backfill stands on it: its weight gives the friction and most of the restoring moment.Bahagian base di belakang stem. Backfill berada di atasnya: beratnya memberi friction dan sebahagian besar restoring moment.
KaRankine’s coefficient of active pressure for a level granular backfill: (1 − sin φ)/(1 + sin φ); 0.271 for φ = 35°.Pekali active pressure Rankine bagi backfill berbutir yang rata: (1 − sin φ)/(1 + sin φ); 0.271 bagi φ = 35°.
lever armThe distance of a load from the toe A: horizontal for a weight, vertical (H/3 or H/2 above the underside) for a thrust.Jarak satu load dari toe A: mendatar bagi berat, menegak (H/3 atau H/2 di atas bahagian bawah) bagi thrust.
middle thirdThe central third of the base. With the resultant inside it (e ≤ B/6) the whole base stays in contact with the ground.Satu pertiga tengah base. Dengan resultant di dalamnya (e ≤ B/6), seluruh base kekal bersentuhan dengan tanah.
overturningRotation of the wall about its toe; checked with moments about A: 1.1Mo,G + 1.5Mo,Q ≤ 0.9Mr,G.Putaran wall pada toe-nya; disemak dengan moment pada A: 1.1Mo,G + 1.5Mo,Q ≤ 0.9Mr,G.
passive resistanceThe resistance of the soil in front of the base when the wall pushes into it. Ignored in the course checks; used for a key under the base in Unit 14.Rintangan tanah di hadapan base apabila wall menolak ke dalamnya. Diabaikan dalam semakan kursus; digunakan bagi key di bawah base dalam Unit 14.
restoring momentThe moment of a weight about the toe, which turns the wall back onto its heel.Moment satu berat pada toe, yang memutar wall kembali ke heel-nya.
resultantThe single force equal to all the loads on the wall; it meets the base at x = ΔM/N from the toe.Daya tunggal yang setara dengan semua load pada wall; ia bertemu base pada x = ΔM/N dari toe.
slidingMovement of the wall forward on its base; resisted by friction, μ times the permanent vertical load.Pergerakan wall ke hadapan di atas base-nya; ditentang oleh friction, μ kali load menegak permanent.
stemThe vertical cantilever that holds the soil; about 80 mm thick at its root for every metre of backfill.Cantilever menegak yang menahan tanah; setebal kira-kira 80 mm di pangkal bagi setiap meter backfill.
surcharge, wA uniform load on the backfill. It acts twice: a thrust KawH at mid-height (unfavourable) and a weight on the heel (favourable, taken as zero in the stability checks).Load seragam di atas backfill. Ia bertindak dua kali: thrust KawH pada separuh tinggi (unfavourable) dan berat di atas heel (favourable, diambil sifar dalam semakan kestabilan).
thrustThe resultant of the active pressure per metre of wall: Ps = ½KaγH² at H/3 from the soil, Pq = KawH at H/2 from the surcharge.Resultant active pressure bagi setiap meter wall: Ps = ½KaγH² pada H/3 daripada tanah, Pq = KawH pada H/2 daripada surcharge.
toeThe part of the base in front of the stem. A longer toe centres the resultant and lowers the toe pressure. Its end, A, is the point the wall would turn about.Bahagian base di hadapan stem. Toe yang lebih panjang memusatkan resultant dan menurunkan tekanan di toe. Hujungnya, A, ialah titik wall akan berputar.
virtual backThe vertical plane through the end of the heel, from the surface to the underside of the base (height H); the thrust for stability acts on it.Satah menegak melalui hujung heel, dari permukaan hingga bahagian bawah base (tinggi H); thrust bagi kestabilan bertindak padanya.
weep holeA drain through the stem that keeps water from building up behind the wall.Saliran melalui stem yang menghalang air daripada bertakung di belakang wall.
