Unit 14 Retaining Walls II

SEAA 4333 Reinforced Concrete Design II, Week 15, the last Unit. Unit 13 made the wall stand; this Unit makes it strong. A cantilever wall is three cantilevers joined at one corner: the stem bends towards the front, the toe bends up and the heel bends down, so each one cracks on a different face and takes its main bars there.     What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

    Unit 13 made the wall stand: it does not overturn, slide or overload the ground. This Unit makes it strong. A cantilever wall is three cantilevers joined at one corner. The soil pushes the stem towards the front; the ground pushes the toe up; the soil standing on the heel pushes it down harder than the ground pushes back. Each part therefore stretches on a different face, and the main bars go there: the earth face of the stem, the bottom of the toe, the top of the heel. The loads are now those of the STR combination, every one with the factor of what harms: 1.35 and 1.5, and the surcharge on the heel is counted. Each part is designed as a slab one metre wide: bending with z ≤ 0.95d, minimum steel, shear without links, and crack control by the bar spacing. The drawing is finished with the secondary steel, the lap of the starter bars at the root of the stem and the curtailment of the stem bars.     1. Three cantilevers: which face is in tension?Tiga cantilever: muka manakah dalam tension?

    ≈ 6 min

    Unit 13 treated the wall as one rigid body and asked whether the ground could hold it. This Unit opens the body up. A cantilever wall is three slabs joined at one corner, and each of them is a cantilever: the stem stands up from the base, the toe reaches forward from the stem and the heel reaches back. Each one bends, and concrete cracks where it is stretched. Before any number, the designer has to see which face of each part stretches, because that is where the main bars go.Unit 13 menganggap wall sebagai satu jasad tegar dan bertanya sama ada tanah boleh menahannya. Unit ini membuka jasad itu. Cantilever wall ialah tiga slab yang bercantum di satu sudut, dan setiap satunya ialah cantilever: stem berdiri dari base, toe menganjur ke hadapan dari stem dan heel menganjur ke belakang. Setiap satu melentur, dan konkrit retak di tempat ia diregangkan. Sebelum sebarang nombor, pereka bentuk mesti nampak muka manakah bagi setiap bahagian yang meregang, kerana di situlah bar utama diletakkan.

    Take the parts one at a time and ask what pushes on each. The stem is pushed by the soil behind it, towards the front. It is held at its foot, so it bends like a diving board stood on end: the face against the earth stretches and the front face is compressed. Its main bars are vertical, close to the earth face.Ambil setiap bahagian satu demi satu dan tanya apa yang menolaknya. Stem ditolak oleh tanah di belakangnya, ke hadapan. Ia dipegang di kakinya, jadi ia melentur seperti papan anjal yang didirikan: muka yang menghadap tanah meregang dan muka hadapan dimampatkan. Bar utamanya menegak, dekat dengan muka tanah.

    The toe is pushed up by the ground: the bearing pressure is largest here. Only its own weight pushes down, because the soil in front may be dug away and is ignored. The toe bends upwards, like the edge of a pad footing: its bottom stretches.Toe ditolak ke atas oleh tanah: bearing pressure paling besar di sini. Hanya berat sendirinya yang menolak ke bawah, kerana tanah di hadapan mungkin digali dan diabaikan. Toe melentur ke atas, seperti tepi pad footing: bahagian bawahnya meregang.

    The heel is the one that surprises. The ground pushes up on it too, but the backfill standing on it, the surcharge and its own weight push down harder: about 144 kN/m² down against 89 to 119 kN/m² up for the wall of Unit 13. Bars in the bottom of the heel, as on the drawing above, would lie in the compression zone and do nothing.Heel ialah bahagian yang mengejutkan. Tanah menolaknya ke atas juga, tetapi backfill yang berada di atasnya, surcharge dan berat sendirinya menolak ke bawah dengan lebih kuat: kira-kira 144 kN/m² ke bawah berbanding 89 hingga 119 kN/m² ke atas bagi wall Unit 13. Net load menghala ke bawah, heel melentur ke bawah seperti balkoni, dan bahagian atasnya meregang. Bar di bahagian bawah heel, seperti dalam lukisan di atas, akan berada dalam compression zone dan tidak berfungsi.

    2. Design loads: the pressures under the base and the net loadsDesign load: tekanan di bawah base dan net load

    ≈ 8 min

    The stability checks asked whether each load helps or harms the wall as a body, and gave the weights 0.9 or 1.0. The design of the concrete asks a different question: what is the largest bending each part can see? That is the STR limit state, and its combination is the familiar one: 1.35 on every permanent action and 1.5 on every variable action, all acting together.Semakan kestabilan bertanya sama ada setiap load membantu atau memudaratkan wall sebagai satu jasad, dan memberi berat faktor 0.9 atau 1.0. Design konkrit bertanya soalan yang berbeza: apakah bending terbesar yang boleh dialami oleh setiap bahagian? Itulah limit state STR, dan combination-nya ialah yang biasa: 1.35 pada setiap permanent action dan 1.5 pada setiap variable action, semuanya bertindak bersama.

    The weights and the earth thrust come from one source, the soil and the wall that holds it, so they take the same γG = 1.35 (EN 1990, 6.4.3.1(4)). The surcharge takes 1.5, both as a thrust and as a weight on the heel: here its weight is no longer favourable, because it bends the heel. The sums are those of the table of Unit 13; only the factors change:Berat dan earth thrust datang daripada satu punca, iaitu tanah dan wall yang menahannya, jadi kedua-duanya menerima γG = 1.35 yang sama (EN 1990, 6.4.3.1(4)). Surcharge menerima 1.5, sebagai thrust dan juga sebagai berat di atas heel: di sini beratnya tidak lagi favourable, kerana ia melenturkan heel.     Answer 1Jawapan 1 The net load decides the faceNet load menentukan muka

    A slab bends towards the side it is pushed to, and what pushes the heel is the difference between the loads above it and the pressure below it. Above: its own weight, 4.5 m of backfill and the surcharge, 143.9 kN/m² at the design values. Below: the bearing pressure, 119.1 kN/m² at the stem falling to 88.7 kN/m² at the heel end. Sketch the net load first; the bars follow.Slab melentur ke arah ia ditolak, dan yang menolak heel ialah beza antara load di atasnya dengan tekanan di bawahnya. Di atas: berat sendirinya, 4.5 m backfill dan surcharge, 143.9 kN/m² pada nilai design. Di bawah: bearing pressure, 119.1 kN/m² di stem berkurang kepada 88.7 kN/m² di hujung heel.     Answer 2Jawapan 2 Two questions, two free bodiesDua soalan, dua free body

    Each check has its own free body. For the bending of the stem the body is the stem alone, cut at the top of the base where it is fixed, and only the pressure above the cut bends it: 4.5 m.     KThe normalised moment MEd/(bd²fck); below 0.167 the section is singly reinforced. In walls it is small (0.01 to 0.05), so z = 0.95d.Moment ternormal MEd/(bd²fck); di bawah 0.167 section adalah singly reinforced. Dalam wall ia kecil (0.01 hingga 0.05), jadi z = 0.95d.
    keyA downstand cast under the base. The soil in front of it resists sliding passively: Hp = ½Kpγak², over its depth below the base only.Unjuran ke bawah yang dituang di bawah base. Tanah di hadapannya menentang sliding secara pasif: Hp = ½Kpγak², sepanjang kedalamannya di bawah base sahaja.
    KpRankine’s coefficient of passive pressure, (1 + sin φ)/(1 − sin φ) = 1/Ka; 3.69 for φ = 35°.Pekali passive pressure Rankine, (1 + sin φ)/(1 − sin φ) = 1/Ka; 3.69 bagi φ = 35°.
    lap length, l0The overlap of two bars that passes the force from one to the other: l0 = α6lb,rqd, with α6 = 1.5 when all bars lap at one section; about 54φ in C30/37.Pertindihan dua bar yang memindahkan daya daripada satu kepada yang lain: l0 = α6lb,rqd, dengan α6 = 1.5 apabila semua bar di-lap pada satu section; kira-kira 54φ dalam C30/37.
    lever arm, zThe distance between the tension steel and the centre of compression: z = d[0.5 + √(0.25 − K/1.134)], not more than 0.95d.Jarak antara tension steel dengan pusat compression: z = d[0.5 + √(0.25 − K/1.134)], tidak melebihi 0.95d.
    net loadWhat is left on the toe or the heel when the pressure from below is set against the loads from above. Upwards on the toe, downwards on the heel.Baki pada toe atau heel apabila tekanan dari bawah dibandingkan dengan load dari atas. Ke atas pada toe, ke bawah pada heel.
    passive pressureThe largest sideways pressure a soil can offer when it is pushed: Kpγz. It needs movement to develop, so a design should not rely on all of it.Tekanan sisi terbesar yang boleh diberikan oleh tanah apabila ia ditolak: Kpγz. Ia memerlukan pergerakan untuk terhasil, jadi sesuatu design tidak wajar bergantung pada kesemuanya.
    Ph, PvThe horizontal and vertical components of the thrust of a sloping backfill: P cos θ at H/3 and P sin θ at the heel end.Komponen mendatar dan menegak bagi thrust backfill bercerun: P cos θ pada H/3 dan P sin θ di hujung heel.
    quasi-permanent loadThe load that acts most of the time: the permanent actions plus ψ2 = 0.3 of the surcharge. Crack control uses the steel stress under it.Load yang bertindak pada kebanyakan masa: permanent action ditambah ψ2 = 0.3 daripada surcharge. Crack control menggunakan steel stress di bawahnya.
    secondary steelBars at right angles to the main bars, along the wall: at least 25 % of the vertical steel of the stem and not less than As,min. Also called distribution steel.Bar yang bersudut tepat dengan bar utama, sepanjang wall: sekurang-kurangnya 25 % daripada steel menegak stem dan tidak kurang daripada As,min. Juga dipanggil distribution steel.
    sloping backfillA backfill whose surface rises behind the wall at θ. Ka is larger, the thrust acts parallel to the surface, and the virtual back is taller by a2 tan θ.Backfill yang permukaannya naik di belakang wall pada θ. Ka lebih besar, thrust bertindak selari dengan permukaan, dan virtual back lebih tinggi sebanyak a2 tan θ.
    starter barsBars cast into the base and standing out of it, to which the stem bars are lapped after the base is concreted.Bar yang dituang ke dalam base dan menonjol keluar daripadanya, yang di-lap dengan bar stem selepas base dikonkrit.
    STRThe limit state of strength of the structure, used for the members: 1.35 on permanent actions, 1.5 on variable actions.Limit state kekuatan struktur, digunakan bagi bahagian-bahagian wall: 1.35 pada permanent action, 1.5 pada variable action.
    submerged unit weight, γ′γsat − γw: the weight with which soil below the water table presses on itself; 10.19 kN/m³ for γsat = 20 kN/m³.γsat − γw: berat yang digunakan oleh tanah di bawah water table untuk menekan dirinya sendiri; 10.19 kN/m³ bagi γsat = 20 kN/m³.
    Table 7.3NThe table of EC2 that gives the largest bar spacing for a steel stress: 300 mm at 160 N/mm² down to 50 mm at 360 N/mm², for a crack width of 0.3 mm.Jadual EC2 yang memberi jarak bar terbesar bagi satu steel stress: 300 mm pada 160 N/mm² hingga 50 mm pada 360 N/mm², bagi crack width 0.3 mm.
    tension faceThe face of a member that stretches when it bends: the earth face of the stem, the bottom of the toe, the top of the heel. The main bars go there.Muka sesuatu bahagian yang meregang apabila ia melentur: muka tanah bagi stem, bahagian bawah toe, bahagian atas heel. Bar utama diletakkan di situ.
    tip load, Pv,d1.35Pv: the vertical component of a sloping thrust, acting as a line load at the end of the heel cantilever.1.35Pv: komponen menegak thrust bercerun, bertindak sebagai line load di hujung cantilever heel.
    upliftThe upward water pressure under the base when water stands in the backfill; it reduces the vertical load and so the friction.Tekanan air ke atas di bawah base apabila air bertakung dalam backfill; ia mengurangkan load menegak dan dengan itu friction.
    water tableThe level below which the soil is saturated. Below it the wall carries the submerged soil pressure and the full water pressure.Aras yang di bawahnya tanah adalah tepu. Di bawahnya wall memikul tekanan tanah tenggelam dan tekanan air sepenuhnya.
    wedgeThe triangle of soil that stands on the heel above the level of the top of the stem when the backfill slopes: a weight in the table and a triangular load on the heel.Segi tiga tanah yang berada di atas heel melebihi aras bahagian atas stem apabila backfill bercerun: satu berat dalam jadual dan load segi tiga pada heel.
    weep holeA drain through the stem that lets the water behind the wall out.Saliran melalui stem yang mengeluarkan air di belakang wall.
