B, C, A, and what each means today

W1

B: the underside of the base

That was the whole body. Today the stem is cut out at the top of the base: its diagram stops there.

W2

C: zero

It helped then, so it was left out. Today it bends the heel: it harms, and takes 1.5.

W3

A: na²/3

The resultant na/2 acts 2a/3 from the support. The net load on the toe and the heel is largest at the free end.
