What this Unit coversApa yang Unit ini liputi

Unit 13 made the wall stand: it does not overturn, slide or overload the ground. This Unit makes it strong. A cantilever wall is three cantilevers joined at one corner. The soil pushes the stem towards the front; the ground pushes the toe up; the soil standing on the heel pushes it down harder than the ground pushes back. Each part therefore stretches on a different face, and the main bars go there: the earth face of the stem, the bottom of the toe, the top of the heel. The loads are now those of the STR combination, every one with the factor of what harms: 1.35 and 1.5, and the surcharge on the heel is counted. Each part is designed as a slab one metre wide: bending with z ≤ 0.95d, minimum steel, shear without links, and crack control by the bar spacing. The drawing is finished with the secondary steel, the lap of the starter bars at the root of the stem and the curtailment of the stem bars. The last section asks what changes when the backfill slopes, when a key is cast under the base, and when water rises behind the wall.Unit 13 menjadikan wall berdiri: ia tidak terbalik, tidak menggelongsor dan tidak membebankan tanah secara berlebihan. Unit ini menjadikannya kuat. Cantilever wall ialah tiga cantilever yang bercantum di satu sudut. Tanah menolak stem ke hadapan; tanah di bawah menolak toe ke atas; tanah yang berada di atas heel menolaknya ke bawah lebih kuat daripada tolakan balas tanah di bawah. Oleh itu setiap bahagian meregang pada muka yang berbeza, dan bar utama diletakkan di situ: muka tanah bagi stem, bahagian bawah toe, bahagian atas heel. Load kini ialah load combination STR, setiap satu dengan faktor bagi perkara yang memudaratkan: 1.35 dan 1.5, dan surcharge di atas heel dikira. Setiap bahagian direka bentuk sebagai slab selebar satu meter: bending dengan z ≤ 0.95d, minimum steel, shear tanpa link, dan crack control melalui jarak bar. Lukisan dilengkapkan dengan secondary steel, lap bagi starter bar di pangkal stem dan curtailment bar stem. Seksyen terakhir bertanya apa yang berubah apabila backfill bercerun, apabila key dituang di bawah base, dan apabila air naik di belakang wall.

Recall 3Ingat semula 3

Unit 13: for stability the soil thrust was taken over H = 4.9 m, to the underside of the base. The stem itself is 4.5 m high. The soil thrust on the stem alone is what fraction of that thrust?Unit 13: bagi kestabilan, thrust tanah diambil sepanjang H = 4.9 m, hingga bahagian bawah base. Stem itu sendiri setinggi 4.5 m. Thrust tanah pada stem sahaja ialah pecahan berapa daripada thrust itu?

The thrust of the soil grows with the square of the height: (4.5/4.9)² = 0.84, 52.1 of 61.8 kN/m. The stem is designed for the pressure on its own height; the whole wall was checked for the pressure on the virtual back (Notes §3).Thrust tanah bertambah dengan kuasa dua tinggi: (4.5/4.9)² = 0.84, 52.1 daripada 61.8 kN/m.
