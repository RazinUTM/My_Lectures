C1 · 2 marksmarkah
The heel of a wall is 2.4 m long on a base 3.6 m wide and 0.4 m thick. The design pressure under the base is 140 kN/m² at the toe and 80 kN/m² at the heel end; the stem is 4.0 m high, γ = 18 kN/m³, surcharge 10 kN/m²; d = 349 mm. One line contains the slip. Which one?Heel satu wall sepanjang 2.4 m di atas base selebar 3.6 m dan setebal 0.4 m. Design pressure di bawah base ialah 140 kN/m² di toe dan 80 kN/m² di hujung heel; stem setinggi 4.0 m, γ = 18 kN/m³, surcharge 10 kN/m²; d = 349 mm. Satu baris mengandungi kesilapan. Yang mana?

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:

Line 4: the net load is largest at the free end, so the triangle has its peak there and its resultant lies 2a/3 from the stem: (45.7 − 5.7) × 2.4²/3 = 76.8, not 38.4. MEd = 16.42 + 76.8 = 93.2 kN m/m and As = 646 mm²/m, more than the 565 mm²/m of H12 at 200: H12 at 150 at least. The a²/6 belongs to a triangle with its peak at the support. The slip hides 41 % of the moment (Notes §4).Baris 4: net load paling besar di hujung bebas, jadi puncak segi tiga berada di situ dan resultant-nya terletak 2a/3 dari stem: (45.7 − 5.7) × 2.4²/3 = 76.8, bukan 38.4. MEd = 16.42 + 76.8 = 93.2 kN m/m dan As = 646 mm²/m, melebihi 565 mm²/m bagi H12 pada 200: sekurang-kurangnya H12 pada 150. a²/6 ialah bagi segi tiga dengan puncak di support. Kesilapan ini menyembunyikan 41 % daripada moment (Nota §4).

Before you answer:Sebelum menjawab:
