# SEAA 4333 — Bahasa Melayu glossary

The Malay text of the Unit pages, the exercises, the Labs and the course home follows one rule, stated in the README:
explanations are in Malay, technical terms stay in English. This file records how that rule is applied, so a later edit
does not decide the grey zone again. Audit of 19 Nov 2026: 929 changes in the sources, listed by kind in `DECISIONS.md`.

## Technical terms kept in English (Tier 2)

A term stays in English when it would appear as it is in an English textbook or in EC2. A multi-word term stays whole
(*shear reinforcement*, not *tetulang shear*). No Malay gloss in brackets after an English term, and no English gloss
after a Malay word.

## Everyday Malay words kept (Tier 1 and decided grey zone)

| Malay | English | Why it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paksi | axis (*paksi z*, *kedua-dua paksi*) | school mathematics word; *axial* is English |
| tanah, tekanan, tekanan tanah | soil, pressure | everyday; English inside fixed terms (*bearing pressure*, *earth pressure coefficient*, *peak velocity pressure*, *passive pressure*) |
| konkrit | concrete | DBP loanword; English inside fixed terms (*plain concrete*, *concrete cover*) |
| tapak | the base of a footing | everyday; *footing* and *base* are also used |
| lapisan, lebar, tebal, ketebalan, putaran, kekuatan, kestabilan | layer, width, thick, thickness, rotation, strength, stability | everyday; *shear strength* is English |
| melentur, bergoyang, terbalik, menggelongsor, mendap, retak, disokong oleh | bend, sway, overturn, slide, settle, crack, supported by | everyday verbs; the nouns of design checks are English (*overturning*, *sliding*, *sway frame*) |

## Spelling and form

- DBP spelling: *reka bentuk*, *direka bentuk*; *tidak* in full (*tidak linear*), never *tak*.
- An English word takes Malay affixes with a hyphen: *shear-nya*, *bar-nya*, *di-design*, *di-anchor*, *meng-anchor*.
- Register: *anda* for the student and *saya* for Dr RJ, *kita* for a step done together; never *awak*, *kamu*, *engkau*;
  no clipped forms (*je*, *ni*, *tu*, *tak*, *nak*, *dah*).
