Reinforced Concrete Design II

Semester 1, 2026/27: 12 October 2026 to 24 January 2027. Five topics in fourteen Units, each designed to Eurocode 2: staircases, frame analysis, columns, foundations and retaining walls. Every Unit page has a plan, notes, slides, model answers, exercises with new numbers on every attempt, and an Explorer.Semester 1, 2026/27: 12 Oktober 2026 hingga 24 Januari 2027. Lima topik dalam empat belas Unit, setiap satu di-design mengikut Eurocode 2: staircase, frame analysis, column, foundation dan retaining wall. Setiap halaman Unit mempunyai pelan, nota, slides, jawapan model, latihan dengan nombor baharu pada setiap cubaan, dan Explorer.
