Critical sections of a pad footing

A square pad under a square column and an axial load. The Lab checks bending at the column face, vertical shear at d, punching at 2d and at every perimeter a ≤ 2d (EC2 6.4.4(2)), and the column face, by the course method.

Course method of SEAA 4333 (course notes; Mosley, Bungey & Hulse §10.1–10.4; MS EN 1992-1-1): the plan from the service loads, A = (Gk + Qk + W)/qall; the ultimate pressure pEd = NEd/A without the self-weight; e ≤ L/6: p = (N/A)(1 ± 6e/L); e > L/6: Lc = 3(L/2 − e), pmax = 2N/(BLc); bending at the column face; vertical shear at d, with ρl only for bars anchored lbd + d beyond the section (EC2 6.2.2, Fig. 6.3); punching at 2d and at every a ≤ 2d with VEd,red = NEd − pEdA(a) and vRd = vRd,c·2d/a (EC2 6.4.4(2)); the column face against 0.4νfcd (EC2 6.4.5(3), 2014 amendment).
