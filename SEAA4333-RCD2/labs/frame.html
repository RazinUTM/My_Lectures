How much do we lose?

Three methods on the same frame and the same loads (Load Set 1): the continuous beam with one-free-joint columns (Unit 3, five cycles), the two-free-joint sub-frames and the one-level sub-frame (four cycles each).

Effect

The values are the envelopes of the Load Set 1 cases. The hogging at B has two values in a frame, one for each beam; the columns take the difference. For your own frame, set its spans and loads in tab 1.

The effects revealed so far

Course method of SEAA 4333 (course notes, Frame Analysis; RDF §11.0 and Table 3.15; Mosley, Bungey & Hulse §3.4; MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.3, with the Malaysian National Annex): the arrangements of Load Set 1 and Load Set 2 with wmax = 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk and wmin = 1.35Gk; the beams of one floor as a continuous beam on knife-edge supports, by moment distribution with the end spans at ¾K and the pins released once; shears and span moments by statics; the column moments by the one-free-joint sub-frame (far ends fixed, beams at half stiffness, wmax on one side and wmin on the other); the one-level sub-frame (the floor with the columns above and below, full K, far ends fixed) and the two-free-joint sub-frames (one beam with its columns, the neighbours at ½K, nothing carried to the fixed ends), four balancing cycles as the notes. Units: m, kN.
