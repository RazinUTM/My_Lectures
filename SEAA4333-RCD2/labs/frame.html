Course method of SEAA 4333 (course notes, Frame Analysis; RDF §11.0 and Table 3.15; Mosley, Bungey & Hulse §3.4; MS EN 1992-1-1, 5.1.3, with the Malaysian National Annex): the arrangements of Load Set 1 and Load Set 2 with wmax = 1.35Gk + 1.5Qk and wmin = 1.35Gk; the beams of one floor as a continuous beam on knife-edge supports, by moment distribution with the end spans at ¾K and the pins released once; shears and span moments by statics; the column moments by the one-free-joint sub-frame (far ends fixed, beams at half stiffness, wmax on one side and wmin on the other). Units: m, kN.
