Design challenge · after class

The notes’ wall slides in EC7 Combination 2. Keep its stem, its base thickness, its soil and its loads; change only the toe and the heel. Make every check pass with no more concrete: a section of 2.72 m² or less, that is, a base of 3.2 m or less.

The passive resistance of the soil in front of the wall and the soil over the toe are ignored, as in the course notes and Mosley. The concrete (the stem, the toe and the heel) is designed in Unit 14: a base that passes here may still be too thin.

Course method of SEAA 4333 (course notes; Mosley, Bungey & Hulse §10.8; MS EN 1990 and MS EN 1997-1), per metre of wall: Rankine Ka = (1 − sin φ)/(1 + sin φ) for a level, granular, drained backfill; the thrust on the virtual back over H, from the surface to the underside of the base; moments about the toe A; overturning (EQU) 1.1Mo,G + 1.5Mo,Q ≤ 0.9Mr,G; sliding 1.35Hk,G + 1.5Hk,Q ≤ μVk,G and, in EC7 Design Approach 1 Combination 2, (Hk,G + 1.3Hk,Q)Ka,d/Ka ≤ (μ/1.25)Vk,G with φd = tan⁻¹(tan φ/1.25); the surcharge on the heel left out of every resistance; bearing with service loads, e = B/2 − x ≤ B/6 and q = N/B ± 6Ne/B² ≤ qa. The toe pressure at the ULS (combination 1 with each weight × 1.35 where it raises the toe pressure, combination 2 and EC7 Combination 2) is shown as the app reports it and compared with qa, on the safe side. Passive resistance in front of the wall is ignored. Units: m, kN.
