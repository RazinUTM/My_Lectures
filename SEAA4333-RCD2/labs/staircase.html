From waist to load on plan

Stair loads are taken per m² on plan, because the span and the people on the stair are both measured on plan. The sloping waist and the steps make the flight heavier than its landing.

Course method (SEAA 4333 notes; Mosley, Bungey & Hulse §8.8): M = FL/10 when at least one end is continuous, FL/8 when both ends are simply supported. The same FL/10 is the hogging moment at a continuous support.

Where the bars go

Bottom bars carry the span. Top bars carry the hogging moment over a continuous support, and a simple support still gets some top steel against partial fixity.

Bottom bars at the kink: which detail?

A

Wrong. Tension straightens the bent bar, which pulls through the cover at the inside corner and spalls the concrete.

B

Right. The bars cross at the corner and each runs on into the far face, where it is anchored in the compression zone.
